What actually ruins floors

It is almost never the material.

Creaking, gapping, cupping and lifting are subfloor problems wearing a flooring costume. A quote that does not mention the subfloor has not looked at it, and the cost of fixing it afterwards is the whole floor.

Every one of these four steps is a line on our quote, priced separately so you can see what you are paying for.

01

We check level before we quote

A 2m straight edge across the room. More than 3mm of deviation and it needs addressing, whatever goes on top.

02

Moisture test on anything solid

Concrete slabs hold water for months after pouring. Laying wood onto a damp slab destroys it and voids every warranty.

03

Levelling compound where needed

Priced separately and shown on the quote as its own line, because it is the thing cheap quotes leave out.

04

Acclimatisation

Wood and vinyl sit in the room for 48 hours before fitting. Skipping this is why floors gap in winter.

Afterwards

Four things that double the life.

Never steam clean wood

Not engineered, not solid, not laminate. Steam drives water into the joints and the damage is permanent.

Felt pads, replaced yearly

They wear flat and start collecting grit, at which point they are sandpaper on legs.

Mats at every external door

Grit is what wears a floor, not feet. A good mat outlasts several refinishes.

Humidity between 40 and 60%

Wood moves with humidity. The gaps that appear in winter usually close again in spring.

Questions

Asked in the showroom, every week.

Which floor is best?+

There is no single answer, which is why this page opens with a comparison rather than a recommendation. It depends on the room, the subfloor, whether it gets wet, and whether anything with claws lives there. Filter by room above and the shortlist gets short quickly.

Do I need to level the subfloor?+

Often, and it is the most common thing missing from a cheap quote. More than 3mm of deviation over 2m makes any floating floor creak and voids most manufacturer warranties. We check before quoting and it appears as its own line.

Can I have wood over underfloor heating?+

Engineered wood yes, solid hardwood no. Solid timber moves too much with the heat cycle and will cup or gap. Tile and vinyl are both excellent over heating.

How long from order to fitted?+

Stock lines are ten to fourteen days. Special order runs four to six weeks, and we will tell you which yours is before you commit rather than after the deposit.

Do you move furniture and take the old floor away?+

Both included on quotes over a certain size, and itemised on smaller ones rather than sprung on you. Old carpet and underlay go for recycling where the facility exists.
