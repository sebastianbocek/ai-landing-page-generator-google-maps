Wood fire. Short menu.

Open Wednesday to Sunday from 5pm. Everything crosses live oak before it reaches the pass, the list changes when the market does, and nobody will rush you out for the second seating. Walk-ins are always welcome at the bar; the dining room takes reservations.

The room

Thirty-eight seats, one open hearth.

Brick, oak, low light and a counter that looks straight into the fire. Loud enough to talk over, quiet enough to be heard.

No reservation?

Fourteen seats never take a booking.

The bar and the counter that faces the fire are walk-in only, every night we are open. Put your name down at the host stand and have a glass while you wait — the wait is usually under thirty minutes before seven, and cancellations tend to open the dining room around six.

Finding us

Street

418 Kiln Street, Riverbend — the black door under the awning, no sign.

Cross street

Between Foundry Row and 9th, mid-block on the north side.

Parking

Metered on Kiln until 6pm, free after. The lot behind the building opens to guests at 5pm.

Transit

Foundry Row station, four minutes on foot. The 14 and 31 buses stop at 9th.

Private dining

One long oak table at the back of the room, twelve to eighteen guests, curtained off when you want it to be.

Set menus start at sixty-five per person and are written with you rather than handed to you. Whole-room buyouts run Wednesday and Thursday evenings from thirty-eight guests, and the kitchen will build around a dietary restriction if we know about it a week out.
