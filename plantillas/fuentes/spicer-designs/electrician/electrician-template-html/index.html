Questions

Asked on nearly every call.

Do I need a permit for this?+

Anything that adds a circuit, alters the panel, or changes the service does. We pull it under our licence, meet the inspector, and hand you the signed card. The fee is inside the quoted price rather than billed after.

Why does my breaker keep tripping?+

A breaker doing its job is not a faulty breaker. Repeated tripping is an overloaded circuit, a failing appliance, or a neutral fault — and swapping the breaker for a larger one is how houses burn down. Diagnosis first, always.

Can you add to my existing panel?+

Often yes. Some brands from the sixties and seventies have known failure modes and cannot be safely added to. We will tell you which you have and show you the bus bar rather than quietly quoting a replacement.

Is the quote fixed?+

Yes for everything on the circuit schedule. Where a job depends on what is inside the wall, you get a survey price and a fixed quote after, before any further work starts.

How fast can you get here?+

Standard work is same-week. Burning smells, hot outlets and open neutrals are taken out of order at any hour, with no after-hours premium.
