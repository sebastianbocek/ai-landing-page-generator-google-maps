The promise that matters

The same person, every time.

A rotating cleaner means explaining your house every fortnight, and it is why most people eventually give up on agencies. Your cleaner is assigned once and stays assigned.

When illness or holiday makes that impossible you will hear from us before the day, not find somebody unfamiliar at the door — and whoever covers arrives with your checklist and your notes already in hand.

Questions

Asked before the first visit.

Will I get the same cleaner?+

Yes, on recurring plans. Where illness or holiday makes that impossible you will be told in advance rather than finding a stranger at the door, and whoever covers gets your checklist and your notes.

What exactly is included?+

The room-by-room checklist on this page is the whole thing. Anything not on it can be added and priced. Nothing on it gets skipped because time ran short — if a visit needs longer, that is our problem rather than yours.

Do I need to be home?+

No, and most clients are not. Keys are held under a numbered system with no address attached, and the key policy on this page sets out exactly how that works.

What if something gets damaged?+

Public liability cover, and the certificate number is printed on every invoice rather than produced on request. Breakages get reported to you the same day, including the ones you would never have noticed.

Can I skip a week?+

Yes, with 48 hours notice and no charge. Skipping repeatedly moves you to the fortnightly rate, which is usually the honest plan anyway.
