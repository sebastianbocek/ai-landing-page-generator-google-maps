Questions

Asked at every valuation.

How do you decide the asking price?+

From completed sales of genuinely comparable properties in the last six months, adjusted for condition, aspect and plot. You get the comparables with the valuation so you can check the reasoning rather than take a number on trust.

What is your commission?+

1.2% on completion, no upfront marketing fee and no withdrawal charge. It is published here rather than saved for the appointment.

How long will it take to sell?+

The sold record on this page lists days on market for every recent sale including the slow ones — one sat for 78 days. That is a more honest answer than quoting an average.

Will you tell me not to buy something?+

Yes, and we have. An agent who has never talked a buyer out of a house is being paid by the wrong outcome.

Do you handle rentals?+

Tenant placement and full management, for landlords who would rather not take the calls at eleven at night.
