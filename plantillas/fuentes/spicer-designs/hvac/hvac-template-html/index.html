Straight answers

Questions we get every peak week.

Q1

How fast can you get here in a heat wave?

Maintenance plan members get priority dispatch and go to the front of the board. Everyone else is same or next day during peak weeks, and we quote that honestly on the call rather than booking you in and slipping the date twice.

Q2

What does the diagnostic actually cost?

A flat $89 diagnostic, charged once, waived entirely for plan members. It covers the full system check and a written repair price. You approve the number before a single part comes off the truck.

Q3

Do you carry parts or is it a second visit?

Capacitors, contactors, ignitors, flame sensors, blower motors and the common control boards ride on every truck. Roughly nine calls in ten are finished on the first visit.
