Before you claim

Four things that catch most claims.

Pre-existing exclusions

The commonest reason claims fail. Anything noted in the records before the policy started, including a passing mention, is normally excluded permanently.

Per-condition limits

A $4,000 lifetime limit on a chronic condition sounds generous and is not, once you are three years into managing it.

Twelve-month policies

Cover a condition for a year from first sign, then stop. Fine for injuries, poor for anything ongoing.

We will tell you first

Before treatment, whether a claim is likely to succeed. Finding out afterwards helps nobody and we would rather have the awkward conversation early.

Questions

Including the ones people feel awkward asking.

How much is a consultation?+

It is published on this page. Standard, extended and out-of-hours are all listed. Being unable to find out is the most common reason people delay bringing an animal in, and delay is what makes things expensive.

Will I see the same vet?+

Where you want to, yes, and it matters most for ongoing conditions. Ask at booking and we will hold the appointment for the clinician who knows the case rather than the next one free.

Will my insurance cover this?+

Often, and often not in the way people expect. Pre-existing exclusions and per-condition limits catch most failed claims. We will tell you before treatment whether a claim is likely to succeed.

What counts as an emergency?+

The six signs listed on this page are the ones where you should ring immediately rather than wait for morning. If your animal is not on that list and you are still worried, ring anyway — nobody here will make you feel foolish for it.

Do you offer payment plans?+

For treatment over a threshold, yes, arranged before rather than after. Ask at the consultation. It is a normal request and it is asked far less often than it should be.
