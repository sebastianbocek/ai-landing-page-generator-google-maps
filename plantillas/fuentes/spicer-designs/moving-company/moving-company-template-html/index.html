Questions

Asked before every booking.

Why do you insist on a survey?+

Because a quote given over the phone is a guess, and guesses get corrected on moving day when you have a van in the drive and no leverage. A twenty-minute video survey is enough for most homes, and the price after it is fixed.

What happens if something breaks?+

The cover, the excess and the claim window are all published on this page before you book. Standard cover is included; full replacement value is available and priced per hundred declared. Damage is recorded at the final walk-through rather than argued about afterwards.

Hourly or fixed price?+

Both are published above with what each suits and where each falls down. Hourly is usually cheaper for small local moves with easy access; fixed is safer for anything with stairs, distance or a completion deadline. We will tell you which is cheaper for your move even when it is the wrong answer for us.

Do you dismantle furniture?+

Yes, and reassemble at the other end. Beds, wardrobes and tables are standard. Anything requiring a specialist — pianos, safes, certain flat-pack that will not survive a second build — is flagged at the survey.

What if my completion is delayed?+

It happens on roughly one move in six. We hold the load in the van until close of day at no charge, and overnight storage is charged at a published daily rate rather than an emergency one.
