What we treat

Four things we do, and do properly.

A short list on purpose. Everything below is planned, priced and finished in this building rather than referred across town halfway through.

01Treatment

Cleanings & exams

A six-month recall with digital X-rays and an oral cancer screening included rather than itemised. Your hygienist keeps the same chair, so somebody who has seen your mouth before is the one telling you what changed.

  • Digital X-rays at a fraction of film dosage
  • Oral cancer screening at every recall
  • Gum measurements charted and compared
02Treatment

Crowns & fillings

Ceramic crowns are scanned, designed and milled in the building, so most cases finish in one visit instead of two. No impression putty, no temporary crown coming loose over a weekend.

  • Same-day ceramic, milled on site
  • Digital scan instead of impression trays
  • Shade matched under natural light
03Treatment

Implants

Placement and restoration are handled under one roof, so nobody is referred out mid-treatment and nobody argues about whose part failed. 3D imaging first, a fixed quote second, surgery third.

  • Cone-beam 3D imaging before planning
  • Guided placement from a printed surgical stent
  • One fixed quote covering post and crown
04Treatment

Clear aligners

Scanned, planned and monitored in-practice. You see the simulated finish and the week-by-week timeline before you commit a dollar, and refinement trays are already in the price.

  • Simulated result before you commit
  • Six-week review appointments
  • Refinements and retainers included

Your first visit

An hour, and no treatment you did not agree to.

  1. 1

    Benefits checked first

    We verify your plan before you arrive, not after treatment. That means the estimate we hand you already reflects what is covered.

  2. 2

    A proper look

    Full exam, digital X-rays, gum charting and an oral cancer screening. Roughly forty-five minutes, and nothing is treated on the same day unless you are in pain.

  3. 3

    The plan, in writing

    You leave with a printed treatment plan showing every item and its price. Urgent, recommended and optional work are listed separately so you can choose.

  4. 4

    You decide the pace

    Book it all, stage it over two years, or do the urgent part only. Interest-free payment plans are available on anything over five hundred dollars.

Before you call

The questions we answer every day.

Do you take my insurance?

Most major PPO plans are accepted, and we verify your benefits before your first visit rather than after treatment. The written estimate you receive already has your plan's share taken off it, so the number at the bottom is the number you pay.

What if I don't have insurance?

The in-house membership plan covers preventive visits outright and discounts restorative work by twenty percent. It is a flat monthly fee with no deductible and no annual maximum, and every price is published on this page rather than quoted on the phone.

How long until I can be seen?

Routine appointments are usually within the same week, and the picker above shows genuine open chairs rather than a request form. Pain, swelling or a broken tooth is triaged the same day — call and describe it, and we will find room.

I am genuinely anxious about dentists. Can you work with that?

Yes, and you should say so when you book so we can put you in a longer slot. Nothing is treated at the first visit unless you are in pain, and we use a stop signal you set yourself. Nitrous oxide and oral sedation are both available.

Can I spread the cost of larger treatment?

Anything over five hundred dollars can be split across interest-free instalments arranged in the practice. There is no credit application for plans under two thousand dollars and no early-settlement penalty on any of them.
