FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do treatments hurt? What about pain management?

Most injectable treatments involve minimal discomfort—many clients describe it as a small pinch. We use ultra-fine needles, topical numbing cream (when appropriate), and ice to maximize your comfort. Laser and energy-based treatments may cause mild warmth or tingling. We'll discuss pain management options during your consultation and ensure you're comfortable throughout your treatment.

How much downtime should I expect?

Most treatments have minimal to no downtime. Injectables: possible mild swelling or bruising for 1-3 days (easily covered with makeup). HydraFacials and light peels: immediate glow, no downtime. Laser resurfacing and deeper peels: 5-7 days of visible peeling/redness. CoolSculpting: possible numbness, redness, or swelling for a few days. We'll provide a detailed aftercare guide specific to your treatment.

How long do results last?

Results vary by treatment. Botox: 3-4 months. Dermal fillers: 6-18 months depending on product and location. Chemical peels and facials: 4-6 weeks with regular maintenance. Laser treatments: several years with proper skincare. CoolSculpting: permanent fat reduction (fat cells don't return). We'll discuss maintenance schedules during your consultation to keep you looking your best.

Am I a good candidate for aesthetic treatments?

Most healthy adults are good candidates for aesthetic treatments. Ideal candidates have realistic expectations, are in good overall health, and are not pregnant or nursing. Certain medical conditions or medications may affect candidacy for specific treatments. During your consultation, we'll review your health history, assess your anatomy, and determine which treatments are safe and effective for you.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing varies based on the treatment, area size, and amount of product needed. Botox starts at $12/unit (most areas need 20-50 units). Fillers range from $650-$900 per syringe. Facials start at $125. During your free consultation, we'll provide a personalized quote based on your specific goals. We offer financing, package discounts, and membership programs to make treatments affordable.

How many treatments will I need?

This depends on your goals and the treatment. Injectables are typically ongoing maintenance (every 3-6 months). Skin treatments like microneedling or chemical peels work best as a series of 3-6 treatments. CoolSculpting may require 1-3 sessions per area. Laser hair removal needs 6-8 sessions for permanent reduction. During your consultation, we'll create a treatment plan with realistic timelines and expectations.

What if I'm not happy with my results?

Your satisfaction is our priority. We provide thorough consultations to set realistic expectations before any treatment. If you're not satisfied with your injectable results, we offer complimentary touch-ups within 2 weeks. For filler treatments, we can adjust or dissolve filler if needed. We'll work with you until you're happy with your outcome. Communication is key—let us know if you have any concerns during your healing process.

Are your products and treatments FDA-approved?

Yes. We exclusively use FDA-approved products and devices from trusted manufacturers like Allergan (Botox, Juvéderm), Galderma (Dysport, Restylane), and Merz (Radiesse). Our laser systems, CoolSculpting technology, and all medical devices are FDA-cleared. We never use off-brand or imported products. Your safety is non-negotiable, and we adhere to the highest medical and safety standards.

Do you offer financing or payment plans?

Yes! We partner with CareCredit and Cherry to offer flexible payment plans for treatments over $500. Apply online in minutes—approval is often immediate. We also offer membership programs with monthly credits and discounts, making regular treatments more affordable. HSA and FSA cards are accepted. Discuss financing options during your consultation—we'll help you find a plan that works for your budget.
