Package Pricing

Save when you purchase treatment packages. For example, buy 3 HydraFacials, get the 4th free. Buy 60 units of Botox, save $2/unit. Ask about our current package specials during your consultation.

Before Your Appointment

Avoid blood-thinning medications (aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil, Vitamin E) for 7 days before injectables to minimize bruising. No alcohol 24 hours before. Arrive with a clean face (no makeup for skin treatments). Complete health intake forms online before your visit.

After Your Treatment

Most treatments require little to no downtime. Injectables: avoid exercise and lying flat for 4 hours. Facials and peels: avoid sun, heat, and retinol for 24-48 hours. Laser and CoolSculpting: temporary redness or swelling for a few days. Full aftercare instructions provided after each treatment.

Results Timeline

Botox: 3-5 days to take effect, peaks at 2 weeks. Fillers: immediate with final results at 2 weeks after swelling resolves. Skin treatments: gradual improvement over 4-6 weeks as collagen builds. CoolSculpting: results at 8-12 weeks. Most treatments require a series for optimal results.
