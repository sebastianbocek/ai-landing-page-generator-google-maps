FAQ
Common Questions

How do I prepare for my color appointment?

Don't wash your hair the day of your appointment. Natural oils help protect your scalp. Arrive with dry hair and no styling products. Bring photos of colors you love!

How long will my appointment take?

Haircuts: 45-60 minutes. Single process color: 1.5-2 hours. Highlights/Balayage: 2-3 hours. Full transformations or color corrections may take longer.

Do you offer consultations?

Yes! Consultations are complimentary and recommended for color corrections, major changes, and extensions. They typically take 15-20 minutes.

Can I bring my kids?

We welcome children for kids' cuts. For adult services, we recommend arranging childcare. Our space works best when it's calm and relaxing for everyone.

Do you sell products?

Yes! We carry Oribe, Olaplex, and other professional products. Your stylist can recommend the perfect products for your hair type and style.

What if I'm unhappy with my service?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If something isn't right, contact us within 7 days and we'll make it right. We want you to love your hair!
