Our Story

A passion for beauty and a commitment to making every client feel extraordinary.

The Beginning

Where Beauty Meets Artistry

Studio Luxe was born from Isabella Romano's dream to create more than just a salon. She wanted to build a space where artistry, expertise, and genuine care came together to transform not just hair, but how people feel about themselves.

After training in Paris and working at top salons in New York City, Isabella returned to her hometown of Seattle in 2010 to open Studio Luxe. What started as a two-chair studio has grown into a team of talented stylists who share her vision.

Today, we're proud to be one of Seattle's most sought-after salons. But our mission remains the same: to make every person who sits in our chair leave feeling beautiful, confident, and cared for.

The Team

Meet Your Stylists

Talented artists with a passion for making you look and feel amazing.

Isabella Romano

Owner & Master Stylist
Color Specialist Balayage Bridal

Isabella's journey in hair began at 16, working in her aunt's salon in Italy. She went on to train at prestigious academies in Paris and worked at top salons in NYC before founding Studio Luxe. With 20+ years of experience, she's known for creating natural-looking color and transformative cuts that enhance each client's unique beauty.

Certifications: Wella Master Colorist, Oribe Educator, Balayage Certified

Marcus Chen

Senior Stylist
Precision Cuts Men's Grooming Texture

Marcus brings 10 years of experience and a background in architectural design to his cutting technique. He's known for creating structured, geometric cuts as well as soft, textured styles. His specialty is working with curly and textured hair, helping clients embrace their natural pattern.

Certifications: DevaCurl Certified, Vidal Sassoon Trained

Sofia Martinez

Color Artist
Vivid Colors Corrective Color Extensions

Sofia is our color wizard. With 8 years of experience, she's built a following for her bold color transformations and intricate techniques. She's the go-to stylist for anyone wanting fashion colors, color corrections, or seamless extensions. Her Instagram-worthy work has been featured in local publications.

Certifications: Pulp Riot Educator, Great Lengths Certified
