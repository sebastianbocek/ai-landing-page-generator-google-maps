Good to Know

Appointment Policy

  • Walk-ins welcome based on availability, but appointments are recommended
  • Please arrive 5 minutes early to ensure your full service time
  • Late arrivals may result in shortened service time or rescheduling
  • 24-hour cancellation notice appreciated to avoid fees

Payment & Gratuity

  • We accept cash, all major credit cards, and digital payments
  • Gratuities are appreciated but never required
  • Standard gratuity is 15-20% for excellent service

Service Duration

  • Manicures: 30-60 minutes depending on service level
  • Pedicures: 45-75 minutes depending on service level
  • Full set enhancements: 90-120 minutes
  • Detailed nail art: Additional 30-60 minutes
