Walk-Ins

Walk-ins are welcome for small to medium pieces based on artist availability. For custom work, we recommend booking a consultation to ensure your artist has time to create the perfect design.

What to Expect

The Process

1

Consultation

We'll discuss your idea, review reference images, and talk placement, size, and style. No commitment required.

2

Design & Quote

Your artist creates a custom design based on your consultation. We'll provide a clear quote and answer any questions.

3

Book & Deposit

Secure your appointment with a deposit ($100 or 20% for larger pieces). This reserves your spot and goes toward your final cost.

4

Tattoo Day

Arrive rested, fed, and hydrated. We'll review the final design, apply the stencil, and get to work creating your piece.

Policies

Studio Policies

A few things to know before your appointment.

Age Requirement

You must be 18 years or older with valid government-issued ID. We do not tattoo minors under any circumstances.

Deposits

Non-refundable deposits of $100 (or 20% for large pieces) are required to book. Deposits apply to your final tattoo cost.

Cancellations

Please provide at least 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations forfeit the deposit.

No-Shows

No-shows forfeit their deposit and may be required to pay full deposit upfront for future appointments.

Sobriety

We will not tattoo anyone under the influence of alcohol or drugs. If you arrive intoxicated, your appointment will be cancelled.

Health

Please reschedule if you're feeling unwell. Certain medical conditions may require doctor approval before tattooing.
