Our Story

Over 15 years of creating custom tattoos that tell your story. From a small garage studio to an award-winning shop.

The Beginning

Built on Passion

Iron & Ink started in 2008 when Marcus Cole converted his garage into a small tattoo studio. With nothing but a tattoo machine, a dream, and an obsessive attention to detail, he began building what would become one of Portland's most respected tattoo shops.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into a team of three award-winning artists, each bringing their unique style and expertise. But some things haven't changed: our commitment to custom work, sterile practices, and treating every client like family.

Today, we've inked thousands of clients from around the world. From first tattoos to full sleeves, memorial pieces to cover-ups, every piece of work that leaves our shop represents our dedication to the craft.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The Team

Meet Our Artists

Each artist at Iron & Ink brings years of experience and a unique perspective to their craft.

Marcus Cole

Owner & Lead Artist
Traditional Neo-Traditional Japanese

Marcus has been tattooing for over 20 years. He apprenticed under legendary artist "Big Mike" Thompson in San Francisco before moving to Portland to open Iron & Ink. His bold traditional work and intricate Japanese pieces have won multiple awards at conventions across the country.

Awards: Best Traditional (Portland Tattoo Expo 2019), Best Japanese (Seattle Ink Fest 2021)

Sarah Chen

Senior Artist
Fine Line Realism Portraits

Sarah joined Iron & Ink in 2015 after studying fine art at RISD. Her background in classical drawing translates into incredibly detailed fine-line work and photorealistic portraits. She's been featured in Inked Magazine and Tattoo Life.

Awards: Best Portrait (Northwest Tattoo Fest 2020), Best Fine Line (Bay Area Tattoo Convention 2022)

Jake Rodriguez

Artist
Blackwork Geometric Ornamental

Jake brings a modern edge to the shop with his precise geometric work and bold blackwork designs. Self-taught through years of practice, he developed his signature style combining sacred geometry with ornamental patterns. He joined the team in 2019.

Awards: Best Geometric (Portland Tattoo Expo 2021)
