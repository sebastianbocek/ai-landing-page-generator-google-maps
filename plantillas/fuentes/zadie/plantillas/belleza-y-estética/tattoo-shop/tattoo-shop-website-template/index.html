Our Specialties

Every Style. Every Story.

Our artists specialize in a wide range of tattoo styles. Whatever your vision, we have the expertise to make it permanent.

Traditional

Bold lines, vibrant colors, and timeless American traditional designs. Anchors, roses, eagles, and classic flash art.

Fine Line

Delicate, intricate work with precise single-needle techniques. Perfect for minimalist designs and detailed portraits.

Japanese

Traditional Irezumi and neo-Japanese styles. Dragons, koi, cherry blossoms, and full sleeve compositions.

Blackwork

Bold black ink designs from geometric patterns to ornamental and tribal work. High contrast, striking results.

Neo-Traditional

Modern evolution of traditional styles with expanded color palettes and intricate detail work.

Realism

Photo-realistic portraits and nature pieces. Black and grey or full color, bringing images to life on skin.
