About Sharp Edge

Three barbers, one mission: keep the neighborhood looking sharp.

The Beginning

How It Started

Sharp Edge started with a simple idea: bring back the classic barbershop experience. Marcus Rivera had been cutting hair for over a decade when he noticed something missing from modern shops. The conversation, the camaraderie, the feeling of walking into a place where everybody knows your name.

In 2019, Marcus took a leap and opened Sharp Edge in a small storefront downtown. The vision was clear from day one. Leather chairs, vintage decor, skilled barbers, and an atmosphere where men could relax, shoot the breeze, and leave looking better than when they walked in.

What started as a one-chair operation has grown into a three-barber shop with a loyal following. But the mission hasn't changed. Every client gets the same attention to detail, whether it's their first visit or their hundredth.

Serving the community since 2019

The Crew

Meet Your Barbers

Three different styles, one shared commitment to excellence.

MR

Marcus Rivera

Owner & Master Barber

15 years of experience and counting. Marcus learned the trade from his uncle and has been perfecting his craft ever since. Known for his classic cuts and hot towel shaves, he brings old-school precision to every appointment. When he's not behind the chair, you'll find him hunting for vintage barbershop memorabilia.

JT

James Thompson

Senior Barber

If you want a fade that turns heads, James is your guy. Eight years in the game with a specialty in precision fades and modern cuts. He joined Sharp Edge in 2020 and quickly became the go-to for clients who want something with a little more edge. Literally. His line work is surgical.

DK

Derek Kim

Barber & Stylist

The newest addition to the Sharp Edge family, Derek brings fresh perspective and modern techniques. Five years of experience with a talent for textured cuts and beard sculpting. He's got an eye for what works with your face shape and hair type. Great with first-timers who aren't sure what they want.
