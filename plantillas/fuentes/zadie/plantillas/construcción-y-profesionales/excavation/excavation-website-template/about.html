Our Story

Built from the Ground Up

Todd Brannigan started Groundbreak Excavation with one machine and a reputation for doing things right. Twenty years later, we run a 12-piece fleet and have completed more than 500 projects across Central Ohio.

Todd Brannigan

Founder & Owner
Todd Brannigan

Twenty Years of Moving Dirt the Right Way

I grew up around heavy equipment. My father ran a small dozer operation in Licking County, and by the time I was 16 I could grade a pad better than most grown men. After finishing my operators certification, I spent seven years working for two of Columbus's larger site work contractors learning the business from the inside out.

In 2004, I bought a used CAT 320 excavator and started taking small residential jobs. Word spread fast. By year three, we were handling commercial site prep for developers across Franklin and Delaware Counties. Today, Groundbreak has a 12-piece fleet, a crew of 14, and a project list that ranges from single-family lot clearing to 80-acre industrial park development.

We're not the biggest excavation company in Central Ohio. That's intentional. Every job that goes out under the Groundbreak name gets my personal attention. I sign off on every estimate, I'm on the job site for every mobilization, and I take every phone call from a client. That's not going to change.

How We Work

What We Stand Behind

We've been doing this long enough to know what separates a good excavation contractor from a great one.

Safety First

Every crew member holds a current OSHA 10 certification. Todd holds OSHA 30. We follow a strict excavation safety protocol on every site, including daily equipment walk-arounds, competent person inspections, and confined space protocols.

Accurate Bids

We visit every site before we quote it. Our estimates include a full breakdown of equipment time, operator hours, disposal fees, and contingency. No lowball bids, no surprise change orders.

Scheduled and On Time

Construction schedules are tight. We commit to mobilization dates and we hold them. If there's a delay, you'll hear from us before you need to call. Communication is not optional on a Groundbreak job.

GPS-Grade Precision

Our primary dozer is GPS grade-control equipped. We can grade to within 0.1 ft of design without constant survey staking, which means faster production and lower cost to you.

Responsible Disposal

All spoils, concrete, and debris are hauled to licensed disposal facilities. We keep documentation of all tipping receipts and can provide environmental compliance records on request.

Owner-Accessible

Todd's number is on every contract. You're not going to get routed through a call center when you have a question. We're a small company because we want to stay accountable to every client we take on.
