What We Do

Heavy Excavation & Site Work

From raw land to a job-ready site. We handle everything underground and underfoot so your project gets built on solid ground.

Site Clearing & Grubbing

Full vegetation removal, stump grinding, and debris hauling. We clear the land and prepare it for what comes next.

Grading & Earthwork

Precision grading for proper drainage, structural support, and compaction. We cut and fill to your engineering specs.

Trenching & Utility Install

Utility trenching for water, sewer, gas, and electrical. Clean cuts, correct depth, proper bedding materials.

Pond & Lake Excavation

Custom pond and lake construction for agricultural, residential, and recreational use. Designed to hold and last.

Demolition & Hauling

Structural demo, concrete breaking, and full-service hauling. We tear it down and haul it away clean.

Rock Breaking

Hydraulic rock breaking and ledge removal for sites where standard digging won't cut it. We handle the hard stuff.

Land Development

Full-service land development from raw acreage to infrastructure-ready parcels. Roads, pads, drainage, and more.

Septic System Excavation

Precise excavation for septic tank and leach field installation. We work with your septic engineer to spec.

How It Works

From First Call to Final Grade

We run a tight operation. No surprises, no excuses. Here's what working with Groundbreak looks like.

01

Request a Bid

Call us or fill out our bid form. Tell us the project scope, location, and timeline. We respond within 24 hours.

02

Site Walk & Estimate

Todd or our lead estimator visits the site. We assess conditions, soil type, access, and produce a detailed written estimate.

03

Mobilize & Execute

We mobilize on schedule, stage equipment efficiently, and execute the work safely. Daily updates are standard.

04

Final Inspection

We walk the completed site with you, confirm grades and tolerances, and don't demobilize until you sign off.
