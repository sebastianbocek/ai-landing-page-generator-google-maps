Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from Charlotte homeowners.

Do I need a permit for a fence in Charlotte?

Most residential fences in Charlotte-Mecklenburg require a zoning permit. Fences over 7 feet may need a building permit as well. We handle all permit applications as part of our service at no additional charge.

How long does a typical fence installation take?

Most residential fence installations are completed in 1-3 days depending on the length and material. A standard 150-foot wood privacy fence typically takes 2 days. Chain link fences are often done in a single day.

Do you verify property lines before installing?

Yes. We review your property survey before every installation. If you don't have a current survey, we can recommend a licensed surveyor. We set fences 2-4 inches inside your property line to avoid encroachment.

What's included in a free estimate?

An on-site visit where we measure your property, discuss fence styles and materials, review your survey, and identify any obstacles. You'll receive a detailed written quote within 24 hours with no obligation.

Do you remove old fences?

Yes. We include removal and disposal of your existing fence at no extra charge on all full-replacement installations. For standalone removal jobs, we provide a separate quote based on the fence type and length.

What warranty do you offer?

We provide a 5-year workmanship warranty covering installation defects, post settlement, and hardware failure. Material warranties vary by manufacturer. Vinyl and aluminum typically carry lifetime warranties from the manufacturer.
