Our Story

Started With a Truck and a Post Hole Digger

Boundary Line Fencing started in 2007 when founder Mike Caldwell left a large construction company to do things his way. He had one truck, a set of tools, and a simple belief: if you build fences right and treat people fairly, the work will follow.

He was right. What started as a one-man operation installing backyard fences in Mecklenburg County has grown into a team of 14 skilled installers and project managers. We've put up over 3,200 fences across the Charlotte metro area.

Some things haven't changed. Mike still shows up to job sites. Every estimate still comes with a handshake. And we still won't cut corners on materials or installation, even when no one's watching.

Our Values

What We Stand For

We give you one price upfront and stick to it. No surprise charges, no hidden fees, no change orders that inflate the bill. What we quote is what you pay.
