Fencing Services & Pricing

From privacy fences to automated gates, we handle every step of the process. All prices include materials, labor, and post-install cleanup.

Most Popular

Wood Fencing

The most versatile and popular fencing option for Charlotte homeowners. We offer three distinct styles in cedar, pressure-treated pine, and premium redwood. Every wood fence we build uses concrete-set posts and galvanized fasteners for maximum lifespan.

Privacy Fence: 6-foot solid board with cap rail
Picket Fence: 3-4 foot classic or dog-ear style
Split Rail: 2-4 rail rustic style, ideal for large lots
Board-on-Board: Overlapping boards for full privacy
Shadow Box: Alternating boards for airflow with partial privacy

Starting Price

$18 - $45 per linear foot
Varies by wood species, height, and style. Cedar privacy fence averages $28/ft installed.
Low Maintenance

Vinyl Fencing

Vinyl fencing gives you the look of a painted wood fence without the upkeep. It won't rot, warp, split, or fade. We install commercial-grade vinyl with reinforced rails and UV-stabilized color that's backed by a lifetime manufacturer warranty.

Privacy: Solid panels in white, tan, and gray
Semi-Privacy: Lattice-top and shadowbox options
Picket: Traditional and contemporary profiles
Ranch Rail: 2, 3, and 4-rail configurations
Lifetime color-fade warranty included

Starting Price

$24 - $55 per linear foot
6-foot vinyl privacy averages $38/ft installed. Includes all hardware and post caps.
Best Value

Chain Link Fencing

The most affordable and durable fencing option available. Chain link is ideal for backyards, pet enclosures, sports courts, and commercial properties. We use heavy-gauge galvanized steel with tension bars and vinyl-coated options for a cleaner look.

Galvanized: Standard silver finish, 4-6 foot heights
Vinyl-Coated: Black or green finish for curb appeal
Privacy Slats: Inserted slats for added screening
Commercial Grade: Heavier gauge for high-traffic areas
Tennis/sport court enclosures available

Starting Price

$12 - $28 per linear foot
4-foot residential galvanized averages $15/ft installed. Vinyl-coated adds $3-5/ft.
Premium

Ornamental Iron & Aluminum

Ornamental fencing adds elegance and security without blocking your view. We install both wrought iron and aluminum options. Aluminum is lighter and maintenance-free, while iron offers unmatched strength and a classic appearance.

Wrought Iron: Hand-welded, powder-coated finish
Aluminum: Rust-free, available in multiple colors
Pool Code Compliant: Self-closing gate hardware
Decorative Finials: Spear, trident, and ball-top options
Custom heights from 3 to 8 feet

Starting Price

$30 - $75 per linear foot
Aluminum averages $35/ft. Wrought iron averages $55/ft. Custom designs quoted separately.
Access Solutions

Gate Installation & Automation

A fence is only as good as its gate. We install single and double swing gates, sliding gates, and cantilever gates in all materials. For driveways and commercial properties, we offer full automation with keypads, remotes, and smart home integration.

Walk Gates: Single swing with self-closing hardware
Double Drive Gates: For vehicles and equipment access
Sliding/Cantilever: Ideal for sloped driveways
Automatic Openers: LiftMaster and Viking brands
Keypad, Remote, and App Control options

Starting Price

$350 - $3,500+ per gate
Walk gates from $350. Automated driveway gates from $2,200 including opener.
Fix It Right

Fence Repair

Wind, age, and accidents take their toll. We repair all fence types and materials. From a single broken board to re-setting a leaning section, our repair crew responds fast and matches existing materials as closely as possible.

Storm Damage: Emergency response within 24 hours
Leaning Posts: Reset or replace with concrete footings
Broken Boards: Matched to existing fence style
Sagging Gates: Re-hung with new hardware
Rust Treatment: For iron and chain link fences

Starting Price

$150 - $800+ per visit
Most repairs completed same-day. Minimum service call is $150.
