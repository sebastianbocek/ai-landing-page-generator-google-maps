Our Story

From One Truck to a Full Operation

Irongate Door Co. started in 2003 when founder Mike Callahan bought a used service truck and started fixing garage doors out of his Westerville home. He'd spent 10 years working for a national chain and got tired of watching customers get overcharged for simple repairs.

The idea was simple: do honest work at fair prices and let the reputation build itself. It worked. Within three years, Mike had a crew of four and a proper shop on Industrial Parkway. Today, Irongate runs eight service trucks and a 6,000-square-foot showroom.

We've installed over 12,000 doors across central Ohio. The philosophy hasn't changed. Show up on time, explain the problem clearly, give an honest price, and stand behind the work.

What Drives Us

Our Values

These aren't motivational posters on the wall. They're the standards every person on this team is held to.

Straight Talk

We tell you what's wrong, what it costs, and how long it takes. If a repair is a waste of money, we'll say so and recommend replacement instead.

Quality First

We only install products we'd put on our own homes. That means premium hardware, commercial-grade springs, and doors that last 25+ years.

Respect Your Time

We give 2-hour arrival windows and call 30 minutes before we show up. If we're late, the service call fee is waived. Your time matters.
