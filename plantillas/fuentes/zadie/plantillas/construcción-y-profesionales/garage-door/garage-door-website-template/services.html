Spring Repair & Replacement

A broken garage door spring is the most common repair call we get. It's also one of the most dangerous DIY jobs a homeowner can attempt. Our technicians replace torsion and extension springs safely using professional tools and high-cycle springs rated for 20,000+ cycles.

  • Torsion and extension spring replacement
  • High-cycle springs (20,000+ cycles)

  • Door balance and alignment
  • Same-day service in most cases
