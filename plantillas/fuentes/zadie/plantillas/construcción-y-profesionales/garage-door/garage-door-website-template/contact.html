FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. Don't see yours? Give us a call.

How long does a garage door installation take?

A standard residential garage door installation takes 4-6 hours. We remove your old door, install the new one, set up the opener, and test everything. Most jobs are completed in a single visit.

How much does a new garage door cost?

Residential garage doors range from $1,200 to $4,500+ depending on material, insulation, and style. We offer free in-home estimates so you can see exact pricing for your specific situation.

Can I repair a garage door spring myself?

We strongly advise against it. Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury if handled improperly. This is one repair that should always be done by a trained technician.

How often should I have my garage door serviced?

We recommend annual maintenance for residential doors and quarterly service for commercial doors. Regular maintenance extends the life of your door, prevents breakdowns, and keeps the warranty valid.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We partner with GreenSky to offer financing options with 0% APR for 12 months on new door installations over $1,500. Apply during your free estimate appointment.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Columbus metro area including Westerville, Dublin, Hilliard, Grove City, Reynoldsburg, Gahanna, Powell, Worthington, New Albany, and surrounding communities within 30 miles of downtown.
