25 Years in Paving
Ray Kowalski

Started With a Shovel. Built a Fleet.

Ray Kowalski started his paving career in 1999 as a crew laborer in Detroit, shoveling hot mix and learning the trade from experienced pavers who didn't take shortcuts. He worked every position on the paving chain — rake man, roller operator, foreman — before striking out on his own in 2007.

BlackTop Pro started as a one-truck operation. Within three years, Ray had three trucks and a reputation for showing up on time and doing the work as quoted. By 2015, the fleet was at six trucks. Today it's eight, with full crew capacity for commercial lots and subdivision paving.

Ray still pulls site visits personally. He still reviews every quote. He still gets calls from property managers who found his number in a drawer from ten years ago because they want the same guy who did their lot the first time.

"I got into this business because I liked the work. You lay a parking lot and you know it's solid. You can stand on it. You drive over it every day and it holds up. That's satisfying in a way that most jobs aren't. The surface tells the truth."

Who We Work With

Commercial & Residential

We're set up to handle both. The approach is different. The standard isn't.

Commercial Clients

Property managers, HOAs, retail centers, warehouses, municipalities, and apartment complexes. We handle large-scale projects with proper scheduling, permitting, and minimal disruption to tenants and operations. Annual maintenance contracts available.

Residential Clients

Homeowners who want their driveway done right, not done cheap. We show up on time, complete the work cleanly, and stand behind it. Ray handles every residential consultation personally — no sales reps.

Industrial & Warehouse

High-load industrial lots require different base specifications than standard parking lots. We understand the difference and build accordingly. Forklift and semi-truck traffic need deeper base courses and heavier mix designs.

Service Area
