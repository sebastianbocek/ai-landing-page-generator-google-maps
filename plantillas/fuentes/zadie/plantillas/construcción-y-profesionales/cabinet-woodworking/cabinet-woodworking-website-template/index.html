Why Graincraft

Built Different. In Every Sense.

We're not a big-box store. We're four craftspeople who take your project personally.

Solid Wood Only

No particleboard. No veneered shortcuts. Every cabinet box, face frame, and door is solid wood or premium Baltic birch plywood.

Dovetail Joinery

Hand-cut and machine-assisted dovetails on every drawer box. The joint that's held fine furniture together for centuries.

18 Years of Craft

Owen Hartley has been building custom cabinetry since 2006. Every technique has been refined through hundreds of real projects.

Local Hardwoods

We source walnut, white oak, cherry, and maple from regional mills in the southern Appalachians. Less travel, better wood.

How We Work

From Sketch to Installed

Custom work takes time. Here's how we approach every project, from the first conversation to the last coat of finish.

01

Consultation

We sit down in your space, talk through your vision, take measurements, and discuss materials. No charge for the first meeting.

02

Design

We produce detailed shop drawings and 3D renderings so you can see exactly what we'll build before we cut a single board.

03

Handcraft

Your project is built in our Asheville shop using traditional methods and modern precision tools. We don't rush this part.

04

Installation

Our crew handles delivery and installation. We leave your space clean and walk you through every feature before we go.
