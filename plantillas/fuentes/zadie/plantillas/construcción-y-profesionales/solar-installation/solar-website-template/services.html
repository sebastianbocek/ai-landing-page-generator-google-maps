Residential

Residential Solar Installation

A custom-designed solar array for your home, sized to match your energy consumption. We handle everything from initial design through permitting, installation, utility interconnection, and final inspection.

  • Free site assessment with shade analysis and roof evaluation
  • Tier 1 monocrystalline panels with 25-year performance warranty
  • Enphase or SolarEdge microinverters for panel-level optimization
  • All permits, HOA approvals, and utility paperwork handled
  • Real-time monitoring app to track production and savings
  • Average system size: 6-12 kW (16-32 panels)

Commercial Solar Systems

Reduce your business's operating costs with a commercial-scale solar installation. We work with offices, warehouses, retail spaces, and multi-tenant properties to design systems that maximize ROI.

  • Accelerated depreciation (MACRS) for additional tax benefits

Storage

Battery Storage Systems

Store your solar energy for use when you need it most. Battery storage gives you backup power during outages, helps you avoid peak electricity rates, and moves you closer to full energy independence.

  • Tesla Powerwall, Enphase IQ, and Franklin WH certified installer
  • Seamless integration with new or existing solar systems
  • Automatic backup power during grid outages
  • Time-of-use optimization to avoid peak rate charges
  • Scalable from single battery (13.5 kWh) to whole-home systems
  • 10-year manufacturer warranty on all battery products

  • Smart scheduling to charge during peak solar production hours

Our Process

From Consultation to Clean Energy

A straightforward four-step process that takes your home from grid-dependent to solar-powered in 6-8 weeks.

1

Free Consultation

We assess your roof, review your utility bills, and design a custom system. You get a detailed proposal with projected savings within 48 hours.

2

Design & Permits

Our engineers create detailed plans. We handle all permitting, HOA approvals, and utility applications. This typically takes 2-4 weeks.

3

Installation

Our certified crew installs your system, usually in 1-3 days for residential projects. We leave your property clean and ready for inspection.

4

Power On

After final inspection and utility approval, we activate your system. You start generating clean energy and watching your savings grow in real time.
