Commercial Solar

Why Go Solar

The Numbers Speak for Themselves

Solar isn't just good for the planet. It's one of the best financial investments you can make for your home.

$25K-$75K

Lifetime Savings

The average solar homeowner saves between $25,000 and $75,000 over a 25-year panel lifespan, depending on local utility rates and system size.

30%

Federal Tax Credit

The Inflation Reduction Act extends the 30% Investment Tax Credit through 2032. A $20,000 system means $6,000 back on your federal taxes.

4.1%

Home Value Increase

Homes with solar panels sell for an average of 4.1% more than comparable homes without them, according to Zillow research.

6-8 Years

Payback Period

Most residential systems pay for themselves within 6 to 8 years through electricity savings and tax incentives. After that, it's free power.
