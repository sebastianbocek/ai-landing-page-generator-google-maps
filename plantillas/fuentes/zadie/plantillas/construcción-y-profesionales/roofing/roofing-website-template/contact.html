Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new roof cost?

Residential roof replacement typically ranges from $7,500 to $25,000+ depending on size, material choice, roof complexity, and your location. Asphalt shingles are the most economical option ($7,500-$15,000 for average homes), while metal roofing runs $15,000-$30,000+ and tile roofing $20,000-$40,000+. We provide free, detailed estimates with no obligation so you know exactly what to expect.

How long does a roof installation take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1-3 days, depending on the size of your home, weather conditions, and roof complexity. Simple ranch homes often take just one day, while larger two-story homes or complex roof designs may require 2-3 days. We provide a project timeline with your estimate and work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes! We have extensive experience working with all major insurance companies and can handle your storm damage claim from start to finish. We'll meet with your insurance adjuster, provide detailed damage documentation, and advocate for fair settlement. We work within your insurance settlement and deductible, and can even arrange financing for your deductible if needed.

What warranty do you offer?

We offer a lifetime workmanship warranty on all new roof installations, meaning if there's ever a problem with our installation, we'll fix it at no cost to you. Additionally, manufacturer warranties range from 20-50 years depending on the shingle type you choose. We're GAF Master Elite certified, which allows us to offer enhanced manufacturer warranties up to 50 years with full coverage.

How often should I replace my roof?

Asphalt shingle roofs typically last 20-30 years, metal roofs 40-70 years, and tile roofs 50-100 years. However, lifespan depends on climate, installation quality, maintenance, and weather exposure. Signs it's time to replace include widespread shingle damage, multiple leaks, sagging areas, significant granule loss, or a roof approaching its expected lifespan. We offer free inspections to assess your roof's remaining life.

Can you repair my roof or does it need full replacement?

We'll always be honest about whether repair or replacement is the right choice. If your roof is relatively new (under 10-15 years), damage is localized, and the rest of the roof is in good condition, repair is usually the best option. If your roof is older, has widespread damage, or has had multiple previous repairs, replacement is often more cost-effective long-term. We'll thoroughly inspect and recommend the most economical solution.

Do you offer financing?

Yes! We offer flexible financing options including 0% interest for 12 months for qualified applicants, as well as extended payment plans up to 60 months with competitive rates. Financing makes it easy to protect your home now and pay over time. Apply online or when you receive your estimate.

Will you damage my landscaping or property?

We take extensive measures to protect your property. We cover plants and landscaping with tarps, use plywood walkways to protect grass and gardens, place dumpsters on plywood to prevent driveway damage, and use magnetic sweeps to collect nails after completion. We treat your property with the same care we'd want for our own homes.

What happens if it rains during my roof installation?

We monitor weather closely and plan accordingly. If rain is forecast, we may delay start until conditions improve. If unexpected rain occurs during installation, we immediately cover exposed areas with tarps. Most roof tear-offs and installations are done in sections, so your entire roof is never fully exposed at once. In 25 years, we've never had weather cause interior damage to a home.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, we are fully licensed (License #123456), bonded, and insured with $2 million in liability coverage and workers' compensation insurance for all employees. This protects you from any liability if an accident occurs on your property. We're also GAF Master Elite certified, CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster, and maintain an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.
