Our Process

What to Expect When You Choose Us

We've streamlined our process to make your roofing project as smooth and stress-free as possible.

1

Free Inspection & Estimate

We visit your property to thoroughly inspect your roof and provide a detailed written estimate with no obligation. Most estimates completed within 48 hours.

2

Material Selection

Choose from premium roofing materials and colors. We help you select the best option for your home, budget, and aesthetic preferences.

3

Project Scheduling

Once you approve the estimate, we schedule your project at your convenience. We handle all permits and coordinate delivery of materials.

4

Professional Installation

Our experienced crew completes most residential roofs in 1-3 days. We protect your landscaping, work safely, and maintain a clean job site.

Final Inspection & Cleanup

We perform a thorough cleanup with magnetic sweeps for nails, conduct a final walkthrough with you, and provide all warranty documentation.
