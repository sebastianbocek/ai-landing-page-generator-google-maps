Our Story

25 Years of Protecting Homes

SkyShield Roofing was founded in 1999 by Tom Davidson, a third-generation roofer who learned the trade from his father and grandfather. What started as a one-truck operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted roofing companies, but our core values remain unchanged: honest work, fair prices, and treating every home like it's our own.

Over the past 25 years, we've installed and repaired over 5,000 roofs across residential and commercial properties. We've weathered economic downturns, industry changes, and countless storms alongside our community. Through it all, we've maintained our commitment to quality workmanship and customer satisfaction that has earned us an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau and countless five-star reviews.

Today, SkyShield operates with a full team of licensed, experienced roofers, project managers, and customer service specialists. We've expanded our services to include metal roofing, commercial installations, and comprehensive exterior solutions, but we've never forgotten our roots in residential roofing. Every member of our team shares Tom's original vision: protect homes and build lasting relationships with the families who live in them.

When you choose SkyShield, you're not just hiring a contractor—you're partnering with a local business that has deep roots in this community and a genuine investment in your home's protection and your complete satisfaction.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Quality First

We use only premium materials from trusted manufacturers and employ rigorous quality control on every project. Our lifetime workmanship warranty backs up our commitment to excellence.

Honest Communication

We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees. We'll tell you what you need, not what makes us the most money. If your roof can be repaired instead of replaced, we'll tell you that.

Respect for Your Home

We treat every property with care, protecting landscaping, keeping job sites clean, and respecting your daily routine. Our crews are background-checked, professional, and courteous.

Customer Satisfaction

We're not satisfied until you are. Our job isn't done when the last shingle is nailed—it's done when you're completely happy with the work and confident in your roof's protection.

Community Investment

We live and work in this community. We sponsor local youth sports, support schools, and give back to the neighborhoods that have supported our business for 25 years.

Continuous Improvement

Roofing technology evolves, and so do we. Our team pursues ongoing training and certifications to stay current with the latest materials, techniques, and safety standards.
