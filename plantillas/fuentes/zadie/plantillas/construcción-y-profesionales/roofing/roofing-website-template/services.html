Expert Roof Repairs

Fast, reliable repairs to extend your roof's life and protect your home from water damage.

Leak Repair

From $350

We quickly locate and repair roof leaks before they cause interior damage. Common causes include damaged shingles, failed flashing, or cracked boots around vents and pipes. Most leak repairs completed same-day or next-day.

Storm Damage Repair

From $500

Wind, hail, and falling debris can damage even the strongest roofs. We repair storm damage quickly and work with your insurance company to streamline the claims process. Emergency tarping available 24/7.

Flashing & Penetration Repair

From $400

Chimney flashing, valley flashing, and penetrations (vents, pipes, skylights) are common failure points. We replace failed flashing and properly seal all roof penetrations to prevent leaks.

Shingle Replacement

Missing, cracked, or curling shingles compromise your roof's protection. We replace damaged shingles, matching your existing roof color and style as closely as possible.

Storm Damage

Storm Damage Assessment & Insurance Claims

We work directly with insurance companies to ensure you receive fair compensation for storm damage.

Free Storm Damage Inspection

After severe weather, we provide complimentary roof inspections to assess hail damage, wind damage, and structural issues. We document all damage with photos and detailed reports for insurance claims.

Insurance Claim Assistance

We handle all communication with your insurance adjuster, provide detailed estimates, and advocate for fair settlements. Our team understands insurance requirements and helps you navigate the claims process.

Emergency Tarping Service

From $500

Available 24/7 for emergency situations. We tarp damaged roofs quickly to prevent further water intrusion while permanent repairs are scheduled.

Complete Restoration

Once your claim is approved, we handle the complete roof replacement or repair. We work within your insurance settlement and deductible, with flexible payment options available.

Common Signs of Storm Damage

Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
Granule loss (dark spots on shingles)
Dents in metal flashing or vents
Damaged or missing ridge caps
Interior water stains or leaks
Dented gutters or downspouts

If you notice any of these signs after a storm, contact us for a free inspection.
