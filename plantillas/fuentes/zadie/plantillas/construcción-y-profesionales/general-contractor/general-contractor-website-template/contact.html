Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How Does Your Estimate Process Work?

After you contact us, we schedule a site visit or phone consultation to understand the scope. We then provide a detailed written estimate within 5-7 business days, broken down line by line so you know exactly what you're paying for. There's no cost and no obligation.

How Long Will My Project Take?

Timelines depend on scope. A kitchen renovation typically takes 6-10 weeks. A custom home can take 6-12 months. We provide a detailed schedule before work begins and keep you updated throughout. We build realistic timelines and stick to them.

Are You Licensed and Insured?

Yes. We hold State General Contractor License #GC-29471 and carry $2 million in general liability insurance, workers' compensation, and builder's risk coverage. We're happy to provide documentation to any client upon request.

What Are Your Payment Terms?

We structure payments in phases tied to project milestones. A typical schedule is 10% at contract signing, then progress payments at foundation, framing, mechanical rough-in, and completion. We never ask for more than the work warrants.

Do You Handle Permits?

Yes. We manage the entire permitting process, from application to final inspection. We know the local codes and have established relationships with building departments across the county. You don't need to deal with any of the paperwork.

What Kind of Warranty Do You Offer?

We provide a 1-year workmanship warranty on all projects, covering any defects in our labor. Many materials carry their own manufacturer warranties of 5-25 years. If something isn't right, call us. We'll make it right.

Prefer to Talk on the Phone?

Give us a call at (555) 743-2100. We're available Monday through Friday, 7am to 5pm. Mike or Lisa will personally take your call.
