About Us

Built on Hard Work

From a one-man crew to a 40-person team, Summit Build Co. has spent 25 years earning the trust of families and businesses across the region.

Our Story

Started With a Pickup Truck and a Handshake

In 1999, Mike Summit left his superintendent position at a large construction firm and went out on his own. Armed with a beat-up Ford F-250, a set of tools, and a reputation for doing honest work, he started taking on small residential jobs around town.

Word spread. Mike showed up on time, delivered quality work, and charged a fair price. One job led to two, two led to ten. By 2005, Summit Build had a proper crew. By 2010, we were handling commercial projects. Today, we're a 40-person team with a fleet of trucks and a shop on Industrial Parkway.

The trucks are nicer now, but the handshake still means the same thing.
