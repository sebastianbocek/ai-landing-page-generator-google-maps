Custom Deck Building

Starting at $15,000

This is what we do best. Every custom deck starts with a conversation about how you want to use your outdoor space, followed by a site evaluation, custom design, and expert construction. We handle the entire process from permits to final walkthrough.

We build decks that are engineered for your specific site conditions, designed around your lifestyle, and constructed with materials that will hold up for decades.

  • Ground-level decks and platforms
  • Raised and elevated decks
  • Multi-level and tiered decks
  • Wraparound and L-shaped decks
  • Pool decks and spa surrounds
  • Rooftop and balcony decks

Bring It Back to Life

Deck Restoration & Repair

Starting at $2,500

Not every deck needs to be replaced. If your deck's structure is still sound, a professional restoration can make it look and feel like new at a fraction of the cost of a full rebuild. We assess the structural integrity first, then recommend the right level of restoration.

From surface-level cosmetic work to full board replacement and structural reinforcement, we bring aging decks back to their best condition.

  • Professional power washing and cleaning
  • Full sanding and surface preparation
  • Premium staining and sealing
  • Individual board replacement
  • Structural repair and reinforcement
  • Hardware and fastener replacement

Shade & Structure

Pergolas & Covered Patios

Starting at $5,000

A pergola or patio cover transforms your deck from a seasonal space into a year-round outdoor room. We design and build structures that complement your home's architecture and provide the right amount of shade and protection for your climate.

Every structure is custom-designed and engineered to handle local wind and snow loads. We build with the same premium materials and attention to detail that go into our decks.

  • Attached pergolas (connected to the home)
  • Freestanding pergolas (garden and patio)
  • Covered patios with solid roofing
  • Louvered pergola systems
  • Shade sail integration
  • Fan, lighting, and heater pre-wiring

Safety Meets Style

Railings & Stairs

Starting at $3,000

Railings are one of the most visible parts of your deck, and the right choice can completely change the look and feel of the space. We install a wide range of railing styles -- from traditional wood balusters to modern cable and glass systems.

All railings are installed to meet or exceed local building codes, with proper post spacing, height, and baluster gaps for safety.

  • Traditional wood balusters
  • Stainless steel cable railing
  • Tempered glass panels
  • Composite and vinyl railing systems
  • Custom stair designs and landings
  • ADA-compliant ramp options

Finishing Touches

Deck Add-Ons & Upgrades

The details that turn a good deck into a great outdoor living space.

Built-In Seating

Custom bench seating integrated into the deck design. Perfect for entertaining without cluttering the space with furniture.

Planter Boxes

Built-in planter boxes that bring greenery onto the deck and create natural privacy screens along the perimeter.

Deck Lighting

Integrated LED lighting for stairs, railings, and post caps. Extend your deck time into the evening with warm ambient light.

Fire Pit Integration

Built-in gas or wood-burning fire pit areas with proper clearances, ventilation, and fire-resistant materials for safe enjoyment.

Outdoor Kitchens

Custom outdoor kitchen areas with countertops, grill surrounds, storage, and utility connections for the ultimate entertaining space.

Privacy Screens

Lattice, slat, or solid privacy screens that provide seclusion from neighbors without sacrificing airflow or aesthetics.
