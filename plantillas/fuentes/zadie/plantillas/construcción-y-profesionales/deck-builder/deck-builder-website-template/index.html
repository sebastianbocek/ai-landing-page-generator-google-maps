How We Work

Our Process

From first conversation to final walkthrough, every step is designed to keep you informed and the project on track.

1

Design Consultation

We visit your home, measure the space, discuss your vision and budget, and walk you through material options. No pressure, no obligations -- just an honest conversation about what is possible.

2

3D Rendering & Plans

Our design team creates detailed 3D renderings so you can see your new deck before a single board is cut. We refine the design until it is exactly what you want, then pull all necessary permits.

3

Build Phase

Our crew arrives on time, keeps a clean job site, and builds your deck to spec. We send daily photo updates so you always know how the project is progressing, even when you are at work.

4

Final Walkthrough

We walk every inch of the finished deck together. We do not consider the job done until you are completely satisfied. You will also receive a maintenance guide and warranty documentation.
