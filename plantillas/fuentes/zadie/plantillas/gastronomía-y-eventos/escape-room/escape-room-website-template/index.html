The Process

How It Works

No experience required. First timers welcome. All you need is a team and the nerve to try.

01

Book Your Room

Choose your room, pick a time slot, and reserve online. You'll get a confirmation email with everything you need to know before you arrive.

02

Enter the Room

Your Game Master briefs you on the story and rules, then locks you in. From that moment, you're completely inside the world we've built for you.

03

Race the Clock

Sixty minutes. Every clue matters. Work together, think sideways, and don't panic. Need a hint? Your Game Master is watching and ready to nudge you back on track.
