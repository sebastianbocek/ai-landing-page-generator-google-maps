Getting Here

Find Your Way to the Estate

Stone Ridge Vineyards is located in the heart of the Willamette Valley wine country, approximately 45 minutes southwest of Portland and 15 minutes east of downtown McMinnville.

1
From Portland, take Hwy 18 West toward McMinnville. Continue approximately 35 miles to the McMinnville exit.
2
Turn left onto NE 3rd Street through downtown McMinnville. Continue east on NE Lafayette Avenue for 6 miles.
3
Turn right onto Stone Ridge Road. The winery entrance is 0.8 miles on your right, marked by the stone gate and vineyard sign.
4
Follow the gravel drive through the Chardonnay block approximately 0.3 miles to the tasting room parking area.

Tip: GPS may not recognize the full address. Search "Stone Ridge Vineyards McMinnville OR" and follow signage once you reach the rural area.

Common Questions

Before You Arrive

Walk-ins are welcome Thursday through Sunday during posted hours. However, weekend afternoons can fill quickly, and we can't guarantee tableside availability without a reservation. Reserve tastings and barrel tastings always require advance booking. Reservations can be made through the form on this page or by calling (503) 555-0192.
Yes, for our standard Flight Tasting. The $25 per person fee is fully waived when each guest purchases two or more bottles. Wine club members always taste complimentary within their annual allotment. Reserve and Barrel tasting fees are separate and not waived with purchase, though both include bottle discounts.
You can sign up in person at the tasting room or contact us by email. Our Cellar Club starts at $120 per shipment (6 bottles, twice per year). The Estate Club is $240 per shipment (12 bottles including reserve wines). There's no long-term commitment. You can cancel or pause at any time with at least 10 days' notice before the next shipment date.
We ship to most US states, including California, Washington, New York, Texas, Colorado, Florida, and 30+ others. Due to state regulations, we're currently unable to ship to Alabama, Mississippi, Kentucky, Utah, and a handful of others. An adult 21+ signature is required at delivery. Contact us for the current list of states and shipping rates before signing up for the wine club.
Weddings and large celebrations should be booked 10–14 months in advance, particularly for dates between May and October. Corporate events and intimate dinners (up to 14 guests) can typically be arranged with 6–8 weeks' notice. Private barrel cave dinners on Fridays and Saturdays book 4–8 weeks out. Contact our events team early to discuss availability.
Children are welcome in our outdoor seating area and on the estate terrace. Due to state licensing requirements, minors may not enter the tasting room itself. Well-behaved dogs on leash are welcome on our outdoor terrace and the vineyard walking paths. We keep a water bowl and occasional biscuits at the ready. Please clean up after your pets on the estate grounds.
Yes. Our full portfolio, including library wines and limited single-block releases, is available for purchase at the tasting room. Some wines are only available on-site or to club members. We occasionally have cases of sold-out vintages available in the cellar for special requests. Wine club members get first access to futures and early release wines before they're available to the public.
