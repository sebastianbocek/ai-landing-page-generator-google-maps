Our Heritage

Rooted in the Land Since 1987

What began as a 40-acre gamble by Harold and Margaret Stone has grown into a multigenerational estate celebrated for wines that capture the distinctive character of Oregon's Willamette Valley. We farm the way the valley deserves: with patience, precision, and a deep respect for terroir.

Today, under the stewardship of their granddaughter, Elena Stone, Stone Ridge Vineyards remains entirely family owned. The philosophy hasn't changed. The wines just keep getting better.

The Cellar Selection

Each vintage begins and ends on the estate. Our winemaker selects only the finest fruit from our oldest blocks, then lets time do the rest.

Cabernet Sauvignon

Stone Ridge Reserve

Dark cherry, cedar, and graphite on the nose. Full-bodied with structured tannins, a long finish of dark chocolate and dried herb. Aged 22 months in French oak.

2021 Vintage • 14.2% ABV • 480 cases
Chardonnay

Parchment Block

Ripe pear, toasted hazelnut, and a hint of vanilla cream. Bright acidity balances generous texture, with a clean, mineral-driven finish. Aged 10 months on the lees.

2022 Vintage • 13.8% ABV • 620 cases
Rosé

Ridge Road Rosé

Delicate strawberry blossom and white peach, with a dry, Provence-inspired finish. Pressed whole-cluster from Pinot Noir fruit grown on the estate's highest elevation block.

2023 Vintage • 12.5% ABV • 800 cases
