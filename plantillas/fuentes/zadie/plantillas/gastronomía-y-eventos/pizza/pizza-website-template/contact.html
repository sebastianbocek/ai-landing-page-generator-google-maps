Getting Here

Parking Church Street is pedestrian-only. Park at the Marketplace Garage on Cherry Street (2-minute walk). Metered street parking available on Pearl Street and College Street.

Public Transit Green Mountain Transit Route 1 stops on Church Street. We're one block from the downtown hub.

Bike Parking Bike racks available directly outside the restaurant and at City Hall Park, 50 feet away.

Accessibility Hearthstone is fully wheelchair accessible. Accessible restrooms available. Please let us know if you need any specific accommodations.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need a reservation? How far in advance should I book?

Reservations are strongly recommended for Friday and Saturday evenings, especially parties of 4 or more. We hold a portion of tables for walk-ins every night, but there's no guarantee during peak hours. For weekend dinners, booking 5–7 days ahead is a good rule. Tuesday through Thursday you can often book same-day with no problem. Use this form or call us directly at (555) 672-3841.

What's your delivery area? Do you use third-party apps?

We deliver within a 5-mile radius of downtown Burlington, which covers most of the city, South Burlington, and parts of Winooski. We do our own delivery — no third-party apps. That means we control quality, we pay our drivers fairly, and you don't pay platform fees on top of your order. Call us to place a delivery order: (555) 672-3841. Delivery hours match our dine-in hours. Minimum order for delivery is $30.

How does catering work? How far out do you book?

We bring a portable wood-fired oven to your venue and cook fresh pizzas on-site throughout your event. We serve parties from 20 to 200 guests. The process starts with an inquiry form or a call — we'll talk through your event, confirm the venue is accessible for the oven setup, and get you a custom quote. We require a 50% deposit to reserve your date. For summer weekends (June through August), we book out 6–10 weeks. Spring and fall dates often have more flexibility. Winter catering is available for indoor venues.

Do you have gluten-free options?

We offer a gluten-free pizza crust made with a rice and tapioca flour blend. It's baked in the same oven as our wheat-based pizzas — we use separate tools and handle it carefully, but we cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination. If you have celiac disease, please let us know and we'll talk through what's safe. Our pasta contains gluten. Several menu items — the caprese, burrata, salads, and most desserts — are naturally gluten-free. We're happy to walk you through the menu; just ask your server or call ahead.

Can we book the restaurant for a private event or full buyout?

Yes. Hearthstone is available for full buyouts on Monday (our usual closed day) and during select off-hours on other days, subject to availability. We can accommodate up to 60 guests for seated dinners and up to 80 for a standing cocktail-style event. A full buyout includes a custom menu, dedicated servers, full bar service, and the full Marco-and-Elena experience. Minimum food and beverage spend applies. For private event inquiries, use the form above and select "Private Event / Buyout" — or call Elena directly at (555) 672-3841.
