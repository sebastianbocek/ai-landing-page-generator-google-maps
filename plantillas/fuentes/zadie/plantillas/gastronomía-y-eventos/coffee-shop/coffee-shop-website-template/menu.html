Good to Know

Milk Options

All espresso drinks can be made with your choice of whole milk, 2%, oat milk, almond milk, or soy milk. Dairy alternatives available at no extra charge.

Dietary Preferences

We offer gluten-free muffins and vegan pastries daily. Ask your barista for today's selection.

Sizes

Most drinks come in 12oz (small), 16oz (medium), or 20oz (large). Prices shown are for medium. Add $0.50 for large.
