Good to Know

Parking & Accessibility

We want your visit to be as smooth as possible. Street parking is available on Main Street and nearby side streets—most spots are free after 6pm and all day Sunday.

There's also a public parking garage just two blocks away on Oak Street with affordable hourly rates. Our shop is fully wheelchair accessible with a ramped entrance and accessible restroom.

  • Street parking available nearby
  • Public garage 2 blocks away
  • Wheelchair accessible entrance
  • Accessible restroom facilities
  • Bike rack out front

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer catering?

Yes! We cater coffee and pastries for meetings, events, and parties. Contact us at least 48 hours in advance to discuss options and pricing.

Can I work on my laptop here?

Absolutely! We welcome laptop users and have free WiFi, plenty of outlets, and comfortable seating. Just be mindful during our busiest hours (7-9am weekdays).

Do you have gluten-free options?

We offer gluten-free muffins and pastries daily. Selection varies, so ask your barista what's available today.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes! Gift cards are available in any amount and can be purchased in-store or by calling us. Perfect for coffee lovers!

Can I reserve the space for a private event?

We offer private events on Sunday evenings after 6pm and occasionally on other days. Email hello@brewhaven.com to discuss availability and options.

Do you sell whole bean coffee?

Yes! We sell 12oz bags of our house blend and rotating single-origin beans. Take home the coffee you love.
