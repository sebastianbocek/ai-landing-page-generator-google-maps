The Artist Behind the Lens

Dedicated to capturing authentic moments and creating timeless portraits that tell your unique story.

My Story

Photography Chose Me

I still remember the first time I looked through a viewfinder—a borrowed film camera at age twelve, capturing my grandmother in her garden. That moment changed everything.

What started as a childhood fascination became a lifelong passion. After studying fine art photography at the Rhode Island School of Design and apprenticing with some of the industry's most respected portrait photographers, I opened Aperture Portrait Studio in 2009 with a simple mission: to create portraits that capture not just how people look, but who they truly are.

Fifteen years later, that mission remains unchanged. Every session is an opportunity to connect, to find the light that reveals something genuine, and to create images that families will treasure for generations.

— Victoria Ashford

Our Approach

Photography Philosophy

The principles that guide every session and shape every image we create.

Authentic Connection

The best portraits emerge from genuine moments. We take time to connect with every client, creating a comfortable environment where authentic expressions naturally unfold.

Timeless Artistry

Trends come and go, but true artistry endures. We focus on classic composition, beautiful light, and emotional depth—creating images that will look as stunning in fifty years as they do today.

Personal Experience

From your first inquiry to final delivery, we provide a boutique experience tailored to you. No templates, no rushing—just dedicated attention to bringing your vision to life.

The Journey

Your Session Experience

From first contact to final delivery, here's what to expect when you book with Aperture.

01

Consultation

We begin with a complimentary consultation—whether in person, by phone, or video call—to discuss your vision, answer questions, and plan every detail.

02

Preparation

You'll receive a detailed preparation guide with wardrobe suggestions, location options, and tips to ensure everyone is ready for their close-up.

03

Session Day

The fun part! Whether in our natural-light studio or on location, we'll create a relaxed atmosphere where genuine moments happen naturally.

04

Reveal & Delivery

2-3 weeks after your session, you'll view your images in a private gallery and select your favorites for professional editing and delivery.

Our Space

The Studio

A welcoming, light-filled space designed for comfort and creativity.

Natural Light Studio

Our 1,800 sq ft main studio features floor-to-ceiling north-facing windows that flood the space with soft, beautiful natural light—perfect for portraits with a timeless, airy quality.

Backdrop Collection

Choose from our curated collection of hand-painted canvas backdrops, modern seamless options, and vintage furniture pieces to complement your unique style and vision.

Client Comfort

Our studio includes a comfortable lounge area with refreshments, a private changing room with full-length mirror, and a nursing-friendly space for new mothers.

Expertise

Professional Excellence

Investing in the best equipment and continuous education ensures that every image we create meets the highest professional standards.

We shoot exclusively with professional-grade Canon and Hasselblad camera systems, utilizing a range of prime lenses chosen for their exceptional sharpness and beautiful rendering. Our lighting equipment includes both natural light modifiers and Profoto studio strobes for complete creative control.

Beyond equipment, we believe in continuous growth. Annual attendance at industry conferences, ongoing mentorship with master photographers, and regular portfolio reviews ensure our artistry continues to evolve and improve.

Giving Back
