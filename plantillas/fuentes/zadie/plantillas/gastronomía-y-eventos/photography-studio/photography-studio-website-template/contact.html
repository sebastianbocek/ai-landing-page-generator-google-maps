Studio Location

Cross streets: Main St & Oak Ave
Building: The Cornerstone Building (brick, 2nd floor)

Parking

Free 2-hour street parking available on Evergreen Terrace and Oak Avenue. Additional parking in the municipal lot on Main Street (first hour free). We validate parking for sessions over 2 hours.

Accessibility

Our building features elevator access to the 2nd floor studio. The studio is wheelchair accessible with wide doorways and an accessible restroom. Please let us know of any special needs when booking.
