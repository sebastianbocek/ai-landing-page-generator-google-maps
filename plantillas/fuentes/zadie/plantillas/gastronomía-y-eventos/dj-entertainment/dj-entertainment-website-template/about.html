The Main Act

Marcus Webb. The One Who Started It All.

I started spinning at 16 years old in my friend's basement in South Austin. I was mixing hip-hop and R&B on a pair of borrowed Technics 1200s. I got my first real paid gig at 19 — a college house party. $100. I thought I'd made it.

By 25, I was touring as a support DJ for touring acts, playing festival stages in three states. I learned more in two years of touring than I could have in a decade of playing bars. How crowds move. How energy builds. When to drop and when to hold back.

I came back to Austin in 2015 and built BassLine from the ground up. Started with weddings and private parties. Grew into corporate work, club residencies, and festival headlining slots. Today we have a team of three DJs and we turn down more gigs than we take, because we refuse to be mediocre.

"Every playlist is custom. Every set is live. No pre-recorded mixes. If I'm booked to play your event, I'm showing up to read your room and perform."

— Marcus Webb, Founder

How We Work

The BassLine Philosophy

Listen First

We do a planning call before every event. We ask about your crowd, your vibe, your must-plays, and your absolute no-gos. We show up knowing your event before we walk in the door.

Read the Room

No two events feel the same, even when you play the same songs. We adapt in real time. If the floor is lagging, we adjust. If the energy spikes, we ride it. That's a live set.

Deliver Every Time

We've never no-showed. We've never had a technical failure that stopped the music. We carry backup gear for every booking because your event is not the night to wing it.
