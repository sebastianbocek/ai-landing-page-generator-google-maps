Quick Answers

Before You Ask

Can we request specific songs?

Absolutely. We share a planning form where you can list must-play songs, do-not-plays, and preferred genres. We build every set around it.

Do you take breaks?

No dead air, ever. If we need a moment, we have a seamless loop queued. For events over 5 hours, we bring a second DJ to rotate.

What music genres do you cover?

Hip-Hop, Top 40, EDM, R&B, Latin, Rock, Country, 80s/90s, House, Jazz/Lounge, and everything in between. Our team covers it all.

Do you travel outside Austin?

Yes — we're available nationwide and have played in 12 states. Travel costs are billed at cost (flight, hotel, per diem). Ask for a quote.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable. Full payment refunded (minus deposit) for cancellations 30+ days out. Within 30 days, 50% of remaining balance retained.

Do you provide the sound system?

Yes — all packages include our professional rig. For venue residencies, we can also work with existing house systems. Just let us know in advance.
