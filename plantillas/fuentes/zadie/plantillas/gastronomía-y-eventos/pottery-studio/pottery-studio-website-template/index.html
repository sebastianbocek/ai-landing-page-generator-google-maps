What We Offer

Find Your Medium

From the meditative spin of the wheel to the tactile freedom of hand building, every class is a chance to slow down and create something with your hands.

Wheel Throwing

Learn to center, pull, and shape clay on the potter's wheel. Our signature course takes you from your first wobbly cylinder to confident bowls, mugs, and vases.

View Course

Hand Building

Pinch, coil, and slab your way to beautiful ceramics. No wheel required. Hand building is tactile, intuitive, and perfect for sculptural work and organic shapes.

View Course

Glazing & Firing

The magic happens in the kiln. Learn about glaze chemistry, surface decoration, underglazes, and firing techniques that bring your pieces to life with color and texture.

View Course
Why The Clay Room

Made for Makers

We built the studio we always wished existed - welcoming, well-equipped, and focused on the joy of making.

Small Class Sizes

No more than 8 students per class. You'll get real one-on-one attention, not just a demo from across a crowded room.

All Materials Included

Clay, glazes, tools, kiln firing, and an apron are all included. Just show up and create. We handle the messy logistics.

Expert Instructors

Our instructors are working ceramicists with MFA degrees and years of teaching experience. They live and breathe clay.

Take Home Your Work

Every piece you make is yours to keep. We'll fire and glaze it for you, and it'll be ready for pickup in 2-3 weeks.
