Our Classes

Find Your Class

Every class includes all materials, tools, kiln firing, and studio access during class hours. Just bring yourself (and clothes you don't mind getting muddy).

Intro to Wheel Throwing

6-Week Course Tuesdays, 6:30 - 9:00 PM Max 8 Students

Our most popular class. Start from scratch and learn to center clay, pull walls, and shape bowls, cups, and cylinders on the wheel. By week six, you'll have a set of pieces to glaze and take home. No experience needed -- just a willingness to get muddy and a tolerance for imperfection.

$280 per person Enroll

Hand Building Workshop

4-Week Course Thursdays, 6:30 - 9:00 PM Max 8 Students

No wheel? No problem. Hand building is the oldest form of pottery -- and some of the most stunning. Learn pinch, coil, and slab techniques to create everything from planters and plates to sculptural forms. This class rewards patience, imagination, and a love for texture.

$220 per person Enroll

Glazing & Surface Design

3-Week Course Saturdays, 10:00 AM - 12:30 PM Max 8 Students

The kiln is where clay becomes ceramic -- and glaze is where personality shows up. Learn about glaze chemistry, dipping, brushing, wax resist, underglazes, and surface decoration techniques. Bring bisque-fired pieces or use ours. This is where your work gets its final voice.

$180 per person Enroll

Date Night Pottery

Single Session Fridays, 7:00 - 9:30 PM Max 12 People (6 Couples)

Think Ghost, but with better instruction and less dramatic music. Grab your partner, a friend, or a first date and spend an evening at the wheel. You'll each throw two pieces, choose glazes, and have a genuinely memorable night that does not involve staring at a menu. Wine and snacks welcome.

$95 per couple Book Now

A full week of clay exploration for young makers. Kids learn pinch pots, coil building, slab construction, and basic wheel throwing in a fun, low-pressure environment. They'll take home 3-5 finished pieces and a deep appreciation for getting their hands dirty. All materials and snacks included.

Before You Come

What to Expect

Everything you need to know before your first class at The Clay Room.

What to Wear

Wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty. Clay is washable, but it splashes. Avoid loose sleeves and long jewelry. We provide aprons, but they only do so much. Closed-toe shoes are required in the studio.

What's Provided

Everything. Clay, tools, glazes, kiln firing, and aprons are all included in your class fee. We also have a small selection of beverages and snacks available. Just bring yourself and a willingness to learn.

Firing Timeline

After you finish a piece, it needs to dry completely (about a week), get bisque fired, then glazed, then glaze fired. The whole process takes 2-3 weeks. We'll email you when your pieces are ready for pickup.
