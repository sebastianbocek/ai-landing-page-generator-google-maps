Frequently Asked

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Do you take reservations?

We accept reservations for parties of 6 or more. For smaller groups, we operate on a first-come, first-served basis. You can call us at (512) 555-0187 or use the contact form above to request a reservation. We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance for weekend dinners.

How long is the typical wait?

On weekdays, you can usually walk right in during lunch and expect a 10-15 minute wait for dinner. Weekends are busier -- Friday and Saturday dinner waits can run 30-45 minutes during peak hours (6-8pm). We don't have a formal waitlist app, but we'll take your name at the door and text you when your table is ready.

Do you cater events?

Yes. We cater everything from backyard cookouts to corporate events and weddings. We have three standard platter sizes (10, 25, and 50 people) and we're happy to build custom packages for larger groups. Catering orders require at least one week's notice. Reach out through the form above or call us to get started.

Do you have options for dietary restrictions?

Our smoked turkey is our leanest protein option, and several of our sides are vegetarian-friendly: the coleslaw, potato salad, cornbread, and collard greens (without the ham hock, available on request). We can accommodate most common allergies if you let us know when ordering. Our menu is not gluten-free, but our meats and most sides are naturally gluten-free.

Where do I park?

We have a dedicated lot behind the building with about 40 spaces. Street parking is also available on Ember Trail Road and the surrounding blocks. On busy weekend nights, the lot can fill up, so street parking or rideshare is a good backup plan. Bike racks are available out front.

Can I book a private event?

We have a semi-private dining area that seats up to 30 guests, available for private events Tuesday through Thursday and for Sunday brunch. Full restaurant buyouts are available on Monday (our regular closed day) for parties of 80 or more. Contact us at least two weeks in advance for private event inquiries.
