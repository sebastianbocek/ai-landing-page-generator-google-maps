Learn With Us

Baking Classes

Hands-on workshops taught by our bakers. Small groups, real techniques, and you take home everything you bake.

Sourdough 101

Learn to create and maintain a sourdough starter, mix and shape a basic boule, and understand fermentation. You will leave with your own starter and a loaf of bread. Perfect for beginners.

$75 per person

Croissant Workshop

A full-day deep dive into laminated dough. Learn to make butter blocks, fold and turn, and bake golden croissants. Taught by pastry chef Yuki Tanaka. Intermediate level recommended.

$95 per person

Seasonal Baking

Each season brings a different class focused on what is fresh and in season. Spring: strawberry galettes. Summer: peach cobbler. Fall: pumpkin bread. Winter: gingerbread and stollen.

$65 per person
