Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. If yours is not here, send us a message above.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. We take custom bread orders, special cake orders, and catering requests. For bread and pastry orders, we ask for at least 48 hours notice. For custom cakes and catering, we recommend reaching out at least one week in advance. Call us or use the contact form above to get started.

Are your items nut-free or gluten-free?

Our bakery handles wheat, tree nuts, dairy, and eggs daily. We cannot guarantee any item is free from allergens. We do not currently offer gluten-free bread or pastries, but we have a few cafe menu items that are naturally gluten-free. Please ask a team member about specific dietary needs when you visit.

Do you make wedding cakes?

We do. Wedding cakes are handled by consultation with Elena and Yuki. We can create anything from a simple buttercream cake to an elaborate multi-tier design. We recommend booking at least two months in advance. Contact us to schedule a tasting and consultation.

Can I pre-order bread?

Absolutely. Our most popular loaves, especially the sourdough boule, sell out early. If you want to guarantee a loaf, call us by 2 PM the day before and we will set one aside for you. We also take bulk pre-orders for holidays and special occasions.

Do you cater events?

Yes. We offer pastry platters, sandwich trays, bread baskets, and full coffee service for events of all sizes. We have catered everything from office meetings to 150-person wedding brunches. Use the contact form and select "Catering" as the inquiry type, or call us to discuss your event.

How do I sign up for cooking classes?

Our baking classes run throughout the year in small groups. You can sign up by contacting us through the form above and selecting "Cooking Class" as the inquiry type. We will send you the upcoming schedule and available dates. Classes fill up fast, so we recommend signing up early.
