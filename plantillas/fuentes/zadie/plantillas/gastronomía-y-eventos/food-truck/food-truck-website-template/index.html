Our Story

From Backyard to Blacktop

Rolling Fire Kitchen started the way all the best things do: a beat-up grill, a cooler of cold drinks, and a group of friends who kept coming back for more. Chef Marco Fuentes turned weekend cookouts into a full-blown food truck operation, and he hasn't looked back since.

Every dish on our menu is cooked over real fire. No microwaves, no heat lamps, no shortcuts. Just open flame, fresh ingredients, and recipes that took years of experimenting to get right.
