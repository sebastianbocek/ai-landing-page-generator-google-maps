FAQ

Common Questions

Do You Cater Private Events?

Absolutely. We cater everything from backyard birthdays to 300-person corporate events. We bring the full truck, the full crew, and cook everything fresh on-site. Fill out the form above or call us to get started.

Where Are You Parked Today?

Our weekly schedule is posted on this page and on our homepage. For real-time updates (including surprise pop-ups and schedule changes), follow us on Instagram @RollingFireKitchen.

Do You Have Vegetarian Options?

Yes. Our fire-roasted veggie tacos, the Rolling Fire Bowl with grilled veggies, elote, regular fries, and chips & salsa are all vegetarian. We also have vegan-friendly options. Just ask at the window.

How Far in Advance Should I Book Catering?

We recommend at least 2-3 weeks for most events. For weddings and large corporate events, 4-6 weeks is ideal. That said, we've pulled off last-minute gigs before. Call us and we'll see what we can do.

Do You Do Food Truck Festivals?

We love festivals. We participate in local food truck rallies, night markets, and street food events regularly. If you're organizing a festival and want Rolling Fire there, reach out through the form or email us directly.

Can I Hire You for a Wedding?

100%. Wedding catering is one of our favorite things to do. We'll work with you on a custom menu, timing, and setup. Your guests get fresh, fire-cooked food instead of the usual banquet hall situation. It's always a hit.
