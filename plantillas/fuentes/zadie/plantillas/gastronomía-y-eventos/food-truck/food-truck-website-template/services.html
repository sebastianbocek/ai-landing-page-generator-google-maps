Fire-Roasted Street Tacos

Choice of Protein, Corn Tortilla, Cilantro, Onion, Charred Salsa

$10

Loaded Fire Fries

Hand-cut fries double-fried until perfectly crispy, piled high with fire-roasted peppers, house queso, applewood bacon bits, and jalapeños.

$8

The Inferno Sandwich

Slow-smoked pulled pork (8 hours over mesquite), topped with Priya's famous ghost pepper slaw and tangy Carolina BBQ sauce on a buttery brioche bun. Not for the faint of heart.

Pulled Pork, Ghost Pepper Slaw, Carolina BBQ, Brioche Bun

$14

Rolling Fire Bowl

Cilantro-lime rice, seasoned black beans, your choice of grilled protein (chicken, steak, or veggie), fresh pico de gallo, crema, and our signature fire sauce drizzled on top.

Rice, Black Beans, Grilled Protein, Pico, Crema, Fire Sauce

$13

Elote on the Cob

Whole cob grilled over open flame until charred and sweet, slathered with mayo, rolled in cotija cheese, and finished with chili-lime seasoning. The perfect side or snack.

Want Rolling Fire at your next party, corporate event, wedding, or festival? We pull up with the full truck, the full crew, and the full menu. Your guests eat fresh, hot food cooked right in front of them. No chafing dishes. No cold trays. Just real food, real fire, and a real good time.
