What We Do

Catering for Every Occasion

Whether it's an intimate gathering or a grand celebration, we bring the same dedication to every event we serve.

Wedding Receptions

From cocktail hour to the last dance, we create a seamless dining experience with custom menus, tableside service, and elegant presentations that match your vision.

Corporate Events

Impress clients and energize your team with polished catering for conferences, product launches, holiday parties, and executive dinners tailored to your brand.

Private Parties

Birthday milestones, anniversary celebrations, engagement parties. We handle the food so you can focus on the people who matter most.

Holiday Celebrations

Thanksgiving feasts, holiday office parties, New Year's Eve galas. Seasonal menus that capture the spirit of every holiday with farm-fresh ingredients.

Milestone Events

Graduations, retirements, reunions, and other once-in-a-lifetime moments deserve food that rises to the occasion. We make sure it does.

Outdoor Gatherings

Garden parties, vineyard dinners, estate celebrations. We bring our full kitchen to outdoor venues with mobile cooking stations and rustic service setups.

Our Approach

Why Harvest Table

We believe great food starts with great ingredients and ends with great memories.

Farm-to-Table Philosophy

We partner with over 45 local farms to source the freshest seasonal ingredients. Every dish reflects what's at peak flavor right now, not what's in a warehouse.

Handcrafted Menus

No cookie-cutter menus here. Chef Marcus works directly with you to design a custom menu that tells your story through food, drink, and presentation.

Full-Service Experience

Beyond the food, we coordinate with your venue, manage setup and breakdown, provide serving staff, and handle every detail so your event runs flawlessly.

Menu Highlights

Appetizer

Burrata with Roasted Figs

Creamy burrata nestled among honey-roasted figs, toasted walnuts, and a drizzle of aged balsamic from our partner vineyard. Served on a bed of wild arugula.
