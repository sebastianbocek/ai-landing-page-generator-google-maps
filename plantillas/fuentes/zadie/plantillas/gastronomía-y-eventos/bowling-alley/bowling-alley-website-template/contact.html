FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often at the front desk.

Do I need a reservation to bowl?

Walk-ins are always welcome, and we rarely turn anyone away during weekday daytime hours. However, we strongly recommend reserving lanes for weekend evenings and cosmic bowling nights, as those fill up fast. You can call us at (555) 732-5867 or use the contact form above to reserve. Birthday parties and corporate events always require advance booking.

Are there age restrictions for cosmic bowling?

Cosmic bowling is open to all ages until 10 PM on Friday and Saturday nights. After 10 PM, cosmic bowling is 18+ only. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian before the cutoff. Our regular open bowling sessions are always family-friendly with no age restrictions.

Do you serve food at the bowling alley?

Absolutely. Our kitchen is open during all bowling hours and serves burgers, chicken wings, loaded nachos, pizza, fries, mozzarella sticks, and more. We also have a full bar with 24 craft beers on tap, cocktails, wine, and non-alcoholic beverages. Food and drinks can be ordered right from your lane and delivered to your seating area.

What shoe sizes do you carry for rental?

We carry rental shoes in all sizes, from toddler size 7 all the way up to men's size 15 and women's size 13. All rental shoes are sanitized between uses. If you prefer, you can bring your own bowling shoes or purchase a pair from our pro shop.

When is cosmic bowling and what does it include?

Cosmic bowling runs every Friday and Saturday night from 9 PM to midnight. The experience includes blacklights, neon glow-in-the-dark pins, fog machines, a laser light show, and a live DJ playing top hits. The cosmic bowl rate is $45 per lane per hour and includes shoe rental for everyone in your group. It's the most popular session we offer, so reserving ahead is highly recommended.

How do I sign up for a league?

League registration opens twice a year: in August for the fall/winter season and in January for the spring/summer season. You can sign up as an individual (we'll place you on a team) or register a full team. Stop by the front desk, call us at (555) 732-5867, or fill out the contact form above with "League Information" as the inquiry type. We'll get you set up and ready to compete.
