What We Offer

Roll Into the Good Times

Whether you're a casual bowler or a league competitor, we've got something for everyone under our neon lights.

Open Bowling

Walk in any time and grab a lane. No reservations needed for weekday open bowl sessions. Shoe rental available for all sizes, from toddler to adult. Perfect for a quick game or an afternoon with friends.

Cosmic Nights

Every Friday and Saturday night, the lights go down and the neon comes up. Blacklights, fog machines, laser shows, and a pumping sound system turn our lanes into the hottest night out in town.

Leagues & Tournaments

Join one of our adult, youth, or senior leagues running throughout the week. We host monthly tournaments with cash prizes and trophies. All skill levels welcome -- beginners to pros.

The Experience

More Than Just Bowling

Lucky Strike Lanes is a full entertainment destination. After you finish your frames, stick around and explore everything else we have to offer under one roof.

  • 32 state-of-the-art lanes with automatic scoring
  • Full-service bar with 24 craft beers on tap
  • Retro arcade with 40+ classic and modern games
  • 3 private party rooms for birthdays and events
  • Pro shop with expert ball drilling on-site
  • Kitchen serving burgers, wings, and pizza until close

Special Events

Let the Good Times Roll

From cosmic nights to corporate team building, Lucky Strike Lanes is the venue for every occasion.

Cosmic Saturday Nights

Our signature event. Every Saturday from 9 PM to midnight, experience bowling like never before. Blacklights, neon pins, fog machines, and a live DJ spinning the best tracks. $35 per lane per hour includes shoes. Must be 18+ after 10 PM.

Kids Birthday Parties

Throw a birthday bash they'll never forget. Our party packages include lane time, shoes, pizza, drinks, and a dedicated party host. We handle everything so you can enjoy the celebration. Packages start at $199 for up to 10 kids.

Corporate Events

Build team spirit on the lanes. Our corporate packages include reserved lanes, catering options, private party rooms, and custom tournament brackets. Perfect for team outings, holiday parties, and client entertainment. Groups of 20 to 200.
