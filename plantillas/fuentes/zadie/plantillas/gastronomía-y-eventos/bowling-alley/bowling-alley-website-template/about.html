Our Story

Rolling Since '87

Lucky Strike Lanes started as a dream shared by Tony and Maria Castellano, two bowling fanatics who wanted to create a place where families and friends could come together over a game and a good time. In 1987, they opened the doors to an 18-lane alley on the south side of Pinsville with nothing but a handshake loan and a whole lot of determination.

Nearly four decades later, we've grown to 32 state-of-the-art lanes, a full-service bar, a retro arcade, and three private party rooms. In 2022, we completed a major renovation that brought in modern automatic scoring systems, cosmic bowling lighting, and a brand-new kitchen -- all while keeping the retro soul that made this place special in the first place.

Today, Lucky Strike Lanes is run by the second generation of Castellanos, and we still treat every bowler who walks through our doors like family. That's the Lucky Strike way.

Meet the Team

The People Behind the Pins

Our crew keeps the lanes oiled, the drinks cold, and the good times rolling.

Danny Castellano

Owner & General Manager

Danny grew up behind the front desk and took over from his parents in 2015. A former competitive bowler with a 220 average, he knows every lane by heart. He oversees day-to-day operations and still personally greets regulars at the door.

Rachel Nguyen

Events Coordinator

Rachel has been organizing events at Lucky Strike for 8 years. From 200-person corporate outings to intimate birthday parties, she handles every detail with precision and a smile. Her cosmic bowling nights are legendary.

Earl "Wrench" Simmons

Head Mechanic

Earl has kept our pinsetters and lane machines running for 22 years. A certified Brunswick and AMF technician, he can diagnose a lane issue by sound alone. Our bowlers trust their strikes to his maintenance work.

The Venue

Everything Under One Roof

Lucky Strike Lanes is more than a bowling alley. It's a 28,000-square-foot entertainment center built for good times. Here's what you'll find when you walk through our doors.

  • 32 Brunswick synthetic lanes with automatic scoring and bumpers
  • Full-service bar with 24 craft beers on tap and a cocktail menu
  • Retro arcade with 40+ games including air hockey and skee-ball
  • 3 private party rooms seating 20-50 guests each
  • Pro shop with expert ball fitting and drilling services
  • Kitchen serving burgers, wings, loaded nachos, and pizza

Community

Part of the Pinsville Family

Lucky Strike Lanes has been a cornerstone of the Pinsville community for nearly 40 years. We believe bowling brings people together, and we're proud to give back to the neighborhood that's given us so much.

  • 12 active adult and youth leagues running year-round
  • Annual charity bowl-a-thon raising $30,000+ for local schools
  • Youth bowling program with coaching for kids ages 6-17
  • Senior bowling league with weekday afternoon discounts
  • Sponsor of the Pinsville High School bowling team
  • Free community open bowl day every first Sunday of the month
