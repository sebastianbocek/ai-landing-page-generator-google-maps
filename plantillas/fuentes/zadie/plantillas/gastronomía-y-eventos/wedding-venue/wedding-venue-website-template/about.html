Our Story

A Legacy of Celebration

From a historic family estate to one of wine country's most sought-after wedding venues, The Evergreen Estate has been the backdrop for love stories since 2008.

Est. 2008

From Family Home to Forever Venue

The Evergreen Estate began as the private residence of the Hartwell family, who built the property in 1923 as a countryside retreat. For decades, the estate hosted intimate family gatherings, holiday celebrations, and summer garden parties beneath its towering oaks.

In 2008, Elizabeth Hartwell opened the estate's doors to couples looking for a venue that felt like more than a rented hall. She wanted every wedding here to carry the warmth and intentionality of a family celebration.

Today, the estate has grown to include three distinct venues, a working vineyard, and a team of dedicated professionals. But the original vision remains the same: every couple who walks through our gates should feel like they are coming home.

What Guides Us

Our Values

Three principles shape every wedding we host, every detail we refine, and every relationship we build with our couples.

Timeless Elegance

We believe the most beautiful weddings are not trendy. They are timeless. Our spaces, styling, and approach are designed to create celebrations that look just as stunning in photographs twenty years from now as they do today.

Personalized Service

No two love stories are the same, and no two weddings here should be either. Our team takes the time to understand your vision, your priorities, and the small details that make your celebration unmistakably yours.

Culinary Excellence

Food is a love language at The Evergreen Estate. Our culinary team sources ingredients from local farms and our own estate gardens, crafting seasonal menus that become one of the most talked-about parts of every wedding.
