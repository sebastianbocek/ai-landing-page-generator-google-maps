Our Spaces

Three Venues, Endless Possibilities

Each venue at The Evergreen Estate offers a completely different atmosphere. Many couples choose to use multiple spaces throughout their celebration.

Indoor Venue

The Grand Ballroom

Our signature indoor venue features 30-foot coffered ceilings, three hand-blown Murano glass chandeliers, and floor-to-ceiling French doors that open onto a private garden terrace. The original hardwood floors and restored plaster walls create a sense of grandeur that photographs beautifully from every angle.

The ballroom accommodates seated dinners, cocktail hours, and dance floors with room to spare. A built-in stage area handles live bands up to 8 members.

Up to 300 guests

5,200 sq ft

Grand receptions

Outdoor Venue

The Garden Pavilion

A covered open-air pavilion nestled within three acres of English rose gardens and century-old oak trees. The structure features hand-hewn timber beams, retractable canvas panels for weather flexibility, and integrated market lighting that creates a warm glow as the sun sets.

The surrounding gardens provide natural backdrops for ceremony processions, cocktail wandering, and portrait sessions among the seasonal blooms.

Capacity
Up to 200 guests
Square Footage
3,800 sq ft
Best For
Garden ceremonies & receptions
Season
April through October
Outdoor Venue

The Vineyard Terrace

Perched on a gentle hillside overlooking our working Pinot Noir vineyard, the terrace offers uninterrupted views of rolling hills and golden-hour sunsets that have become a signature of Evergreen Estate weddings.

The natural stone terrace features built-in seating walls, a custom pergola draped with grapevines, and a dedicated bar area. It is the most intimate of our three venues and a favorite for ceremonies and smaller celebrations.

Capacity
Up to 150 guests
Square Footage
2,400 sq ft
Best For
Intimate ceremonies & dinners
Season
May through September

How It Works

Your Planning Journey

From your first visit to your grand exit, here is what the planning process looks like with our team by your side.

01

Tour & Discovery

Schedule a private tour of the estate. Walk the grounds, explore each venue, and share your vision with our team. We will answer every question and help you envision your day.

02

Design & Planning

Your dedicated coordinator works with you to design every detail: layout, timeline, vendor recommendations, menu tastings, and all the small touches that make your day yours.

03

Rehearsal & Prep

A full rehearsal walk-through on the estate, final vendor confirmations, and timeline finalization. By this point, you should feel relaxed knowing every detail is handled.

04

Your Perfect Day

Our team manages setup, vendor arrival, timeline execution, and breakdown. You and your guests simply enjoy the celebration while we handle everything behind the scenes.
