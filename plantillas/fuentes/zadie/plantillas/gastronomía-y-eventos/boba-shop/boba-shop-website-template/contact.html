Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the answers to the questions we get asked the most. Still curious? Send us a message above.

Yes! We offer oat milk, coconut milk, and almond milk as dairy-free alternatives for any milk tea on our menu. Our fruit teas are naturally dairy-free as well. Just let us know your preference when ordering and we'll make it happen.
If you're new to boba, we recommend starting at 50% (half sweet) -- it's our most popular choice. If you love your drinks sweet, go for 75% or 100%. If you prefer just a hint of sweetness, try 25%. And for the tea purists, 0% lets the natural tea flavor shine through. Our staff is always happy to help you decide.
Absolutely! Our Boba Bliss Rewards program gives you 1 point for every dollar spent. Earn 50 points and get a free drink of your choice. We also run special double-point days and exclusive member promotions. Ask any team member to sign you up -- it only takes a minute.
We sure do! Boba Bliss catering is available for parties, corporate events, weddings, and any occasion that needs a boba bar. We bring the full setup including cups, toppings, and a dedicated boba barista. Send us a catering inquiry through the form above or call us to discuss your event details.
We take allergies seriously. Our tapioca pearls are gluten-free. Many drinks can be made without dairy. However, our kitchen does handle common allergens including milk, soy, tree nuts, and wheat. Please let our team know about any allergies before ordering and we'll walk you through safe options.
Our tapioca pearls are made from tapioca starch and brown sugar, then cooked fresh every four hours throughout the day. This ensures they always have that perfect chewy, QQ texture that boba lovers crave. We never use pre-cooked or preserved pearls. If a batch has been sitting too long, we start over. That's the Boba Bliss promise.
