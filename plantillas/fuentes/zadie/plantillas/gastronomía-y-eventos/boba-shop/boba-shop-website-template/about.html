Our Story

How two boba-obsessed friends turned a dream into your favorite tea shop

How It Started

From College Dorm to Boba Dream

It all started in a tiny college dorm room where two friends, Mia and Jason, bonded over their shared obsession with authentic bubble tea. Late-night study sessions were always fueled by boba runs, and they quickly became the go-to people for tea recommendations on campus.

After graduating, they took a life-changing trip to Taiwan -- the birthplace of bubble tea. Wandering through the night markets of Taipei and Taichung, they tasted hundreds of drinks, learned traditional tea brewing methods, and studied how tapioca pearls are made from scratch.

They came back with one mission: bring that authentic boba experience home. In 2022, Boba Bliss opened its doors with a simple promise -- every drink made fresh, with real ingredients, and a whole lot of love. What started as a tiny storefront has grown into the neighborhood's favorite spot for handcrafted bubble tea.

Quality First

Our Ingredients

We believe great boba starts with great ingredients. Here's what goes into every cup we serve.

Real Tea Leaves

Premium loose-leaf teas sourced from trusted farms in Taiwan, Japan, and Thailand. Brewed fresh every single morning.

Fresh Fruit

Real mangoes, strawberries, passion fruit, and more. We puree our fruits in-house daily. No artificial flavors, ever.

House-Made Syrups

Our brown sugar, taro, and specialty syrups are all made from scratch in our kitchen. No shortcuts, no preservatives.

Fresh Tapioca Pearls

Our tapioca pearls are cooked fresh every four hours to guarantee that perfect chewy texture. If they sit too long, we toss them out.
