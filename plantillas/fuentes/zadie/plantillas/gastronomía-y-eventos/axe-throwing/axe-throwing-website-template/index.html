What We Offer

Your Axe, Your Rules

Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned thrower, we've got the perfect session for you.

Walk-In Throwing

No reservation? No problem. Drop in for a one-hour session on any open lane. Our coaches will get you throwing bullseyes in minutes. Perfect for date nights, casual hangouts, or blowing off steam after work.

League Night

Join our 8-week axe throwing league every Wednesday night. Compete against other throwers, climb the leaderboard, and fight for bragging rights. Cash prizes for the top three finishers each season.

Private Events

Birthday parties, bachelor and bachelorette blowouts, corporate team building, holiday celebrations -- we handle it all. Dedicated lanes, a private event room, and custom packages for groups up to 60.

How It Works

Three Steps to Glory

Getting started is simple. We handle the rest.

01

Book Your Session

Reserve a lane online or walk in. Pick your time slot, choose your group size, and you're locked in. We recommend booking ahead on weekends.

02

Get Coached Up

Every session starts with a safety briefing and a hands-on coaching tutorial from one of our certified instructors. You'll be throwing with confidence in under ten minutes.

03

Start Throwing

Grab your axe, line up your shot, and let it fly. Challenge your friends to a round, try trick shots, or just aim for that satisfying bullseye thud. Beers are on tap between rounds.
