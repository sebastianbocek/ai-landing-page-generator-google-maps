League Night

Wednesday Night Showdown

Our 8-week axe throwing league runs every Wednesday night from 7pm to 10pm. Compete against other throwers, track your stats on our digital leaderboard, and fight for end-of-season prizes. Whether you're a regular or a rookie, league night is where the real competition lives.

  • 8-week season
  • Wednesday nights, 7pm - 10pm
  • $20 per week, per person
  • Digital stat tracking and leaderboard
  • Cash prizes for top 3 finishers
  • End-of-season championship tournament
  • Drink specials for league members

Tournaments

Monthly Open Tournaments

Think you're the best thrower in Portland? Prove it. Our monthly open tournaments are single-elimination, bracket-style competitions open to anyone. Show up, sign up, and throw your way to the top.

  • First Saturday of every month
  • $40 entry fee
  • Single-elimination bracket format
  • Cash prizes for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd
  • Live bracket display on big screens
  • Open to all skill levels
