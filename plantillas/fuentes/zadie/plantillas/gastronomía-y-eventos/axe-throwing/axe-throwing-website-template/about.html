The Story Behind the Throw

Two friends, one wild idea, and a whole lot of axes. Here's how Hatchet Hall came to be.

Our Story

It Started With a Throw

In the summer of 2019, best friends Danny Kowalski and Maria Chen stumbled into an axe throwing booth at a music festival in the Columbia River Gorge. Neither of them had ever thrown an axe before. By the end of the night, they were hooked -- completely, obsessively hooked.

Danny was managing a construction company. Maria was running events for a hotel chain. Both were good at their jobs. Neither was passionate about them. Within six months, they'd quit, pooled their savings, and signed a lease on a 6,000-square-foot warehouse in Portland's Pearl District.

They spent five months building it out themselves -- framing the lanes, installing the targets, sanding and staining every piece of reclaimed timber by hand. Hatchet Hall opened its doors in March 2020, closed two weeks later for the pandemic, and then came roaring back that summer with a loyal following that hasn't stopped growing.

Today, Hatchet Hall is Portland's top-rated axe throwing venue. But at its core, it's still the same thing it was on day one: two friends who want you to have the most fun you've had in a long time.

The Venue

6,000 Square Feet of Awesome

Hatchet Hall is built in a converted Pearl District warehouse with exposed brick, reclaimed timber beams, and industrial lighting. It's rugged, warm, and loud in all the right ways.

  • 12 regulation throwing lanes with digital scoreboards
  • Full-service lounge area with leather seating
  • Full bar with 20 rotating craft beer taps
  • Private event room for up to 30 guests
  • Tournament setup with bracket displays
  • Outdoor patio (seasonal)
Safety First

Seriously Fun, Seriously Safe

We take safety as seriously as we take fun. Every throw happens under the watch of a certified coach.

Certified Coaches

Every coach on staff is IATF-certified and trained in first aid. They're with you from your first throw to your last, making sure form and safety stay on point.

Safety Briefing Every Session

No one picks up an axe without a full safety briefing first. We cover grip, stance, throwing technique, and lane etiquette before every single session.

IATF Safety Standards

Our facility is built to International Axe Throwing Federation standards. Lane barriers, target distances, and axe specifications all meet or exceed IATF requirements.

Supervised Lanes

Lanes are never unsupervised. Our coaches monitor all active lanes and enforce a strict one-thrower-at-a-time policy. Your safety is always the top priority.
