Weekly Calendar

What's On This Week

Something going on nearly every night. No cover charge on any of it.

Every Monday

Pub Quiz Night

Seven rounds. Teams of up to six. Topics span current events, history, music, geography, and things you absolutely should know but somehow don't. Entry is free. Prize is a round of drinks.

Starts 8pm. Registration from 7pm.
Every Wednesday

Live Music Night

Local bands and solo acts performing original and classic material. Over 200 local musicians hosted since 2008. Check the board inside or our Instagram for this week's performer.

Doors from 7pm. Music from 9pm.
Fri and Sat, 4pm - 7pm

Happy Hour

All draft pints $1 off. House spirits half price. Our Copper Sour and Irish Mule at $10. Bar gets busy from 5:30pm. Come earlier for a seat.

4pm - 7pm, Friday and Saturday only.
Every Saturday

Saturday Sessions

Our weekly live music headliner night. Bigger acts, longer sets, and the best atmosphere of the week. These shows fill up. Come early or book a table.

Music from 9:30pm until late.
All Weekend

Game Day Specials

All major sports on four screens. NFL, NBA, NHL, Premier League, Champions League, Six Nations. Pitchers of Copper Lager from $18. Ask about team-specific specials during big matches.

Open from 12pm Saturday and Sunday.
Monthly

Open Mic Night

Last Thursday of every month. The stage is open to anyone. Original music only. Sign-up starts at 7pm. First slot at 8pm. Free entry.

Last Thursday of the month.
