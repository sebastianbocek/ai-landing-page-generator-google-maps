Common Questions

We Get Asked This a Lot

Do you take walk-ins?

Always. Bar stools are always first-come-first-served. We keep a section open for walk-in dining most nights too. Weekends get busy after 7pm, so reservations help.

Is there a dress code?

None whatsoever. Come as you are. We've had people show up in tuxedos and overalls on the same night. Both were welcome.

Do you show sports?

Yes — we have four screens and a projector in the back room. NFL, Premier League, NBA, MLB. If there's a big game on, we'll have it. Check with us for major event nights.

Is the pub dog-friendly?

Dogs are welcome in the bar area and the garden patio. Not in the dining room, for health regs. Water bowls behind the bar — just ask.

Do you have a happy hour?

Every Friday 4pm–7pm. Half-price draft pints, $2 off house cocktails, and $1 off all shots. No booking required.

Can I bring a cake?

Absolutely. Let us know when you book and we'll have plates and candles ready. We don't charge a corkage fee for celebration cakes. Just bring enough for the table.
