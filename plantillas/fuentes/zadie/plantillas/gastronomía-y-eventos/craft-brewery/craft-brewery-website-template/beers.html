YEAR-ROUND LINEUP

Our core beers, always available

COPPER IPA

WEST COAST IPA

Our flagship beer and the one that started it all. Bright citrus and pine hop character from Cascade and Centennial hops, balanced with a solid malt backbone. Crisp, bitter, and endlessly drinkable. 2024 Gold Medal Winner.

6.8% ABV
72 IBU

MIDNIGHT PORTER

ROBUST PORTER

Dark as the night sky with notes of chocolate, coffee, and toasted bread. Made with six different malts including chocolate malt and roasted barley. Smooth and rich without being heavy. 2024 Silver Medal Winner.

5.4% ABV
35 IBU

GOLDEN LAGER

GERMAN PILSNER

Crisp and clean with subtle noble hop character from German Hallertau hops. Lagered for 6 weeks for maximum smoothness. Perfect for sessionable drinking and food pairing.

4.8% ABV
28 IBU

HAZY SUNRISE

NEW ENGLAND IPA

Juicy and tropical with big mango, pineapple, and citrus flavors from Citra, Mosaic, and Galaxy hops. Soft, pillowy mouthfeel from flaked oats and wheat. Low bitterness, high drinkability.

7.2% ABV
45 IBU

AMBER ALE

AMERICAN AMBER

Balanced and approachable with caramel malt sweetness and moderate hop bitterness. Notes of toffee, biscuit, and subtle citrus. The beer that converts non-beer-drinkers into craft beer fans.

5.6% ABV
38 IBU

KETTLE SOUR

BERLINER WEISSE

Tart and refreshing with bright acidity from kettle souring. Light-bodied and crushable. Currently featuring raspberry and passionfruit, but the fruit changes seasonally.

4.2% ABV
8 IBU

SEASONAL & LIMITED RELEASES

Rotating beers that keep things interesting

BARREL AGED STOUT

IMPERIAL STOUT

12 months in bourbon barrels from a local distillery. Rich vanilla, oak, dark chocolate, and warming booze. Velvety smooth with layers of complexity. Released once per year in November. Extremely limited.

11.5% ABV
52 IBU

PUMPKIN SPICE ALE

SPICED ALE (FALL)

Real roasted pumpkin, cinnamon, nutmeg, and allspice. Not too sweet, not too spicy. Available September through November. Pairs perfectly with Thanksgiving dinner.

5.8% ABV
22 IBU

SUMMER WHEAT

AMERICAN WHEAT (SUMMER)

Light, crisp, and refreshing with orange peel and coriander. Perfect for hot days on the patio. Available May through August. Serve with a lemon wedge.

4.5% ABV
15 IBU

DOPPELBOCK

GERMAN LAGER (WINTER)

Rich, malty, and warming with notes of toffee, dark fruit, and bread crust. Lagered for 8 weeks. A traditional German strong lager perfect for winter. Available December through February.

8.2% ABV
25 IBU

TRIPLE IPA

IMPERIAL IPA (LIMITED)

Hop bomb with intense tropical fruit, resinous pine, and citrus. Dangerously smooth despite the high ABV. Released quarterly in small batches. Limit 2 per customer.

10.8% ABV
98 IBU

BLACKBERRY SOUR

FRUITED SOUR (SPRING)

Tart base beer with 2 lbs per gallon of Oregon blackberries. Bright pink color, intense fruit flavor, puckering acidity. Available March through May while supplies last.

5.2% ABV
6 IBU

BREWER'S RESERVE

Experimental small-batch beers (ask what's on tap today)

INNOVATION SERIES

Our brewers get creative with the 1-barrel pilot system. Past experiments include jalapeño pale ale, coffee milk stout, blood orange saison, and smoked maple porter.

Changes weekly — follow us on Instagram @copperkettlebrew for updates.

BARREL PROGRAM

Beyond the annual barrel aged stout, we age sours, barleywines, and old ales in wine, whiskey, and rum barrels. Bottle releases announced via our mailing list.

Cellar Club members get first access to all barrel aged releases.

COLLABORATIONS

We team up with other breweries, coffee roasters, distilleries, and local businesses for unique one-off beers.

Recent collabs: Stumptown Coffee, Hood River Distillery, Cascade Brewing.
