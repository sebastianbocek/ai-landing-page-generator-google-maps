FAQ

Are you family-friendly?

Yes! Families are welcome until 8PM. We have a kids menu, board games, and non-alcoholic beverages. After 8PM we're 21+ only.

Do you allow dogs?

Dogs are welcome on our covered patio year-round. We have water bowls and treats behind the bar. Please keep your pup leashed and well-behaved.

Can I bring outside food?

Our kitchen serves food Wed-Sun 4-10PM. You're welcome to bring outside food when the kitchen is closed (Mon-Tue, and before 4PM Wed-Sun).

Do you take reservations?

We don't take reservations for general seating - it's first-come, first-served. However, we do reserve the mezzanine for private events (contact us for details).

Where can I buy your beer to go?

Our taproom sells crowlers, growler fills, 4-packs, 6-packs, and cases. We're also distributed to bottle shops and grocery stores throughout the Portland metro area. Check our website for a list of retail locations.

Do you offer gluten-free options?

Yes! We always have at least one gluten-free cider on tap. Our kitchen also offers gluten-free pizza crust and several naturally gluten-free menu items.

Can I host a private event?

Absolutely! Our mezzanine level accommodates 30-50 people for birthday parties, corporate events, rehearsal dinners, and more. Email hello@copperkettlebrew.com for pricing and availability.

Do you distribute outside Portland?

Currently we distribute throughout the Portland metro area and parts of the Willamette Valley. We're expanding! If you're a retailer or bar interested in carrying our beer, email wholesale@copperkettlebrew.com.

How do I join the Cellar Club?

Stop by the taproom and ask your bartender for a membership application. We're currently limited to 200 members total. Benefits include first access to limited releases, 15% off beer purchases, and exclusive release parties.

Are you hiring?

We're always looking for passionate people to join our team! Check our Instagram and website for current openings. Email your resume to jobs@copperkettlebrew.com.
