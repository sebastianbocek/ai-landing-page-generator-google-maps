THE TAPROOM

Great Beer • Good Food • Better Company

Industrial-chic taproom with 16 rotating taps and a full kitchen

The Space

Where community and craft beer collide

Industrial Charm

Exposed brick, copper accents, reclaimed wood tables, and our gleaming brewing equipment visible behind glass. Instagram-worthy corner booth with kettle views.

Outdoor Patio

Year-round covered patio with string lights, fire pits, and heaters. Dog-friendly! Bring your pup and we'll bring the water bowl and treats.

Games & Entertainment

100+ board games, shuffleboard table, darts, cornhole on the patio. Projector screen for Blazers games and big sporting events.

Private Events

Mezzanine level available for private parties (30-50 people). Birthday parties, corporate events, rehearsal dinners. Contact us for details.

Events Calendar

Always something happening at Copper Kettle

Every Tuesday • 7:00 PM
Trivia Night

Test your knowledge with our weekly trivia competition. Prizes for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place teams. Free to play. Teams of 2-6 people. Hosted by Portland Trivia League.

Every Thursday • 6:00 PM
Vinyl Night

Bring your favorite vinyl record and our DJ will spin it. Rotating guest DJs from the local music scene. Chill vibes, great music, better beer.

Every Friday • 8:00 PM
Live Music

Local bands and touring acts. Genres range from indie rock to folk to funk. No cover charge. Check our Instagram for this week's lineup.

Every Saturday • 8:00 PM
Live Music

Weekend headline shows featuring Portland's best local talent. Occasionally touring bands. No cover, but tips for the musicians are always appreciated.

Every Sunday • 3:00 PM
Acoustic Sessions

Laid-back Sunday afternoon vibes with acoustic performances. Singer-songwriters, acoustic duos, mellow tunes. Perfect soundtrack for Sunday Funday.

First Saturday • 2:00 PM
Brewer's Meet & Greet

Meet our brewers, ask questions about our beers, learn about the brewing process. Free beer samples of new experimental batches. Feedback welcome!

Last Sunday • 12:00 PM
Cornhole Tournament

Monthly cornhole competition on the patio. $20 per team (2 people). Winner takes home a $100 gift card and bragging rights. Register at the bar.
