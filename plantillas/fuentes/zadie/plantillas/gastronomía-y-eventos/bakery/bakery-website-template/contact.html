Ordering Info

How to Order

Whether you're picking up a baguette or ordering a wedding cake, here's what to know.

Walk-In Purchases

Stop by anytime during bakery hours. First come, first served on all daily items. Popular items sell out early, especially on weekends.

Same-Day Reservations

Want to guarantee your favorites? Call before 10 AM and we'll set aside items for pickup that day. Payment at pickup.

Special Orders

Custom cakes and large orders require 48-72 hours notice. Wedding cakes require consultation at least 4-6 weeks in advance.

Wholesale Delivery

Free delivery within 15 miles for wholesale accounts meeting minimum order requirements. Morning delivery, 6 days a week.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to order a custom cake? +
For simple custom cakes (our existing flavors, basic decoration), we need at least 48-72 hours notice. For wedding cakes or elaborate custom designs, please contact us at least 4-6 weeks in advance. During busy seasons (May-June, November-December), we recommend booking even earlier.
Do you offer wedding cake tastings? +
Yes! We offer complimentary tastings for couples who book their wedding cake with us. Tastings are held on Tuesday afternoons by appointment only. You'll sample up to four cake and filling combinations. Contact us to schedule.
What are the minimums for wholesale accounts? +
Our minimum order is 10 loaves (or equivalent value in other products) per delivery. We deliver free within 15 miles for accounts meeting this minimum. Orders are placed weekly, with orders due by 4 PM two days before delivery. Contact us to set up an account and discuss your needs.
Can I reserve items for same-day pickup? +
Yes! Call us before 10 AM and we'll set aside your items. We hold reservations until 2 PM—after that, items may be returned to the case. This is great for popular items like croissants and sourdough that sell out early on weekends.
Do you deliver to homes or offices? +
We don't offer regular retail delivery, but we do deliver catering orders over $150 within 10 miles for a $20 fee. For large special orders (like wedding cakes), we can arrange delivery—just ask when you order.
Do you accommodate dietary restrictions? +
Some items are naturally vegan (sourdough, baguettes) or can be made without nuts. We offer gluten-free brownies. However, everything is made in a shared kitchen with wheat, dairy, eggs, and nuts—we cannot guarantee any item is completely allergen-free. Please discuss specific needs with our staff.
What's the best time to come for fresh bread? +
Our pastries come out around 6 AM when we open. Artisan breads are ready by 7:30 AM. If you want something warm from the oven, those are the times to be here. By mid-morning, selection is at its peak. By afternoon, popular items may be sold out, but we often bake cookies around 2 PM.
Can I tour the bakery or take a baking class? +
We host a free bread-making class on the first Sunday of every month—check our social media for sign-ups. Private bakery tours aren't available due to space constraints and food safety requirements, but you can see our bakers at work through the window from the front counter!
