Important

Allergen Information

Gluten-Free Options
We offer gluten-free brownies and occasional gluten-free items. However, all items are prepared in a shared kitchen with wheat flour. We cannot guarantee any item is completely free of cross-contamination.
Nut Allergies
Many of our products contain or are made alongside tree nuts (almonds, pecans, walnuts). Our kitchen is not nut-free. Please alert staff to any allergies when ordering.
Dairy & Eggs
Most of our pastries, cakes, and enriched breads contain dairy and eggs. Our plain sourdough, baguettes, and several other breads are vegan. Ask our staff for details.
Complete Ingredient Lists
Complete ingredient lists for all products are available upon request. Please ask at the counter or email us in advance of your visit if you have specific dietary concerns.
