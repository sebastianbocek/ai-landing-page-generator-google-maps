About The Corner Deli

Three generations of Rossis, one counter, and a whole lot of sandwiches. This is how a neighborhood institution gets built.

Since 1987

A Family Legacy on Every Plate

It started with a handshake and a small loan. In 1987, Giuseppe "Joe" Rossi and his wife Rosa opened a tiny storefront on Oak Street with a meat slicer, a bread oven, and recipes from the old country. The plan was simple: make the best sandwiches in Brooklyn, treat every customer like family, and never cut corners.

Word got around fast. Construction crews came for the meatball heroes. Office workers ordered the turkey clubs. The after-school crowd lined up for Italian combos. Within a year, the line stretched out the door at lunchtime, and it has not stopped since.

Today, their son Tony runs the show with the same philosophy. Grandma Rosa's potato salad recipe is still on a flour-dusted index card taped to the kitchen wall. The bread is still baked before dawn. And if you sit at the counter long enough, Tony will tell you about the time his father arm-wrestled a delivery driver over the price of provolone.

Nearly four decades later, The Corner Deli is not just a restaurant. It is where neighbors catch up, where Little League teams celebrate, and where the first thing people do when they move to the neighborhood is grab a Reuben and introduce themselves.

The Family

Meet the Rossis

Three family members, one mission: feed the neighborhood right.

Tony Rossi

Owner & Head Chef

Tony grew up behind the counter and took over from his parents in 2005. He still makes every batch of corned beef by hand and refuses to serve anything he would not eat himself. His Philly cheesesteak recipe took two years to perfect.

Maria Rossi

Head Baker

Maria married into the family and brought her own gift: baking. She arrives at 4 AM every morning to start the bread. Her seeded rolls are legendary, and her rye bread has won three local bake-off trophies. She says the secret is patience and good flour.

Eddie Rossi

Front Counter

Eddie is the youngest Rossi and the first face you see when you walk in. He knows most regulars by name and their orders by heart. If you are new to the neighborhood, Eddie will make you feel like you have been coming here for years.

What Goes In

Our Ingredients

Quality you can taste. We are picky about what goes on our counter so you never have to be.

Boar's Head Meats

Premium Boar's Head deli meats sliced fresh to order. No pre-packaged, no pre-sliced. Every sandwich starts at the slicer.

Fresh-Baked Bread

Baked in-house every morning. Seeded heroes, marble rye, ciabatta, and hoagie rolls. Still warm when the doors open at 6 AM.

Local Produce

Tomatoes, lettuce, peppers, and onions sourced from local farms and the Hunts Point market. Delivered fresh every morning.

Homemade Condiments

Our Russian dressing, pesto, herb mayo, and hot sauce are all made from scratch. Grandma Rosa would not have it any other way.

Full Service

The Deli Counter

Our full-service deli counter is the heart of the shop. Step up, grab a number, and watch us slice your order fresh. We carry over 30 varieties of Boar's Head meats and cheeses, all sliced to your exact thickness.

Beyond the slicer, our prepared foods case is stocked daily with homemade salads -- potato salad, coleslaw, macaroni salad, pasta salad, and rotating seasonal options. Perfect for a quick lunch or dinner side.

In a rush? Our grab-and-go section has pre-made sandwiches, wraps, salads, and snacks ready to walk out the door. Same quality, same recipes, just faster.

Giving Back

Part of the Neighborhood

We do not just work here. We live here. And we believe in showing up for our community the same way it has shown up for us.

Little League Sponsor

We have sponsored the Oak Street Little League team for over 15 years. Every season starts with a team lunch at the deli, and every win gets celebrated with free sodas. The kids call us "Team Sandwich."

School Lunch Partner

We partner with two local schools to provide affordable, nutritious lunch options for students. Real food, not processed. Every kid deserves a good sandwich, and we are proud to help make that happen.

Neighborhood Fixture

Block parties, holiday events, fundraiser platters -- if something is happening in the neighborhood, there is a good chance The Corner Deli is involved. This community built us, and we will never forget it.
