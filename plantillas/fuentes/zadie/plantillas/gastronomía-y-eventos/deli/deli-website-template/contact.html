Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? Chances are we have answered it before. If not, give us a call.

Do you deliver?

We offer delivery within a 2-mile radius through DoorDash and Uber Eats. For large catering orders, we handle delivery ourselves -- just ask when you place the order. There is no delivery fee for catering orders over $200.

Can I order ahead?

Absolutely. Call us at (718) 555-0147 and we will have your order ready when you arrive. For the lunch rush (11 AM - 1 PM), we recommend calling at least 30 minutes ahead so you do not have to wait.

Do you have gluten-free options?

We offer gluten-free wraps as a bread substitute for any sandwich. Our prepared salads (potato salad, coleslaw) are naturally gluten-free. Just let us know your dietary needs and we will work with you.

What is the minimum for catering?

Our smallest catering platters serve 10 people, starting at $29.95 for sides and $79.95 for sandwich trays. There is no order minimum beyond the platter sizes. We ask for 24-hour advance notice for all catering orders.

Do you slice meats and cheeses to order?

Yes, that is what the deli counter is for. We carry over 30 varieties of Boar's Head meats and cheeses, all sliced fresh to your preferred thickness. No pre-packaged products. Grab a number and we will take care of you.

Is there parking?

There is metered street parking available on Oak Street and the surrounding blocks. The municipal lot on Elm Avenue (one block south) offers 2-hour free parking. Most of our regulars walk or bike over.
