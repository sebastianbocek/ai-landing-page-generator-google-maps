Why Corner Deli

What Sets Us Apart

Three generations of doing things the right way. No shortcuts, no compromises.

House-Cured Meats

Our corned beef, pastrami, and roast beef are cured and cooked in-house the way Grandpa Rossi taught us. You can taste the difference in every bite. No factory-processed meats behind our counter.

Baked Fresh Daily

Maria starts baking at 4 AM so every roll, hero, and loaf is fresh by the time we open. Our rye bread alone has won three neighborhood bake-off trophies. We go through 200 rolls on a busy Saturday.

Family Recipes Since 1987

From Grandma Rossi's potato salad to Tony's secret hot sauce, every recipe has a story. We have been feeding this neighborhood for nearly four decades, and we are just getting started.
