Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

For everyday arrangements, we can accommodate same-day orders if placed by noon. For weddings and large events, we recommend booking 6-12 months in advance to ensure date availability. Holiday orders (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day) should be placed at least one week ahead.

What are your delivery fees?

Delivery is free on orders over $75 within 5 miles of our studio. For orders under $75 or deliveries 5-15 miles away, there's a $10 delivery fee. Deliveries 15-25 miles have a $20 fee. Same-day delivery has an additional $15 rush fee.

Can I customize my arrangement?

Absolutely! We love creating custom arrangements. When ordering, simply note your color preferences, favorite flowers, style preferences, or any flowers to avoid. For more complex custom requests, feel free to call us or visit the studio to discuss your vision.

What if you can't source a specific flower?

If we're unable to source a requested flower due to seasonal availability or supply issues, we'll substitute with a similar bloom in the same color family and value. We always maintain the overall style, size, and value of the arrangement. If you'd prefer to approve substitutions first, please note that in your order.

Do you have allergy-friendly options?

Yes! We can create arrangements avoiding high-pollen flowers like lilies for those with allergies or sensitivities. We also offer low-pollen options featuring roses, orchids, and certain other blooms. Just mention any allergies or sensitivities when ordering.

What's the etiquette for sympathy flowers?

For funeral services, flowers are typically sent to the funeral home with the service date noted. For home deliveries to the family, it's thoughtful to send within the first few weeks. We recommend elegant, peaceful arrangements in soft colors. We're experienced in sympathy florals and can guide you through the process with sensitivity.

Can I pause my flower subscription?

Yes, subscriptions are completely flexible. You can pause deliveries when you're traveling, skip a week, or cancel anytime with no penalty. Just give us 3 days notice before your next scheduled delivery.

Do you offer corporate accounts?

Yes! We work with many businesses for regular office arrangements, corporate gifts, and event florals. Corporate accounts receive volume discounts, flexible billing, and priority service. Contact us at hello@bloomandvine.com to set up a corporate account.

How long will my flowers last?

With proper care, most arrangements last 5-10 days. Some hardy blooms like alstroemeria and chrysanthemums can last up to two weeks. We include care instructions with every delivery. Key tips: change water every 2-3 days, keep away from direct sunlight and heat, and re-trim stems every few days.
