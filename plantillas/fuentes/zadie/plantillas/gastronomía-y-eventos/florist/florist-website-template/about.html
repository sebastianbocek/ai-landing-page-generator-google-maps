Our Story

Passionate about flowers, committed to artistry, devoted to making every moment beautiful

Where Artistry Meets Nature

Bloom & Vine was born from a simple belief: flowers have the power to transform ordinary moments into extraordinary memories. What started as a passion project in Elena Martinez's garage studio in 2018 has blossomed into Portland's beloved neighborhood floral design studio.

Elena, a former art teacher with a lifelong love of gardening, began creating bouquets for friends and family as a creative outlet. Word spread quickly about her romantic, garden-inspired designs that looked like they'd been plucked from a secret English countryside estate. Soon, those friends were asking her to design for their weddings, their dinner parties, their "just because I need beauty today" moments.

Today, Bloom & Vine is a full-service floral studio committed to creating arrangements that feel both timeless and fresh, romantic and natural. We believe in the beauty of imperfection, the magic of seasonal blooms, and the joy that comes from giving and receiving flowers.

Our Philosophy

How We Work

Seasonal First

We design with the seasons, celebrating what's naturally blooming and at its peak. This means fresher flowers, more sustainable choices, and arrangements that feel connected to the moment.

Locally Sourced

Whenever possible, we source from local flower farms within 50 miles of Portland. This supports our community, reduces our carbon footprint, and ensures the freshest possible blooms.

Sustainably Minded

We use eco-friendly floral foam alternatives, recyclable packaging, and composting practices. Our goal is to create beauty that doesn't come at the earth's expense.

Artisan Crafted

Every single arrangement is handcrafted by our skilled designers. We never use pre-made templates or assembly-line methods. Your flowers are truly one-of-a-kind.

Garden Style

Our signature aesthetic is romantic, organic, and garden-inspired. We embrace natural movement, asymmetry, and the wild beauty of flowers as they grow in nature.

Client Collaboration

We listen carefully to your vision, style, and story. The best arrangements come from genuine collaboration and understanding what makes your moment special.

Our Studio Space

Visit us at our bright, welcoming design studio in Portland's Pearl District

Our studio is designed to inspire creativity and connection. Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the space with natural light, highlighting the natural beauty of our flowers. Large farmhouse tables serve as our design stations, where we craft each arrangement with care.

Our Freshness Commitment

We source our flowers from trusted suppliers and local farms, hand-selecting each stem for quality and freshness. All flowers are delivered to us within 48 hours of being cut. We process and hydrate every stem properly, ensuring they arrive at your door in peak condition.

If you're ever unsatisfied with the freshness or quality of your flowers, we'll make it right. Beautiful, long-lasting flowers aren't just our goal—they're our guarantee.

Giving Back

Community Roots

Bloom & Vine is proud to be part of the Portland community. We believe in using our skills and resources to give back and spread beauty beyond commercial transactions.

Petal It Forward

Every month, we donate bouquets to local hospitals, nursing homes, and women's shelters. Small gestures of beauty can make a big difference.

Workshops & Classes

We offer monthly floral design workshops, with proceeds supporting local environmental conservation efforts and urban gardening programs.

Local Partnerships

We partner with Oregon flower farms, supporting local agriculture and reducing the environmental impact of imported flowers.

Wedding Grants

Each year, we provide complimentary wedding florals to one deserving couple through our "Love in Bloom" grant program.
