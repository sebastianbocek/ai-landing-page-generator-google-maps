Good to Know

Reservation Policies

Booking Window

Reservations open 30 days in advance. For peak times (Friday/Saturday 7-8pm), we recommend booking at least 1 week ahead.

Cancellations

Please cancel or modify at least 24 hours before your reservation. Late cancellations or no-shows may result in a $25 per person fee.

Arrival Time

We hold tables for 15 minutes past the reservation time. If you're running late, please call so we can accommodate you.

Large Parties

Groups of 6+ require a credit card to hold the reservation. A 20% gratuity is automatically added for parties of 6 or more.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes! We always save a few tables for walk-in guests. Bar seating is first-come, first-served and a great option for smaller parties.

Is there a dress code?

We're smart casual. Jeans are welcome, but please no athletic wear, flip-flops, or beachwear. When in doubt, dress a little nicer. This is a place for memorable occasions.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Please note any allergies or dietary needs when booking. We offer vegetarian options and can prepare gluten-free pasta for any pasta dish.

Is there parking available?

Street parking is available. We also have a partnership with the Main Street Garage (one block east) offering validated parking for $5 for up to 3 hours.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes! Gift cards are available in any denomination. Purchase in-restaurant or call us to order by phone. We'll mail it directly to your recipient.

Can you accommodate children?

Children are welcome. We don't have a kids' menu, but we're happy to prepare smaller portions of pasta dishes or simple preparations. High chairs available.
