Good to Know

Dietary Information

Vegetarian Options

Many dishes can be prepared vegetarian. Our pasta and risotto selections offer excellent meat-free options.

Gluten-Free

Gluten-free pasta available for any pasta dish. Please inform your server of any allergies or dietary restrictions.

Ask Your Server

Our team is happy to accommodate special requests. Please let us know how we can make your meal perfect.

*Consuming raw or undercooked meats, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness. Prices subject to change. Gratuity of 20% added for parties of 6 or more.
