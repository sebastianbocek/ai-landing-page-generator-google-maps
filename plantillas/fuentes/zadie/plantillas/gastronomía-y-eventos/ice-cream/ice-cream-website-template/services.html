Ice Cream Cakes
Custom orders only • 1 week minimum notice • Call or email to order

All cakes are layered with your choice of two ice cream flavors and a fudge or caramel middle layer, on a brownie or waffle cookie crust. Decorated with whipped cream frosting. Custom messages written in chocolate or strawberry sauce. We do not do fondant or elaborate exterior decorations — we're an ice cream shop, not a cake decorator. What we do, we do beautifully.

Call us at (555) 847-2293 or email hello@scoopsandswirls.com with your event date, size needed, and flavor preferences. We require a 50% deposit at the time of ordering. Cakes must be picked up at the shop — we do not deliver. Full payment due at pickup.
