Why We're Different

Made Fresh Daily

We don't cut corners. Every batch starts with real ingredients and a whole lot of care from Patty and Jim's original recipes.

Small-Batch Craft

We make ice cream in small batches every morning before we open. That means you're always getting the freshest scoop possible — not something that's been sitting in a freezer truck for weeks.

Local Georgia Dairy

Our milk and cream come from two Georgia farms within 80 miles of Savannah. We've worked with the same families for over a decade. Fresh, clean dairy is the backbone of great ice cream — and ours shows it.

No Artificial Colors

Every color you see in our ice cream comes from real ingredients. Strawberry gets its blush from berries. Mint gets its green from real peppermint. Nothing artificial, ever. Your kids are eating the real thing.
