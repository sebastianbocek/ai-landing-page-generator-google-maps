Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to the things people ask us most.

What does "cold-pressed" actually mean?

Cold-pressed juice is made using a hydraulic press that extracts juice without generating heat. This preserves more vitamins, minerals, and enzymes compared to centrifugal juicers that create heat and oxidation during the process.

Are all your ingredients really organic?

Yes. Every single ingredient we use is certified organic. We work with 25+ local farms and suppliers, and we verify certifications regularly. We're transparent about our sourcing because we believe you deserve to know what you're drinking.

How long do your juices stay fresh?

Our cold-pressed juices are best consumed within 72 hours and should be kept refrigerated. Since we don't pasteurize or add preservatives, freshness is everything. For the best nutrition, drink them within 24 hours.

Can I customize my order?

Absolutely. Smoothie bowls and protein smoothies can be customized with different bases, proteins, and toppings. For cold-pressed juices, we stick to our tested recipes to ensure the best flavor and nutrition balance.

Do you accommodate allergies?

We take allergies seriously. Our menu clearly labels common allergens, and our staff is trained to handle allergy requests. Let us know about your allergies when ordering and we'll make sure your drink is safe.

What should I expect during a cleanse?

Most people feel great by day two. The first day you might experience mild hunger or fatigue as your body adjusts. Our dietitian provides a detailed prep guide and checks in with you daily to make sure you're feeling good throughout the process.
