The Difference

Why People Choose Us

We're not just another juice bar. Here's what makes Pressed & Pure different.

Organic Ingredients

Every fruit, vegetable, and supplement we use is certified organic. We work directly with 25+ local farms to source the freshest produce available.

Cold-Pressed Process

Our hydraulic press extracts juice without heat or oxidation, preserving up to 5x more vitamins and enzymes than traditional juicing methods.

Made to Order

Nothing sits in a fridge for days. Every juice, smoothie, and bowl is prepared fresh when you order it. Maximum freshness, maximum nutrition.

Eco-Friendly Packaging

All our cups, bottles, and bowls are made from plant-based, compostable materials. We've eliminated single-use plastics from our entire operation.
