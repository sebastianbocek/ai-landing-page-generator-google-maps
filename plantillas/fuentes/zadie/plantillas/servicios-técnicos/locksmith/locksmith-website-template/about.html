The Story

From One Van to a Team of Experts

KeyMaster started in 2009 when Marcus Webb, a former locksmith apprentice who'd spent eight years learning the trade, decided to build the kind of service he'd always wanted to call. Not a franchise. Not a call center. A local company where a real, licensed technician answers the phone and shows up.

The first few years were just Marcus and a van. He covered the whole metro area himself, sleeping with his phone on in case a late-night call came in. Word spread. Repeat customers turned into referrals, referrals turned into commercial contracts, and the business grew.

Today, KeyMaster employs four licensed technicians covering the full service area. The van count has grown, but the philosophy hasn't changed: show up fast, quote honestly, do the job right the first time.

Meet the Owner

Marcus Webb

Master Locksmith & Founder

Marcus spent eight years as an apprentice and journeyman locksmith before founding KeyMaster in 2009. He holds a Master Locksmith certification from the Associated Locksmiths of America (ALOA) and maintains state licensing in both residential and commercial categories.

He's the kind of guy who reads lock mechanism patents for fun and still takes on hands-on jobs most days. When he's not in the field, he's training the technicians and running quality checks on completed work.

"I built this company because I knew what it felt like to call a locksmith at 2am and get a call center that gave you a vague time window and a bait-and-switch price. I wanted to offer the opposite of that."
