The Process

How It Works

From call to fix in three straightforward steps. No guesswork, no surprises.

01

Book

Call us or book online. Tell us what's wrong and we'll schedule a same-day or next-day appointment that fits your schedule. We arrive in a 2-hour window — no all-day waits.

02

Diagnose

Your technician inspects the appliance, identifies the problem, and gives you a clear, upfront quote — before touching anything. You approve the price, then we proceed.

03

Fix

We complete the repair using genuine OEM parts wherever possible. You test the appliance before we leave. Every repair is covered by our 90-day parts and labor warranty.
