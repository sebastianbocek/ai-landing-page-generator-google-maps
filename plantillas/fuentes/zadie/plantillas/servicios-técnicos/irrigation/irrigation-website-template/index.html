Our Process

How It Works

Getting a new irrigation system is straightforward. We handle the complexity so you just enjoy a green, healthy landscape.

1

Free Consultation

We visit your property to assess the landscape, soil, water pressure, and sun exposure. You tell us your goals, and we put together a custom plan.

2

Design & Install

Our design team creates a zone-by-zone layout with the right heads, nozzles, and drip lines for each area. Installation typically takes 1-3 days.

3

Ongoing Maintenance

We program your controller for the season, walk you through the system, and offer maintenance plans to keep everything running at peak efficiency year-round.
