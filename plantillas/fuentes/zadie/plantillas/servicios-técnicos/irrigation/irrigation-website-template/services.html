Drip Systems

Drip Irrigation Systems

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone of each plant, reducing evaporation and runoff by up to 50% compared to traditional spray heads. Ideal for desert landscaping, flower beds, vegetable gardens, and any area with trees, shrubs, or ground cover.

  • Inline emitter tubing
  • Point-source emitters
  • Micro-spray heads
  • Pressure regulation
  • Filtration systems
  • Zone integration
$1,500 - $4,000
Seasonal Care

Seasonal System Maintenance

Arizona's extreme heat puts unique demands on irrigation systems. Our seasonal tune-ups ensure your system is ready for the scorching summer months and stays efficient through the cooler winter watering schedule. We inspect every zone, adjust run times, check for leaks, and clean filters and nozzles.

  • Full system inspection
  • Head and nozzle cleaning
  • Controller reprogramming
  • Valve and wiring check
  • Pressure testing
  • Filter and screen service
Spring Startup: $125 | Winterization: $150
