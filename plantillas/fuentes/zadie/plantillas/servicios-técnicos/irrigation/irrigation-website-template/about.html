About AquaGreen

Keeping the Valley Green Since 2009

We started with a truck, a trencher, and a commitment to water conservation. Today we are Scottsdale's trusted irrigation team with over 3,000 systems to our name.

Our Story

Built on Water Conservation

AquaGreen Irrigation was founded in 2009 by Jason Rivera, a third-generation Arizonan who grew up watching water get wasted across the Valley. After earning his irrigation certification and spending years working for one of the region's largest landscape companies, Jason knew he could do things differently.

He started AquaGreen with a simple idea: every irrigation system should be designed to deliver exactly the right amount of water, exactly where it's needed, and not a drop more. That philosophy attracted homeowners and property managers who were tired of sky-high water bills and brown spots in their lawns.

Today, our team of certified technicians installs and maintains irrigation systems across the greater Phoenix metro area. We have grown from a one-man operation to a full-service irrigation company, but our focus on efficiency and craftsmanship has never changed.

Our Equipment

Technology & Equipment

We invest in the best tools and products so your system is built to last and perform in the Arizona climate.

Smart Controllers

We install and program Rachio, Rain Bird ESP-TM2, and Hunter Pro-HC controllers with weather-based adjustment and WiFi connectivity for remote monitoring.

CAD System Design

Every installation starts with a detailed CAD layout that maps head placement, pipe routing, valve locations, and zone boundaries for precision coverage.

Professional Trenching

Ditch Witch trenchers and vibratory plows for clean, low-impact installation that minimizes disruption to existing landscape, hardscape, and root systems.

Leak Detection

Electronic listening equipment and pressure testing tools that pinpoint underground leaks without unnecessary excavation, saving time and reducing repair costs.

Precision Nozzles

Rain Bird and Hunter rotary nozzles with matched precipitation rates deliver uniform coverage across every zone, eliminating dry spots and runoff.

Flow Sensors

Inline flow sensors detect broken pipes and stuck valves in real time, automatically shutting down zones to prevent water waste and property damage.
