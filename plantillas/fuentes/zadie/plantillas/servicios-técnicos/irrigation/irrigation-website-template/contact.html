FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from homeowners and property managers about irrigation systems and our services.

How much water will I actually save with a new system?

Most of our clients see a 20-30% reduction in water usage after upgrading to a properly designed system with matched precipitation rates and smart controller scheduling. The exact savings depend on your current system's condition, your landscape, and your watering habits. We can provide a projected savings estimate during your free consultation.

How long does a sprinkler installation take?

A typical residential installation takes 1-3 days depending on the size of the property and complexity of the design. Smaller front-yard-only systems can be completed in a single day. Larger commercial projects usually run 3-7 days. We will give you an accurate timeline during the estimate process.

What is a smart controller and is it worth the upgrade?

A smart controller is a WiFi-connected irrigation timer that automatically adjusts watering schedules based on local weather, soil type, sun exposure, and plant type. Most clients recoup the cost of the upgrade within 6-12 months through water savings alone. You can also monitor and control your system from your phone anywhere in the world.

How often should my irrigation system be serviced?

We recommend a minimum of two maintenance visits per year: one in early spring to prepare for summer and one in late fall to adjust for cooler months. Properties with larger systems or high water usage benefit from quarterly visits. Our maintenance plans cover all of this at a discounted rate.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater Phoenix metro area, including Scottsdale, Phoenix, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Paradise Valley, and Fountain Hills. We occasionally take on projects in other parts of Maricopa County. If you are unsure whether you are in our service area, give us a call and we will let you know.

What warranties do you offer?

All new installations come with a 2-year workmanship warranty covering labor and materials. The equipment we install (Rain Bird, Hunter, Rachio) carries manufacturer warranties ranging from 2-5 years depending on the product. Repairs carry a 90-day warranty on both parts and labor. Maintenance plan members receive extended warranty coverage.

Prefer to Talk to Someone Directly?

Our team is available Monday through Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday mornings. Call us for a quick answer or to schedule your free on-site estimate.
