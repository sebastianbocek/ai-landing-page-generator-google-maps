Our Services

From routine pumping to complete system installations, we handle every aspect of septic system care. Transparent pricing on every service.

Septic Pumping

Regular septic pumping is the single most important thing you can do to protect your system. We recommend pumping every 3-5 years for most households, more frequently for larger families or heavy water usage.

Residential: $350 - $500
Commercial: $500 - $1,200

Signs you need pumping:

  • Slow drains throughout the house
  • Sewage odor in yard or near tank
  • Pooling water over the drain field
  • Lush green grass over the tank area
  • Gurgling sounds in plumbing
  • More than 3-5 years since last pump

Price includes pumping, basic inspection of tank condition, and proper waste disposal at a licensed facility. No hidden fees.

Septic Repair

When something goes wrong with your system, fast and accurate repair prevents small issues from becoming major failures. We diagnose the problem first, explain your options, and repair it right.

Common repairs we handle:

  • Tank crack and leak repair
  • Baffle replacement
  • Distribution box repair or replacement
  • Effluent filter installation
  • Riser and lid replacement
  • Pump replacement (for pump systems)

Repair pricing varies by scope. We always provide a written estimate before starting work. No surprises.

Drain Field Services

The drain field is the most expensive component of your septic system. We offer services to extend its life, restore failing fields, and replace fields that have reached the end of their lifespan.

Drain field services include:

  • Drain field rejuvenation (terralift/aeration)
  • Full drain field replacement
  • Aeration system installation
  • Root intrusion remediation
  • Drain field loading tests
  • Soil percolation testing

Grease Trap Service

We provide commercial grease trap pumping and cleaning for restaurants, cafeterias, and food service operations. Regular grease trap maintenance keeps you compliant with health department regulations and prevents costly backups.

Commercial grease trap services:

  • Grease trap pumping and cleaning
  • Grease interceptor maintenance
  • Scheduled service contracts
  • Manifests and compliance documentation
  • Emergency grease trap service
  • All sizes from 20 to 2,000 gallons
