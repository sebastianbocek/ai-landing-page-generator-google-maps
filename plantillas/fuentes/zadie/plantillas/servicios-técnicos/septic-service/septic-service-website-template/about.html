Our Story

Started With One Truck and a Handshake

ClearFlow Septic Solutions was founded in 2003 by Tom Garrett, a third-generation tradesman who saw a need for honest, reliable septic service in the tri-county area. He started with a single vacuum truck, a state installer's license, and a commitment to doing the job right.

Over two decades later, ClearFlow has grown to a fleet of six trucks and a team of certified technicians. We've pumped over 5,000 tanks, installed hundreds of new systems, and built a reputation as the most trusted name in septic services in our area.

We're still family-owned, still locally operated, and still answer the phone when you call. Whether it's a scheduled pumping or a midnight emergency, ClearFlow shows up.

  • State licensed septic installer
  • EPA compliant waste handling and disposal
  • NAWT (National Association of Wastewater Technicians) certified
  • Fully insured with $2M liability coverage
