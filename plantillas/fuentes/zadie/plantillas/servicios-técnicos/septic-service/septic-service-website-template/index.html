Homeowner Education

How Your Septic System Works

Understanding your septic system helps you maintain it properly and avoid costly repairs down the line.

1

Wastewater Enters the Tank

All water from your home flows into the septic tank, where solids settle to the bottom and oils float to the top. The tank acts as a natural separator.

2

Effluent Flows to Drain Field

Partially treated water exits the tank into the drain field, where it percolates through soil. The soil provides natural filtration, removing harmful bacteria.

3

Regular Maintenance Keeps It Working

The solids that accumulate in your tank need to be pumped out every 3-5 years. Skipping maintenance leads to backups, drain field failure, and expensive repairs.
