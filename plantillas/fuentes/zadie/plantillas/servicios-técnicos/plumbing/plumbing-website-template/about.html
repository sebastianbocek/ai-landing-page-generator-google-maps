Our Story

Built on Trust

Clearwater Plumbing was founded in 1987 by Harold Clearwater, a third-generation plumber who believed that honest work and fair prices were the foundation of any successful business. What started as a one-truck operation has grown into the area's most trusted plumbing company.

Today, Harold's sons David and Michael continue that legacy, leading a team of 25 licensed technicians who share the same commitment to excellence that built this company. We're not just fixing pipes — we're building relationships that last for generations.

  • Family owned and operated since 1987
  • All technicians are licensed and background-checked
  • Upfront pricing with no hidden fees
  • 100% satisfaction guarantee on all work
