FAQ

Common Questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. We provide free estimates for all plumbing work. There's no trip charge or obligation.

Are your plumbers licensed?

Yes. All our technicians are fully licensed, background-checked, and insured. We're also bonded for your protection.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes. All our work is backed by a satisfaction guarantee. Parts and labor warranties vary by service — ask your technician for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards. Financing is available for larger projects.

How quickly can you come out?

For emergencies, we typically arrive within 1–2 hours. For non-urgent service, we can often schedule same-day or next-day appointments.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes. We're available Saturdays from 8am–4pm for scheduled appointments, and our emergency line is available 24/7.
