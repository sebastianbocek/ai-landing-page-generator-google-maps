Why Summit

Craft That Shows Up in Every Square Foot

We're a small crew that takes on the right number of jobs at a time. That means you get Nate on every project — not a sub-crew you've never met.

CFI Certified Installation

Nate holds active CFI certification — the flooring industry's highest credential for installers. We follow manufacturer specs on every job.

Precision Subfloor Prep

A floor is only as good as what's underneath it. We never skip subfloor inspection and repair. It's where most installs fail, and where we invest the most time.

Full Warranty on Labor

Every installation we do carries a 2-year labor warranty. If something shifts, separates, or squeaks because of how it was installed, we fix it. No arguments.

Transparent Pricing

You get a line-item quote before any work begins. No surprise charges at the end. If we find subfloor issues during demo, we call you before we touch anything extra.

How It Works

From First Call to Final Reveal

1

Free Consultation

We visit your space, measure the area, inspect the subfloor, and talk through your goals, timeline, and budget. No pressure, no sales pitch.

2

Detailed Quote

You receive a line-item written estimate within 48 hours. Materials, labor, and any subfloor work are broken out separately so you know exactly what you're paying for.

3

Installation Day

Our crew arrives on schedule, protects your home, and gets to work. We keep the site clean daily. Most projects are completed in 1 to 3 days.

4

Final Walkthrough

Before we pack up, we do a joint walkthrough to make sure everything meets your expectations. You'll also get care instructions specific to your new floor.
