Our Story

Built on Craft, Run on Reputation

Summit Flooring Co. started in Nate Callahan's garage in 2008 with a borrowed nail gun and one good client referral. Sixteen years later, we've installed floors in more than 800 homes and businesses across Middle Tennessee.

The Founder

Nate Callahan

I grew up watching my father restore hardwood floors in old Louisville homes on weekends. He wasn't a flooring contractor — he was a high school shop teacher who just happened to know how to do things right. That habit stuck with me.

I got into flooring professionally after college, working for a large installation company in Nashville. Good training, but the production-line approach bothered me. Crews were measured by square footage per day, not quality of installation. I saw a lot of floors fail that shouldn't have.

In 2008, I went out on my own. My first client was a neighbor whose kitchen tile had been installed by someone else and was already cracking after eight months. I fixed it, charged fairly, and she told five people. That's basically the Summit business model.

I earned my CFI certification in 2011 — the Certified Flooring Installer credential from the World Floor Covering Association. I keep it current because it matters. It means I know how different substrates behave, how humidity affects expansion gaps, and which adhesives belong in which applications.

We're small on purpose. I take on the number of jobs I can personally oversee. That means you get my phone number, my opinion on your material choices, and my hands on your floor.

Credentials

The Certifications Behind the Work

These aren't frames on a wall. They represent specific training in installation science, material behavior, and jobsite safety.

CFI Certified

Certified Flooring Installer credential from the World Floor Covering Association. Nate holds active CFI certification, renewed every three years through continuing education.

OSHA 10 Certified

All lead crew members hold OSHA 10-hour construction safety certification. We run a clean, safe jobsite on every project, whether it's a 200 sq ft bathroom or a 5,000 sq ft commercial space.

Moisture Mitigation

Certified in moisture testing and mitigation procedures for concrete substrates. Concrete moisture is the leading cause of wood and LVP floor failures. We test before we install, every time.

Fully Licensed & Insured

Tennessee contractor license in good standing. $2M general liability coverage and full workers' compensation. Certificates of insurance available on request for any project.

FloorScore Certified

We stock and install materials that meet FloorScore indoor air quality standards. Low-VOC adhesives and finishes are available on all projects at no additional cost.

NWFA Member

Active member of the National Wood Flooring Association. NWFA membership provides access to the latest installation standards, species guides, and technical resources for wood flooring.

Showroom

See It Before You Commit to It

Our Donelson showroom has more than 400 material samples on display — hardwood species and stain combinations, LVP collections, tile and stone options, and carpet samples across every price point.

We know that making flooring decisions from a 3-inch sample chip is hard. That's why we offer large 12x12 sample boards you can borrow and bring home to check against your lighting, furniture, and existing finishes.

Showroom hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday by appointment. Walk-ins are welcome, but if you want Nate there to answer technical questions, a quick call ahead helps.
