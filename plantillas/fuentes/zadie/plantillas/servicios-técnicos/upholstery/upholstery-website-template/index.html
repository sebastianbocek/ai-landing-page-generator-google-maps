How It Works

Our Process

From the first conversation to the final delivery, we keep the process simple and transparent so you know exactly what to expect.

1

Consultation

Schedule a free in-home or in-shop consultation. We will evaluate your piece, discuss your vision, and provide an honest assessment.

2

Fabric Selection

Browse our library of over 500 fabrics with help from our design consultant. We will find the perfect match for your style and needs.

3

Expert Craftsmanship

Our master upholsterers get to work in our Savannah workshop: stripping, rebuilding, padding, cutting, and sewing every detail by hand.

4

Delivery & Installation
