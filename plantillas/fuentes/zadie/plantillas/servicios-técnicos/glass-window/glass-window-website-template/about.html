Our Story

Glass Work Built on Precision, Not Shortcuts

ClearPane Glass & Mirror started in Phoenix in 2011 when Diane Reeves decided there was a better way to do custom glass work. She'd spent six years working for a large regional glazier and watched too many jobs go sideways because of rushed measurements and mismanaged installations.

She started small: one truck, one fabrication table, and a simple rule. Never cut until you're certain. That still holds today. Every project is measured on site before we touch a piece of glass. Every installation is scheduled with enough time to do it properly.

ClearPane now serves residential clients, interior designers, commercial developers, and hospitality groups across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Chandler, Mesa, and Tempe. The business has grown, but the work process hasn't changed.

The Glass We Work With

Not all glass is the same. We work with four primary types and help clients choose the right one for each application.

Tempered Glass

Heat-treated for strength and safety. When broken, it fragments into small blunt pieces rather than sharp shards. Required by code for shower enclosures, railings, and doors. Available in 6mm, 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm thicknesses.

Laminated Glass

Two or more panes bonded with a PVB interlayer. Holds together when shattered, making it ideal for railings, skylights, and overhead glazing. Excellent for sound reduction. Meets most structural glazing requirements.

Frosted & Satin Glass

Acid-etched or sandblasted for a translucent finish. Allows light transmission while obscuring direct sightlines. Used for shower screens, office partitions, cabinet doors, and decorative panels. Can be done to any degree of opacity.

Starphire Ultra-Clear

Low-iron glass with significantly less green tint than standard float glass. Delivers true optical clarity, making it the preferred choice for mirrors, frameless shower enclosures, and display applications where color accuracy matters.
