What We Do

Eight Ways We Work with Glass

Every service is handled in-house from initial measure to final installation. No middlemen, no guesswork.

Custom Mirrors

Vanity, full-length, decorative, and wall mirrors cut to any size or shape. Beveled, frameless, or framed.

Shower Enclosures

Frameless and semi-frameless glass shower enclosures. Heavy-duty hardware and precision-sealed panels.

Glass Railings

Structural glass balustrades and railings for balconies, staircases, and pool surrounds. Code-compliant.

Storefront Glass

Commercial storefronts, entrance systems, partition walls, and office glazing. Fast turnaround available.

Window Replacement

Insulated, single-pane, and specialty window glass replacement for residential and light commercial.

Table Tops & Shelves

Custom glass table tops with polished edges, and floating glass shelves in any thickness or tint.

Glass Splashbacks

Toughened and painted glass splashbacks for kitchens and bathrooms. Seamless, hygienic, and striking.

Mirror Wall Installations

Floor-to-ceiling mirror panels for gyms, studios, dining rooms, and hospitality spaces.

What Sets Us Apart

14 years of hands-on experience means we've seen the problems other companies create. We don't repeat them.

Measured on Site, Cut to Fit

We come to you before we cut anything. Dimensions are verified in person, every time. No assumptions, no return trips to fix off-spec panels.

Licensed Glazier, Not a Handyman

Diane holds a current Arizona ROC glazier license. Your job is done by a credentialed professional who carries full liability insurance on every project.

Commercial-Grade Glass on Every Job

We don't stock different glass for residential versus commercial. Tempered, laminated, and Starphire ultra-clear glass is standard across the board.

One Point of Contact, Start to Finish

You deal with Diane or one of our senior installers. No call centers, no hand-offs to subcontractors. The person who quotes your job shows up to do it.
