FAQ

Common Questions

How long does an interior paint job take?

A single room typically takes 1-2 days including prep. A full house interior usually takes 5-10 days depending on the number of rooms, amount of repair work, and whether ceilings and trim are included.

What paint brands do you use?

We exclusively use Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams top-tier product lines. These brands offer superior coverage, richer colors, and better durability than builder-grade paints. The price difference is worth the result.

Do I need to move furniture before you arrive?

No. We move all furniture to the center of the room and cover it completely with plastic. Heavy items like pianos or large bookshelves may need to be moved together. We discuss this during the estimate walkthrough.

What does your 5-year warranty cover?

Our warranty covers peeling, cracking, blistering, and fading that results from product or workmanship issues. Normal wear and tear, impact damage, and water intrusion from structural issues are not covered.

Can you match existing colors?

Yes. We can color-match any existing paint using a sample chip or a section of your current wall. We bring sample pots to your home so you can see the match in your actual lighting before we commit.

Do you offer low-VOC or zero-VOC options?

Absolutely. Both Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams offer excellent zero-VOC lines that have no odor and are safe for occupied homes. We recommend these for nurseries, bedrooms, and homes with sensitive occupants.
