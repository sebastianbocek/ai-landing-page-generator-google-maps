Why TrueCoat

The Difference Is in the Details

Meticulous Prep Work

We spend more time prepping than painting. Sanding, caulking, patching, and priming are what make a paint job last 8-10 years instead of 3.

Premium Materials Only

We use Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams top-tier paints. No cheap fillers, no cut corners. You get the finish and durability you are paying for.

Clean Work Sites

Drop cloths on every surface, tape on every edge, and a full cleanup at the end of every day. Your home stays livable throughout the project.

5-Year Warranty

We stand behind every job with a 5-year warranty on labor and materials. If it peels, cracks, or fades, we come back and fix it at no charge.
