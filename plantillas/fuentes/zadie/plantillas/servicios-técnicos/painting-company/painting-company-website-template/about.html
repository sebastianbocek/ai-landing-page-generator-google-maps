About TrueCoat

A small team of painters who believe prep work matters more than anything, and that every surface deserves a flawless finish.

Our Story

Built on Prep Work and Standards

TrueCoat started in 2014 when Ryan Chen left a national painting franchise because he was tired of cutting corners. The company prioritized speed over quality, used cheap paint, and skipped critical prep steps. He knew homeowners deserved better.

He started TrueCoat with one simple rule: do the prep work right. That means sanding, caulking, priming, and patching before any color goes on the wall. It takes longer, but the results last three times as long as a rush job.

Today, TrueCoat has a crew of 14 trained painters and has completed over 2,800 projects. We use Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams exclusively, and every job comes with a 5-year warranty. We are still a small company by choice. That is how we keep our standards high.

Our Values

What We Believe

Preparation First

A great paint job is 70% prep and 30% paint. We sand, patch, caulk, and prime every surface thoroughly before applying a single coat of color. No shortcuts, ever.

Premium Materials

We only use top-tier paints from Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams. Better paint means richer colors, smoother finishes, and surfaces that look great for years.

Honest Communication

We give accurate timelines, transparent pricing, and daily progress updates. If we discover an issue during prep, we tell you right away and discuss options.
