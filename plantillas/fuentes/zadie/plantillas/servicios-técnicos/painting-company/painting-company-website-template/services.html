What We Do

Professional Painting Services

Interior Painting

Complete interior painting for any room. Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and closets. We protect floors and furniture, tape every edge, and deliver smooth, even coverage.

  • Wall, ceiling, and trim painting
  • Drywall repair and patching
  • Caulking and gap filling
  • Full furniture and floor protection
  • Benjamin Moore or Sherwin-Williams paint
From $2.50 / sq ft

Exterior Painting

Full exterior painting including siding, trim, doors, shutters, and decks. Power washing, scraping, and priming included for surfaces that need it.

  • Siding, trim, and fascia
  • Power washing and surface prep
  • Scraping and sanding as needed
  • Caulking all joints and seams
  • Weather-resistant coatings
From $3.00 / sq ft

Cabinet Refinishing

Transform your kitchen or bathroom cabinets without a full remodel. Professional sanding, priming, and sprayed finish for a factory-smooth result.

  • Door and drawer removal
  • Degreasing and sanding
  • Spray-applied finish coats
  • New hardware installation available
  • 10-day average turnaround
From $3,500 / average kitchen
