Common Questions

Service FAQs

Do I need a permit for electrical work?

Most electrical work requires a permit. We handle all permit applications and inspections as part of our service for larger projects.

When should I upgrade my panel?

If you have a fuse box, frequently trip breakers, are adding major appliances, or installing an EV charger, a panel upgrade is likely needed.

Can I do electrical work myself?

While minor tasks like changing a light fixture may be DIY-friendly, most electrical work requires a licensed electrician for safety and code compliance.

How do I know if my wiring is safe?

Warning signs include flickering lights, warm outlets, frequent breaker trips, or a burning smell. We offer electrical safety inspections to assess your system.

What type of EV charger do I need?

Most homeowners benefit from a Level 2 charger (240V), which charges most EVs overnight. We can assess your needs and recommend the right solution.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes. All our work comes with a 5-year workmanship warranty. If there's an issue with our installation, we'll fix it at no charge.
