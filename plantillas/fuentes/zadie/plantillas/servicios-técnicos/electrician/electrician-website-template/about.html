Our Story

From Apprentice to Master Electrician

Bright Spark Electric was founded in 2009 by Mike Brennan, a third-generation electrician who learned the trade from his father and grandfather. After earning his master electrician license and working for large contractors, Mike saw an opportunity to do things differently.

He wanted to build a company focused on doing the job right, treating customers with respect, and providing clear, honest pricing. No pressure tactics. No unnecessary upsells. Just quality electrical work from people who take pride in their craft.

Fifteen years later, Bright Spark has grown to a team of six licensed electricians. We've completed over 8,000 residential and commercial jobs, built relationships with general contractors across the metro area, and earned a reputation for reliability and quality.
