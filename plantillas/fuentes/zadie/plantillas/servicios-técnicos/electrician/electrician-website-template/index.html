Safety First

Know the Warning Signs

Electrical problems can be dangerous. Call a professional if you notice any of these warning signs.

01

Flickering Lights

Lights that flicker or dim could indicate loose wiring or an overloaded circuit. Don't ignore it.

02

Warm Outlets

Outlets or switches that feel warm to the touch may have dangerous wiring issues behind the wall.

03

Burning Smell

A burning or smoky smell near outlets or your panel is an emergency. Cut power and call immediately.

04

Frequent Trips

Breakers that trip repeatedly may indicate a circuit overload or a more serious electrical fault.
