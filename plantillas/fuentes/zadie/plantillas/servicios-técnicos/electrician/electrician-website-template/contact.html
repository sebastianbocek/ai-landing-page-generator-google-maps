FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

For electrical emergencies, we aim to have a licensed electrician at your location within 1-2 hours. Our 24/7 line is (555) 759-2473.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. We provide free written estimates for all residential and commercial projects. There's no obligation and no pressure.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Bright Spark Electric holds a Master Electrician license (#ELEC-13579), carries $2M in liability insurance, and is fully bonded.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards. For larger projects, we also offer financing options through our partners.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. For work that requires permits, we handle the permit application, coordinate inspections, and ensure everything passes code.

What's your warranty policy?

All our work comes with a 5-year workmanship warranty. If there's any issue with our installation, we'll return and fix it at no charge.

Can you work around my schedule?

Absolutely. We offer morning, afternoon, and evening appointments. We'll work with you to find a time that fits your schedule.

Do you clean up after the job?

Always. We treat your home with respect. When the job is done, we clean up completely. You'll never know we were there except for the work we did.
