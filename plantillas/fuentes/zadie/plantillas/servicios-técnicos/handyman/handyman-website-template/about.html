About FixIt Pro

Good People Doing Good Work

We started FixIt Pro because homeowners deserve skilled, reliable help at a fair price. Twelve years later, that's still what drives us.

Our Story

Built on Honest Work

FixIt Pro started in 2013 when founder Dave Kowalski got tired of watching friends and neighbors get ripped off by unreliable contractors. He'd been doing side jobs for years, fixing things around the neighborhood, and realized there was a real need for honest, skilled handyman work at reasonable prices.

He started with a truck, a toolbox, and a simple promise: show up on time, do the job right, and charge a fair price. Word spread fast. Within a year, he'd hired two more handymen. Today, our team of six serves hundreds of homeowners across the metro area.

We haven't changed the formula because it works. Every member of our team is licensed, insured, and background-checked. We give free estimates, we don't charge for travel, and we guarantee our work. That's it. No gimmicks.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Three principles guide every job we take on. They're simple, but they're non-negotiable.

Honest Pricing

We quote before we start and we stick to it. If something changes mid-job, we talk to you first. No hidden fees, no "while we're here" upsells. What we quote is what you pay.

Quality Work

We don't cut corners. We use the right materials, the right techniques, and we take the time to do things properly. Every repair comes with a 90-day workmanship guarantee.

Respect for Your Home

Your home is your biggest investment. We wear shoe covers, lay down drop cloths, and clean up completely when we're done. You'll never know we were there, except the repair is fixed.
