Our Services

Comprehensive Garment Care

From everyday essentials to your most treasured pieces, we deliver precision cleaning and expert craftsmanship for every fabric and occasion.

Dry Cleaning

Gentle, thorough cleaning for suits, dresses, coats, and delicate fabrics using eco-friendly GreenEarth solvents that protect your garments and the environment.

Starting at $8.95/garment

Shirt Laundry

Professionally laundered and pressed dress shirts returned on hangers or folded. We handle cotton, linen, and blended fabrics with equal care.

Why Prestige

A Standard of Care You Can See and Feel

Individual Inspection

Every garment is inspected by hand before and after cleaning. We check for stains, loose buttons, and wear so nothing is overlooked.

Eco-Friendly Process

Our GreenEarth cleaning system uses liquid silicone instead of harsh chemicals. It is gentler on fabrics, safer for our team, and better for the planet.

Hand-Finished Details

Buttons are covered before pressing, pleats are set by hand, and collars are shaped on custom forms. The details are what set us apart.

Same-Day Turnaround

Drop off before 10am and pick up by 5pm the same day. Rush service is available at no extra charge for our regular customers.
