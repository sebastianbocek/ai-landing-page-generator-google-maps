Our Services

Expert Care for Every Garment

From everyday shirts to once-in-a-lifetime wedding gowns, every piece receives the same meticulous attention.

Core Service

Professional Dry Cleaning

Our GreenEarth dry cleaning system uses liquid silicone instead of harsh petrochemicals. This means your fabrics retain their color, texture, and shape longer while being cleaned just as thoroughly.

We clean suits, dresses, blazers, coats, silk blouses, cashmere sweaters, and any garment that requires solvent-based cleaning. Each piece is individually inspected and hand-finished.

  • GreenEarth eco-friendly solvent system
  • Individual stain pre-treatment
  • Hand pressing and finishing
  • Same-day service available (in by 10am)
  • Starting at $8.95 per garment
Daily Essentials

Shirt Laundry & Pressing

Your dress shirts deserve better than a home iron. We launder each shirt individually using temperature-controlled water and premium detergents, then press them on specialized equipment designed for collars, cuffs, and plackets.

Choose hangers or box folding. We replace missing buttons at no charge and reinforce loose ones before they fall off.

  • Individual washing (never batch processed)
  • Collar and cuff specialist pressing
  • Free button replacement
  • Hanger or box fold options
  • Starting at $3.50 per shirt
Expert Craftsmanship

Alterations & Tailoring

Our master tailor Robert Harwood brings 35 years of experience to every alteration. From simple hems and tapering to complex structural modifications, we ensure your garments fit as if they were made for you.

We work with all garment types: suits, dresses, jeans, outerwear, uniforms, and formal wear. Bridal and groom alterations are a specialty.

  • Milan-trained master tailor on site
  • Hems, tapering, and taking in/out
  • Bridal and formal wear alterations
  • Zipper and lining replacement
  • Starting at $15.00
Heirloom Care

Wedding Dress Preservation

Your wedding dress represents one of the most meaningful days of your life. Our museum-quality preservation process ensures it remains as beautiful decades from now as it was on your wedding day.

Elena Harwood personally handles every bridal preservation. The gown is hand-cleaned using methods specific to its fabrics and embellishments, then carefully wrapped in acid-free tissue and sealed in an archival-grade box.

  • Hand cleaning by our lead specialist
  • Acid-free tissue and archival box
  • Invisible stain treatment for wine, food, and soil
  • Viewing window for storage monitoring
  • Starting at $295.00
Specialty Service

Leather & Suede Care

Leather and suede require entirely different cleaning methods than fabric. We use specialized conditioning treatments that clean, restore moisture, and protect the natural oils in your leather goods.

From jackets and coats to handbags, boots, and gloves, we handle all leather and suede items. Color restoration and re-dyeing are available for faded pieces.

  • Deep cleaning and conditioning
  • Color restoration and re-dyeing
  • Water and stain repellent treatment
  • Handbag and accessory care
  • Starting at $45.00
Convenience

Free Pickup & Delivery

We bring the Prestige experience to your door. Schedule a pickup online or by phone, and our uniformed driver will collect your garments from your home or office. Cleaned items are returned on your next scheduled pickup day.

Our route covers Greenwich, Old Greenwich, Riverside, Cos Cob, and Stamford. Same-day delivery is available for rush orders placed before 10am.

  • Always free, no minimums
  • Scheduled weekly or on-demand
  • Home and office pickup available
  • Garment bags provided
  • Greenwich and surrounding areas
