FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, process, and policies.

Schedule a pickup by phone, email, or through our website. Our uniformed driver will arrive at your home or office during your selected time window. We provide garment bags for your items. Cleaned garments are returned on your next scheduled pickup day, or you can request a separate delivery. There is no minimum order and the service is always free within our coverage area.

GreenEarth is a cleaning system that uses liquid silicone (D5) instead of traditional petrochemical solvents like perc. It is odorless, gentle on fabrics, and breaks down into sand, water, and carbon dioxide. Your clothes come back softer, colors stay brighter, and there is no chemical smell. It is also safer for our team and the environment.

Standard turnaround for dry cleaning and shirt laundry is two business days. Same-day service is available for items dropped off before 10:00am (pickup by 5:00pm). Alterations typically take 3-5 business days depending on complexity. Wedding dress preservation takes 4-6 weeks to ensure the highest quality.

Yes. Every garment is fully insured from the moment we take possession until it is returned to you. We carry comprehensive liability coverage that covers loss, damage, and theft. Each item is logged and tracked throughout our facility. In the rare event of an issue, we handle claims promptly and fairly.

We have a high success rate with stain removal, but we are always honest about expectations. Our specialists identify stain types and apply targeted treatments before cleaning. Many stains that other cleaners have given up on respond to our process. If a stain cannot be fully removed without risking fabric damage, we will let you know before proceeding.

New customers receive 20% off their first order. We also offer a loyalty program: after every 10 orders, you receive a $25 credit toward your next service. Corporate accounts with regular weekly service receive preferred pricing. Contact us for a custom corporate rate.
