Our Story

25 Years of Exceptional Care

What started as a single pressing station has grown into Greenwich's most trusted name in garment care. Our commitment has never wavered.

Est. 2001

Built on a Simple Promise

Prestige Garment Care was founded in 2001 by Robert and Elena Harwood with a straightforward idea: treat every garment as if it were irreplaceable. Robert, a third-generation tailor trained in Milan, and Elena, a textile chemist, combined their expertise to create something different.

They started with a 600-square-foot shop on Mason Street. Word spread quickly. Customers noticed that their clothes came back not just clean, but noticeably better. Stains other cleaners had given up on were gone. Suits hung differently. Shirts felt crisper without being stiff.

Twenty-five years later, Prestige serves over 3,000 families across Fairfield County. The shop has grown, the equipment has advanced, but the founding principle remains the same: every garment deserves individual attention.

Our Values

What Guides Every Decision

These are not slogans on a wall. They are the standards our team holds each other to, every day.

Precision

We inspect every garment before and after cleaning. Buttons, seams, stains, fabric condition. Nothing leaves our facility without a final quality check.

Sustainability

Our GreenEarth cleaning system eliminates toxic solvents. We use biodegradable packaging, energy-efficient equipment, and recycled hangers wherever possible.

Trust

Your garments are fully insured from the moment we pick them up. We document every item, track every order, and guarantee satisfaction on every piece.

Our Process

Four Steps to Perfection

Every garment follows the same meticulous journey through our facility, from the moment it arrives to the moment it is returned to you.

1

Intake & Inspection

Each garment is logged, photographed, and inspected for existing stains, damage, or special care needs.

2

Pre-Treatment

Stains are identified by type and treated individually with targeted solutions before the main cleaning cycle.

3

Clean & Press

Garments are cleaned using the appropriate method for their fabric, then hand-pressed on specialized equipment.

4

Final Quality Check

Every piece is reviewed under proper lighting, packaged in protective covering, and prepared for pickup or delivery.
