FAQ

Common Questions

How often should I change my air filter?

Most filters should be changed every 1-3 months, depending on the type and your household (pets, allergies, etc.). Check your filter monthly and replace when it looks dirty.

How often should my HVAC system be serviced?

We recommend professional maintenance twice a year. Schedule a heating tune-up in fall and a cooling tune-up in spring to keep your system running efficiently.

What's the average lifespan of an HVAC system?

With proper maintenance, most systems last 15-20 years. Furnaces tend to last longer (20-25 years) than air conditioners (12-15 years). Age, usage, and maintenance all affect lifespan.

Should I repair or replace my old system?

Generally, if your system is over 10 years old and the repair costs more than half the price of a new unit, replacement makes more sense. We'll give you honest advice.

How much can I save with a new high-efficiency system?

Modern high-efficiency systems can reduce energy bills by 30-50% compared to older units. The exact savings depend on your current system's age and efficiency rating.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes! We have technicians on call 24/7, 365 days a year. Call (555) 932-8476 anytime for emergency heating or cooling service. No extra charges for emergencies.

What brands do you service and install?

We service all major brands and are factory-authorized dealers for Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Bryant, American Standard, and York.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! We provide free in-home estimates for new system installations and major projects. For service calls, there's a diagnostic fee that's waived if you proceed with the repair.
