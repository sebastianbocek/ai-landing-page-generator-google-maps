Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Service

ClimateControl Pros started in 2003 when founder Robert Martinez left a national HVAC chain to start his own company. His vision was simple: provide honest, expert service at fair prices, and treat every customer's home like his own.

What began as a one-man operation has grown into a team of 15 certified technicians serving the entire metro area. But our values haven't changed. We still answer the phone ourselves, show up on time, and stand behind every job we do.

Today, Robert's daughter Maria runs day-to-day operations, while he still makes house calls for longtime customers. That's the ClimateControl difference. We're not just a company. We're your neighbors.
