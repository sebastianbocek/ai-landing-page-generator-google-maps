What We Do

Full-Service Machining Capabilities

From raw stock to finished part, our facility handles every stage of precision manufacturing under one roof.

CNC Milling
±0.0005" | 3/4/5-Axis

3-axis, 4-axis, and full 5-axis simultaneous machining. Complex geometries, pockets, contours, and precision bores across aluminum, steel, titanium, and engineering plastics.

CNC Turning
OD to 18" | Length to 48"

High-precision turned parts with live tooling capability. Threading, boring, grooving, and multi-feature turned components in a single setup for reduced cycle times.

EDM
Wire & Sinker | Hardened Materials

Wire EDM and sinker EDM for complex geometries and hardened tool steels. Ideal for dies, molds, and intricate features that are impossible to achieve through conventional cutting.

Grinding
±0.0001" | Ra 4 µin

Surface grinding and cylindrical grinding to the tightest tolerances we offer. Used for final sizing on hardened components, precision flatness, and exacting surface finish requirements.

Fabrication & Welding
MIG / TIG | Structural & Sheet

Sheet metal fabrication, structural steel, MIG and TIG welding. Weld assemblies integrated with machined components for complete subassembly delivery.

Inspection & Quality
CMM | First Article | AS9100D

Dedicated CMM inspection room with full dimensional reporting. First article inspection (FAI), material certifications, and AS9100D-compliant documentation packages available on every order.
