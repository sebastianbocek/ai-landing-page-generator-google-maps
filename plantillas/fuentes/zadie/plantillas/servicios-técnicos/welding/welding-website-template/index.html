The Process

How We Work

No surprises, no runaround. We walk every client through a straightforward process that keeps your project on schedule and on budget.

01

Consult

We start with a conversation. Tell us what you need, your timeline, and your material requirements. We ask the questions that prevent problems later.

02

Design

Working from your drawings or designing from scratch, we produce shop drawings for your review and approval before a single piece of steel is cut.

03

Fabricate

Our certified welders execute the work in our 8,000 sq ft shop using the right process for the material. Quality checks happen throughout, not just at the end.

04

Deliver

We coordinate delivery or installation and follow up to make sure everything fits and performs as designed. The job isn't done until you say it's done.
