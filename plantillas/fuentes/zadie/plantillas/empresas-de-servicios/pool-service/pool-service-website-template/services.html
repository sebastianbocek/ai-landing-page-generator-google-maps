Our Services

Everything your pool and spa needs, handled by certified professionals. One company, complete care.

Most Popular

Weekly Pool Maintenance

Our signature service. A CPO-certified technician visits your property on the same day each week to keep your pool in perfect condition. Every visit includes a full inspection and detailed service report sent to your phone.

  • Surface skimming and debris removal
  • Brushing of walls, steps, and tile line
  • Vacuum pool floor (manual or automatic)
  • Test and balance water chemistry (pH, chlorine, alkalinity)
  • Clean skimmer and pump baskets
  • Inspect equipment for proper operation
  • Backwash or clean filter as needed
Starting at $120/month (weekly visits)

Equipment Repair & Installation

From a noisy pump to a complete equipment pad replacement, our senior technicians diagnose and repair all major brands. We stock common parts on our trucks so most repairs are completed in a single visit.

  • Pump and motor repair or replacement
  • Filter rebuilds (cartridge, DE, and sand)
  • Heater diagnostics and repair (gas and heat pump)
  • Salt chlorine generator service
  • Automation system installation (Pentair, Hayward, Jandy)
  • LED pool and spa light replacement
  • Variable-speed pump upgrades (energy savings up to 80%)
Free diagnostics with any repair service

Pool Opening & Closing

Seasonal service done right. Our opening process gets your pool swim-ready in 24-48 hours. Our closing process protects plumbing, equipment, and surfaces through the entire winter.

  • Cover removal, cleaning, and storage (opening)
  • Full equipment startup and inspection
  • Shock treatment and chemical balancing
  • Plumbing line blowout and antifreeze (closing)
  • Winter plug installation
  • Safety cover installation (closing)
Opening from $275 | Closing from $325

Liner & Surface Renovation

Whether your vinyl liner is faded and wrinkled or your plaster needs resurfacing, we handle the full renovation from measurement to final fill. Most liner replacements are completed in one day.

  • Precise laser measurement for perfect fit
  • Hundreds of liner patterns from top manufacturers
  • Professional drain-down and installation
  • Bead and track replacement if needed
  • Plaster and pebble resurfacing options
  • Tile repair and waterline tile replacement
Vinyl liner replacement from $2,800
