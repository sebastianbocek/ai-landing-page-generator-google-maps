Our Story

Started with One Truck and a Simple Promise

AquaClear Pool & Spa was founded in 2010 by Marcus Rivera, a former competitive swimmer who spent summers working for his uncle's pool company. After earning his Certified Pool Operator license and a degree in water chemistry, Marcus started AquaClear with a single truck, a test kit, and a commitment to doing the job right the first time.

What began as a one-person operation servicing 20 residential pools has grown into a full-service pool and spa company with a team of 14 CPO-certified technicians. We now maintain over 800 pools and spas across the metro area, from backyard pools to large commercial aquatic facilities.

Our approach has not changed since day one: show up on time, do thorough work, communicate clearly, and never cut corners on water chemistry. That consistency is why 92% of our customers have been with us for more than three years.

Our Values

What Drives Everything We Do

Three principles guide every decision we make, from hiring to how we treat your pool.

Water Chemistry First

A clean-looking pool is not the same as a safe pool. We prioritize proper chemical balance because it protects swimmers, extends equipment life, and prevents costly damage to surfaces.

Honest Communication

We explain what your pool needs and why. If something is not broken, we will not recommend replacing it. If there is a problem, we tell you straight. Every visit includes a detailed service report.

Reliability Above All

Pool service only works if it happens consistently. Our scheduling system, backup technicians, and management oversight ensure your pool gets serviced on time, every time. No skipped weeks, no excuses.
