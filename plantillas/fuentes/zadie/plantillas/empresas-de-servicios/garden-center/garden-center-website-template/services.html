Core Service

Plant Nursery

Our nursery carries over 500 plant varieties chosen specifically for the Pacific Northwest climate. Everything is grown in conditions similar to what you'll find in an Oregon garden, so transplant shock is minimal and plants hit the ground ready to establish.

We rotate stock weekly throughout the growing season. What you see today may be gone tomorrow, and something new will take its place. Our newsletter and social pages stay current with new arrivals.

Annuals and perennials, updated weekly through the season
Shrubs and ornamental trees, including rare and native varieties
Fruit trees, berries, and edible plants for kitchen gardens
Vegetable and herb starts, seeded in our own greenhouse from January
Indoor houseplants and tropicals, stocked year-round
Bulbs seasonally: spring-blooming bulbs in fall, summer bulbs in spring

Design Consultation

Landscape Design

Maria and our design team offer one-on-one landscape consultations for residential and small commercial properties. We work across the full Eugene and Springfield area and can visit your site or work from photos and measurements you bring to the shop.

Our approach is practical first. We design for the conditions that actually exist in your yard — soil, sun, drainage, and how much time you want to spend maintaining it. Beautiful is the goal, but sustainable comes first.

Consultations from $75/hour
On-site visits for full garden assessments (Eugene & Springfield area)
Sketch plans and plant lists appropriate to your conditions
Native plant and pollinator garden specialty
Water-wise and low-maintenance design options
Formal design packages available for larger projects

Installation Service

Garden Installation

We can do the planting for you. Our installation crew handles everything from single-bed projects to full-yard transformations. We source the plants from our own nursery, so what goes in the ground is exactly what we'd recommend for the site.

Installation is available to customers who've completed a design consultation, or as a standalone service for customers with an existing plan or simple needs. Call or stop in to discuss your project.

Full bed preparation including soil amendment and edging
Plant sourcing, delivery, and installation in a single visit
Mulching and post-installation watering instructions
30-day establishment follow-up available
Service area: Eugene, Springfield, and surrounding communities

Supplies & Materials

Soil, Amendments & Garden Supplies

We stock a carefully chosen range of soils, composts, fertilizers, and amendments. Nothing we sell is something we wouldn't use in our own greenhouses. No synthetic pesticides. All organic or naturally derived where available.

We also carry pottery and planters from local ceramicists and regional producers, and a curated selection of quality garden tools designed to actually last.

Bagged and bulk compost, garden soil, and planting mix
Organic fertilizers, liquid feeds, and soil amendments
Mulch, bark, and ground cover materials
Pottery and glazed planters in a wide range of sizes
Hand tools, watering equipment, and pruning supplies
Drip irrigation components and soaker hose systems

Delivery Service

We'll Bring It to You

Not everyone can fit a Japanese maple in their Subaru. We get it. We deliver plants, soil, and supplies throughout the Eugene metro area, with basic planting service available as an add-on.

Delivery Options

Standard delivery — Plants and materials dropped at your door. Available Tuesday through Saturday.
Delivery + placement — We bring it, position it where you want it, and you do the planting.
Delivery + planting — Full service. We arrive, plant, and leave the bed looking finished.
Bulk soil delivery — Minimum one cubic yard. Available in garden soil, compost, or blend.

Delivery pricing is based on distance and order size. Call or stop in for a quote. Minimum order for delivery is $80 in product.
