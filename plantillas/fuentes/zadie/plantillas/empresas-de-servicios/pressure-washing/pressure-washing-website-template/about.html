About PowerClean

A family-run pressure washing company built on hard work, proper technique, and respect for your property.

Our Story

Started with a Truck and a Pressure Washer

PowerClean Exteriors started in 2014 when founder Marcus Daniels bought a used pressure washer and started cleaning driveways on weekends. What began as side work turned into a full-time business within six months.

Marcus had spent a decade in construction and knew firsthand how much of a difference a clean exterior makes. He also saw how many crews were using the wrong pressure, wrong detergents, and damaging surfaces in the process. He wanted to do it right.

Today, PowerClean runs three trucks with a crew of trained technicians. We serve residential and commercial clients across the Springfield metro area. Every job gets the same attention to detail, whether it's a single driveway or a 50,000 square foot warehouse.

Our Values

What Drives Our Work

Three principles that guide every job we take on.

Thorough Work

We don't cut corners. Every job includes a full property walk-through, surface assessment, and post-clean inspection. If a spot doesn't meet our standards, we hit it again before we leave.

Property Protection

Different surfaces need different approaches. We adjust PSI, nozzle distance, and cleaning solutions for every material. Your siding, windows, landscaping, and paint are always safe with us.

Environmental Responsibility

All our detergents are biodegradable and safe for plants, pets, and waterways. We use water reclamation systems on commercial jobs and minimize runoff on every residential project.
