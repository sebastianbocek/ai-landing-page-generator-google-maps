What We Specialize In

Our most-requested services, each tailored to the specific surface and level of cleaning required.

House Washing (Soft Wash)

Your home's siding collects more than dirt. Algae, mold, mildew, and pollen build up over time and can cause permanent discoloration if left untreated. Our soft wash method uses low pressure and specialized biodegradable detergents to safely clean without damaging your surfaces.

  • Safe for vinyl, wood, stucco, brick, and Hardie board
  • Kills mold and algae at the root to prevent regrowth
  • Includes eaves, soffits, and downspout cleaning
  • Window frames and shutters hand-wiped
$250 - $450 based on home size (avg. 1,500 - 3,000 sq ft)

Driveway & Concrete Cleaning

Concrete is porous and absorbs everything: oil, tire rubber, mold, rust, and organic stains. Our surface cleaner system delivers uniform high-pressure cleaning that removes deep stains without leaving streaks or wand marks.

  • Hot water system for oil and grease removal
  • 20-inch surface cleaner for streak-free results
  • Sidewalks, patios, pool decks, and garage floors
  • Post-treatment sealant available (additional cost)
$0.15 - $0.25 per sq ft (min. $150)

Deck & Patio Restoration

Wood and composite decking require a careful approach. Too much pressure splinters wood and voids composite warranties. We use calibrated low-pressure cleaning with wood-safe detergents that lift gray weathering, mold, and foot traffic stains.

  • PSI adjusted for cedar, pine, composite, and Trex
  • Railing spindles and stairs included
  • Optional brightening treatment restores natural wood tone
  • Stain and seal services available as add-on
$200 - $400 based on deck size and material

Roof Cleaning (Soft Wash Only)

Those black streaks on your roof are Gloeocapsa magma, a type of algae that feeds on limestone in your shingles. Pressure washing a roof voids most warranties and damages shingles. Our soft wash treatment kills the algae chemically without any high-pressure contact.

  • No high-pressure contact with shingles, ever
  • Approved method by GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed
  • Safe for asphalt, tile, metal, and slate
  • Results visible within 24-48 hours as algae dies off
$350 - $600 based on roof size and pitch
