Our Tree Care Services

From routine maintenance to complex removals, we handle every aspect of tree care with the skill and safety your property deserves.

Core Service

Tree Removal

When a tree is dead, structurally compromised, or poses a risk to your property, our crew removes it safely and efficiently. We handle everything from straightforward backyard removals to technical operations requiring cranes and specialized rigging near homes, power lines, and other structures.

  • Hazardous and dead tree removal
  • Crane-assisted removal for difficult access
  • Sectional dismantling near structures
  • Wood chipping and complete debris hauling
  • Permit coordination when required
$500 - $5,000+ depending on size and complexity
Core Service

Trimming & Pruning

Proper pruning improves tree structure, removes hazards, and promotes healthy growth. Our arborists follow ANSI A300 pruning standards to ensure every cut benefits the tree. We never top trees or use harmful techniques that compromise structural integrity.

  • Crown thinning to improve light and airflow
  • Deadwood removal and hazard reduction
  • Structural pruning for young trees
  • Canopy raising for clearance over structures
  • Vista pruning to restore views
$250 - $1,500 per tree
Core Service

Stump Grinding & Removal

Old stumps are eyesores, tripping hazards, and breeding grounds for termites and carpenter ants. Our commercial stump grinders reduce stumps to mulch 6 to 12 inches below grade, letting you reclaim the space for new plantings, lawn, or landscaping.

  • Grinding 6-12 inches below grade
  • Surface root removal included
  • Backfill with clean wood chip mulch
  • Accessible stumps and tight-space stumps
  • Volume discounts for multiple stumps
$150 - $500 per stump
24/7 Service

Emergency Storm Damage

Storms do not wait for business hours, and neither do we. When a tree falls on your roof, blocks your driveway, or threatens power lines, our emergency crews mobilize quickly to secure the scene and begin cleanup. We work directly with your insurance company to document damage and streamline the claims process.

  • Rapid response crews available 24/7/365
  • Fallen tree removal from structures
  • Temporary tarping and structural protection
  • Direct insurance company coordination
  • Hazard assessment and follow-up care
