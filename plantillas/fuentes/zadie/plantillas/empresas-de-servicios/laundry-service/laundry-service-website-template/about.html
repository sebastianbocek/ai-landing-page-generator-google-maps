How It Started

Built on Convenience and Quality

FreshWave was founded in 2015 by Alex Thompson, a busy professional who was frustrated by the time and hassle of traditional laundromats. Alex envisioned a service that would combine professional-quality cleaning with modern convenience—pickup and delivery, online scheduling, and eco-friendly practices.

What began as a small operation with one van and a rented commercial space has grown into the area's premier laundry and dry cleaning service. Today, we serve over 2,000 residential customers and 50+ commercial clients, processing thousands of pounds of laundry every week.

  • Over 2,000 satisfied residential customers
  • 50+ commercial accounts including hotels, restaurants, and offices
  • 10,000+ pounds of laundry processed weekly
  • State-of-the-art eco-friendly facility
  • Same-day and 24-hour turnaround options available
  • 100% customer satisfaction guarantee
How We Work

Our Professional Process

Every item receives expert care through our thorough, multi-step cleaning process.

1. Inspection & Sorting

Upon arrival at our facility, every garment is carefully inspected for stains, damage, and special care requirements. We sort items by color, fabric type, and washing instructions to ensure optimal cleaning results.

2. Stain Treatment

Identified stains receive pre-treatment using professional-grade, eco-friendly spot removers. Different stains require different treatments—we know exactly what works best for each type.

3. Professional Washing

Garments are washed in our high-efficiency commercial machines using premium, hypoallergenic detergents. We carefully select water temperature and cycle settings based on fabric type and care labels.

4. Expert Drying

Items are dried at controlled temperatures to prevent shrinkage and damage. Delicate items are air-dried or tumbled on low heat. We never over-dry, which helps fabrics last longer.

5. Precise Folding & Packaging

Every item is neatly folded or hung according to garment type. We package everything carefully to prevent wrinkles during transport. Dry cleaned items are placed on hangers with protective coverings.

6. Quality Check & Delivery

Before delivery, we perform a final quality inspection to ensure everything meets our high standards. Your clean laundry is then delivered fresh to your door at your scheduled time.

Going Green

Our Commitment to Sustainability

At FreshWave, we believe in doing what's right for our customers and our planet. That's why we've invested in eco-friendly practices and products that deliver exceptional cleaning results while minimizing environmental impact.

Eco-Friendly Products

We exclusively use biodegradable, plant-based detergents that are gentle on fabrics and safe for sensitive skin. Our dry cleaning process uses environmentally responsible solvents that don't release harmful chemicals into the air or water.

Energy & Water Conservation

Our state-of-the-art machines are Energy Star certified and use up to 50% less water and energy than conventional equipment. We also capture and reuse heat from our dryers to warm incoming water, further reducing energy consumption.
