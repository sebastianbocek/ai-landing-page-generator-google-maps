Pickup & Delivery

Convenient service right to your door

Why waste time at the laundromat? Our pickup and delivery service brings professional laundry care right to your doorstep. Schedule online, leave your laundry out, and we'll handle the rest.

Service available 7 days a week
Flexible pickup windows (morning, afternoon, evening)
Real-time order tracking via text updates
Contactless pickup and delivery available
Free delivery on orders over $30
Secure online payment and scheduling

How It Works

  1. Schedule Online: Choose your preferred pickup time through our website or app. Available time slots from 7am to 9pm.
  2. Leave Out Your Laundry: Place your items in a bag and leave them at your door. Use our reusable FreshWave bags or your own.
  3. We Pick Up & Clean: Our driver collects your laundry and takes it to our facility for professional cleaning.
  4. Track Your Order: Receive text updates when we pick up, when cleaning is complete, and when we're on our way to deliver.
  5. Fresh Delivery: Your clean laundry is returned within 24-48 hours, neatly folded or hung.

Service Areas

We currently serve Downtown, Westside, Eastside, Northside, and surrounding neighborhoods within a 10-mile radius of our facility. Check our website to confirm we service your address.

Delivery Fees

Free delivery on orders $30 or more. Orders under $30 incur a $5.99 delivery fee.
