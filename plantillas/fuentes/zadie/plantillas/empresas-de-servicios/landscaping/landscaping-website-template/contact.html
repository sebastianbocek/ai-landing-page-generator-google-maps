Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing, and process.

How long does a typical landscape design project take from start to finish?

The timeline varies based on project scope. Design consultations and plans typically take 2-3 weeks. Installation can range from a few days for simple projects to 4-6 weeks for comprehensive landscape renovations. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer payment plans or financing for larger projects?

Yes! We offer flexible payment schedules for projects over $5,000. Typically, we require a 30% deposit to begin, 40% at mid-project, and 30% upon completion. We also partner with financing companies that offer low-interest payment plans for qualified customers.

Are maintenance contracts required, or can I hire you for one-time services?

We offer both! While maintenance contracts ensure consistent care and priority scheduling, we're happy to provide one-time services like spring cleanup, mulch installation, or seasonal pruning. Contact us to discuss your needs.

What happens if I'm not satisfied with the work?

Your satisfaction is our top priority. We stand behind our work with a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with any aspect of our service, we'll work with you to make it right. We also offer a one-year warranty on plant installations (with proper care) and workmanship.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes! We offer free, no-obligation consultations and estimates for all services. We'll visit your property, assess your needs, and provide a detailed written quote. Design services have a fee that's credited toward your project if you proceed with installation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. GreenScape is fully licensed as a landscape contractor in North Carolina (License #12345) and carries comprehensive liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage. We're happy to provide proof of insurance upon request.

Do you use organic or eco-friendly products?

Yes! We prioritize sustainable practices and offer organic fertilization programs, natural pest control methods, and environmentally responsible products. We can customize our approach based on your preferences for organic or traditional methods.

How far in advance should I schedule services?

For maintenance contracts, we recommend contacting us 2-4 weeks before your desired start date. Design and installation projects should be scheduled 4-8 weeks in advance, especially during our busy spring and fall seasons. Emergency tree services are available 24/7.

Do you handle irrigation system repairs and winterization?

Yes! Our irrigation specialists handle all system repairs, maintenance, upgrades, winterization (blow-outs), and spring start-ups. We also offer annual service contracts that include seasonal system checks and adjustments.

Can you work with HOA guidelines and restrictions?

Absolutely. We have extensive experience working within HOA requirements and can help ensure your landscape design meets all community guidelines. We can also attend HOA meetings on your behalf if architectural approval is needed.
