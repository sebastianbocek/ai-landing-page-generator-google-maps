Our Story

Rooted in Excellence

GreenScape Design & Maintenance was founded in 2009 by David Martinez, a landscape architect with a vision to bring professional, eco-conscious landscaping services to the metro area. What started as a small operation with a pickup truck and a mower has grown into a full-service landscape company with over 30 team members.

Our journey has been guided by a simple philosophy: exceptional quality, sustainable practices, and genuine care for every property we touch. We believe that beautiful landscapes aren't just about aesthetics—they're about creating outdoor spaces that improve quality of life, support local ecosystems, and stand the test of time.

Today, we're proud to be one of the most trusted landscaping companies in the region, with hundreds of satisfied residential and commercial clients who return to us year after year.

Our Philosophy

Sustainable Practices, Beautiful Results

We're committed to creating landscapes that are not only beautiful but also environmentally responsible and sustainable for the long term.

Native Plants

We prioritize native and adapted plants that thrive in our climate, require less water, and support local wildlife and pollinators.

Water-Wise Design

Smart irrigation systems, drought-tolerant plantings, and water-efficient practices that conserve resources and reduce costs.

Organic Methods

We use organic fertilizers, integrated pest management, and sustainable lawn care practices to minimize environmental impact.

Quality Materials

Premium quality plants, stone, and hardscaping materials sourced from reputable suppliers ensure lasting beauty and durability.
