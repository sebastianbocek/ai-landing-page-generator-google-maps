Irrigation Services

Irrigation Systems

Efficient irrigation systems that keep your landscape healthy while conserving water and reducing costs. We design, install, and maintain systems tailored to your property.

Services We Offer:

  • Custom system design and installation
  • Smart controller installation and programming
  • Zone optimization for plant needs
  • Drip irrigation for gardens and beds
  • Sprinkler head replacement and repairs
  • Winterization and spring start-up
  • System audits and water efficiency upgrades
  • Rain sensors and weather-based controllers

Installation Pricing: New system installations start at $2,500 for small residential properties. Smart controller upgrades start at $400.
