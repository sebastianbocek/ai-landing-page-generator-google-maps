About Spotless

Our mission is simple: give you back your time by taking cleaning off your plate.

Our Story

Started with a Simple Question

In 2018, our founder Emma was juggling a demanding career and two young kids. One evening, after yet another weekend spent scrubbing instead of spending time with family, she asked herself: "Why is it so hard to find reliable cleaning help?"

The cleaning services she tried were inconsistent. Different people each time. Missed corners. Unclear pricing. Emma knew there had to be a better way.

Spotless Home Cleaning was born from that frustration. We built a company around the things that matter most: consistent teams you can trust, transparent pricing you can count on, and quality you can see. Today, we clean over 400 homes each week, and we still obsess over every detail.

How We Work

Our Cleaning Process

A proven system that ensures consistent, thorough results every time.

Vetted Teams

Every cleaner passes background checks, reference verification, and in-person interviews. Then they complete 40 hours of training before their first solo job.

Detailed Checklists

Our 50+ point cleaning checklist ensures nothing gets missed. Each room has specific tasks, and cleaners digitally confirm completion. No guesswork involved.

Quality Checks

Random inspections, photo documentation, and customer feedback loops. When we spot an issue, we address it immediately. That's how we maintain our 4.9-star rating.

Our Products

Safe, Effective, Eco-Friendly

We bring all our own cleaning supplies and equipment. No need to stock up before we arrive. Our standard supplies are professional-grade products that are effective on dirt and grime while being safe for your home.

Want to go greener? Our eco-friendly option uses plant-based, biodegradable products that are free from harsh chemicals. Perfect for homes with young children, pets, or anyone who prefers a gentler approach. Same great clean, easier on the planet.

If you have specific products you prefer, just let us know. We're happy to use your supplies if that's what makes you comfortable.
