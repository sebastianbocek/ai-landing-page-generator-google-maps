FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

What's included in a standard cleaning?

Our standard cleaning covers all living areas: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen surfaces, bathroom sanitization, and bed making. Check our Services page for the full list.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No! We bring all our own professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you prefer we use your products, just let us know. We also offer eco-friendly options for an additional $15.

Do I need to be home during cleaning?

Nope. Most clients give us a key, door code, or use a lockbox. We're fully insured and bonded, and every team member passes background checks.

What about pets?

We love pets! Just let us know what to expect. If your pet is anxious around strangers, it might help to keep them in a separate room. We're happy to work around their needs.

How do I pay and when?

We accept all major credit cards. Payment is processed after your cleaning is complete. No cash needed, and you can save a card on file for recurring services.

What's your cancellation policy?

We understand plans change. Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment at no charge. Less than 24 hours notice incurs a $50 fee.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Let us know within 24 hours and we'll send a team back to make it right, free of charge. That's our satisfaction guarantee. We want you to love coming home.

How long does a cleaning take?

It depends on your home size and service type. A standard cleaning for a 3-bedroom home typically takes 2-3 hours. Deep cleans take longer. We'll give you an estimate when you book.

Questions? We're Here to Help

Call us directly or send a message. We respond within 2 hours during business hours.
