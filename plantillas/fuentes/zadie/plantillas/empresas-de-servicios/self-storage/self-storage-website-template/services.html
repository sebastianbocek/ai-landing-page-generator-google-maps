Storage Tips

Get the Most Out of Your Unit

A few tips from our team to help you pack smarter and keep everything in great condition.

01

Use Uniform Box Sizes

Same-size boxes stack cleanly and use vertical space much better than a mix of random containers. Medium boxes (18x18x16) are the sweet spot.

02

Create an Aisle

Leave a walkway down the center of your unit so you can reach items in the back without unpacking everything in front.

03

Label Everything

Label boxes on at least two sides with contents and which room they belong to. You'll thank yourself when you need to find something six months later.

04

Protect Upholstery

Cover couches and mattresses with moving blankets or plastic covers. Even in a clean facility, dust settles over time.

05

Store Heavy Items Low

Put the heaviest boxes and furniture on the bottom. Stack lighter items on top. This prevents crushing and makes the stack more stable.

06

Don't Store Perishables

No food, plants, or anything organic. These attract pests and can cause odor problems. If it can spoil, keep it out of storage.
