The Technology

Why We Use Pure Water

Most window cleaners use squeegees and soapy water. That works fine on the first pass, but soap leaves a microscopic film on the glass. Dust sticks to that film. Your windows look dirty again within weeks.

We use a multi-stage filtration system that reduces water to 0 parts per million of dissolved solids. This is called pure water, and it behaves differently from tap water.

Pure water is chemically "hungry." It wants to bond with something. When it contacts a dirty window, it attracts and lifts contaminants. As it dries, it takes those contaminants with it. Nothing is left on the glass when it evaporates. No residue. No water spots. No film.

No Chemical Detergents

Zero soap, surfactants, or cleaning agents required on standard jobs. Better for your landscaping, better for storm drains.

Stays Clean Longer

Without a soap film, there's nothing for dust to stick to. Windows treated with pure water stay cleaner 30-50% longer than traditionally cleaned glass.

Safe for All Glass Types

No abrasives. No harsh chemicals. Safe for coated glass, Low-E windows, tinted glass, and solar panels.

How the System Works

1
Sediment Pre-Filter Removes large particles, rust, and sediment from the source water.
2
Carbon Block Filter Eliminates chlorine, chloramine, and organic compounds.
3
Reverse Osmosis Membrane Removes 95-99% of dissolved solids, minerals, and metals.
4
Deionization Resin Final polish stage. Output measures 0 TDS (totally dissolved solids).
5
Water-Fed Pole Delivery Pure water pumped through a carbon fiber pole with a brush head to the glass surface.
We check TDS levels before every job to confirm the water is properly filtered.
