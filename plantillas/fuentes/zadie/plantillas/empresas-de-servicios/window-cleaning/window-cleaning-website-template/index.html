Why Crystal View

We Do This One Thing Well

Window cleaning is all we do. That focus means better equipment, better training, and better results.

Pure Water. No Residue.

Our water is filtered to 0 parts per million through a multi-stage process including reverse osmosis and deionization. Pure water attracts and holds dirt particles as it rinses. When it dries, nothing is left behind. No soap. No streaks. No smudges.

WCR Certified Technician

Mike is certified through the Window Cleaning Resource (WCR), the industry's leading professional organization. Certification covers safe ladder practices, water-fed pole operation, rope access safety, and proper chemical handling for specialty glass.

Fully Insured. No Exceptions.

We carry full general liability and workers' compensation insurance on every job. You can request a certificate of insurance at any time. We won't pressure you to skip that step. Proper insurance protects you, your property, and everyone on the crew.

Genuinely Eco-Friendly

Pure water cleaning uses no surfactants, no chemical detergents, and no harsh cleaners on standard jobs. Less chemical runoff into storm drains. Better for landscaping around your home. For specialty situations we select the safest effective option available.
