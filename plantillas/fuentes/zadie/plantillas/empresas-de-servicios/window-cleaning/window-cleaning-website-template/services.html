Solar

Solar Panel Cleaning

Dirty solar panels underperform. Dust, bird droppings, pollen, and mineral deposits create a film that blocks light from reaching the photovoltaic cells. Studies show soiled panels can lose 15-25% of their output.

We clean solar panels using pure water only. No brushes with hard bristles. No chemical detergents. Pure water is safe for all panel types and leaves no residue that could attract more dust.

What's Included

  • Pure water rinse and brush clean of panel surface
  • Frame and edge cleaning
  • Visual inspection for cracked panels or loose mounts
  • No abrasives or chemical detergents
  • Safe for all major panel manufacturers
Recommended 2-4 times per year From $99 for residential arrays

Gutters

Gutter Cleaning

Blocked gutters cause water to back up and overflow. In Seattle's wet climate, that means foundation issues, fascia rot, and landscape damage. We clean gutters thoroughly and flush the downspouts to confirm they're flowing freely.

After cleaning, we do a visual inspection and let you know if we spot anything that warrants attention: sagging sections, holes, or loose hangers. We don't repair, but we'll tell you what we found.

What's Included

  • Removal of all debris by hand and blower
  • Downspout flush to confirm clear flow
  • Minor clogs in downspouts cleared
  • Visual inspection of gutter condition
  • Debris bagged and removed from property
Recommended twice yearly From $129 for single-story homes

Screens

Screen Cleaning & Repair

Window screens trap dust, pollen, and debris. Dirty screens block light and push contaminants onto your freshly cleaned glass. We clean every screen as part of our standard residential service, and we offer standalone screen services when needed.

For damaged screens, we offer reframing and re-screening on-site using mesh stock we carry on the van. New screens can often be made the same visit.

What's Included

  • Screen removal, wash, and reinstallation
  • Both sides of each screen cleaned
  • Frame straightening for minor bends
  • Re-screening for torn or perforated mesh
  • Spline replacement if worn
Included with window cleaning visits Re-screening from $20 per screen

Pressure Washing

Pressure Washing

Our pressure washing service handles driveways, walkways, patios, decks, fences, and exterior siding. We use variable pressure equipment adjusted to each surface type — high pressure for concrete, lower for wood and painted surfaces.

Seattle's climate creates ideal conditions for moss, algae, and lichen growth on hard surfaces. Our soft-wash approach on siding and decks removes biological growth without risking surface damage or paint stripping.

What's Included

  • Driveways and parking areas
  • Walkways and patios
  • Wood and composite decks
  • Exterior siding (soft wash)
  • Fences and garden walls
