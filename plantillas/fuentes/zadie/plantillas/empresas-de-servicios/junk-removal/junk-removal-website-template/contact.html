FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the things people ask us most.

How quickly can you pick up my junk?

Most jobs get done same-day or next-day. If you book before noon, we can usually be there the same afternoon. For larger estate or commercial jobs, we may schedule within 48 hours.

Do I need to be home during the pickup?

Not necessarily. As long as we can access the items safely, we can handle the job. Many customers leave items in the garage or driveway. Just let us know the plan when you book.

How does your pricing work?

We charge based on how much space your items take up in our truck. We give you the price before we start, and that's what you pay. No hidden fees for labor, loading, or disposal.

What happens to my stuff after you take it?

We sort every load. Usable items go to local charities. Recyclable materials go to certified recyclers. Only items that truly can't be reused or recycled go to the landfill. Over 60% of what we haul gets diverted.

Do you move items from inside the house?

Absolutely. We come inside, carry items out, load them up, and sweep up after ourselves. You don't need to move anything to the curb. Just point at what goes and we handle the rest.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. ClearOut Hauling Co. is fully licensed and carries general liability insurance and workers' compensation for our entire crew. We can provide proof of insurance on request.

Still Have Questions?

Give us a call. We're happy to talk through your project, give you a ballpark estimate, or answer anything we missed above.
