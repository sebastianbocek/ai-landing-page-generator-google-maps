Item Guide

What We Take (and What We Don't)

We haul almost everything, but a few items require specialized handling that we can't provide.

We Take

  • Furniture (couches, tables, chairs, dressers)
  • Mattresses and box springs
  • Appliances (fridges, washers, dryers, stoves)
  • Electronics and TVs
  • Yard waste (branches, stumps, dirt)
  • Construction debris (drywall, lumber, tile)
  • Carpeting and flooring
  • Hot tubs and play sets
  • Exercise equipment
  • General household clutter

We Don't Take

  • Hazardous chemicals and solvents
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Medical waste and sharps
  • Paint cans (unless dried out)
  • Propane tanks
  • Automotive fluids and batteries
  • Ammunition and firearms
  • Biological waste
