About GuardLine

Science-based pest management, built on 15 years of protecting homes and families in our community.

Our Story

Built on Better Pest Management

GuardLine Pest Solutions was founded in 2010 by Jason Ward, a certified entomologist who spent a decade watching the pest control industry rely on outdated, heavy-chemical approaches that often created more problems than they solved.

Jason believed homeowners deserved a smarter option. One rooted in science, focused on prevention, and transparent about every product used in their home. He started GuardLine with a truck, a state license, and a commitment to Integrated Pest Management.

Fifteen years later, that commitment hasn't changed. We've grown from a one-person operation to a team of certified technicians serving over 10,000 homes across a 50-mile radius. But we still treat every home like it's our own.

Our Approach

Integrated Pest Management

We don't just spray and pray. IPM is a systematic, science-based approach that minimizes risk while maximizing results.

Identify First

Proper identification determines the right solution. We never treat until we know exactly what we're dealing with and why it's there.

Prevention Focus

Sealing entry points, eliminating attractants, and modifying habitats. The best pest control stops problems before they start.

Minimal Chemistry

Targeted application of EPA-approved products only when necessary. We use the least amount of chemical needed for effective results.
