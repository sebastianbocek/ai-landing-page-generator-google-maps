FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your treatments safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use EPA-registered products applied according to strict safety protocols. Most treatments are dry within 30 minutes and safe for re-entry. We'll provide specific instructions for your situation, and we always offer pet-safe and organic options when requested.

How long does a typical treatment take?

A standard treatment takes 30-60 minutes depending on the size of your home and the type of pest. Specialized treatments like termite barriers or bed bug heat treatments can take 4-8 hours. We'll give you an accurate estimate before we start.

Do I need to leave the house during treatment?

For most standard treatments, no. We use targeted application methods that are safe for occupied homes. For bed bug heat treatments and certain fumigation services, we'll ask you to vacate for a specific period and will let you know in advance.

What's your satisfaction guarantee?

If pests return between scheduled treatments, we come back and re-treat at no additional cost. For one-time services, our guarantee covers 60-90 days depending on the treatment. If you're not satisfied, we make it right.

How often should I schedule treatments?

For ongoing protection, we recommend quarterly treatments at minimum. Homes with a history of pest issues or in high-activity areas benefit from bi-monthly service. Our annual plans include regular treatments on a schedule that fits your needs.

Do you offer organic or green options?

Absolutely. We offer botanical-based and organic treatment options for customers who prefer them. These products are effective for many common pest situations. During your inspection, we'll discuss which approach makes the most sense for your specific problem.
