About Swift Haul

A family-owned moving company built on hard work, honest pricing, and the belief that your belongings deserve professional care.

Our Story

Started with One Truck and a Handshake

In 2013, Mike Callahan bought a used box truck and started helping neighbors move on weekends. The work was honest, the prices were fair, and word spread fast. Within a year, he had three trucks and a small crew of movers who shared his standards.

Today, Swift Haul operates a fleet of 24 trucks and employs over 60 trained professionals. We have completed more than 15,000 moves across the region. The company has grown, but the approach has not changed. Every move gets the same attention, whether it is a studio apartment or a corporate headquarters.

We are still a family business. Mike runs operations. His wife Sarah manages customer relations. Their focus is simple: show up on time, handle everything with care, and charge a fair price. That formula has worked for 12 years and counting.

Our Values

What Drives Us

Respect

We respect your home, your belongings, and your time. Every crew member treats your property as if it were their own. That means blanket-wrapping furniture, wearing shoe covers, and never cutting corners.

Honesty

Our quotes are straightforward and detailed. No bait-and-switch tactics, no inflated estimates, no hidden surcharges. We earn trust through transparency, and that starts with the very first phone call.

Efficiency

Moving is stressful enough without wasting your time. Our crews are trained to work quickly without sacrificing care. We plan routes, pre-stage equipment, and keep communication constant throughout your move.
