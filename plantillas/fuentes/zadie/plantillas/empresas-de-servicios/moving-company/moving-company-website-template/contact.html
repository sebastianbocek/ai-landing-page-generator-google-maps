FAQ

Common Questions

How accurate are your estimates?

Our estimates are based on an in-home or virtual walkthrough of your belongings. We price by the job, not by the hour. The price we quote is the price you pay unless you add items after the estimate.

Are my belongings insured during the move?

Yes. Every move includes basic liability coverage at no extra cost. We also offer Full Value Protection for an additional fee, which covers repair or replacement at current market value.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks ahead, especially for summer and month-end moves. However, we do offer same-day and next-day service for urgent situations when crews are available.

Do I need to be present during the move?

Someone over 18 needs to be present at both the origin and destination to authorize the move and sign off on the inventory. This can be a friend or family member if you are unavailable.

What happens if something gets damaged?

It rarely happens, but we handle it immediately. Report damage within 24 hours and we will file a claim, schedule a repair, or issue a replacement. Our 99.2% damage-free rate speaks for itself.

Do you move items into storage?

Absolutely. We can move your items directly to our climate-controlled storage facility, or to any third-party storage unit. We handle the logistics so you do not have to make multiple trips.
