Our Cleaning Services

From residential carpets to commercial tile floors, we have the tools and expertise to make every surface look its best.

Residential

Residential Carpet Cleaning

Our residential carpet cleaning service uses truck-mounted hot water extraction -- the method recommended by all major carpet manufacturers -- to remove embedded dirt, allergens, pet dander, and bacteria from deep within your carpet fibers. For delicate or moisture-sensitive carpets, we also offer low-moisture cleaning that dries in as little as two hours.

Whether you're dealing with high-traffic wear, stubborn pet stains, or just want to refresh your home for the season, our technicians will customize the approach to your carpet type and your specific concerns.

What's Included

  • Pre-inspection and fiber identification
  • Furniture moving (standard items)
  • Pre-treatment of high-traffic areas
  • Hot water extraction deep clean
  • Spot treatment for stains
  • Post-cleaning grooming and speed drying
  • Final walk-through with homeowner
Starting at $35/room | Whole house packages from $199

Commercial Carpet Cleaning

First impressions matter. Dirty, worn carpets send the wrong message to your customers and employees. Our commercial carpet cleaning service is designed for offices, restaurants, retail spaces, and multi-unit properties that need reliable, thorough cleaning on a schedule that works with their business hours.

We offer after-hours and weekend service so your business stays open while we work. Our low-moisture commercial cleaning method means carpets are dry and ready for foot traffic within hours, not days.

  • Flexible scheduling (evenings and weekends)
  • Recurring maintenance programs available

Upholstery

Upholstery Cleaning

Your sofa, armchairs, and mattresses collect dust, body oils, pet dander, and allergens every single day. Regular upholstery cleaning extends the life of your furniture and creates a healthier living environment for your family.

We clean all fabric types, including microfiber, cotton, linen, and synthetic blends. Our technicians always test a hidden area first and adjust their approach based on the manufacturer's cleaning code. We also clean auto interiors -- seats, headliners, and floor mats.

What's Included

  • Fabric type identification and pre-testing
  • Vacuuming and dry soil removal
  • Pre-treatment of stains and high-use areas
  • Hot water extraction or dry cleaning (based on fabric)
  • Deodorizing treatment
  • Speed drying with air movers
Sofas from $99 | Chairs from $49 | Mattresses from $79
Tile & Grout

Tile & Grout Cleaning

Mopping only cleans the surface. Over time, grout lines absorb dirt, grease, and bacteria that regular cleaning can't reach. Our high-pressure tile and grout cleaning service restores the original color and brightness of your floors, backsplashes, and shower walls.

We work with ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and quarry tile in kitchens, bathrooms, entryways, and commercial spaces. After cleaning, we can apply a professional-grade sealant that protects your grout from future staining for up to two years.

What's Included

  • Tile and grout inspection
  • Alkaline pre-treatment of grout lines
  • High-pressure hot water extraction
  • Detail brushing of stubborn areas
  • Optional grout sealing (recommended)
  • Streak-free rinse and dry
Starting at $1.50/sq ft | Grout sealing from $0.75/sq ft
