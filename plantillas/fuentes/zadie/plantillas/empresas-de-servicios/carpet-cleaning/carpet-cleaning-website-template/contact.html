Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from homeowners and business managers.

Most carpets are dry within 4 to 8 hours after hot water extraction cleaning. Drying time depends on humidity, carpet thickness, and air circulation. We leave air movers running when possible and recommend keeping windows open or fans on to speed things up. Our low-moisture cleaning method dries in as little as 1 to 2 hours.

Absolutely. We use plant-based, non-toxic cleaning solutions that are EPA Safer Choice certified. There are no harsh chemicals, no strong fumes, and no residues that could harm children or pets. Once the carpet is dry, it's completely safe for everyone -- including crawling babies and curious dogs.

Most carpet manufacturers recommend professional cleaning every 12 to 18 months to maintain your warranty and keep carpets in good condition. If you have pets, young children, allergies, or heavy foot traffic, we recommend every 6 to 12 months. Regular cleaning extends the life of your carpet and improves indoor air quality.

Yes, we move standard furniture like dining chairs, small tables, and lightweight items at no extra charge. For larger or heavier pieces -- like sectional sofas, beds, and entertainment centers -- we clean around and under them as much as possible. If you'd like us to move heavy furniture, just let us know ahead of time and we can arrange it.

We treat tough stains with specialized pre-treatment solutions before the main cleaning. Most stains come out completely. For older or set-in stains (like pet urine that has soaked into the padding), we'll be upfront about what to expect. In some cases, we may recommend enzyme treatments or additional visits. We'll never promise results we can't deliver.
