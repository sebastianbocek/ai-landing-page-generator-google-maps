How It Works

Our 4-Step Cleaning Process

A thorough, systematic approach that delivers consistently outstanding results every time.

1

Inspection

We start with a full walkthrough of your home, identifying problem areas, stain types, and carpet fiber to determine the best cleaning approach.

2

Pre-Treatment

High-traffic areas and tough stains get a targeted pre-treatment solution that breaks down dirt and grime before the deep clean begins.

3

Deep Clean

Using truck-mounted hot water extraction, we deep clean every fiber, pulling out embedded dirt, allergens, and bacteria from the base of your carpet.

4

Final Walk-Through

We walk through every room with you, making sure you're 100% satisfied with the results. Any spots that need extra attention get treated on the spot.
