About FreshFiber Carpet Care

A family-owned company built on clean carpets, honest work, and a genuine care for the homes we serve.

Our Story

How a Family Need Became a Family Business

FreshFiber Carpet Care started in 2010, when husband-and-wife team Marcus and Elena Rivera noticed their youngest son's allergies were getting worse every spring. After having their carpets professionally cleaned by a local company, they were disappointed by the results -- and concerned about the chemical smell left behind.

Marcus, who had spent a decade in facilities management, decided to learn everything he could about carpet cleaning science. He earned his IICRC certification, invested in a truck-mounted hot water extraction system, and started cleaning carpets for friends and neighbors using plant-based, non-toxic products.

Word spread quickly. Within two years, FreshFiber had a full client roster and a growing reputation for thorough work and honest pricing. Today, we've cleaned over 10,000 homes across northern New Jersey, and we still approach every job the same way -- as if we were cleaning our own home.
