Our Story

Built in Chicago. Doing it right.

We started with two trucks and a handshake deal. Fourteen years later, we're still the same company — just bigger, faster, and with a lot more satisfied customers behind us.

From two trucks to Chicago's go-to.

BinDrop was started in 2011 by brothers Marco and Denny Reyes, both lifelong Chicagoans who grew up watching their father haul scrap on weekends to put food on the table. They understood how messy and expensive renovation cleanup could get, and how badly people just needed someone to show up and handle it.

They bought their first roll-off truck used out of Gary, Indiana, painted it yellow, and started knocking on doors in Logan Square and Pilsen. Word spread fast. They were on time, they charged exactly what they quoted, and they didn't leave a mess behind. Simple things that turned out to be rare.

Today BinDrop operates a full fleet of trucks and hundreds of dumpsters across the Chicago metro. Marco runs operations, Denny handles the driver team, and they still take calls themselves when things get busy. It's still a family business. That part hasn't changed.

Why BinDrop

We do what we say we'll do.

We're not the only dumpster company in Chicago. But we like to think we're the most reliable one.

Same-Day Delivery

Order by 10am and we'll have a dumpster in your driveway the same day. No waiting a week for a confirmation that never comes. We pick up the phone and we show up.

Transparent, Flat Pricing

The price we quote is the price you pay. No fuel surcharges tacked on at the last minute. No "environmental fees." No gotchas when we come to pick it up.

Extended Rentals Available

Standard rentals are one week. But renovation projects don't always stay on schedule. We offer extended rental periods at fair daily rates — just let us know upfront.

Clean Placement

We use rubber track protectors on driveways, spot the bin exactly where you want it, and sweep up before we leave. Your driveway looks the same when we're done.

Local Knowledge

We know Chicago's permit requirements, neighborhood setback rules, and alley widths. If you need a street permit, we'll tell you exactly what to do — or pull it ourselves.

Proper Disposal

Everything we haul goes to licensed transfer stations and recycling facilities. We divert material from landfills where we can. Doing it the right way matters to us.
