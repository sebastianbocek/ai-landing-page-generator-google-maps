What Can Go In

Know before you throw.

Most common renovation and cleanout debris is fine. Here's a quick breakdown.

Accepted Materials

  • Drywall and plaster
  • Wood, lumber, and trim
  • Carpet and flooring
  • Furniture and mattresses
  • Household junk and clutter
  • Yard waste and brush
  • Roofing shingles
  • Metal and scrap
  • Packaging and cardboard
  • Windows and doors

Not Accepted

  • Hazardous waste (paint, solvents)
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Tires (any quantity)
  • Electronics and TVs
  • Propane tanks
  • Batteries (automotive or industrial)
  • Medical waste
  • Liquids of any kind
  • Freon-containing appliances
  • Railroad ties

Call Us First

  • Concrete and brick
  • Dirt and soil
  • Asphalt
  • Heavy stone or tile
  • Mixed heavy debris
  • Appliances (with Freon removed)

Heavy materials like concrete require a specialty dumpster. We have dedicated concrete/dirt bins available.
