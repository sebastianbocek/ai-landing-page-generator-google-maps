Additional Charges

The only extras you'll see.

We keep it simple. Here are the only situations where your bill goes above the quoted price.

Overage Weight

$65 / ton

Each size includes a weight allowance. If you exceed it, we charge $65 per additional ton — weighed at the transfer station. We'll call you before pickup if it looks close.

Extended Rental

$15 / day

Standard rentals are 7 days. Need more time? No problem. We charge $15 per additional day. Just give us a call before the pickup date — we'll extend it automatically.

Non-Approved Items

Varies

If prohibited items are found at the transfer station, you'll be charged a removal fee based on the disposal cost. Common offenders: tires, appliances with Freon, propane tanks.

Know the Rules

Prohibited items.

These items cannot go in any of our standard dumpsters. Putting prohibited materials in a bin can result in additional fees and delays.

Hazardous Materials

  • Paint (liquid or dried)
  • Solvents and thinners
  • Motor oil and fluids
  • Pesticides and herbicides
  • Propane or fuel tanks
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Medical or biohazard waste
  • Radioactive materials

Electronics & Appliances

  • TVs and monitors
  • Computers and printers
  • Refrigerators or AC units (Freon)
  • Freezers
  • Dehumidifiers
  • Fluorescent light bulbs
  • Batteries (automotive size)

Other Restricted Items

  • Tires (any size or quantity)
  • Railroad ties
  • Liquids of any kind
  • Food waste
  • Ammunition or firearms
  • Concrete over weight limit
  • Contaminated soil

Not sure about something? Call us before you toss it. We'd rather answer a quick question than have you charged a disposal fee at the other end. (773) 555-0149.

Street Permits

Do you need a permit?

If the dumpster fits on your private property (driveway, side yard), you typically don't need a permit. If it goes on a public street or alley, you'll need one.

Chicago permit costs vary by ward and container size but generally run $75–$175. Permits are required for placements on public ROW and must be displayed on the container. We can pull the permit for you — just ask when you order.
