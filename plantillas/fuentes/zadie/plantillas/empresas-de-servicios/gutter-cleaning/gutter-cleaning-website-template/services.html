Services & Pricing

Transparent pricing for every gutter service. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Gutter Cleaning

Our most popular service

A full gutter cleaning removes all leaves, moss, shingle grit, and debris from your gutters and downspouts. We hand-scoop every section, flush the entire system with water, and check for leaks, sagging, or damage. Every job includes before-and-after photos sent directly to your phone or email.

Home Type Price Range
Single-story home (up to 150 linear ft) $125 - $175
Two-story home (up to 200 linear ft) $175 - $275
Three-story home or complex roofline $275 - $400

Pricing varies based on total gutter length, roof access, and debris levels. We provide an exact quote after inspecting your home.

Gutter Guard Installation

Stop cleaning gutters for good

Gutter guards prevent leaves, pine needles, and debris from entering your gutters while allowing water to flow through freely. We install micro-mesh and surface-tension guard systems that work with your existing gutters. Installation includes a full gutter cleaning before the guards go on.

Guard Type Price Per Linear Foot
Micro-mesh gutter guards (standard) $15 - $22/ft
Premium surface-tension guards $22 - $30/ft

Most homes have 120-200 linear feet of gutters. We will measure your home and provide an exact quote. Gutter guards come with a manufacturer warranty.

Gutter Repair

Fix leaks, sags, and damage before they get worse

Leaking joints, sagging sections, and disconnected downspouts are common issues in the Portland area. Heavy rain and moss growth put constant stress on gutter systems. We repair all types of gutter problems and can typically handle repairs on the same visit as a cleaning.

Repair Type Price Range
Joint patch and sealant repair $75 - $150
Section replacement (up to 10 ft) $150 - $350
Downspout rerouting or extension $100 - $250

We identify repair needs during every gutter cleaning and let you know before doing any additional work. No surprises on your bill.

New Gutter Installation

Custom-fitted seamless gutters for your home

If your gutters are beyond repair or you are building a new addition, we install custom seamless gutters that are fabricated on-site to fit your home perfectly. Seamless gutters have fewer joints, which means fewer leaks and less maintenance over the life of the system.

Material Price Per Linear Foot
Aluminum (5" or 6" seamless) $8 - $12/ft
Copper (half-round or K-style) $25 - $40/ft

Aluminum is the most popular choice for Portland homes. Copper is ideal for historic or high-end properties. Both come with a 20-year warranty on materials and labor.

Downspout & Drainage Solutions

Direct water away from your foundation

Even clean gutters can cause problems if your downspouts dump water too close to your foundation. We install extensions, underground drain lines, and French drain connections to move water safely away from your home. This is one of the most effective things you can do to prevent basement moisture and foundation settling.

Service Price Range
Downspout extensions (surface) $50 - $100 each
Underground drain line (per downspout) $200 - $500
French drain connection $350 - $750

Roof Debris Removal

Add-on service with any gutter cleaning

Moss, fallen branches, and leaf buildup on your roof eventually end up in your gutters. We can clear debris from valleys, around chimneys, and along the roof edge while we are up there cleaning your gutters. This helps your gutters stay cleaner longer and protects your shingles from moss damage.

Service Price Range
Roof debris removal (add-on to gutter cleaning) $75 - $200

Price depends on roof size and amount of debris. This service is only available as an add-on to a gutter cleaning appointment.
