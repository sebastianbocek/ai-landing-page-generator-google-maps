About StormGuard Gutters

12+ years of protecting Portland homes from water damage. Meet the team behind the gutters.

Our Story

StormGuard Gutters was founded in 2013 by Ryan Mitchell after he watched a neighbor deal with thousands of dollars in water damage from neglected gutters. The fascia boards were rotted, water had seeped into the foundation, and mold was growing behind the siding. All of it could have been prevented with a simple gutter cleaning.

Ryan had been working in construction and roofing for years, but that experience changed his focus. He realized that most Portland homeowners did not think about their gutters until it was too late. So he started StormGuard with a simple mission: keep Portland homes dry and protected, one gutter at a time.

What started as a one-person operation with a truck and a ladder has grown into a three-person crew that has serviced over 8,000 homes across the Portland metro area. We still operate with the same philosophy: show up on time, do thorough work, document everything, and charge a fair price.

Our Safety Commitment

Gutter work means ladders, rooftops, and heights. We take safety seriously on every job.

Fall Protection Harnesses

Every crew member wears a full-body harness when working on two-story homes and above. No exceptions, no shortcuts.

Full Insurance Coverage

We carry comprehensive general liability and workers' compensation insurance. If anything happens on your property, you are fully covered.

Ladder Safety Protocols

We use commercial-grade ladders with stabilizers and standoffs to protect your gutters, roof, and siding during every service call.

Ongoing Training

Our team completes annual safety training and stays current on OSHA guidelines for working at heights. Safety is built into how we operate.
