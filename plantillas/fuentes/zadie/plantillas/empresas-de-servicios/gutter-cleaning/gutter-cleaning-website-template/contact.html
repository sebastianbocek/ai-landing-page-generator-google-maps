Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from Portland homeowners.

In the Portland area, we recommend cleaning your gutters at least twice a year: once in late spring after the pollen and blossoms fall, and once in late fall after the leaves drop. If you have large trees overhanging your roof, such as maples, firs, or oaks, you may need three cleanings per year. Our maintenance plans are designed around these seasonal needs.
Most single-story homes take about 45 minutes to 1 hour. Two-story homes typically take 1 to 1.5 hours, depending on the amount of debris and the complexity of the roofline. We always take the time to do the job thoroughly rather than rushing through it.
No, you do not need to be home. As long as we can access the exterior of your home and there are no locked gates blocking our path, we can complete the service while you are away. We will send you before-and-after photos and a summary of what we found and cleaned when the job is done.
Gutter guards dramatically reduce the amount of debris that enters your gutters, which means less frequent cleanings and fewer clogs. They are especially useful if you have pine trees or heavy leaf cover. While no guard system is 100% maintenance-free, most homeowners with gutter guards go from two cleanings per year to one or none. They also help prevent ice dams in the winter and reduce the chance of animals nesting in your gutters.

Workers' compensation covers our crew in case of injury on the job. We are happy to provide a certificate of insurance upon request. You should never hire a gutter company that does not carry both.
