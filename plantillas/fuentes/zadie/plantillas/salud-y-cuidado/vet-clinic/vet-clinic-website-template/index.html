Our Team

Meet Our Veterinarians

Our caring, experienced veterinarians are dedicated to providing the highest quality medical care for your beloved pets.

Dr. Sarah Martinez, DVM

Chief Veterinarian

Dr. Martinez founded Paws & Claws in 2015 with a vision of compassionate, fear-free veterinary care. She specializes in internal medicine and has 15 years of experience treating dogs, cats, and exotic pets.

Dr. James Chen, DVM

Senior Veterinarian

With expertise in surgery and emergency medicine, Dr. Chen brings 12 years of experience to our team. He's known for his gentle approach with nervous pets and his skill in complex surgical procedures.

Dr. Emily Rodriguez, DVM

Associate Veterinarian

Dr. Rodriguez specializes in avian and exotic animal medicine. She completed advanced training in zoo medicine and brings unique expertise in caring for birds, reptiles, and small mammals.
