Our Story

Where Wellness Begins

For over 15 years, Serenity Wellness Spa has been a haven for those seeking respite from the demands of daily life. Our mission is simple: to help you feel your best.

Founded on a Simple Belief

Serenity was born from founder Maria Santos' conviction that massage therapy shouldn't be a luxury reserved for special occasions. After two decades as a physical therapist, she saw firsthand how regular bodywork transformed her patients' lives.

In 2009, Maria opened Serenity in a small storefront with just two treatment rooms and a vision: create a space where anyone could access professional massage therapy in an environment that felt like an escape. No sterile clinic vibes. No rushed appointments. Just genuine care in a beautiful setting.

Today, we've grown to six treatment rooms and a team of eight therapists, but our philosophy remains unchanged. Every guest is welcomed by name. Every treatment is tailored. Every visit should leave you feeling restored.

Maria Santos, Founder
