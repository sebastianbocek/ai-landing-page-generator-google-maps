How We Train

The Good Form Philosophy

Training works when dogs understand what's expected and feel good about doing it. We build that foundation every session.

Science-Based Methods

Everything we do is grounded in behavioral science. We use positive reinforcement and clear markers to build behaviors that stick. No punishment, no dominance theory, no outdated pack concepts.

Consistency That Transfers

A dog that performs in class but falls apart at home isn't trained. We coach you as much as your dog, building handling skills and proofing behaviors across real-world environments.

Small Classes, Real Attention

We cap group classes at 6 dogs. That's intentional. Every team gets personalized coaching every session, not a demonstration you watch from the back of a crowded room.
