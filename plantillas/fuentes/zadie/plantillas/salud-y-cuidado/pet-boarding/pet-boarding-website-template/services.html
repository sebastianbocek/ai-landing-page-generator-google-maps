Holiday Pricing Notice

During peak holiday periods (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's, July 4th, and Memorial Day weekend), all boarding and daycare rates increase by $10/night or day. Holiday weeks fill up fast. We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks in advance for any major holiday. A deposit of one night's stay is required to hold holiday reservations.
