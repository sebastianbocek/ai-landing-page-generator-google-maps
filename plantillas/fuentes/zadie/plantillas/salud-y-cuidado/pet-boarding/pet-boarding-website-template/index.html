The Lodge

A facility built for pets first

We designed every inch of this place with animal comfort and safety in mind — not just convenience for us.

Outdoor Play Yards

Three fully fenced play yards separate dogs by size and temperament. Shade structures, water stations, and agility equipment keep them busy all day.

Webcam Access

Log in anytime from the road and check on your dog live. We update you with photos and texts at morning, noon, and night too.

Climate-Controlled Suites

Heated floors in winter, cool air in summer. Every suite maintains a comfortable temperature year-round regardless of what's happening outside.

Why Families Trust Us

We take this seriously

Your pet is family. We treat them that way — and we've built everything here to back that up.

Licensed & Fully Insured

Tennessee state licensed pet care facility, fully bonded and insured for your protection.

Emergency Vet Partnership

We have a standing relationship with Nashville Animal Care Clinic for emergency medical situations.

Vaccination Requirements

All pets must be current on core vaccines before staying. Keeps every animal in our care safe.

Staff on Site Overnight

A trained caretaker is on premises 24 hours a day, every day. Your pet is never left unattended.
