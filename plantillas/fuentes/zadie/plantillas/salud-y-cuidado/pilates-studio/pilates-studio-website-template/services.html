Intro Series for Beginners

Four classes designed to give you a genuine foundation in classical Pilates before you join regular group classes. You will learn how to use the reformer, understand the principles, and feel confident walking into any class on the schedule.

Includes: 2 semi-private reformer sessions (max 3 students) and 2 mat fundamentals classes.

Small Group

Reformer Classes

Classical reformer work in groups of no more than six students. The reformer's spring resistance can support or challenge you depending on how it is set, making this format genuinely appropriate for a wide range of abilities within one class.

$32 per class

Reformer Foundations

Classical mat-to-reformer sequence for students within their first six months of practice. Slower pacing, detailed cueing.

Reformer Level 1-2

Intermediate repertoire for students comfortable with the basic series. Increases complexity and flow between exercises.

Reformer All-Levels

The full classical reformer order. Appropriate for students who have completed the intro series or have prior Pilates experience.

Group Class

Mat Pilates

The original Pilates practice. Mat work is harder than most people expect because you supply all the resistance yourself. Technically demanding, deeply effective, and the ideal complement to reformer training.

$22 per class

Mat Fundamentals

The foundational mat sequence for students learning the work. Included in the Intro Series. Open to any student with less than three months of experience.

Classical Mat

The full classical mat order, thirty-four exercises in sequence. A complete physical system. Challenging for every level.

Mat with Props

Classical mat supplemented with the magic circle, foam roller, and small ball. Adds variety and targeted challenge.

Small Group

Tower & Cadillac

Work with the full-length trapeze table and wall-mounted tower unit for a session with enormous range. Excellent for rehabilitation, hypermobility, and students looking to explore exercises not possible on the reformer alone.

$38 per class

Tower Flow

Spring-based work on the wall tower unit. Full body, with an emphasis on spinal mobility and hip stability.

Cadillac & Trapeze

Exercises unique to the trapeze table, including hanging work and advanced spinal articulation. Max 4 students.

Rehabilitative Tower

Gentler pacing for students recovering from injury or managing chronic pain. Always taught by Sarah. Limited enrollment.

One-on-One

Private Sessions

The most direct path to progress. Private sessions are entirely personalized to your body, your goals, and your history. We recommend beginning with at least four private sessions before joining group classes if you are new to Pilates.

$85 per session

Initial Assessment

75-minute first session including postural assessment, history intake, and your first reformer and mat session.

Ongoing Private

55-minute session with access to the full apparatus. Curriculum is planned and progressed by your instructor.

Rehabilitation Sessions

For students with specific injuries or post-surgical needs. Coordinated with your physical therapist when appropriate.

Two-Person

Duet Sessions

All the personalization of a private session, shared between two students at similar levels. A good choice for couples, siblings, or friends who want to practice together without the cost of two separate privates.

$55 per person

Reformer Duet

Work side by side on adjacent reformers with individualized cues and adjustments for each person.

Mat Duet

Classical mat session for two. Particularly effective for students who learn from watching each other work.

Mixed Apparatus Duet

Full studio access for two. Your instructor will plan a session using the apparatus best suited to both students.
