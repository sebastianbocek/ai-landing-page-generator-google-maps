The Method

Classical Pilates Principles

Joseph Pilates developed his method around six foundational principles. Every class at Core Balance is built on all six.

01

Breath

Deliberate, full breathing oxygenates the body and drives movement. It is the engine behind every exercise.

02

Concentration

Complete focus on each movement brings quality that no amount of repetition can replicate.

03

Control

Every movement is executed with full muscular control. Nothing sloppy, nothing rushed, nothing wasted.

04

Centering

All movement flows from a stable, strong center. Pilates called it the powerhouse; we build it methodically.

05

Flow

Exercises move seamlessly from one to the next, creating elegance and efficiency in the body.

06

Precision

One precise repetition is worth ten careless ones. We cue the details that make the difference.

Your Instructor

Sarah Ellison

Lead Instructor & Studio Founder

Sarah discovered classical Pilates after a decade as a professional dancer. What began as physical therapy became a calling. She trained under a second-generation Pilates elder, studying the work as Romana Kryzanowska taught it, which means as close to the original method as you can get.

She opened Core Balance in 2018 with one simple conviction: small classes taught with rigor and warmth transform bodies in ways that crowded studios never can.
