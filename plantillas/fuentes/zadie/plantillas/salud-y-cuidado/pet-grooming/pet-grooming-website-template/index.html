How It Works

What to Expect at Your Appointment

1. Check-In & Consultation

We discuss your pet's needs, any health concerns, and exactly how you'd like them groomed. First-time visitors get a facility tour.

2. Pre-Groom Assessment

Gentle health check examining skin, coat condition, ears, and nails. We'll notify you of any concerns we spot before starting.

3. Grooming Service

Your pet receives one-on-one attention from a dedicated groomer. No assembly line. No rushing. Just careful, personalized care.

4. Final Touches

Finishing details like bows, bandanas, cologne spray, and a special treat. We want your pet to leave feeling pampered and happy.

5. Pickup & Report

We'll walk you through everything we did and make recommendations for at-home care between visits. Photos available upon request.

6. Schedule Next Visit

Most pets benefit from grooming every 4-8 weeks. We'll help you set up a regular schedule that works for your pet's coat type.
