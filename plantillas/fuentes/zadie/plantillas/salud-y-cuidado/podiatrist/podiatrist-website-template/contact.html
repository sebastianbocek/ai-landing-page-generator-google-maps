Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from new and existing patients.

Do I need a referral to see Dr. Patel?

No referral is needed for most insurance plans. You can call us directly or use the form above to schedule an appointment. If your plan requires a referral, our office will help coordinate with your primary care provider.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or imaging. If you wear orthotics or have been prescribed special footwear, bring those as well. Wear or bring the shoes you most commonly wear.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Humana, Medicare, and United Healthcare. Call our office to verify your specific plan before your visit, and we'll confirm your coverage and benefits.

How long is a typical appointment?

New patient visits typically last 45-60 minutes, which includes a thorough examination, any necessary imaging, and time to discuss your diagnosis and treatment plan. Follow-up visits are usually 20-30 minutes.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Dr. Patel treats patients of all ages, including children. Common pediatric conditions we see include flat feet, in-toeing, growing pains, sports injuries, and plantar warts. We make the experience comfortable for young patients.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Yes, we keep slots available each day for urgent cases like acute injuries, infections, and severe pain. Call us directly in the morning for the best chance at a same-day appointment. Walk-ins are also welcome based on availability.

Questions? We're Here to Help.

Our team is available Monday through Saturday. Call us for immediate assistance or use the form above to request an appointment online.
