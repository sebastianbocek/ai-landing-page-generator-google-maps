Our Services

Comprehensive foot and ankle care from diagnosis through recovery. Conservative treatment first, surgery only when necessary.

General Podiatry

Complete Foot Care for Every Patient

From routine nail care to complex deformity correction, our general podiatry services cover the full spectrum of foot conditions. We take the time to understand your symptoms, identify the cause, and build a treatment plan that makes sense for your situation.

Whether you're dealing with a nagging issue or a sudden injury, we're here to help.

Conditions Treated

  • Bunions and bunionettes
  • Hammertoes and claw toes
  • Corns and calluses
  • Ingrown toenails
  • Plantar warts
  • Fungal toenails (onychomycosis)

Treatment Options

  • Conservative padding and taping
  • Topical and oral medications
  • In-office nail procedures
  • Laser treatment for fungal nails
  • Minimally invasive surgery when needed
Sports Medicine

Get Back in the Game

Dr. Patel's fellowship training in sports medicine means you get expert-level care for athletic injuries. Whether you're a competitive runner, a weekend hiker, or just trying to stay active, we understand the biomechanics of movement and how to get you back to full performance.

We work with athletes of all levels to treat acute injuries, manage chronic overuse conditions, and prevent future problems.

Conditions Treated

  • Stress fractures
  • Achilles tendinitis and rupture
  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Posterior tibial tendon dysfunction
  • Turf toe and sesamoiditis
  • Shin splints and compartment syndrome

Treatment Options

  • Biomechanical analysis and gait correction
  • Custom sport-specific orthotics
  • PRP and regenerative therapy
  • Shockwave therapy
  • Bracing and taping protocols
  • Surgical repair when conservative care falls short
Diabetic Foot Care

Protecting Your Feet for the Long Run

Diabetes significantly increases your risk of foot complications, from neuropathy and poor circulation to slow-healing wounds and infection. Regular podiatric care is one of the most important steps you can take to prevent serious problems.

We partner with your primary care provider and endocrinologist to deliver coordinated, proactive foot care tailored to your needs.

Conditions Managed

  • Peripheral neuropathy
  • Diabetic foot ulcers
  • Charcot foot
  • Poor circulation and vascular assessment
  • Skin and nail changes
  • Risk stratification and preventive screening

Treatment Options

  • Comprehensive diabetic foot exams
  • Advanced wound care and debridement
  • Custom diabetic shoes and insoles
  • Neuropathy management programs
  • Patient education and self-care training
  • Referral coordination with your care team
Surgical Services

When Surgery Is the Right Answer

We always start with conservative treatment. But when your condition doesn't respond, or when the structural problem requires correction, Dr. Patel brings board-certified surgical expertise and a minimally invasive approach to get the best possible outcome.

Every surgical recommendation includes a full explanation of the procedure, recovery timeline, and expected results. No surprises.

Procedures Offered

  • Bunion correction (traditional and minimally invasive)
  • Hammertoe correction
  • Ankle ligament reconstruction
  • Achilles tendon repair
  • Fracture fixation
  • Neurectomy for Morton's neuroma

Our Surgical Approach

  • Minimally invasive techniques when appropriate
  • Outpatient same-day surgery
  • Detailed pre-operative planning
  • Structured post-operative follow-up
  • Coordinated physical therapy referrals
Custom Orthotics

Precision Support for Every Step

Off-the-shelf insoles treat the symptoms. Custom orthotics treat the cause. Using 3D scanning technology and computerized gait analysis, we design orthotic devices that correct your specific biomechanical issues and fit the way you actually move.

Whether you need everyday support, sport-specific performance orthotics, or diabetic accommodative devices, we fabricate them in-house for a precise fit.

Our Process

  • Computerized gait analysis
  • 3D foot scanning (no messy plaster casts)
  • Biomechanical assessment
  • Custom design and in-house fabrication
  • Fitting, adjustment, and follow-up

Orthotic Types

  • Functional orthotics for biomechanical correction
  • Accommodative orthotics for diabetic patients
  • Sport-specific orthotics (running, cycling, cleats)
  • Pediatric orthotics for growing feet
  • Dress shoe and work boot orthotics
Heel Pain Program

End Heel Pain for Good

Heel pain is the most common reason patients visit a podiatrist, and we've developed a structured program to resolve it efficiently. Our approach combines precise diagnosis with a multi-modal treatment plan that addresses both the symptoms and the underlying cause.

Most patients see significant improvement within 4-6 weeks. For chronic cases that haven't responded to other treatments, we offer advanced therapies like shockwave that can break through the plateau.

Conditions Addressed

  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Heel spurs
  • Achilles insertional tendinitis
  • Baxter's nerve entrapment
  • Fat pad atrophy
  • Calcaneal stress fractures

Treatment Options

  • Stretching and strengthening protocols
  • Custom orthotics with targeted arch support
  • Corticosteroid injections
  • Extracorporeal shockwave therapy (ESWT)
  • Night splints and bracing
  • MLS laser therapy
