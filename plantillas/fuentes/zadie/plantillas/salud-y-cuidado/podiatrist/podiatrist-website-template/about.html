About Stride Foot & Ankle

Meet the team behind your care. Board-certified expertise, advanced technology, and a genuine commitment to keeping you active.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Ryan Patel, DPM, FACFAS

Board Certified | Fellowship-Trained in Sports Medicine

Dr. Ryan Patel founded Stride Foot & Ankle with a clear mission: to provide the kind of podiatric care that actually gets people back to their lives. Not just managing symptoms, but resolving the root cause.

After earning his Doctor of Podiatric Medicine degree from the Temple University School of Podiatric Medicine, Dr. Patel completed a three-year surgical residency at Baylor University Medical Center in Dallas. He then pursued a fellowship in sports medicine podiatry, working with collegiate and professional athletes on complex lower-extremity injuries.

Dr. Patel is board certified by the American Board of Foot and Ankle Surgery (ABFAS) and is a Fellow of the American College of Foot and Ankle Surgeons (FACFAS). He is an active member of the American Podiatric Medical Association and regularly attends national conferences to stay current with the latest treatment advances.

When he's not in the office, Dr. Patel is an avid trail runner and cycling enthusiast. He understands firsthand what it means to rely on healthy feet and the frustration of being sidelined by an injury.

Our Technology

Advanced Tools for Precise Care

We invest in the latest diagnostic and treatment technology so you get accurate answers and effective results.

Digital X-Ray

High-resolution imaging with lower radiation exposure. Results in minutes, right in the office, for fast and accurate diagnosis.

Diagnostic Ultrasound

Real-time imaging of soft tissues, tendons, and ligaments. Ideal for identifying plantar fasciitis, tendon tears, and neuromas without radiation.

Laser Therapy

MLS laser treatment reduces pain and inflammation, speeds healing, and promotes tissue repair for chronic and acute conditions.

Custom Orthotics Lab

3D foot scanning and in-house fabrication of custom orthotic devices. Precision-molded to your foot for optimal support and correction.

Shockwave Therapy

Extracorporeal shockwave therapy (ESWT) for chronic heel pain, plantar fasciitis, and Achilles tendinitis. Non-invasive with proven results.

Gait Analysis System

Computerized pressure mapping and video gait analysis to identify biomechanical issues affecting your feet, ankles, knees, and hips.
