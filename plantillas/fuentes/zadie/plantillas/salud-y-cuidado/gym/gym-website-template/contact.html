FAQ

Common Questions

Do I Need CrossFit Experience?

No. Every class is coached and every movement is scalable. We take beginners through a foundations series in their first week so you learn proper technique before jumping into regular classes.

What Should I Bring?

Workout clothes, athletic shoes (cross-trainers are best), a water bottle, and a towel. We have chalk, bands, and all the equipment you need. Leave the lifting shoes at home until you're ready for the Oly program.

Is There Parking?

Yes. We have a dedicated parking lot with over 40 spaces right outside the warehouse. There's also street parking on Industrial Blvd if the lot is full during peak hours.

Can I Cancel Anytime?

Monthly memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract. Give us 30 days notice and you're done. Annual memberships require a 12-month commitment but save you $300/year.

Do You Offer Corporate Rates?

Yes. We offer group discounts for companies with 5+ employees. Get in touch and we'll put together a custom package. We also do corporate team-building sessions.

What Does the Free Trial Include?

One full week of unlimited classes across all programs. You'll also get a facility tour, a foundations session with a coach, and a body composition baseline if you want one. No credit card required to start.
