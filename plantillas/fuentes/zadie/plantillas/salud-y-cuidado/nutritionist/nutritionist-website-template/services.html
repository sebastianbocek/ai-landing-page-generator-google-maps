Areas of Expertise

Specialty Areas

Conditions and goals I work with most frequently. If you do not see yours listed, reach out. Chances are I can help or point you to someone who can.

Weight Management

Sustainable, non-restrictive approaches grounded in behavior change and self-compassion. We focus on habits, hunger cues, and nourishment rather than calorie counting and willpower. This is about building a body and a life you feel good in, not chasing a number on a scale.

Digestive Health & IBS

If your gut is running your life, it is time to take it back. I use evidence-based protocols including low-FODMAP elimination and reintroduction, fiber optimization, and gut microbiome support to identify your triggers and build a diet you can actually enjoy.

Food Allergies & Intolerances

Navigating food allergies and intolerances is stressful, isolating, and confusing. I help you identify safe foods, build balanced meals around your restrictions, read labels with confidence, and eat out without anxiety. You deserve to enjoy food, not fear it.

Sports & Performance

From weekend 5K runners to competitive athletes, proper fueling makes the difference between a good day and a great one. I build periodized nutrition plans that match your training load, optimize recovery, and help you perform when it matters most.

Prenatal & Postpartum

Growing and feeding a human is the most nutritionally demanding thing your body will ever do. I provide trimester-specific guidance, manage pregnancy-related nutrition concerns, support breastfeeding nutrition, and help you recover and rebuild postpartum.

Disordered Eating Recovery

Healing your relationship with food takes time, patience, and professional support. I work alongside therapists and treatment teams to support nutritional rehabilitation, challenge food rules, and rebuild trust between you and your body. You deserve to eat in peace.
