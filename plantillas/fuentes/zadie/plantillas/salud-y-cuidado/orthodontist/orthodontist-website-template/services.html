Most Popular

Traditional Metal Braces

Modern metal braces are nothing like the bulky brackets of the past. Today's braces are smaller, sleeker, and more comfortable than ever. Using high-grade stainless steel, they apply gentle, consistent pressure to guide your teeth into perfect alignment. Metal braces are the most versatile option and can correct even the most complex orthodontic issues, including severe crowding, spacing, and bite problems.

With self-ligating technology, our metal braces require fewer adjustments and shorter appointment times. Many patients also enjoy personalizing their braces with colorful elastic bands at each visit.

Discreet Option

Clear Ceramic Braces

Clear ceramic braces work just like traditional metal braces but use tooth-colored or translucent brackets that blend naturally with your teeth. They're an excellent choice for patients who want the reliability and precision of braces with a more subtle appearance. The ceramic material is stain-resistant and durable, maintaining its discreet look throughout your treatment.

Ceramic braces are particularly popular with adult patients and older teens who want effective treatment without the visible look of metal. They can treat the same range of orthodontic issues as metal braces.

Invisible Treatment

Invisalign / Clear Aligners

Invisalign clear aligners are a virtually invisible way to straighten your teeth. Using a series of custom-made, removable trays, your teeth are gradually moved into their ideal positions. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before switching to the next in the series, with each set bringing you closer to your final result.

Because the aligners are removable, you can eat, drink, brush, and floss normally. There are no brackets or wires to deal with, and most people won't even notice you're wearing them. Invisalign is ideal for mild to moderate alignment issues, and our advanced digital planning lets you preview your results before you begin.

Completely Hidden

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces are placed on the back (tongue side) of your teeth, making them completely invisible from the front. They offer the same corrective power as traditional braces with zero visible hardware. Each bracket is custom-made to fit the unique contours of the back of your teeth for maximum comfort and effectiveness.

This is an excellent option for adults and professionals who want comprehensive orthodontic treatment without any visible signs that they're wearing braces. Lingual braces can treat a wide range of alignment and bite issues, and our team has specialized training in this advanced technique.

Additional Services

Comprehensive Orthodontic Care

Beyond our core treatments, we offer a full range of services to support your orthodontic journey at every stage.

Custom Retainers

Protect your beautiful new smile with custom-fit retainers. We offer both fixed and removable options, and we include retainer check-ups as part of your post-treatment care to ensure lasting results.

Emergency Visits

Broken bracket? Poking wire? We offer same-day emergency appointments to address urgent orthodontic issues quickly and comfortably. Your comfort is always our priority.

Early Treatment (Phase 1)

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early intervention can guide jaw growth, correct harmful habits, and create space for permanent teeth.

Adult Orthodontics

It's never too late to straighten your smile. More adults are choosing orthodontic treatment than ever before. We offer discreet options designed to fit your professional lifestyle and schedule.
