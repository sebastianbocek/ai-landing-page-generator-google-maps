How It Works

Your Journey to a Perfect Smile

Getting started is simple. Here's what to expect from your first visit to your final smile reveal.

1

Free Consultation

Meet our team, get a comprehensive exam, and discuss your smile goals. We'll take digital scans and X-rays to understand your unique needs.

2

Custom Treatment Plan

Using 3D imaging, we design a personalized treatment plan. You'll see a preview of your future smile and understand every step of the process.

3

Treatment Begins

Whether it's braces or aligners, we get started right away. Regular check-ups ensure everything is progressing on schedule toward your goal.

4

Smile Reveal

The big day arrives! Your braces come off or you finish your last aligner. You'll walk out with the confident, beautiful smile you've always wanted.
