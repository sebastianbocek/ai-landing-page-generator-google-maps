Our Story

Founded on a Passion for Perfect Smiles

BrightAlign Orthodontics was founded in 2009 by Dr. Sarah Chen with a simple mission: to provide exceptional orthodontic care in a comfortable, patient-first environment. After completing her orthodontic residency at the University of Illinois and earning her board certification, Dr. Chen knew she wanted to build a practice that felt different from the rest.

What started as a single-chair office has grown into a state-of-the-art practice serving over 5,000 patients. But our core values haven't changed. Every patient who walks through our doors is treated like family. We take the time to listen, explain every option, and create treatment plans that are truly personalized.

Today, BrightAlign is proud to be one of the most trusted orthodontic practices in Springfield, known for combining clinical excellence with genuine warmth and care. Whether you're 7 or 70, we're here to help you achieve the smile you deserve.

Our Technology

Advanced Tools for Better Results

We invest in the latest orthodontic technology to ensure faster treatment times, greater comfort, and more predictable outcomes.

Digital Scanning

Say goodbye to messy impressions. Our iTero digital scanner captures a precise 3D model of your teeth in minutes, with zero discomfort. The highly accurate scans lead to better-fitting appliances and faster treatment starts.

3D Treatment Planning

Using advanced software, we simulate your entire treatment from start to finish before placing a single bracket. You can see your predicted results on screen and understand exactly how your smile will transform over time.

Self-Ligating Brackets

Our self-ligating bracket system uses a slide mechanism instead of elastic ties, reducing friction and allowing teeth to move more freely. This means fewer adjustments, shorter appointments, and a more comfortable experience overall.
