Skin Cancer Screening

Early Detection Saves Lives

Skin cancer is the most common cancer in the United States, but it's also one of the most treatable when caught early. Our comprehensive screening includes dermoscopic analysis and same-week biopsy results from our on-site lab.

Full-body skin examinations with dermoscopy
Mole mapping and digital monitoring
Skin biopsies with 48-hour pathology results
Mohs micrographic surgery for skin cancer removal
Pre-cancer treatment (actinic keratosis)
Screening from $175 | Most insurance covers annual exams

Customized topical and oral medication plans
Isotretinoin (Accutane) management with full monitoring
Hormonal acne evaluation and treatment
Acne scar treatment (laser, microneedling, fillers)
Rosacea management with IPL and topical therapy
