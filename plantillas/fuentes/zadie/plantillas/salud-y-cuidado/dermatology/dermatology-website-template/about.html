Our Story

Founded on a Simple Principle

Clarity Dermatology was founded in 2004 by Dr. Rachel Chen with a clear mission: bring academic-quality dermatology care to a private practice setting where patients actually feel heard.

After years in academic medicine, Dr. Chen saw a gap. Patients were getting rushed through 10-minute appointments without real explanations of their conditions or treatment options. She wanted something better.

Today, Clarity has grown to a team of three board-certified dermatologists, but the founding principle hasn't changed. We take time with every patient. We explain what's happening with your skin, why we recommend a particular treatment, and what to expect at every step.

Our Scottsdale clinic is equipped with the latest diagnostic and treatment technology, including an on-site dermatopathology lab that delivers biopsy results in as little as 48 hours. We believe faster answers lead to better outcomes.

Our Core Values
