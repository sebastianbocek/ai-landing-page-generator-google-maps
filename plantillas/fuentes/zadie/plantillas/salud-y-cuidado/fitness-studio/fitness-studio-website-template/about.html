Our Story

Built by coaches who believe fitness should be accessible, effective, and sustainable for everyone.

The Beginning

From Struggling to Strong

Peak Performance Training started in 2009 with a simple observation: most people fail at fitness not because they lack motivation, but because they lack proper guidance.

Founder Marcus Chen spent years as an overweight teenager, trying every fad diet and workout program he could find. Nothing worked until he finally connected with a trainer who taught him the fundamentals. The science of training, the importance of progressive overload, and how nutrition actually affects your body.

"I built Peak Performance to be the gym I wish I had when I was starting out. No gimmicks. No shame. Just real coaching that actually works."

That experience changed everything. Marcus went on to earn his CSCS certification, competed in powerlifting, and eventually opened Peak Performance to share what he'd learned with others who felt lost in their fitness journey.

Our Approach

The Peak Method

Our training philosophy is built on three core principles that have driven every client transformation.

01

Assess First

Every program starts with a comprehensive assessment. We evaluate your movement patterns, strength levels, injury history, and lifestyle factors before writing a single workout.

02

Progressive Overload

Your body adapts to stress. We carefully track and increase training variables over time to ensure you're always progressing without burning out or getting injured.

03

Sustainable Habits

Quick fixes don't work. We focus on building habits that fit your life so you can maintain your results long after you leave our facility.

Marcus Chen, CSCS

Head Coach & Founder

Marcus holds a Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist (CSCS) credential from the NSCA and has been coaching for over 15 years. A former competitive powerlifter, he's helped everyone from first-time gym-goers to professional athletes achieve their goals.

His specialty is building strength programs that translate to real-world performance. Whether you want to deadlift 400 pounds or just carry your groceries without back pain, Marcus will get you there.

Certifications: CSCS, USAW Level 1, Precision Nutrition Level 1

Sarah Mitchell, CPT

Senior Trainer & Nutrition Coach

Sarah combines her NASM Certified Personal Trainer credential with a Precision Nutrition certification to offer comprehensive coaching. She specializes in sustainable weight loss and has helped over 200 clients lose a combined 10,000+ pounds.

Her approach focuses on building habits that last, not crash diets that fail. Sarah's clients don't just lose weight. They keep it off.

Certifications: NASM-CPT, Precision Nutrition Level 2, Corrective Exercise Specialist

David Torres, PES

Performance Coach

David brings a background in collegiate athletics and a Performance Enhancement Specialization to the team. He's worked with high school, college, and recreational athletes looking to improve their speed, agility, and overall athletic performance.

His HIIT classes are legendary for their intensity, and his mobility work has helped countless clients move better and feel better in their daily lives.

Certifications: NASM-PES, CPT, TRX Suspension Training, Functional Movement Screen Level 2
