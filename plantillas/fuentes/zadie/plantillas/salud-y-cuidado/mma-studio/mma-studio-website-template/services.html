What to Expect

New to martial arts? Here's what your first class will look like.

1

Check In

Arrive 15 minutes early. We'll give you a tour, have you sign a waiver, and get you set up with any loaner gear you need.

2

Warm Up

Classes start with a structured warm-up. Expect movement drills, stretching, and exercises to prepare your body for training.

3

Technique

The coach demonstrates techniques. You'll drill them with a partner at your own pace. Coaches help with corrections.

4

Application

Put it together with positional sparring or live drilling. Beginners can sit out live rounds until they're comfortable.

5

Cool Down

Classes end with stretching and sometimes conditioning. Ask questions, meet training partners, and cool down.

6

Come Back

That's it! You survived. Soreness is normal. Come back in a day or two and do it again. Consistency is everything.
