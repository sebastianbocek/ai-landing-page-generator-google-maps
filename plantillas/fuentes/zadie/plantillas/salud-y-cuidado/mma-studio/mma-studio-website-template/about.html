About Apex Combat

Building champions in the cage and in life since 2009. More than a gym. A fighting family.

Our Story

Apex Combat Academy was founded in 2009 by Marcus Torres, a professional MMA fighter who saw a need for a training facility that combined elite-level instruction with a welcoming environment for everyone.

What started as a small space with a few mats and heavy bags has grown into one of the region's premier combat sports facilities. We've trained professional fighters who have competed on the biggest stages in the world, amateur champions, and thousands of everyday athletes who discovered something powerful through martial arts.

Our philosophy is simple: everyone who walks through our doors deserves the same level of instruction, respect, and opportunity to grow. Whether you're training for your first UFC bout or your first day on the mats, you'll find coaches who care about your development and teammates who push you to be your best.

What We Stand For

These principles guide everything we do at Apex Combat Academy.

Excellence

We don't cut corners. Every technique is taught correctly, every training session has purpose. We hold ourselves and each other to the highest standards.

Respect

The martial arts are built on respect. For your training partners, your coaches, your opponents, and yourself. We leave egos at the door.

Perseverance

Growth happens outside the comfort zone. We embrace the grind, push through adversity, and understand that the struggle is where champions are forged.

Community

You don't train alone here. Our members become family. We celebrate each other's victories and support each other through challenges on and off the mats.

Growth

Every day is an opportunity to improve. Whether it's refining a technique, building conditioning, or developing mental toughness. Progress is the goal.

Safety

Smart training is sustainable training. We emphasize technique over power, controlled sparring, and taking care of training partners so everyone can train for life.

Meet Our Coaches

Learn from fighters and instructors who have competed and coached at the highest levels of combat sports.

Marcus "The Hammer" Torres

Founder & Head MMA Coach

Marcus founded Apex Combat after a 10-year professional MMA career that included fights in the UFC, Bellator, and WEC. He's dedicated the past 15 years to developing the next generation of fighters and martial artists.

  • Former UFC Lightweight Contender
  • Professional Record: 18-6
  • Certified MMA Coach (UMMAF)
  • Trained 6 Regional Champions

Ana "Anaconda" Reyes

Head Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Coach

Ana is a 3rd degree black belt under Renzo Gracie with multiple IBJJF World Championship medals. Her technical approach and patient instruction have produced countless competitors and black belts.

  • 3rd Degree BJJ Black Belt
  • 5x IBJJF World Medalist
  • ADCC Trials Finalist
  • 15+ Years Teaching Experience

Somchai "Iron Shin" Prasert

Muay Thai Coach

Born and raised in Thailand, Somchai trained and competed at Lumpinee and Rajadamnern stadiums before bringing authentic Muay Thai instruction to the United States. His classes emphasize proper technique and the traditional art.

  • 47 Professional Muay Thai Fights
  • 3x Stadium Champion (Thailand)
  • 8 Years Training in Bangkok
  • Level 4 Kru Certification

Tyler "TD" Davidson

Wrestling Coach

Tyler was a two-time NCAA Division I All-American wrestler before transitioning to MMA coaching. His wrestling program has become essential for fighters looking to develop elite takedown offense and defense.

  • 2x NCAA Division I All-American

  • USA Wrestling Certified Coach
  • 10+ Years Coaching Experience

Apex Fight Team

For athletes ready to take their training to the competition level.

The Apex Fight Team is our competitive program for athletes who want to test themselves in amateur or professional competition. Members train alongside current professionals in a structured fight camp environment.

What's Included:

  • > Priority access to all classes and open mat times

  • > Personalized fight camp programming
  • > Corner support at competitions
  • > Video analysis and game planning
  • > Nutrition and weight cutting guidance

Fight team membership is by invitation after completing our fundamentals program and demonstrating commitment to training. Speak to a coach if you're interested in competing.
