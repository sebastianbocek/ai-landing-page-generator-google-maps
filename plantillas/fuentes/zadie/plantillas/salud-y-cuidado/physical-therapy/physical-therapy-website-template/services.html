What We Offer

Our Services

Every treatment plan is built around you. Here's an overview of our core specialties and what each includes.

Sports Rehabilitation

From $125/session

Comprehensive return-to-sport programs designed for athletes who need to get back stronger than before the injury.

  • Sport-specific movement analysis
  • Progressive strength and conditioning
  • Agility and plyometric training
  • Return-to-play testing protocols
  • Injury prevention screening
  • Coach/trainer communication

Post-Surgery Recovery

From $135/session

Structured rehabilitation following surgical procedures. We work closely with your surgeon to follow their protocols precisely.

  • ACL/MCL reconstruction rehab
  • Total joint replacement recovery
  • Rotator cuff repair rehab
  • Spinal surgery rehabilitation
  • Surgeon protocol adherence
  • Progress reporting to your doctor

Chronic Pain Management

From $120/session

Long-term strategies for persistent pain conditions. We address the root cause and teach you to manage symptoms independently.

  • Lower back and neck pain
  • Fibromyalgia management
  • Pain neuroscience education
  • Graded exercise therapy
  • Self-management strategies
  • Home exercise programming

Manual Therapy

From $110/session

Skilled hands-on techniques that restore joint mobility, reduce muscle tension, and improve tissue healing.

  • Joint mobilization and manipulation
  • Soft tissue mobilization
  • Myofascial release
  • Trigger point dry needling
  • Instrument-assisted soft tissue work
  • Combined with therapeutic exercise

Balance & Fall Prevention

From $100/session

Comprehensive balance programs for older adults and anyone at risk for falls. Build strength, coordination, and confidence.

  • Fall risk assessment
  • Vestibular rehabilitation
  • Strength and stability training
  • Gait analysis and correction
  • Home safety recommendations
  • Assistive device training

Workplace Ergonomics

From $250/assessment

On-site or virtual workstation assessments that identify and correct ergonomic risk factors before they become injuries.

  • Workstation evaluation
  • Posture and movement analysis
  • Equipment recommendations
  • Corrective exercise program
  • Written report with photos
  • 30-day follow-up included
