About Restore Motion

Founded by a former collegiate athlete who knows what it takes to recover. Eight years of helping our community move better.

Our Story

Built on Personal Experience

Restore Motion PT was founded in 2016 by Dr. Elena Vasquez, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and former collegiate volleyball player. After tearing her ACL during her junior season, Elena experienced firsthand how transformative great physical therapy can be. She also experienced how frustrating it is when the care falls short.

That experience shaped everything about this clinic. Elena built Restore Motion around one idea: every patient deserves focused, expert attention for the full duration of their session. No being shuffled between aides. No cookie-cutter exercise sheets. Just skilled, hands-on care from a licensed therapist who knows your name and your goals.

Eight years later, we've treated over 8,500 patients and grown to a team of six licensed therapists. But the core principle hasn't changed. When you walk through our door, you're not a chart number. You're a person who wants to get back to living.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Three principles that guide every treatment plan, every session, and every interaction.

Evidence-Based Care

We don't guess. Every treatment we prescribe is backed by current research and clinical evidence. Our therapists complete over 40 hours of continuing education annually to stay at the forefront of rehabilitation science.

Patient Empowerment

Our goal is to make you not need us. We teach you to understand your body, manage your condition, and maintain your progress independently. You leave with knowledge, not just relief.

Holistic Approach

Pain doesn't exist in a vacuum. We consider your whole life: sleep, stress, work habits, activity level, and goals. Treatment addresses the full picture, not just the site of pain.
