What We Treat

Our Specialties

From sports injuries to chronic pain, we provide targeted therapy that addresses the root cause, not just the symptoms.

Sports Rehabilitation

Return-to-sport programs for athletes at every level. We combine manual therapy, progressive loading, and sport-specific training to get you back in the game.

Post-Surgery Recovery

Structured rehabilitation after joint replacement, ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, and other surgical procedures. We follow surgeon protocols to the letter.

Chronic Pain Management

Long-term strategies for managing back pain, neck pain, fibromyalgia, and other persistent conditions. We focus on functional improvement and lasting relief.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques including joint mobilization, soft tissue work, and myofascial release. Skilled touch that restores movement and reduces pain.

Balance & Fall Prevention

Comprehensive balance assessment and training for older adults. We identify fall risk factors and build strength, coordination, and confidence.

Workplace Ergonomics

Workstation assessments and corrective exercise programs for desk workers, tradespeople, and anyone dealing with repetitive strain or posture-related pain.

Your Journey

How It Works

Three steps to getting back to your best. Simple, thorough, and built around your goals.

1

Evaluate

A thorough initial evaluation identifies the root cause of your pain or limitation. We assess movement, strength, and function to build a complete picture.

2

Treat

Your personalized treatment plan combines hands-on therapy, targeted exercises, and education. Every session builds on the last toward measurable goals.

3

Thrive

Graduate with a home exercise program and the knowledge to stay healthy. We equip you with tools to maintain your progress and prevent re-injury.
