About QuickCare Medical Center

Providing fast, affordable, and compassionate urgent care to our community since 2015.

Our Mission

QuickCare Medical Center was founded with a clear purpose: to give our community access to quality healthcare without the long waits, high costs, and hassle of the emergency room.

We believe that when you're sick or injured, you shouldn't have to wait days for an appointment or spend hours in an ER waiting room for something that isn't life-threatening. You deserve prompt, professional care from people who take the time to listen and explain your treatment.

That's why we built a clinic around convenience. Walk in any time we're open, seven days a week. No appointment necessary. Our board-certified providers will see you quickly, diagnose accurately with on-site X-ray and lab services, and get you on the road to recovery the same day.

Meet Our Providers

Experienced, board-certified medical professionals dedicated to your health and comfort.

Dr. James Patterson, MD
Medical Director
Board-Certified Emergency Medicine | University of Illinois College of Medicine | 15+ years experience

Dr. Patterson founded QuickCare after spending a decade in emergency medicine. He saw firsthand how many ER visits could be handled more efficiently in an urgent care setting and set out to build a clinic that combines hospital-quality care with neighborhood convenience.

Rachel Kim, PA-C
Physician Assistant
NCCPA Certified | Rush University PA Program | 8+ years experience

Rachel specializes in family and urgent care medicine with particular expertise in pediatric urgent care. She is known for her calm demeanor with anxious patients and her ability to quickly and accurately diagnose common illness and injury.

Michael Torres, MSN, FNP-C
Family Nurse Practitioner
AANP Board Certified | SIU Edwardsville MSN Program | 10+ years experience

Michael brings a decade of nursing and advanced practice experience to QuickCare. He has a special interest in occupational health and preventive medicine, and he leads our DOT physical and employer health screening programs.

Our Facility

A modern, fully equipped urgent care clinic designed for efficiency and patient comfort.

Built for Better Care

Our 5,000-square-foot clinic was designed from the ground up to move patients through their visit quickly and comfortably. From our welcoming reception area to our private exam rooms, every detail serves our mission of fast, quality care.

  • 6 Private Exam Rooms

    Spacious, clean rooms equipped for a full range of urgent care procedures.

  • On-Site Digital X-Ray

    Results in minutes, not days. No need for a separate imaging appointment.

  • In-House Laboratory

    Rapid strep, flu, COVID, urinalysis, and basic blood work done on-site.

  • Electronic Health Records

    Secure digital records for faster check-in and seamless coordination with your primary care provider.

Our Commitment to You

Every decision we make starts with one question: what's best for the patient?

Short Wait Times

We staff our clinic to keep wait times short. Most patients are seen within 15-25 minutes of arrival. We respect your time because we know you don't feel well.

Transparent Pricing

No surprise bills. We accept most major insurance plans and offer clear self-pay pricing starting at $150 per visit. We'll always discuss costs before treatment when possible.

Patient-First Care

You're not a number here. Our providers take time to listen, explain your diagnosis in plain language, and make sure you understand your treatment plan before you leave.

Ready to Be Seen?

No appointment needed. Walk in during our hours and we'll take care of the rest.
