What We Treat

From minor illnesses to injuries, we handle the conditions that need prompt attention but don't require an emergency room visit.

Colds & Flu

Fever, sore throat, congestion, body aches, and other cold and flu symptoms. Rapid flu testing available on-site.

Minor Injuries

Cuts, scrapes, lacerations, and minor wounds. We provide stitches, wound care, and tetanus shots as needed.

Sprains & Strains

Ankle sprains, muscle strains, and minor fractures. On-site X-ray for fast diagnosis and splinting available.

Infections

UTIs, ear infections, sinus infections, strep throat, and more. Rapid testing and same-day prescriptions.

Allergies

Seasonal allergies, allergic reactions, rashes, and hay fever. Relief when your symptoms can't wait for a regular appointment.

Skin Conditions

Rashes, insect bites, minor burns, poison ivy, and skin infections. Fast diagnosis and treatment to get you comfortable.

When to Visit Us vs. the ER

Urgent care can treat most non-life-threatening conditions faster and at a fraction of the ER cost.

Visit QuickCare For
  • Cold, flu, and fever
  • Sore throat and ear infections
  • Minor cuts needing stitches
  • Sprains, strains, minor fractures
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Rashes and skin irritation
  • Minor allergic reactions
  • Back and joint pain
Go to the ER For
  • Chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Signs of stroke (facial drooping, slurred speech)
  • Severe or uncontrolled bleeding
  • Head injuries with loss of consciousness
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Compound fractures or dislocations
  • Seizures
  • Severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis)
