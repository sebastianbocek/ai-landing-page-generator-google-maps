Our Services

Comprehensive urgent care, diagnostic testing, and preventive health services. All available walk-in, seven days a week.

Illness & Infection

Walk in for fast diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses. Most visits include rapid testing and same-day prescriptions so you can start feeling better right away.

  • Cold and flu symptoms
  • Strep throat
  • Urinary tract infections (UTI)
  • Ear infections
  • Sinus infections
  • Bronchitis
  • Pink eye (conjunctivitis)
  • Stomach flu and nausea

Injuries

From minor cuts to sprains and fractures, our providers can treat most non-life-threatening injuries on-site with X-ray and wound care capabilities.

  • Cuts and lacerations (stitches and wound closure)
  • Sprains and strains
  • Minor fractures and hairline cracks
  • Minor burns (first and second degree)
  • Insect bites and stings
  • Foreign body removal (splinters, etc.)

Preventive & Wellness

Stay ahead of illness with physicals, vaccinations, and health screenings. Many available on a walk-in basis with no appointment needed.

  • DOT physicals
  • Sports physicals
  • School and camp physicals
  • Annual flu shots
  • Vaccinations (Tdap, Hepatitis, MMR, and more)
  • TB skin testing
  • Drug screening (urine and hair)

Diagnostic Services

Get answers fast with on-site testing and imaging. Our in-house capabilities mean you leave with a diagnosis, not a referral.

  • Digital X-ray
  • Rapid strep test
  • Rapid flu test
  • Urinalysis
  • Basic blood work (CBC, metabolic panel)
  • EKG (electrocardiogram)

Occupational Health

Employer health services for businesses of all sizes. We work with companies to keep their workforce healthy, compliant, and on the job.

  • Pre-employment physicals
  • Drug and alcohol testing
  • Workers' compensation injury treatment
  • DOT exams and certification

When to Go to the ER Instead

Urgent care is ideal for non-life-threatening conditions. Please call 911 or go directly to the nearest emergency room if you experience any of the following:

  • Chest pain, pressure, or tightness
  • Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
  • Signs of stroke (sudden numbness, confusion, trouble speaking)
  • Severe or uncontrolled bleeding
  • Loss of consciousness or altered mental state
  • Severe head, neck, or spinal injuries
  • Seizures
  • Poisoning or drug overdose
  • Severe allergic reactions (swelling of throat, difficulty breathing)
