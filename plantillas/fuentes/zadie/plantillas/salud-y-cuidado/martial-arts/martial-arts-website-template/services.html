The Journey

Belt Progression System

In Shotokan Karate, rank is earned. Each promotion requires demonstrated proficiency in kata, kihon (basics), and, at higher levels, kumite. Testing occurs approximately every three to four months. There is no fast track.

Testing Requirements

Each test covers kihon (basics), kata (forms), and at brown belt and above, kumite (sparring). Sensei evaluates readiness and recommends students when they are prepared, not on a fixed calendar.

Testing Fees

Belt testing fees range from $35 (lower kyu grades) to $75 (brown and black belt). Black belt examinations are conducted under JKA oversight and include a separate JKA certification fee.

Timeline

Dedicated adult students typically reach shodan (1st degree black belt) in 4 to 6 years of consistent training. Children's timelines vary. There is no shortcut and no guarantee. Rank is earned.
