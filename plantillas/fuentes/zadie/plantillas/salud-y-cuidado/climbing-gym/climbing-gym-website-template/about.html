How It Started

Founded on the Wall

Basecamp Climbing Co. started the way most good things do -- on a road trip. In 2018, co-founders Alex Chen and Maya Rodriguez were living out of a van, climbing their way through Red River Gorge, Hueco Tanks, and Bishop. Somewhere between a V8 send and a flat tire in the desert, they realized something: the climbing communities they loved most were not just about the rock. They were about the people who showed up, shared beta, and spotted each other through the crux.

Back home, the nearest gym was an hour away. The local options were outdated, poorly maintained, or treated climbing as an afterthought next to a weight room. Alex and Maya knew they could do better. They spent a year planning, fundraising, and studying the best climbing gyms in the country. In 2020, Basecamp opened its doors.

Today, Basecamp is a 15,000 square foot facility with bouldering, top rope, lead climbing, a training zone, yoga room, and a gear shop. But the numbers do not tell the real story. What matters is the crew that gathers here every morning before work, the kids who discovered climbing through our youth program, and the complete beginners who walked in nervous and left already planning their next visit.

Beyond the Gym

Community & Events

Basecamp is more than a place to train. It is where friendships start, goals get crushed, and the stoke stays high.

Monthly Competitions

From casual "Boulders & Brews" nights to sanctioned competitions, we host events that challenge every level. Categories for beginners, intermediates, and open division. Cash prizes for the open.

Outdoor Trip Series

Monthly guided trips to local crags and destination climbing areas. We handle transportation, beta, and safety. You bring the chalk and the send energy. Trips range from single-day outings to weekend camping trips.

Youth Climbing Team

Our competitive youth team trains three days a week and competes regionally. Open to climbers ages 8 through 18. Tryouts held every September. Recreational youth programs are also available year-round.

Weekly Climbing Meetups

New to climbing or new to the area? Our Tuesday night meetups are the easiest way to find partners and get plugged into the community. All levels welcome. We pair experienced climbers with newcomers.

COME SEE FOR YOURSELF

Walk in any time we are open. No appointment needed. We will show you around.
