Classes & Instruction

Every class is taught by certified instructors. Small group sizes so you get real attention and real improvement.

Intro to Climbing

$45
2 hours Sat & Sun, 10am Max 8 students

Everything you need to start climbing safely and confidently. Covers basic technique, belaying, knot tying, climbing etiquette, and how to read routes. Gear included. No experience necessary.

Beginner

Lead Climbing Cert

$65
3 hours Wed 6pm & Sat 1pm Max 6 students

Learn to lead climb and lead belay in our gym. Covers clipping technique, fall practice, communication, and lead belay skills. Must be able to climb 5.9 on top rope and have a current belay certification.

Intermediate

Advanced Technique

$55
90 min Tue & Thu, 7pm Max 8 students

Break through your plateau. Focused drills on footwork, body positioning, dynamic movement, and route reading. Video analysis included. Rotating focus each week: one week overhangs, next week slabs, next week cracks.

All Levels

Training Zone

Train Harder, Climb Harder

Dedicated training equipment for climbers who want to push their grade. Included with every membership and day pass.

Campus Board

Full-size campus board with rungs in multiple depths. Progressive training from beginner rungs to advanced contact strength. Wall-mounted training guides available.

Hangboard Stations

Six hangboard stations with Beastmaker 1000 and 2000 models, plus a Tension Grindstone. Pulley systems for assisted hangs. Structured protocols posted at each station.

Spray Wall

40-degree overhanging spray wall with hundreds of holds. Set your own problems or follow community-created benchmarks logged in our app. Reset quarterly with fresh holds.

Fitness Area

Free weights, pull-up bars, TRX systems, resistance bands, and climbing-specific conditioning equipment. Everything you need for antagonist training and injury prevention.

Yoga Room

Dedicated space for yoga classes, stretching, and mobility work. Mats and props provided. Open for personal use when classes are not in session.

Training Programs

Structured 8-week training programs designed by our coaches. Targeted at specific goals: finger strength, power endurance, movement technique. Available as add-on for $99.
