Common Questions

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Still have questions? Reach out -- we are happy to help.

Do I need any experience to come climb?

Not at all. Many of our members had never touched a climbing wall before their first visit. Our staff will walk you through the basics -- how to put on a harness, how to belay, and how to read the routes. If you want more structured instruction, our Intro to Climbing class covers everything in depth.

Is there a minimum age to climb?

We welcome climbers as young as 6 years old. Climbers under 14 must be accompanied by an adult. All minors need a parent or guardian to sign the liability waiver. Our Kids Climbing Club is designed specifically for ages 6 through 14.

What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic clothing that you can move freely in. Avoid anything too baggy that could get caught on holds. Climbing shoes are available to rent for $6 -- we strongly recommend them over street shoes. Skip the jewelry, especially rings.

Do I need a belay certification?

For bouldering, no -- it is just you and the wall. For top rope and lead climbing, yes. If you have belayed at another gym, we offer a quick belay check (free, takes about 5 minutes). If you are new to belaying, our Intro to Climbing class includes belay instruction and certification.

Can I bring a friend who is not a member?

Absolutely. All members receive free guest passes each month (the exact number depends on your membership tier). Guests pay the standard day pass rate if you have used your guest passes. There are no limits on how often a guest can visit -- we just ask that each guest signs a waiver on their first visit.

What is your cancellation policy?

We keep it simple. All memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract. To cancel, just give us 30 days written notice (email works fine). We also offer membership freezes for up to 3 months per year if you are traveling or injured -- just ask at the front desk.
