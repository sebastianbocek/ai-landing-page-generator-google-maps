Do Any of These Sound Familiar?

Signs You Should Get Your Hearing Tested

Hearing loss often develops gradually. If you recognize any of these situations, it may be time for a professional evaluation.

Frequently Asking People to Repeat Themselves

If "What did you say?" has become your most common phrase, you may be missing sounds in the speech frequency range. This is one of the earliest and most common signs of hearing change.

Turning the TV Volume Higher Than Others Prefer

When family members comment that the TV is too loud, or you struggle to hear dialogue clearly even at higher volumes, your hearing may have shifted without you realizing it.

Difficulty Following Conversations in Noisy Places

Restaurants, family gatherings, and crowded spaces become exhausting when your brain struggles to separate speech from background noise. This is a hallmark sign of hearing loss.

Ringing, Buzzing, or Humming in Your Ears

Persistent sounds in your ears, known as tinnitus, often accompany hearing loss. Whether constant or intermittent, these phantom sounds deserve professional attention.
