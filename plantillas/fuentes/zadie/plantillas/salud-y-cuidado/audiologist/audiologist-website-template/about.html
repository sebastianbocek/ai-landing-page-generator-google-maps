About SoundLife

Patient-centered hearing care built on expertise, empathy, and the latest audiology science.

Our Story

Founded on a Simple Belief: Everyone Deserves to Hear Well

SoundLife Hearing Center was founded by Dr. Lisa Chen after spending a decade in hospital-based audiology. She saw firsthand how hearing loss affected not just communication, but relationships, confidence, and overall quality of life. Too many patients were being rushed through appointments or pressured into expensive devices they didn't need.

Dr. Chen opened SoundLife in Nashville with a different philosophy: take the time to listen before diagnosing, educate before prescribing, and support patients for the long term. That approach has guided every decision since, from the audiologists we hire to the technology we invest in.

Today, SoundLife serves patients across Middle Tennessee with a team of three dedicated professionals and a reputation built on trust, transparency, and results.

Our Team

Meet the People Behind Your Care

Our team combines advanced training with genuine compassion. Every member is dedicated to helping you hear your best.

Dr. Lisa Chen, Au.D.

Lead Audiologist & Owner

Dr. Chen holds a Doctor of Audiology from Vanderbilt University and is board-certified by the American Board of Audiology. With over 12 years of clinical experience, she specializes in complex hearing aid fittings and adult diagnostic audiology. Audiologist

Our Philosophy

Treating the Whole Person, Not Just the Ears

Hearing loss affects every part of your life. Our approach reflects that.

Listen First

Before any test or recommendation, we take the time to understand your experience, your goals, and what matters most to you. Your hearing journey starts with a real conversation.

Educate, Don't Pressure

We explain your results in plain language, present your options honestly, and let you make informed decisions. We never push products you don't need.

Measure Results

We use real-ear measurement and validated questionnaires to verify that your treatment is working, not just assume. If something isn't right, we adjust until it is.

Support for Life

Hearing care is an ongoing relationship. We provide annual check-ups, device maintenance, and support for as long as you need it. You're never just a number here.

Our Technology

Advanced Equipment for Accurate Results

We invest in gold-standard audiology equipment so you get precise diagnostics and perfectly tuned devices.

Sound Booth

Our double-walled sound booth provides a controlled environment for the most accurate hearing test results, free from outside noise interference.

Real-Ear Measurement

This probe-based system measures the actual sound output in your ear canal, ensuring your hearing aids are programmed precisely to your prescription.

Speech-in-Noise Testing

We assess how well you hear speech in realistic noisy environments, giving us a clearer picture of your day-to-day hearing ability than standard tests alone.

Video Otoscopy

High-definition imaging lets us examine your ear canal and eardrum in detail, and lets you see exactly what we see on screen during your appointment.
