Our Services

Complete Hearing Care Under One Roof

Every service is delivered by our licensed audiologists using the latest evidence-based practices and technology.

Comprehensive Hearing Evaluation

A thorough diagnostic assessment that goes beyond a basic screening. We test pure-tone thresholds, speech recognition, middle ear function, and otoacoustic emissions. You'll leave with a complete picture of your hearing and clear next steps.

  • Pure-tone audiometry
  • Speech recognition testing
  • Tympanometry & middle ear analysis
  • Results review & recommendations
$150 - $250

Hearing Aid Fitting & Programming

We work with all major hearing aid manufacturers to find the right match for your hearing profile, lifestyle, and budget. Every fitting includes real-ear measurement verification and follow-up adjustments to ensure optimal performance.

  • All major brands available
  • Real-ear measurement verification
  • Programming & fine-tuning
  • 30-day adjustment period included
$1,500 - $6,000 / pair

Hearing Aid Repair & Maintenance

Keep your hearing aids performing at their best. We offer professional cleaning, inspection, and repair services for all brands. In-warranty repairs are handled at no cost, and we provide loaner devices when repairs require manufacturer turnaround.

  • Professional cleaning & inspection
  • In-office minor repairs
  • Manufacturer warranty repairs (free)
  • Loaner devices available
$75 - $200

Tinnitus Evaluation & Management

If you experience ringing, buzzing, or humming in your ears, we can help. Our tinnitus program includes a specialized evaluation, pitch and loudness matching, and a customized treatment plan that may include sound therapy, counseling, or combination devices.

  • Tinnitus-specific audiologic evaluation
  • Pitch & loudness matching
  • Sound therapy & masking devices
  • Counseling & coping strategies
$200 - $350

Custom Ear Protection

Protect your hearing in loud environments with professionally molded ear protection. We serve musicians, industrial workers, hunters, and anyone regularly exposed to damaging noise levels. Custom molds provide superior comfort and noise reduction compared to off-the-shelf options.

  • Musicians: $150 - $250
  • Industrial: $100 - $175
  • Custom-molded for perfect fit
  • In-ear monitors for performers
$100 - $250

Cochlear Implant Support

For patients with severe to profound hearing loss who may not benefit from hearing aids, we provide comprehensive cochlear implant support. This includes candidacy evaluation, post-surgical mapping and programming, and ongoing auditory rehabilitation.

  • Candidacy evaluation
  • Post-surgical mapping
  • Programming & adjustments
  • Auditory rehabilitation
Contact for pricing

Pediatric Hearing Services

Children need specialized hearing care. We offer newborn hearing screenings, pediatric diagnostic evaluations, and hearing aid fittings designed for growing ears. Dr. Webb has extensive experience working with children of all ages and developmental stages.

  • Newborn hearing screening
  • Pediatric diagnostic evaluation
  • Pediatric hearing aid fitting
  • Coordination with schools & ENTs
Contact for pricing
