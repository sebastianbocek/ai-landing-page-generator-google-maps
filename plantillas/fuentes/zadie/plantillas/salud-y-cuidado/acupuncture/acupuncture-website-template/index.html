Getting Started

Your First Visit

We want your first experience with us to feel comfortable and unhurried. Here's what to expect from the moment you arrive.

1

Comprehensive Intake

Your initial consultation lasts 75 minutes. Dr. Wei will take a thorough health history, ask about your sleep, digestion, emotions, and lifestyle, and perform a tongue and pulse assessment. This holistic intake is the foundation of your treatment plan.

2

Personalized Treatment

Based on your intake, Dr. Wei will design a treatment specific to your constitution and current patterns. Your first acupuncture session follows immediately. We explain every step as we go, and you're always in control of the pace.

3

Your Care Plan

After your first treatment, Dr. Wei will discuss a recommended care plan. Most acute conditions respond in 4 to 6 sessions; chronic conditions typically benefit from 8 to 12. We reassess regularly and adjust based on how your body responds.
