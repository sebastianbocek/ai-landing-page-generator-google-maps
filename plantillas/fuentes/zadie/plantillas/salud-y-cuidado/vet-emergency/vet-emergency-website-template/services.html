Emergency Services & Pricing

Transparent pricing for every emergency service we offer. No surprises -- just honest, upfront costs so you can focus on your pet.

About Our Pricing

The ranges below reflect typical costs. Your pet's specific condition, size, and required treatments may affect the final price. We always provide a written estimate and discuss costs with you before proceeding with treatment.

Emergency Triage & Stabilization

Every visit begins with an immediate triage assessment by a veterinary nurse, followed by a full examination by an emergency veterinarian. We stabilize your pet's condition, manage pain, and determine the next steps.

  • Rapid triage assessment on arrival
  • Full physical examination
  • Initial stabilization (IV access, oxygen, pain relief)
  • Treatment plan discussion with owner
$150 - $250

Emergency Surgery

Our surgical suite is equipped and staffed around the clock for emergency procedures. Board-certified surgeons handle everything from foreign body removal to complex orthopedic repairs.

  • Soft tissue surgery: $1,500 - $4,000
  • Orthopedic surgery: $2,500 - $6,000
  • Foreign body removal: $2,000 - $4,500
  • Includes anesthesia, monitoring, and recovery
$1,500 - $6,000

Critical Care ICU

Our ICU provides round-the-clock monitoring and intensive treatment for critically ill patients. Dedicated ICU nurses provide continuous observation, with veterinarian oversight at all times.

  • 24-hour continuous monitoring
  • IV fluid therapy and medication administration
  • Ventilator support when needed
  • Daily reassessment and treatment plan updates
$500 - $1,500 / day

Diagnostic Imaging

On-site digital radiography and ultrasound give us fast, high-quality images to diagnose fractures, foreign objects, organ abnormalities, and internal bleeding without delay.

  • Digital X-rays: $200 - $400
  • Abdominal/cardiac ultrasound: $300 - $500
  • Results available within minutes
  • Interpreted by experienced emergency veterinarians
$200 - $500

In-House Laboratory

Our complete on-site lab runs blood work, urinalysis, and other critical tests in minutes rather than hours. Fast results mean faster treatment decisions for your pet.

  • Complete blood count (CBC): $75 - $150
  • Chemistry panel: $100 - $200
  • Blood gas & electrolytes: $75 - $125
  • Urinalysis: $75 - $125
  • Coagulation panel: $100 - $150
$75 - $300

Toxin & Poison Treatment

Time-critical decontamination and treatment for pets who have ingested toxic substances. We carry antidotes for common poisons and provide aggressive supportive care.

  • Decontamination (emesis induction): $300 - $500
  • Activated charcoal administration: $100 - $200
  • IV fluid therapy: $200 - $400
  • Antidote administration when available
  • Monitoring and supportive care: $300 - $800
$300 - $800

Wound Care & Trauma

From lacerations and bite wounds to fracture stabilization and burn treatment, our team handles all levels of traumatic injury with expert, compassionate care.

  • Laceration repair: $300 - $1,000
  • Bite wound treatment: $400 - $1,200
  • Fracture stabilization: $500 - $2,000
  • Burn treatment and wound management
  • Bandaging and splinting
$300 - $2,000

Oxygen Therapy & Blood Transfusions

Life-saving respiratory support and blood products for pets in critical condition. We maintain blood products on-site for immediate transfusion when needed.

  • Oxygen cage therapy: $200 - $400
  • Nasal oxygen cannula: $150 - $300
  • Packed red blood cell transfusion: $300 - $500
  • Plasma transfusion: $250 - $500
  • Continuous monitoring during therapy
$200 - $500
Payment Options

We Work With You on Cost

We believe financial concerns should never prevent your pet from receiving emergency care. We offer multiple payment options and our team is trained to help you find a solution that works.

We accept all major credit cards, cash, and checks. We also work with several financing partners to help spread the cost of emergency care over time.

Payment Details

  • CareCredit: Apply in our lobby or online. Promotional financing with 0% APR options available for qualifying applicants.
  • Scratchpay: Simple, transparent payment plans with no hard credit check. Apply in minutes from your phone.
  • Pet Insurance: We work with all major pet insurance providers. We provide detailed invoices and medical records for your claim.
  • Payment Plans: For qualifying cases, we can arrange custom payment schedules. Ask our front desk team for details.
