Our Story

Founded on a Simple Idea

ClearView Eye Care was founded in 2010 with one goal: to provide comprehensive, personalized eye care without the rushed, impersonal experience that had become the norm in optometry.

Dr. Emily Chen had spent years working in large corporate optical chains and saw firsthand how patients were being underserved. Appointments were too short. Technology was outdated. And patients were treated like numbers, not people.

So she opened ClearView with a different approach. Every patient gets the time they deserve. Every exam uses the latest diagnostic tools. And every treatment plan is built around you, not a corporate checklist.

Our Technology

Advanced Diagnostics for Better Outcomes

We invest in the latest equipment so we can catch problems early and provide the most accurate prescriptions possible.

Digital Retinal Imaging

High-resolution photos of the inside of your eye, allowing us to detect conditions like diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, and glaucoma in their earliest stages.

OCT Scanning

Optical coherence tomography creates detailed cross-section images of your retina. It's like an MRI for your eyes, revealing structures invisible to standard exams.

Visual Field Testing

Automated perimetry maps your peripheral vision to detect blind spots and early signs of glaucoma or neurological conditions affecting your sight.

Autorefractor

Quick, precise measurements of your eye's focusing ability give us an accurate starting point for your prescription, making your exam faster and more comfortable.
