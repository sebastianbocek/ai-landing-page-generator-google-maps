Services & Pricing

From routine vision care to complex eye disease management, we offer a full range of optometric services for patients of all ages.

Comprehensive Eye Exams

A comprehensive eye exam goes far beyond reading letters on a chart. We evaluate your entire visual system, from the front of your eye to the back, checking for common conditions and hidden problems that could affect your sight.

  • Visual acuity testing for distance and near vision
  • Refraction to determine your exact prescription
  • Complete eye health evaluation with dilation
  • Glaucoma screening with intraocular pressure check
  • Digital retinal imaging included at no extra charge
  • Color vision and depth perception assessment
Starting at $150

Contact Lens Exam & Fitting

Contact lens prescriptions require additional measurements and evaluations beyond a standard eye exam. We take the time to find the right lens type, fit, and wearing schedule for your specific eyes and lifestyle.

  • Corneal curvature and diameter measurements
  • Trial lens fitting and evaluation
  • Follow-up visit to check fit and comfort
  • Specialty lenses for astigmatism (toric lenses)
  • Multifocal contact lenses for presbyopia
  • Scleral lens fittings for irregular corneas
From $100

Additional to comprehensive exam fee

Eyeglasses & Lenses

Our in-house optical shop carries over 2,000 frames from designer and boutique brands. Our licensed optician, Nicole, personally helps every patient find frames that look great, fit well, and match their prescription needs.

  • Designer and budget-friendly frame options
  • Single vision, bifocal, and progressive lenses
  • Blue light filtering for digital screen use
  • Photochromic (Transitions) lenses available
  • Anti-reflective and scratch-resistant coatings
  • Same-day availability for select prescriptions
Frames from $129

Pediatric Eye Care

Children often don't realize they have a vision problem. Our pediatric eye exams are designed to detect and address issues early, when treatment is most effective. Dr. Park specializes in making kids feel comfortable throughout the process.

  • Age-appropriate eye exams for infants through teens
  • Myopia management and control programs
  • Sports vision evaluations and protective eyewear
  • Lazy eye (amblyopia) detection and treatment
  • Learning-related vision assessments
  • Fun, kid-friendly frame selection
Starting at $125

Eye Disease Management

Many serious eye conditions develop silently. Regular monitoring and early intervention can preserve your vision for years to come. We manage a wide range of ocular diseases and coordinate with specialists when needed.

  • Glaucoma monitoring and treatment
  • Macular degeneration management
  • Diabetic eye disease screenings
  • Dry eye diagnosis and treatment plans
  • Cataracts evaluation and surgical referrals
  • Coordination with ophthalmologists as needed
Pricing Varies

Often covered by medical insurance. Contact us for details.

Emergency Eye Care

Eye emergencies can happen at any time. If you're experiencing sudden vision changes, eye pain, or injury, don't wait. Call us immediately. We reserve same-day slots for urgent eye care needs.

  • Foreign body detection and removal
  • Eye infection diagnosis and treatment
  • Sudden vision changes or loss evaluation
  • Chemical exposure treatment and flush
  • Red eye and inflammation assessment
  • Same-day emergency appointments available
Starting at $200
