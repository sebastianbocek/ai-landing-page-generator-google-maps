Using controlled force, we address subluxations (misalignments) that cause pain, restrict movement, and interfere with nervous system function. Extremity Adjustments

Conditions We Treat

Back Pain & Sciatica

Lower back pain is one of the most common reasons patients seek chiropractic care. Whether caused by poor posture, injury, disc problems, or muscle strain, we provide effective relief. Sciatica treatment addresses the radiating leg pain caused by nerve compression, using specific adjustments and therapies to reduce inflammation and restore function.

Neck Pain & Headaches

Cervical spine adjustments relieve neck pain caused by whiplash, poor posture, sleeping wrong, or chronic tension. Many headaches, including tension headaches and cervicogenic headaches, originate from neck problems and respond excellently to chiropractic care. We also treat migraines with a comprehensive approach addressing spinal alignment and trigger points.

Sports Injuries

From weekend warriors to competitive athletes, we treat strains, sprains, tendinitis, and overuse injuries. Our sports-focused approach combines adjustments with Active Release Technique, rehabilitation exercises, and performance optimization strategies to get you back in the game stronger.

Auto Accident Injuries

Whiplash and other auto injury symptoms often don't appear immediately. We provide thorough evaluation and treatment for post-accident pain, stiffness, and dysfunction. Documentation provided for insurance claims. Most auto insurance policies cover chiropractic care.

Work-Related Injuries

Repetitive strain injuries, lifting injuries, and postural problems from desk work. We treat carpal tunnel syndrome, rotator cuff issues, and work-related back problems. Workers' compensation cases accepted.

Arthritis & Joint Pain

While we can't cure arthritis, chiropractic care significantly improves joint mobility, reduces pain, and helps maintain function. Gentle adjustments combined with exercises help you stay active despite arthritic changes.

Complementary Therapies

Electrical Stimulation (E-Stim)

$25

Therapeutic electrical current reduces muscle spasms, decreases inflammation, and promotes healing. Particularly effective for acute injuries and chronic muscle tension. Patients typically describe a pleasant tingling or massaging sensation.

Therapeutic Ultrasound

$30

Sound waves penetrate deep into tissues to reduce inflammation, break up scar tissue, and accelerate healing. Excellent for tendinitis, bursitis, and chronic soft tissue injuries.

Mechanical Traction

$35

Gentle stretching of the spine relieves pressure on compressed discs and nerves. Highly effective for disc herniations, sciatica, and chronic neck pain. Creates space between vertebrae to reduce nerve irritation.

Cold Laser Therapy

$40

Low-level laser therapy stimulates cellular healing and reduces inflammation at the source. Non-invasive, painless treatment for both acute and chronic conditions. Accelerates tissue repair and reduces pain.

Soft Tissue Work (ART/Graston)

$45

Advanced techniques addressing muscle adhesions, scar tissue, and fascial restrictions that limit movement and cause pain. Active Release Technique and Graston instrument-assisted therapy break down problematic tissue and restore normal function.

Rehabilitation & Exercise Therapy

Therapeutic Exercise Programs

Included

Customized exercise prescriptions to strengthen weak areas, improve flexibility, and prevent re-injury. We teach you specific stretches and strengthening exercises you can do at home to maintain and improve your results between visits.

Posture & Ergonomic Training

Included

Core Stabilization

Included

A strong core protects your spine and prevents injury. We teach proper core activation and progressive exercises to build the stability needed for pain-free movement and activity.

Specialized Care

Prenatal Chiropractic (Webster Technique)

$70

Pregnancy places unique stress on the body. Gentle, pregnancy-specific adjustments relieve back pain, hip pain, and round ligament discomfort. The Webster Technique focuses on pelvic alignment to support optimal fetal positioning. Safe throughout pregnancy.

Pediatric Chiropractic

$45

Gentle care for children of all ages. We treat growing pains, sports injuries, postural issues, and support healthy development. Adjustments for children use minimal force appropriate for their size and age.

Senior Care

$60

Modified techniques appropriate for older adults. We help maintain mobility, reduce pain from arthritis and degenerative changes, and improve balance to prevent falls. Gentle approaches ensure comfort and safety.
