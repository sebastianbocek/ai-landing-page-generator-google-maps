About Align Wellness

Meet our team and learn about our approach to chiropractic care

Dr. Steven Martinez, D.C.

Doctor of Chiropractic | Certified Sports Physician | Active Release Technique Certified

Dr. Martinez founded Align Wellness Chiropractic in 2009 with a simple mission: help people heal naturally and return to the active lifestyles they love. With over 15 years of experience treating everyone from weekend warriors to professional athletes, he brings both expertise and genuine compassion to every patient interaction.

After experiencing his own sports injury in college that traditional medicine couldn't resolve, Dr. Martinez discovered chiropractic care. The transformation was so profound that he changed his pre-med major and pursued a Doctor of Chiropractic degree at Palmer College of Chiropractic, graduating summa cum laude in 2008.

"I don't just treat symptoms. I work to understand the root cause of your pain and create a comprehensive plan to not only relieve it but prevent it from returning. Every patient deserves to live without pain limiting what they can do."

Treatment Methods

Techniques We Use

We're trained in multiple chiropractic techniques and select the best approach for each patient's needs and comfort level.

Diversified Technique

The most commonly used chiropractic technique. Precise manual adjustments using quick, controlled thrusts to restore proper movement and alignment to the spine and joints. Effective for most conditions and suitable for patients of all ages.

Activator Method

A gentle, low-force technique using a specialized instrument to deliver precise adjustments. Ideal for patients who prefer a softer approach, including seniors, children, and those with acute pain or osteoporosis.

Thompson Drop Table

Uses a specialized table with sections that drop slightly during adjustment. This adds momentum to the adjustment while minimizing the force required. Comfortable and effective for a wide range of conditions.

Active Release Technique (ART)

Advanced soft tissue treatment that addresses muscle adhesions, scar tissue, and fascial restrictions. Particularly effective for sports injuries, repetitive strain, and chronic muscle tension.

Graston Technique

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization that breaks down scar tissue and fascial restrictions. Accelerates healing and improves range of motion for chronic soft tissue conditions.

Webster Technique

Specialized prenatal chiropractic care focused on pelvic alignment. Helps reduce discomfort during pregnancy and may contribute to optimal fetal positioning for birth.
