Our Classes

Move With Intention

From energizing flows to deeply restorative practices, find the class that meets you exactly where you are today.

Vinyasa Flow

Intermediate

Dynamic sequences synchronized with breath. Build strength, flexibility, and focus through continuous, flowing movement.

Hatha Yoga

All Levels

The foundation of all yoga styles. Hold poses longer, perfect your alignment, and cultivate mindfulness at a gentler pace.

Yin Yoga

All Levels

Slow, meditative practice targeting deep connective tissues. Hold poses for 3-5 minutes to release tension and increase flexibility.

Restorative

All Levels

Complete relaxation through fully supported poses. Activate your parasympathetic nervous system and melt away stress.

Hot Yoga

Advanced

Practiced in our heated room (95-100°F). Increase flexibility, detoxify through sweat, and challenge your edge.

Prenatal Yoga

All Levels

Safe, nurturing practice for expecting mothers. Build strength for labor, ease common discomforts, and connect with your baby.

Why Yoga

Transform Mind, Body & Spirit

Strength

Build functional strength that supports your body in daily life and prevents injury.

Stress Relief

Activate your relaxation response and cultivate tools to manage life's challenges.

Mindfulness

Develop present-moment awareness that extends far beyond the mat.
