Our Classes

Explore Your Practice

Each class style offers unique benefits. Try them all to discover what resonates with your body and goals.

Vinyasa Flow

Intermediate

Dynamic, breath-synchronized movement linking poses in continuous flow. Build heat, strength, and cardiovascular endurance while cultivating moving meditation.

Duration: 60-75 min
Best for: Building strength, improving focus, stress relief

Hatha Yoga

All Levels

The foundation of all yoga styles. Hold poses longer to perfect alignment, build strength, and develop body awareness at a gentle, accessible pace.

Duration: 60 min
Best for: Beginners, alignment focus, mindful movement

Yin Yoga

All Levels

Slow, meditative practice targeting deep connective tissues. Hold floor-based poses for 3-5 minutes to release tension, increase flexibility, and calm the nervous system.

Duration: 75 min
Best for: Flexibility, stress relief, athletes, injury recovery

Restorative Yoga

All Levels

Complete relaxation through fully supported poses using props. Activate your parasympathetic nervous system, reduce cortisol, and experience profound rest.

Duration: 75 min
Best for: Deep relaxation, anxiety relief, burnout recovery

Hot Yoga

Intermediate-Advanced

Practiced in our heated room (95-100°F). The heat increases flexibility, promotes detoxification through sweat, and adds cardiovascular challenge to your practice.

Duration: 60-75 min
Best for: Detox, deep stretching, athletic training

Prenatal Yoga

All Levels

Safe, nurturing practice designed specifically for pregnancy. Build strength for labor, ease common discomforts, and connect with your baby through mindful movement.

Duration: 60 min
Best for: Expecting mothers (all trimesters)

Power Yoga

Advanced

Athletic, fast-paced vinyasa with emphasis on strength-building poses. Expect core work, arm balances, and inversions. Come ready to sweat and be challenged.

Duration: 60 min
Best for: Fitness enthusiasts, athletes, experienced yogis

Gentle Flow

All Levels

Slow, accessible movement perfect for beginners or anyone seeking a lighter practice. Focus on breath awareness and foundational poses with plenty of modifications.

Duration: 60 min
Best for: Beginners, seniors, injury recovery

Meditation & Breathwork

All Levels

Guided meditation and pranayama (breathing techniques). No physical practice—simply sit, breathe, and cultivate inner stillness and mental clarity.

Duration: 30-45 min
Best for: Stress management, mental clarity, spiritual practice
