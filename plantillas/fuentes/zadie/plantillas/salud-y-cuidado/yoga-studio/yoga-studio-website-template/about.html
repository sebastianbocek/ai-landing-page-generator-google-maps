Our Story

Zenith was born from a simple belief: yoga should be accessible, welcoming, and transformative for every body.

Our Beginning

From Dream to Reality

In 2018, after years of teaching in studios across the country, Maya Chen returned to her hometown of Boulder with a vision: to create a yoga space that felt less like a gym and more like a home.

She found a sun-drenched space in an old brick building downtown, gathered a small team of passionate teachers, and opened Zenith Yoga & Wellness with just two classes a day and a dozen loyal students.

Today, we offer over 40 classes weekly and have welcomed thousands of practitioners through our doors—from complete beginners to advanced yogis, from athletes to seniors, from expecting mothers to anyone seeking a moment of peace.

What hasn't changed is our founding intention: to meet every person exactly where they are and guide them gently toward their own unique expression of wellness.

Our Philosophy

What We Believe

Inclusive

Every body is a yoga body. We adapt practices to meet you, not the other way around. Props, modifications, and variations are always offered.

Non-Competitive

Your mat is your own space. We encourage turning inward, honoring your limits, and celebrating your unique journey without comparison.

Mind-Body Connection

Yoga is more than exercise. We integrate breath, movement, and mindfulness to create a holistic practice that nourishes all aspects of being.

Community First

We're more than a studio—we're a sangha. Connection, support, and authentic relationships are at the heart of everything we do.

The Studio

Your Sanctuary

Step through our doors and leave the outside world behind. Our space is designed to nurture your practice and calm your mind.

Main Practice Room

Our largest space features soaring 14-foot ceilings, natural hardwood floors, and floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the room with natural light. Accommodates up to 25 practitioners.

Heated Studio

Purpose-built for hot yoga with radiant floor heating, specialized ventilation, and humidity control. Maintains a consistent 95-100°F for optimal practice.

Amenities

Full changing rooms with showers, complimentary toiletries, hair dryers, and cubbies for your belongings. Arrive rushed, leave refreshed.

Props Library

High-quality mats, blocks, straps, bolsters, blankets, and eye pillows available for every class. We provide everything you need—just bring yourself.

Boutique

Curated selection of yoga essentials, sustainable activewear, aromatherapy products, and wellness books. Support local makers and small brands.

Tea Lounge

Linger after class in our cozy lounge. Enjoy complimentary herbal tea, connect with fellow practitioners, or simply sit in peaceful reflection.

Our Values

Practicing Sustainability

We believe in ahimsa (non-harm) not just on the mat, but in how we care for our planet.

Zero Waste Goal

We've eliminated single-use plastics, offer water refill stations, and compost all organic materials.

Solar Powered

100% of our electricity comes from rooftop solar panels installed in 2021.

Eco-Friendly Products

Our props, cleaning supplies, and boutique products are selected for sustainability and non-toxicity.

Community Giving

5% of profits support local environmental organizations and bring free yoga to underserved communities.
