Complete Dental Care for Every Smile

From routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic procedures, we offer comprehensive services to meet all your family's dental needs.

Foundation of Oral Health

Preventive Care

Regular preventive care is the key to maintaining healthy teeth and gums, preventing problems before they start, and catching issues early when they're easiest to treat.

Dental Cleanings & Exams

Professional teeth cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, comprehensive oral examination, and early detection of potential issues.

  • Thorough plaque and tartar removal
  • Comprehensive oral health examination
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gum disease evaluation
  • Tooth decay detection
  • Personalized home care recommendations

Recommended: Every 6 months

Digital X-Rays

State-of-the-art digital radiography with 90% less radiation to detect problems invisible to the naked eye.

  • Low-radiation digital imaging
  • Instant image processing
  • Cavity detection between teeth
  • Bone loss evaluation
  • Infection identification
  • Wisdom tooth positioning

Recommended: Annually or as needed

Fluoride Treatments

Professional-strength fluoride application to strengthen enamel and prevent tooth decay, especially beneficial for children and high-risk patients.

  • Strengthens tooth enamel
  • Prevents cavities and decay
  • Reverses early decay
  • Quick, painless application
  • Safe for all ages
  • Especially important for children

Recommended: Every 6-12 months

Dental Sealants

Protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to prevent cavities in the deep grooves and pits.

  • Protects vulnerable tooth surfaces
  • Prevents cavities in molars
  • Quick, painless procedure
  • Lasts several years with care
  • Ideal for children and teens
  • Can benefit adults too

Ideal for: Children ages 6-14

Restore Function & Health

Restorative Dentistry

When decay or damage occurs, we offer a full range of restorative treatments to return your teeth to full function and appearance.

Fillings

Tooth-colored composite resin fillings that blend naturally with your smile to repair cavities and minor damage.

  • Natural-looking composite material
  • Mercury-free option
  • Preserves more natural tooth structure
  • Bonds directly to tooth
  • Same-day treatment
  • Durable and long-lasting

Crowns & Bridges

Custom-crafted restorations to rebuild damaged teeth or replace missing ones, restoring both function and aesthetics.

  • CEREC same-day crowns available
  • Natural-looking porcelain
  • Strengthens weakened teeth
  • Replaces missing teeth
  • Improves bite and chewing
  • Long-lasting solution

Dental Implants

The gold standard for tooth replacement - permanent, natural-looking solutions that function just like real teeth.

  • Most natural tooth replacement
  • Prevents bone loss
  • Doesn't affect adjacent teeth
  • Permanent solution
  • Eat and speak normally
  • 95% success rate

Dentures

Full and partial dentures custom-designed to restore your smile, speech, and ability to eat comfortably.

  • Custom-fitted to your mouth
  • Full or partial options
  • Natural appearance
  • Improves speech and eating
  • Supports facial structure
  • Removable or implant-supported

Root Canal Therapy

Gentle, modern root canal treatment to save infected teeth and eliminate pain - not cause it!

  • Saves your natural tooth
  • Eliminates infection and pain
  • Virtually painless procedure
  • Single-visit treatment often possible
  • Prevents extraction
  • High success rate
Transform Your Smile

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile's appearance with our advanced cosmetic procedures. Whether subtle improvements or complete smile makeovers, we can help you achieve the smile you've always wanted.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening systems that safely brighten your smile several shades in just one visit or at home.

  • In-office power whitening
  • Take-home custom trays
  • Results in 1-2 weeks
  • Safe for enamel
  • Long-lasting brightness
  • Removes years of stains

Starting at: $399 (take-home) | $599 (in-office)

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain shells that cover the front of teeth to create a flawless, natural-looking smile.

  • Corrects multiple imperfections
  • Natural appearance
  • Stain-resistant material
  • Minimally invasive
  • Long-lasting results (10-15 years)
  • Custom color matching

Investment: $1,200-$2,000 per tooth

Cosmetic Bonding

Tooth-colored resin expertly applied to repair chips, close gaps, or reshape teeth - often in a single visit.

  • Fixes chips and cracks
  • Closes small gaps
  • Reshapes irregular teeth
  • Covers discoloration
  • Completed in one visit
  • Conservative approach

Starting at: $300-$600 per tooth

  • Combines multiple procedures
  • Digital smile design preview
  • Addresses all concerns
  • Dramatic, beautiful results
  • Flexible payment plans

Orthodontic Services

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth without metal braces using custom, clear aligners that are virtually invisible and removable for eating and cleaning.

  • Virtually invisible treatment
  • Remove for eating and cleaning
  • More comfortable than braces
  • Fewer office visits required
  • Treat most alignment issues

  • Teen and adult options

We're Here When You Need Us

Emergency & Additional Services

Emergency Dental Care

Same-day appointments for dental emergencies including severe pain, broken teeth, knocked-out teeth, and infections. Call immediately - we'll get you in quickly.

Call: (555) 789-SMILE

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, child-friendly dental care starting from age 1. We make visits fun and establish healthy habits early. Board-certified pediatric specialist on staff.

Sedation Dentistry

Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation options for anxious patients or complex procedures. Wake up with your dental work complete and no memory of treatment.

TMJ & Sleep Apnea

Treatment for temporomandibular joint disorders and custom oral appliances for sleep apnea and snoring. Non-invasive solutions for better sleep and pain relief.

Sports Guards

Custom-fitted mouthguards to protect teeth during sports and athletic activities. More comfortable and protective than store-bought options.

Night Guards

Custom guards to protect teeth from grinding and clenching during sleep. Prevent wear, sensitivity, and jaw pain from bruxism.
