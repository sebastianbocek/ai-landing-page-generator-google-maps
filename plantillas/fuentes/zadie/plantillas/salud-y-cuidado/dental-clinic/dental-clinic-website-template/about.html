Our Story

A Practice Built on Care & Trust

Bright Smile Family Dentistry was founded in 2004 by Dr. Michael Chen with a simple mission: to provide exceptional dental care in a compassionate, patient-centered environment. What started as a small practice with one dentist and two staff members has grown into a thriving dental home for thousands of families.

Our philosophy has always been simple: treat every patient the way we'd want our own family treated. We believe dental care should never be rushed, intimidating, or uncomfortable. Instead, we focus on building lasting relationships, taking time to listen to your concerns, and creating personalized treatment plans that fit your needs and budget.

Today, our practice continues to grow while maintaining the same warm, personal touch that our patients have come to expect. We're proud to serve multiple generations of families and grateful for the trust our community has placed in us.

Lead Dentist & Practice Owner

With over 20 years of experience, he specializes in comprehensive family dentistry and is known for his gentle chairside manner. Dr. Chen is dedicated to continuing education and holds advanced certifications in dental implants and sedation dentistry.
