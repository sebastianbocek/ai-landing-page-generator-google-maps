FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

What financing options do you offer?

We work with multiple lenders to secure competitive rates starting at 4.99% APR with terms up to 20 years. Our finance director Tammy Nguyen handles all paperwork and works with all credit situations. Pre-approval is available before you even visit the lot.

How does the trade-in process work?

Bring your current RV to our lot and we'll perform a free appraisal, usually within an hour. We consider condition, mileage, market demand, and maintenance history. Trade-in value is applied directly to your new purchase, reducing the amount you need to finance.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we deliver throughout Colorado and to neighboring states. Delivery fees vary based on distance. For travel trailers and fifth wheels, we can tow directly to your home or campsite. Motorhomes can be driven to your location by one of our licensed drivers.

How do I schedule a service appointment?

You can schedule service by calling our service department at (303) 555-1235, emailing service@openroadrv.com, or using the contact form on this page. We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance, though we do accommodate same-day emergencies when possible.

What's included in the purchase price?

Every RV purchase includes a full pre-delivery inspection, propane fill, battery charge, walk-through orientation with a technician, and all standard equipment listed on the unit. Taxes, registration, and optional add-ons like extended warranties are separate.

What RV is best for beginners?

For first-time buyers, we usually recommend a travel trailer in the 22-28 foot range. They're easy to tow with a half-ton truck, affordable to maintain, and offer enough space for a family of four. Come walk our lot and we'll help you narrow it down based on your tow vehicle and camping style.

Ready to Hit the Open Road?

Stop by our Denver lot, give us a call, or send a message. No appointment needed to browse our 20-acre inventory.
