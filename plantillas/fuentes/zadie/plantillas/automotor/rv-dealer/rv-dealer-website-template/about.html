Our Story

Family-owned, customer-focused, and passionate about the RV lifestyle since 2005.

Who We Are

Born on the Open Road

OpenRoad RV Center was founded by Mike and Sarah Brennan, lifelong RV enthusiasts who spent years traveling the country in everything from pop-up campers to Class A motorhomes. After decades of buying, selling, and living the RV lifestyle, they decided to open their own dealership in Denver, Colorado.

What started as a small lot with 15 pre-owned units has grown into one of Colorado's top RV dealerships. Today, we sit on over 20 acres with hundreds of new and pre-owned RVs, a full-service center, and a parts department stocked with everything you need for life on the road.

But our size hasn't changed our approach. Every customer who walks onto our lot is treated like a neighbor, not a number. We take the time to understand what you need, match you with the right RV, and support you long after the sale is done.

Our Values

How We Do Business

Three principles that guide every interaction at OpenRoad RV Center.

No-Pressure Sales

We will never rush you or push you toward a unit that doesn't fit your needs. Take your time, ask every question, and walk the lot as many times as you want. We're here to help when you're ready.

Transparent Pricing

Our prices are posted clearly on every unit. No hidden fees, no last-minute add-ons at the finance desk. The price you see is the price you pay, and we're happy to walk you through every line item.

Lifetime Support

The sale is just the beginning of our relationship. We're here for you years down the road with service, parts, upgrade advice, and even help trading up when you're ready for your next adventure.

Built for the RV Lifestyle

20-Acre Lot

Hundreds of RVs on display with clear signage, paved paths, and plenty of room to hitch up and test tow before you buy.
