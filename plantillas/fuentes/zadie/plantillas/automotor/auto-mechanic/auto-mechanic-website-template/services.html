Common Questions

Answers to questions we hear most often.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the repair. Oil changes take about 30 minutes. Brake jobs typically take 1-2 hours per axle. For larger repairs, we'll give you an estimate when we diagnose the issue. We'll also call you before starting any work to confirm the timeline.

What's covered under your warranty?

We offer a 24-month/24,000-mile warranty on parts and labor for most repairs. This covers defects in parts and our workmanship. Some specialty parts may have different coverage. We'll explain the warranty terms before any work begins.

Do you use OEM or aftermarket parts?

We use OEM-equivalent parts that meet or exceed factory specifications. If you specifically want OEM parts, just let us know and we'll source them. We never use low-quality parts that might fail prematurely.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For larger repairs, we offer financing through Synchrony Car Care. You can apply in-shop or online. We also accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and cash.

Can I wait while my car is serviced?

Absolutely. Our waiting area has comfortable seating, free WiFi, coffee, and a TV. For longer repairs, we also offer a free shuttle service within 10 miles of the shop.
