A Shop You Can Trust

When Frank Martinez opened Honest Wrench in 1985, he had one simple goal: treat every customer the way he'd want to be treated. That meant honest diagnoses, fair prices, and repairs done right the first time.

Nearly four decades later, that philosophy hasn't changed. Now run by the second generation, we've grown from a two-bay garage to a full-service facility, but we've never lost sight of what matters most: earning your trust, one repair at a time.
