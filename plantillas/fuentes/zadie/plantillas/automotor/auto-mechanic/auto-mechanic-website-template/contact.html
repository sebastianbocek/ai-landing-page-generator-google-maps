Before Your Visit

What to know before your appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended but not required for most services. Walk-ins are welcome, though wait times may be longer. For major repairs or specific time slots, we recommend booking ahead.

Can I drop off my car early?

Yes. We have a secure key drop box near our front door. Just fill out the envelope with your contact info and vehicle details, drop your keys inside, and we'll call you when we've looked at it.

Do you offer loaner cars or shuttle service?

We offer a free shuttle service within 10 miles of the shop. We can drop you off at home or work and pick you up when your car is ready. Loaner vehicles are available for major repairs by appointment.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover). For larger repairs, we also offer financing through Synchrony Car Care.

Will you call before doing any work?

Always. We'll diagnose the issue, explain what we found, and give you a detailed estimate. We won't turn a wrench until you've approved the work. No surprises, no unauthorized repairs.
