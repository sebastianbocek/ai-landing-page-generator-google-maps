Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from drivers who need our help.

How fast can you get to me?

Our average response time is 30 minutes or less within our primary service area. In most cases, we have a truck rolling within 5 minutes of your call. Response times may vary during severe weather or high-volume periods, but we always give you an honest ETA upfront.

Do you accept my insurance?

Yes. We work with all major auto insurance providers and are an authorized AAA service provider. In most cases, we can bill your insurance company directly so you don't have to pay out of pocket and wait for reimbursement.

How much does a tow cost?

Light-duty towing starts at $75 for the hookup plus $3.50 per mile. Flatbed service starts at $95 plus $4.00 per mile. Heavy-duty starts at $150 plus $5.00 per mile. We always quote a total price before we dispatch so there are no surprises.

Can you tow my car to a specific shop?

Absolutely. You choose the destination. We'll tow your vehicle to any repair shop, dealership, body shop, or private address you specify. If you're not sure where to go, our drivers can recommend trusted local shops.

Do you offer long-distance towing?

Yes. While our primary service area covers a 50-mile radius, we regularly handle long-distance tows across the state and beyond. Call our dispatch team for a custom quote on any long-distance job.

What if I'm not with my vehicle?

That's fine. As long as we can access the vehicle and you authorize the tow, we can pick it up without you present. We'll need your name, the exact location, vehicle description, keys (if available), and the drop-off address. We send photo confirmation when the job is complete.
