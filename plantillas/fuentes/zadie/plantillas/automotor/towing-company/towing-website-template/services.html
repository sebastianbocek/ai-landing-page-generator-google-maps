Towing & Roadside Assistance

From a flat tire to a 50-ton recovery, we have the trucks, the tools, and the training to handle it. Every service available 24/7.

Light-Duty Towing

Our most-requested service. Light-duty towing covers standard passenger vehicles, SUVs, crossovers, and small trucks up to 10,000 lbs. Whether you've been in a fender bender, your engine quit on the highway, or you need a vehicle relocated across town, our light-duty fleet gets the job done without damage.

  • Cars, SUVs, crossovers, and small trucks
  • Accident recovery and breakdown towing
  • Soft-strap wheel-lift systems to prevent damage
  • Direct-to-shop or home delivery available
From $75

Heavy-Duty Towing

When standard tow trucks aren't enough, our heavy-duty division steps in. We operate 50-ton rotator wreckers and heavy-recovery units capable of handling semi-trucks, buses, RVs, construction equipment, and large commercial vehicles. Our heavy-duty operators carry additional certifications and years of specialized experience.

  • Semi-trucks, trailers, and tractor units
  • Buses, RVs, and motorhomes
  • Construction and industrial equipment
  • Load shifts, rollovers, and accident recovery
From $150

Flatbed Transport

Flatbed towing is the safest way to move a vehicle. The entire car rides on the bed of the truck with zero wheel contact on the road. This is the standard for luxury vehicles, classics, all-wheel-drive cars, and any vehicle with significant damage that makes conventional towing risky.

  • Zero road contact during transport
  • Required for AWD and low-clearance vehicles
  • Long-distance and interstate transport
  • Ideal for luxury, classic, and exotic cars
From $95

Our accident recovery teams work with law enforcement, insurance adjusters, and body shops to get vehicles off the road safely and documented properly. We handle everything from minor collisions to multi-vehicle incidents.

  • Direct billing to insurance companies
  • Debris cleanup and scene clearing
  • Secure storage available for damaged vehicles

Roadside Assistance

More Than Just Towing

Not every roadside problem needs a tow truck. Our service vehicles carry the tools and supplies to fix many issues right where you are.

Lockout Service

Locked your keys in the car? Our technicians use professional locksmith tools for non-destructive entry. Most vehicles opened in under 15 minutes. No damage to your door or lock.

Jump Starts

Dead battery getting you nowhere? We bring commercial-grade jump packs that work on everything from compact cars to diesel trucks. Back on the road in minutes.

Tire Changes

Flat tire on the shoulder? We swap it for your spare and make sure it's torqued to spec. No spare? We can tow you to the nearest tire shop or arrange a mobile tire delivery.

Fuel Delivery

Ran out of gas? We deliver enough fuel to get you to the nearest station. Available for gasoline and diesel vehicles. No need to walk miles down the highway.

Minor Roadside Repairs

Some breakdowns have simple fixes. Loose battery cables, overheating, blown fuses. Our operators carry basic tools and supplies to attempt on-site repairs before resorting to a tow.

Winch-Out Service

Stuck in a ditch, mud, snow, or sand? Our trucks are equipped with heavy-duty winch systems to pull your vehicle back onto solid ground safely and without body damage.
