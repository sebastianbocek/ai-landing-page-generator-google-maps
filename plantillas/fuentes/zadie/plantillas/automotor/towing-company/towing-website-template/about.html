About Us

Built on Reliability, Driven by Service

For over 15 years, Rapid Response Towing has been the name drivers trust when they're stuck. We built this company one call at a time.

Started with One Truck and a Simple Promise

Rapid Response Towing started in 2009 with a single tow truck and a commitment that every stranded driver deserves fast, honest help. Founder Mike Torres spent a decade working for other towing companies before launching his own. He knew the industry inside out and he knew what most companies got wrong: slow response, hidden fees, and dispatchers who treated every call like an inconvenience.

Mike built Rapid Response around a different idea. Answer every call in under a minute. Have a truck rolling in five. Arrive in thirty. Charge a fair price and explain every dollar. That approach turned one truck into a fleet of twenty-three, and a one-person operation into a team of forty professionals who show up when it matters most.

Today, we serve the entire metro area and surrounding counties. We handle everything from a dead battery in a grocery store parking lot to a jackknifed semi on the interstate. The trucks are bigger, but the promise is the same: we get there fast, we do the job right, and we treat every customer the way we'd want to be treated on the worst day of their commute.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They're the standards every driver on our team is held to, every shift, every call.

Fast Response

Speed saves. When you're stranded on a dark highway or blocking traffic, every minute counts. Our dispatch system is built for speed. We track every truck in real time so the closest available unit gets your call. Average response: 30 minutes or less.

Fair Pricing

No surprises on the invoice. We quote a price before we roll, and that's the price you pay. Our rates are competitive and transparent. We list our base hookup fees and per-mile charges on our website because we have nothing to hide.

Professional Operators

Every driver on our team is licensed, insured, background-checked, and trained in damage-free towing. They show up in uniform, introduce themselves, and explain the process before they touch your vehicle. Professionalism isn't optional here.

Our Fleet

Equipped for Every Situation

Our fleet of 23 specialized vehicles means we always have the right truck for the job, whether it's a compact car or a loaded semi.

Light-Duty Wreckers

Our light-duty fleet handles cars, SUVs, and small trucks up to 10,000 lbs. Equipped with wheel-lift systems and soft straps to prevent damage during transport.

Flatbed Carriers

Hydraulic tilt-bed carriers for zero-contact transport. The preferred choice for luxury vehicles, classics, all-wheel drive, and low-clearance cars.

Heavy-Duty Wreckers

50-ton rotator wreckers and heavy-duty trucks for commercial vehicles, buses, RVs, and equipment. Operated by certified heavy-recovery specialists.
