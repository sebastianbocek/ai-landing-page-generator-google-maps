FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a detail take?

Detail times vary by service. Essential packages take 2-3 hours, Premium packages 4-6 hours, and Elite packages with paint correction and ceramic coating can take 1-2 days. We'll provide an accurate timeframe when you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, we work by appointment only to ensure each vehicle receives the time and attention it deserves. This also allows us to prepare the appropriate products and schedule our certified technicians.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, and digital payments including Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payment is due upon completion of service.

Do you detail all types of vehicles?

Yes, we detail everything from compact cars to luxury SUVs, exotic sports cars, classic collectibles, and even motorcycles. Our team has experience with all makes, models, and specialty finishes.

How often should I detail my car?

We recommend full detailing every 3-6 months depending on use and storage conditions. Premium or Elite packages annually, with Essential maintenance details in between, keeps your vehicle in top condition.

Is ceramic coating worth it?

Absolutely. Ceramic coating provides years of protection, maintains gloss longer than traditional wax, and makes regular washing easier. For vehicles you plan to keep long-term or high-value cars, it's an excellent investment.
