Interior Detailing

Your vehicle's interior deserves the same attention as its exterior. Our comprehensive interior detailing service deep cleans every surface, removes stains and odors, and restores materials to their original condition.

We use specialized tools and premium products designed for automotive surfaces, ensuring safe and effective cleaning of leather, fabric, vinyl, plastics, and glass. The result is a cabin that looks, smells, and feels brand new.

  • Vacuum all surfaces & crevices
  • Deep carpet shampooing
  • Leather cleaning & conditioning
  • Dashboard & trim detailing
  • Door jamb cleaning
  • Interior glass polishing
  • Odor elimination treatment
  • UV protection application

Exterior Detailing

Our exterior detailing service goes far beyond a basic car wash. We employ a multi-stage process that safely removes contaminants, enhances gloss, and applies durable protection to keep your vehicle looking stunning.

From hand washing with pH-balanced soap to clay bar treatment and machine polishing, every step is designed to bring out the best in your paint. We finish with premium wax or sealant for long-lasting protection and shine.

  • Two-bucket hand wash
  • Clay bar decontamination
  • Machine polishing
  • Premium wax or sealant
  • Wheel & tire detailing
  • Chrome & trim polishing
  • Glass cleaning & treatment
  • Tire dressing application

Paint Correction

Over time, your vehicle's paint accumulates swirls, scratches, oxidation, and other imperfections that dull its appearance. Our professional paint correction service uses advanced machine polishing to remove these defects and restore clarity.

We assess your paint's condition and select the appropriate compounds and pads to safely remove imperfections while preserving clear coat thickness. The result is a mirror-like finish that reveals your paint's true color and depth.

  • Paint thickness measurement
  • Multi-stage correction
  • Swirl & scratch removal
  • Oxidation elimination
  • Water spot removal
  • Final jeweling polish
  • Paint depth enhancement
  • Before/after documentation

Ceramic Coating

Ceramic coating represents the pinnacle of paint protection technology. This liquid polymer chemically bonds with your vehicle's paint to create a permanent protective layer that repels water, resists UV damage, and maintains incredible gloss.

Unlike traditional waxes that last weeks or months, our professional-grade ceramic coatings provide protection for years. The hydrophobic surface makes washing easier and keeps your vehicle looking freshly detailed longer.

  • Full paint correction prep
  • Professional-grade coating
  • Multi-layer application
  • Hydrophobic protection
  • UV resistance
  • Chemical resistance
  • Up to 5 years durability
  • Lifetime maintenance support

Headlight Restoration

Cloudy, yellowed headlights don't just look bad — they significantly reduce nighttime visibility and safety. Our professional restoration process removes oxidation and UV damage to restore crystal-clear lenses.

Using specialized compounds and wet sanding techniques, we remove the damaged outer layer and refinish the lens to optical clarity. We finish with UV protective sealant to prevent future yellowing and keep your headlights clear.

  • Multi-grit wet sanding
  • Compound polishing
  • Final clarity buffing
  • UV protective sealant
  • Improved visibility
  • Enhanced appearance

Engine Bay Detailing

A clean engine bay not only looks impressive, it also makes maintenance easier and can help you spot potential issues. Our careful approach safely cleans and enhances all visible engine components without risking damage to sensitive electronics.

We protect vulnerable components, apply appropriate degreasers and cleaners, and finish with protective dressings that resist heat and dust. The result is an engine bay that looks as good as the rest of your vehicle.

  • Component protection
  • Safe degreasing
  • Detail brushing
  • Low-pressure rinsing
  • Plastic & rubber dressing
  • Metal polishing
