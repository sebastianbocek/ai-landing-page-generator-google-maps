Our Story

Passion Meets Precision

Elite Auto Detailing was founded in 2010 by Marcus Sterling, a lifelong automotive enthusiast who believed that every vehicle deserves to look its absolute best. What started as a one-bay operation has grown into the region's premier destination for discerning car owners who demand excellence.

Today, our team of master detailers brings together decades of combined experience, advanced certifications, and an unwavering commitment to perfection. We've detailed everything from classic collectibles to modern supercars, treating each one with the care and precision it deserves.

  • Family-owned and operated since 2010
  • All technicians are IDA certified professionals
  • Climate-controlled indoor facility
  • Premium products exclusively from top brands
  • Fully insured with liability coverage
