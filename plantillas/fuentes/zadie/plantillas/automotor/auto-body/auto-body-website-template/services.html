Our Services

What We Do and How We Do It

Body Work

Collision Repair

Free estimate — most work insurance-covered

Collision repair at ProFinish covers everything from a scuffed bumper to a completely crushed front or rear end. We assess the full extent of structural and cosmetic damage before writing any estimate, which means you don't get surprise add-ons after the fact.

  • Minor to severe collision damage
  • Front and rear end reconstruction
  • Side impact panel work
  • Bumper replacement or repair
  • Fender, hood, and door replacement
  • Headlight and tail light restoration
  • Trim and molding replacement
  • Airbag system coordination
Dent Repair

Paintless Dent Repair (PDR)

Starts at $75 per dent — no paint, no filler

PDR is the ideal solution for dents and dings where the paint is intact. We use specialized metal-shaping tools to push or pull the dent from behind the panel, restoring the original contour without any body filler or repainting. This is faster, less expensive, and preserves your original factory finish.

  • Door dings and parking lot dents
  • Hail damage repair
  • Shopping cart impact dents
  • Minor crease dents
  • Round impact dents up to 6 inches
  • Rooftop and hood dents
Dent Repair

Conventional Dent & Body Repair

Quote provided after assessment

When paint has been damaged or the dent is too deep or complex for PDR, we use conventional body repair methods. This involves reshaping metal, applying filler where necessary, and refinishing the panel so the repair is completely invisible.

  • Deep crease and fold repair
  • Paint-broken dent repair
  • Rusted panel restoration
  • Plastic bumper reconstruction
  • Panel alignment correction
  • Filler application and blocking
Refinishing

Paint Services

Spot repair from $250 — Full respray by quote

Every paint job that leaves our shop is color-matched using a spectrophotometer reading of your vehicle's actual finish, adjusted for fading and weathering. We use PPG Envirobase HP waterborne paint, which produces a cleaner, more durable finish and meets modern environmental standards.

  • Computer-matched color mixing
  • Spot repair and blending
  • Full panel respray
  • Full vehicle respray
  • Tri-coat and pearl finishes
  • Matte and satin clearcoat
  • Factory texture matching
  • Paint correction and polishing
Structural

Frame and Structural Repair

Included in collision repair — Insurance typically covers

Frame and unibody damage affects how your car handles, how your doors close, and whether your vehicle is safe to drive. We use a Chief Genesis measuring system to compare your vehicle's structure against factory specifications down to the millimeter. Corrections are made with our frame pulling equipment before any cosmetic work begins.

  • Full frame rail repair
  • Unibody straightening
  • Computer-measured alignment
  • Structural section replacement
  • Floor pan and rocker work
  • Post-repair alignment verification
Glass

Glass Replacement

Most glass replacement covered by comprehensive insurance

Windshield cracks spread, and compromised glass weakens the roof structure in rollover accidents. We replace windshields, rear glass, and door glass using OEM and OEM-equivalent glass. If your vehicle has ADAS cameras or rain sensors mounted to the windshield, we handle the necessary recalibration in-house.

  • Windshield replacement
  • Rear glass replacement
  • Door glass and quarter glass
  • Sunroof glass
  • ADAS camera recalibration
  • Rain sensor and heated glass
Claims Assistance

No charge — We handle this as part of every repair

We work directly with your insurance company from the first estimate through final payment.   • Diminished value documentation
