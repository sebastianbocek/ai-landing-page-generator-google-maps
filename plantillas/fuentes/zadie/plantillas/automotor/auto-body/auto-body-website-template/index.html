What We Do

Every Type of Repair

From minor dents to major structural damage. We handle the full scope of collision and body repair work.

Collision Repair

Full structural and cosmetic restoration after accidents of any severity. We handle everything from a scraped bumper to total front-end reconstruction.

Computerized Color Matching

Our spectrophotometer reads your vehicle's exact paint code and adjusts for weathering and fade. The repair will be invisible.

Dent Removal

Paintless dent repair (PDR) for minor dings and dents. No filler, no repainting. We reshape the metal back to factory spec.

Frame Straightening

Computer-measured frame and unibody correction using our Chief Genesis measuring system. We restore factory alignment tolerances.

Insurance Claims

We work directly with all major insurance carriers. We handle the paperwork, supplements, and adjuster communication so you don't have to.

Glass Replacement

Windshield, rear glass, and door glass. OEM and OEM-equivalent glass with professional bonding and sealing.
