Our Story

From One Bay to a Full-Service Shop

Gary Voss started ProFinish Collision Center in 2003 with a two-bay shop, one employee, and a belief that St. Louis deserved a collision repair shop that treated every car like their own. He'd spent twelve years working at a dealership body shop and watched too many repairs leave through the door without being done right.

"I got tired of watching guys rush repairs to hit a flat-rate number. I wanted to build a place where the job was done right the first time, every time. That was the whole idea."

— Gary Voss, Owner

The shop grew steadily through referrals. By 2008, ProFinish had expanded to a 12,000-square-foot facility with eight repair bays, a dedicated paint booth, and a frame straightening system. In 2011, they earned I-CAR Gold Class certification. In 2016, they became a State Farm Select Service shop. Today, ProFinish handles over 600 vehicles per year and has won the St. Louis Post-Dispatch's Best Auto Body Shop award three consecutive times.

Gary still walks the shop floor every day and signs off on every finished repair personally. The philosophy hasn't changed: do the job right and stand behind it.
