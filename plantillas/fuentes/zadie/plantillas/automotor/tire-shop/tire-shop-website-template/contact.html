Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you're looking for? Give us a call.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome for tire rotations, oil changes, and quick services. For tire installations, alignments, and brake work, we recommend calling ahead so we can have parts ready and a bay reserved for you.

How long does a tire installation take?

A full set of four tires typically takes 45 minutes to an hour. If you also need an alignment, add about 30 minutes. We'll give you an accurate time estimate when you check in.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes. All labor is covered by our 12-month/12,000-mile warranty. Tires carry their own manufacturer warranty, and we handle warranty claims in-house so you don't have to deal with the paperwork.

Can you match online tire prices?

In most cases, yes. Bring us a quote from a major online retailer for the same tire, and we'll do our best to match or beat it. Plus, our price includes installation, which online retailers charge separately for.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, debit cards, and Apple Pay. For larger jobs, we also offer financing through a third-party provider. Ask the front desk for details.

Do you work on commercial vehicles?

Yes. We service light and medium-duty commercial vehicles including vans, box trucks, and work trucks. For heavy-duty or semi tires, we can refer you to a trusted partner shop nearby.
