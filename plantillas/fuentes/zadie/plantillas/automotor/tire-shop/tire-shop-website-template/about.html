Built on Hard Work & Handshakes

We started in a two-bay garage with one lift and a belief that tire shops didn't have to feel like a gamble. That still drives everything we do.

Our Story

TreadMax started in 2008 when Mike Treadwell left his job at a corporate tire chain because he was tired of watching good people get sold things they didn't need. He opened a two-bay shop on Motor Ave with one goal: do the right job at the right price and let the work speak for itself.

The first year was lean. Word of mouth was slow. But every customer who came in left knowing exactly what they paid for and why. That reputation spread, and the phone started ringing.

Today, TreadMax operates a 6-bay facility with a team of five full-time technicians. We've installed over 40,000 tires, serviced thousands of brakes, and earned a 4.9-star Google rating with over 800 reviews. The shop is bigger, but the approach hasn't changed.

We still explain every repair before we touch a wrench. We still price the job, not the customer. And we still think the best advertising is a customer who comes back.

What We Stand For

Three principles that guide every repair, every quote, and every conversation we have.

No Surprises

We quote upfront and stick to it. If something changes during the job, we call you first. You'll never get a bill that doesn't match what we discussed.

Do It Right

We don't cut corners, skip steps, or use cheap parts to pad margins. Every vehicle gets the same attention, whether it's a new truck or a car with 200,000 miles.

Talk Straight

We'll tell you what needs fixing now and what can wait. We explain things in plain English, not mechanic jargon. Ask questions. That's what we're here for.
