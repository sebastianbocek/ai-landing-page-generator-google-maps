Wheel Alignment

Misaligned wheels cause uneven tire wear, pulling, and reduced fuel economy. Our Hunter HawkEye alignment system provides precision measurements and adjustments down to thousandths of an inch.

We perform a complete 4-wheel alignment with before-and-after printouts so you can see exactly what was adjusted and why.

  • Hunter HawkEye precision equipment
  • Full 4-wheel alignment
  • Camber, caster, and toe adjustments
  • Before-and-after printout included
  • 6-month alignment warranty
From $79 / 4-wheel alignment

Tire Rotation & Balancing

Regular rotation moves tires to different positions on your vehicle to promote even wear and extend tire life. Most manufacturers recommend rotation every 5,000 to 7,500 miles.

We combine rotation with computer balancing to eliminate vibrations and ensure the smoothest possible ride. Quick service. Most vehicles done in under 30 minutes.

  • 4-tire rotation per manufacturer spec
  • Computer spin balancing on all 4 wheels
  • Tread depth measurement and wear check
  • Tire pressure set to spec
  • Visual brake inspection included
From $39 / rotation & balance

Brake Service

Brakes are not something to gamble on. We provide complete brake inspections, pad replacement, rotor resurfacing or replacement, caliper service, and brake fluid flushes.

Every brake job starts with a thorough inspection. We measure rotor thickness and pad depth, then walk you through what's needed before we start any work.

  • Free brake inspection with any service
  • Premium ceramic or semi-metallic pads
  • Rotor resurfacing or replacement
  • Caliper service and hardware replacement
  • Brake fluid flush available
From $149 / axle
