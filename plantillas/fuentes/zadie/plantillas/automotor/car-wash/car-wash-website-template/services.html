Our Process

How It Works

From arrival to departure, we've designed every step for speed, quality, and convenience.

1

Drive In

Pull up to our entrance kiosk. Members scan their RFID tag. Walk-ins choose a wash package on screen.

2

Pre-Soak

Our team applies a pre-soak treatment to loosen dirt, bugs, and road grime before your car enters the tunnel.

3

Tunnel Wash

Your car moves through our 120-foot tunnel with soft-touch brushes, foam applicators, and high-pressure rinse arches.

4

Dry & Finish

Forced-air dryers remove water. Pull into our covered vacuum area to finish up at your own pace.
