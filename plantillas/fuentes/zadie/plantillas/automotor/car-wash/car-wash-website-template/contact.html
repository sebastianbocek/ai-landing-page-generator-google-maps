FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most. Can't find what you need? Just give us a call.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment needed for any wash package. Just drive in during our operating hours (7am-8pm, 7 days a week). For detailing services, we recommend calling ahead or booking online to reserve a time slot.

How long does a wash take?

Express washes take under 10 minutes. Premium and Ultimate washes take about 12-15 minutes through the tunnel. Interior detailing takes about 2 hours, and full detailing takes approximately 4 hours.

Will the wash scratch my paint?

No. We use soft-touch foam brushes and pH-balanced, paint-safe formulas. Our equipment is inspected daily and calibrated weekly. We back every wash with a scratch-free guarantee.

How does the monthly membership work?

Sign up at our kiosk or online, and we'll install an RFID tag on your windshield. Just drive up and the gate opens automatically. Wash as many times as you want during the month. Cancel anytime with no penalties.

Do you wash trucks, SUVs, and vans?

Yes. Our tunnel accommodates vehicles up to 84 inches tall and 96 inches wide, which covers most consumer trucks, SUVs, and full-size vans. Oversized commercial vehicles should contact us about fleet services.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. Monthly memberships are billed to a credit or debit card on file. Fleet accounts can be invoiced monthly.
