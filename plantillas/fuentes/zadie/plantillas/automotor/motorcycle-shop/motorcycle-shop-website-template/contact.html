Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer test rides?

Yes. We offer test rides on all new, in-stock motorcycles. You'll need a valid motorcycle endorsement on your license and proof of insurance. We recommend calling ahead so we can have the bike warmed up and ready when you arrive. No appointment needed for sitting on bikes in the showroom.

What financing options do you have?

We work with several major lenders and credit unions to find the best rate for your situation. We offer financing on new and pre-owned motorcycles with terms ranging from 24 to 84 months. You can apply in-store or over the phone. Most approvals come back within an hour. We welcome all credit levels and will work to find an option that fits your budget.

How does the trade-in process work?

Ride your current bike to the shop and we'll give you a no-obligation appraisal on the spot. We consider condition, mileage, service history, and current market values. If you agree to the trade-in value, we apply it directly to the purchase of your new bike. The whole process takes about 30 minutes. We accept trade-ins on all makes and models, regardless of where you originally bought the bike.

Do I need an appointment for service?

Appointments are recommended, especially for major work. For routine jobs like oil changes and tire service, we can often get you in same-day or next-day. Call or use the contact form to schedule. We'll confirm your appointment and give you an estimated completion time so you can plan your day.

Do you ship motorcycles?

We can arrange shipping anywhere in the continental United States through our vetted transport partners. Shipping costs depend on distance, but most deliveries within a 500-mile radius run between $400 and $700. We handle all the logistics and make sure the bike arrives exactly as it left the showroom. For long-distance buyers, we also offer video walkarounds so you can inspect the bike before committing.

What brands do you carry?

Our new inventory includes motorcycles from several major manufacturers across cruiser, sport, adventure, and dirt categories. Our pre-owned selection is even broader. We've had everything from vintage Japanese bikes to European sport tourers on the floor. If there's a specific make or model you're looking for, let us know. If we don't have it, there's a good chance we can find it through our dealer network.
