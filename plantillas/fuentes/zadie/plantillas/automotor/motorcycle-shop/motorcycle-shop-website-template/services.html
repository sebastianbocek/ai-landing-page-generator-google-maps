Pre-Owned

Certified Pre-Owned Program

Every pre-owned bike at Iron Road goes through a rigorous multi-point inspection before it hits the showroom floor. We check the engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, electrical system, tires, chain or belt, and frame. If something doesn't pass, we fix it or we don't sell it.

Our certified pre-owned bikes come with a 30-day / 1,000-mile powertrain warranty and a full vehicle history report. We stand behind what we sell because our name is on the door.

We also take trade-ins on all makes and models. Ride in on your old bike and ride out on something new. We'll give you a fair, no-pressure appraisal on the spot.
