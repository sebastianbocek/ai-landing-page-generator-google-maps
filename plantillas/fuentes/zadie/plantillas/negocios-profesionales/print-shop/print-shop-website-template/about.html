Our Story

From Design Screen to Print Press

InkWorks Print Studio started in 2016 when our founder, Nina Alvarez, got tired of sending designs to print shops and getting back something that didn't match what was on her screen. Colors were off, paper was wrong, and the attention to detail was nonexistent.

So she opened her own shop. The idea was simple: a print studio run by someone who actually understands design. Someone who knows the difference between Pantone 186 C and "close enough." Someone who checks registration marks and inspects every cut.

What started as a one-person operation in a garage has grown into a full-service studio with a team of five, a suite of professional equipment, and a client list that includes restaurants, law firms, startups, and nonprofits across the region.

We still operate with the same obsession Nina started with: every job gets checked, every color gets matched, and nothing leaves the studio unless it's right.

Our Equipment

The Studio

Come See the Presses Run

InkWorks operates out of a 3,200 square foot open-concept studio where the design workstations sit right next to the presses. It's intentional. When our designers can walk ten feet to check how a color looks on paper, the results are better.

Clients are always welcome to visit. We love giving tours and showing people how their projects move from file to finished product. There's something satisfying about watching a press run -- the precision, the rhythm, the smell of fresh ink on quality stock.

Our front area doubles as a mini showroom with paper samples, finish options, and completed projects you can touch and hold. It's the best way to choose the right materials for your next job.

Sustainability

Printing Responsibly

Good print should not come at the planet's expense. We've built sustainability into every part of our process.

Soy-Based Inks All of our offset printing uses soy-based inks instead of petroleum-based. Better color, lower VOCs, easier recycling.
Recycled Paper Options We stock a range of recycled and FSC-certified paper options. Ask us about eco-friendly alternatives for any project.
Waste Reduction Program Paper trimming and misprints are recycled. We optimize print layouts to minimize waste and track our monthly reduction goals.
