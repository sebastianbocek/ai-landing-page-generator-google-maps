How It Works

From File to Finished Product

Four simple steps. We keep you in the loop at every stage so there are no surprises at pickup.

1

Submit Your Files

Upload your print-ready files or share your ideas. We accept PDF, AI, PSD, and most image formats.

2

Proof & Approve

We send you a digital proof for review. You approve, request changes, or let our designers fine-tune it.

3

We Print

Your project goes to press. Offset, digital, or large format -- we match the right process to your job.

4

Pick Up or Deliver

Swing by the studio to grab your order, or we'll ship it right to your door. Local delivery is always free.
