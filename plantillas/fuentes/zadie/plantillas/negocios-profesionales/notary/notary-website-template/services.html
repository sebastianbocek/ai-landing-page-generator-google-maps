Services & Pricing

Transparent pricing, professional service. From a single notarization to complex loan closings, we have you covered.

General Notarization

Standard notarization of documents including acknowledgments, jurats, oaths, and affirmations. We verify the signer's identity, witness the signature, and apply our official notary seal to make your document legally binding.

$10 per signature (NC statutory rate) + travel fee $25-$50
  • Identity verification with valid government-issued photo ID
  • Official notary seal and signature applied
  • Notary journal entry maintained for your protection
  • Multiple documents can be notarized in a single appointment

Mobile Notary

We come to you -- wherever you are in the Charlotte metro area. Our mobile notaries travel to your home, office, hospital, nursing home, coffee shop, or any other convenient location. You choose the place and time, and we handle the rest.

$25-$75 travel fee based on distance + per-signature fee
  • Service within 25 miles of uptown Charlotte
  • Same-day appointments frequently available
  • Evening and weekend appointments at no extra charge
  • Hospital, jail, and nursing home visits available
  • All notary supplies and stamps brought to your location

Loan Signing Agent

Our NNA Certified Signing Agents are experienced in handling the full range of real estate loan document packages. We work with title companies, lenders, and signing services to ensure every page is signed, initialed, and dated correctly -- keeping your closing on schedule.

$150-$200 per signing
  • Purchase closings, refinances, HELOCs, and reverse mortgages
  • NNA Certified Signing Agents with background checks
  • Same-day and next-day closings available
  • Document printing, scanning, and return shipping included
  • Familiar with all major title company document packages

Remote Online Notarization (RON)

Complete your notarization from anywhere using secure audio-video technology. No need to meet in person -- you connect with our notary through a secure online platform, verify your identity, and sign your documents electronically. Legally recognized in North Carolina and accepted by most entities nationwide.

$25 per session + $10 per signature
  • Complete notarization from your computer or tablet
  • Knowledge-based authentication and ID verification
  • Secure, encrypted audio-video session recorded for compliance
  • Digital notary seal and tamper-evident certificate
  • Ideal for out-of-state signers and remote transactions

Apostille & Authentication

If your documents need to be used in another country, they may require an apostille or authentication from the North Carolina Secretary of State. We handle the entire process for you -- from notarization through submission and retrieval -- so you do not have to navigate the state office yourself.

$50-$100 processing fee + state fees
  • Document review and preparation for apostille
  • Submission to the NC Secretary of State on your behalf
  • Tracking and retrieval of apostilled documents
  • Rush processing available for urgent requests
  • Guidance on Hague Convention vs. authentication requirements

Certified Copy Services

Need a certified copy of a document that is not available from the issuing agency? We can make and certify copies of original documents where permitted by North Carolina law, including diplomas, passports, and other personal records.

$15 per document
  • Copy certification by document custodian
  • Compliant with NC notary law requirements
  • Available for personal and business documents
  • Multiple copies can be certified in one appointment
