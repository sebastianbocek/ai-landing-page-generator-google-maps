Our Story

Started in a Parking Lot, Now on Every Road in Springfield

GreenLight Driving Academy was founded in 2012 by Marcus Webb, a certified driving instructor who spent eight years teaching for a large chain school before deciding he could do it better on his own terms.

"The big schools were focused on volume. Get students in, get them through the checklist, collect the fee. I wanted to actually teach people," Marcus said. He opened GreenLight with two cars, a folding table in a rented office, and a clear goal: get his students to pass the first time, every time.

Twelve years later, GreenLight has three full-time instructors, a fleet of six vehicles, and over 2,400 graduates. The mission hasn't changed.

Our Mission

Building Safe, Confident Drivers

We don't teach people to pass a test. We teach people to drive. The license follows naturally when the foundation is solid. Every lesson at GreenLight is built around building skill, building judgment, and building the kind of calm confidence that stays with you for life.
