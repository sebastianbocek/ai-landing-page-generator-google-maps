Why Harbor Point

A Marina Run by Boaters, For Boaters

When Bill Hartley started Harbor Point in 1962, he had one goal: build a marina he'd actually want to use himself. That philosophy hasn't changed. His granddaughter runs it now, and the crew on the docks and in the shop are all local boaters who spend their weekends out on the same water you do.

We're not the flashiest marina on the coast. We're the one where your neighbors have kept their boats for 20 years and the mechanic remembers your engine from last season. That consistency is hard to find and easy to keep once you've got it.

  • Family-owned since 1962
  • ABYC certified technicians
  • Mercury & Yamaha authorized dealer
  • 24-hour marina access for slipholders
  • Pump-out station at every dock
  • On-site ship's store
  • Transient slips available
  • Covered and open storage options
