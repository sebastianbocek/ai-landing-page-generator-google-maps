Annual Slip Agreements

Annual leaseholders receive a 10% discount on the combined seasonal rate, guaranteed renewal rights, and priority access to service scheduling. Most of our annual slipholders have been here for over a decade.

  • Access to liveaboard program consideration
  • Discounted winter storage rate

  • 30-minute notice for launch

  • Annual bottom paint included in premium plan

Boat Repair & Service

Eight-bay full-service shop with ABYC certified technicians. Mercury and Yamaha authorized dealer. We work on all makes, all systems.

Engine Service

Factory-trained technicians handle everything from routine maintenance to complete inboard and outboard engine rebuilds. We work on all major brands including Mercury, Yamaha, Evinrude, Honda, Suzuki, Volvo Penta, and Mercruiser stern drives.

  • 100-hour service and tune-up
  • Winterization and de-winterization
  • Lower unit service and replacement
  • Impeller and cooling system service
  • Fuel system diagnostics and repair
  • Computer diagnostics for all brands
  • Prop repair and replacement
  • Full engine replacement and rigging

Electrical Systems

Marine electrical work requires a different standard than automotive. Our ABYC-certified electricians handle everything from battery banks to chartplotter installation to full rewiring of older boats.

  • Battery and charging system service
  • Electronics installation (chartplotter, VHF, radar)
  • Shore power and inverter systems
  • Bilge pump and float switch service
  • Running lights and navigation lighting
  • Stereo and entertainment systems
  • Electrical system survey and rewiring
  • Solar and generator integration

Fiberglass & Gelcoat

Osmotic blistering, stress cracks, collision repairs, and complete hull refinishing. Our fiberglass team works in a dedicated enclosed bay with proper ventilation and climate control.

  • Blister repair and prevention
  • Stress crack repair
  • Impact and collision repair
  • Gelcoat color matching and polishing
  • Transom rebuild and reinforcement
  • Deck core replacement
  • Stringer and structural repair
  • Full hull restoration

Bottom Paint & Detailing

Fuel

Fuel Dock

Gas and diesel, seven days a week. High-flow pumps, courteous attendants, no appointment required.

Fuel Dock Details

Our fuel dock is located on the south end of the main pier, clearly marked with red and green pennants. Approach from the main channel bearing 285 degrees. We have attendants on the dock from 7am to 6pm daily who will take your lines and handle the pump.

  • Unleaded gasoline (ethanol-free available)
  • Marine diesel (clear diesel)
  • High-flow pumps, both sides of dock
  • Attendant service 7am to 6pm daily
  • Self-serve available off-hours
  • Credit and debit cards accepted
  • Fuel cards for annual slipholders
  • Complimentary pump-out with 30+ gallon fill

Current fuel pricing is posted at the dock and updated daily. Prices are competitive with regional marina averages. Annual slipholders receive a 3-cent per gallon discount on all fuel purchases.
