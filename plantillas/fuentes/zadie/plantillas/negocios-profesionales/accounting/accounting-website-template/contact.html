FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during the free consultation?

We'll spend about 30 minutes learning about your financial situation, answering your questions, and recommending the services that make sense for you. There's no obligation, no hard sell. If we're a good fit, great. If not, we'll point you in the right direction.

What documents do I need to bring?

For the initial consultation, just bring a general idea of your financial situation. For tax preparation, we'll send you a detailed checklist once you become a client. Common items include W-2s, 1099s, mortgage interest statements, and charitable donation receipts.

How does your pricing work?

We provide a written quote before starting any work, based on the complexity of your situation. Our starting prices are listed on our Services page. We never bill more than the quoted amount without discussing it with you first.

Can I work with you remotely?

Absolutely. Many of our clients never step foot in our office. We use a secure client portal for document sharing, and meetings can happen over phone or video call. We serve clients across the entire state.

What are the important tax deadlines?

Individual returns are due April 15th. Business returns (S-Corps and partnerships) are due March 15th. Quarterly estimated tax payments are due in April, June, September, and January. We'll make sure you never miss a deadline.

What should I expect at my first meeting?

A relaxed conversation, not an interrogation. We'll ask about your goals, walk through your current financial picture, and explain how we can help. You'll leave with a clear understanding of next steps and a written quote if you'd like to move forward.
