About Us

Two CPAs on a Mission to Make Accounting Less Intimidating

We started Ledger & Lane because we believed accounting didn't have to be confusing, cold, or stressful. Here's our story.

Our Story

Built on a Simple Idea

Sarah Lane and Marcus Ledger met at a Big Four accounting firm where they spent years working with Fortune 500 companies. The work was challenging, but something felt off. The clients who needed the most help, small businesses and everyday people, couldn't afford or access the same quality of service.

In 2012, they left the corporate world behind and opened Ledger & Lane with a clear purpose: bring the same expertise they gave billion-dollar companies to the business owner down the street and the family filing their taxes.

Twelve years later, that mission hasn't changed. We've grown from two desks in a shared office to a team of four serving over 2,400 clients. But we still pick up the phone. We still explain things in plain English. And we still believe everyone deserves an accountant who actually cares.
