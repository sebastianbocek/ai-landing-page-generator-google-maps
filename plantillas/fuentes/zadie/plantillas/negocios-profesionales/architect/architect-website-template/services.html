Our Services

From initial concept through construction, we offer comprehensive architectural services tailored to the scale and ambition of your project.

01

Residential Design

Your home is the most personal architecture there is. We design custom residences, renovations, and additions that reflect how you live, not how a catalog thinks you should. Every project begins with deep listening and ends with a space that feels unmistakably yours.

Our residential work spans from compact urban infill homes to expansive rural estates. We bring the same rigor and care to a kitchen renovation as we do to a ground-up build, because the details matter at every scale.

Starting at $15,000+
02

Commercial Architecture

Great commercial architecture does more than house a business. It expresses a brand, attracts talent, and drives performance. We design office buildings, retail spaces, restaurants, and mixed-use developments that are as strategic as they are beautiful.

Our commercial projects are defined by efficient floor plans, adaptable systems, and the kind of spatial quality that makes people want to show up. We understand the financial realities of development and design accordingly.

Starting at $50,000+
03

Urban Planning

Cities are built one decision at a time. Our urban planning practice helps communities, developers, and municipalities make those decisions with clarity and vision. We create master plans, design public spaces, and develop strategies for neighborhoods in transition.

We believe that the best public spaces emerge from genuine community engagement. Our planning process is collaborative, transparent, and grounded in real data about how people move, gather, and connect.

Starting at $75,000+
04

Interior Architecture

Interior architecture goes beyond decoration. It is the design of space itself: how rooms connect, how light enters, how materials meet. We offer comprehensive interior services including space planning, material selection, custom fixture design, and millwork detailing.

Whether integrated into a larger project or as a standalone commission, our interior work carries the same architectural rigor and material sensitivity that defines everything we do.

Starting at $10,000+
05

Sustainable Design

Sustainability is not an add-on. It is embedded in how we think about architecture from day one. Our sustainable design services include LEED consulting, passive design strategies, energy modeling, and net-zero planning.

As a LEED Platinum Partner, we have the technical expertise to meet the most ambitious environmental goals without compromising design quality. The most sustainable building is one that people love enough to maintain for a hundred years.

Starting at $8,000+
06

Feasibility Studies

Before you commit to a project, you need to understand what is possible. Our feasibility studies provide clear-eyed analysis of site conditions, zoning constraints, construction costs, and design potential. We help you make informed decisions before the first dollar is spent.

A thorough feasibility study can save months of time and significant expense by identifying opportunities and challenges early. It is the smartest investment you can make at the start of any project.

Starting at $5,000+
How We Work

Our Process

Every project follows a clear, proven path from initial idea to built reality.

Discovery

We listen. Site visits, client interviews, research, and analysis lay the groundwork for everything that follows. We define the program, budget, and timeline together.

Concept

Ideas take shape through sketches, models, and diagrams. We explore multiple directions, testing each against your goals and the realities of the site. The strongest concept emerges.

Development

The chosen design is refined in full detail. Materials are selected, systems are engineered, and construction documents are produced with precision. Nothing is left to chance.

Construction

We remain actively involved through construction, conducting regular site visits to ensure the design intent is realized. The gap between drawing and building stays as small as possible.
