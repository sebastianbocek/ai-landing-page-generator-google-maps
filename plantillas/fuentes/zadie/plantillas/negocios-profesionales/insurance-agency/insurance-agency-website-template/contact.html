Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Answers to the questions we hear most from new and existing clients.

What does "independent agency" mean?

We are not employed by one insurance company. We represent multiple carriers, which means we can shop around for the best rate and coverage for your specific needs. We work for you, not the insurance company.

How much does a home insurance policy cost?

Home insurance premiums vary based on your home's value, location, age, construction type, and coverage level. In our area, typical homeowner policies range from $1,200 to $3,500 per year. We can usually find savings by comparing multiple carriers.

Can you help with a claim?

Absolutely. That is one of the biggest advantages of working with a local agent. When you have a claim, call us first. We will guide you through the process, handle the paperwork, and advocate on your behalf with the insurance company.

How often should I review my insurance?

At least once a year, or whenever you have a major life change like buying a home, getting married, starting a business, or adding a teen driver. We proactively reach out for annual reviews, but you can request one anytime.

Do you offer payment plans?

Most carriers offer monthly, quarterly, or semi-annual payment plans. Paying in full typically saves you billing fees. We will outline all payment options when we present your quote so you can choose what works best for your budget.

How fast can I get coverage?

For most personal lines (home, auto, renters), we can bind coverage the same day. Business insurance may take a few days depending on the complexity of the policy. In an emergency, call us and we will prioritize your request.
