How We Started

From One Agent to a Full Team

Harbor Shield Insurance was founded in 1992 by David Mercer, a former claims adjuster who saw firsthand how impersonal the insurance industry had become. Families were buying policies from call centers without anyone explaining what they were actually covered for. Claims were denied on technicalities. Clients felt like numbers.

David opened a storefront office on Harbor Drive with a simple promise: treat every client like a neighbor. No high-pressure sales, no confusing jargon, no surprises when you need to file a claim. That philosophy has guided us for over 30 years.

Today, Harbor Shield is a team of six licensed agents representing 20+ insurance carriers. We still operate from the same street where David started, and we still answer our own phones. The industry has changed, but our approach has not.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Three principles that shape every policy we write and every claim we handle.

Client First

We do not work for insurance companies. We work for you. When your interests conflict with a carrier's, we advocate for you every time. That is the whole point of having an independent agent.

Transparency

Insurance is full of fine print. We explain every coverage, every exclusion, and every deductible in plain language before you sign anything. If you have questions six months later, call us. We will explain it again.

Proactive Service

We do not wait for you to call us. We review your policies annually, alert you to rate changes, and reach out when life events like a new home, a new driver, or a growing business mean your coverage needs to be updated.
