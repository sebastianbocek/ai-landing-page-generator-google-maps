Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from new families.

What age can students start lessons?

We accept students as young as five for piano and violin (using the Suzuki method). Guitar, drums, and voice lessons typically start at age seven or eight, depending on the child. Adults are welcome at any age. We have students in their 70s who are just getting started.

Do I need to own an instrument before starting?

Not at all. We have instruments available for use during lessons, and your instructor can help you choose the right instrument to purchase when you're ready. We also partner with local music shops for student discounts on rentals and purchases.

How does the free trial lesson work?

Your trial is a full 30-minute lesson with one of our instructors. There's no obligation and no sales pitch. It's a chance for you (or your child) to try the instrument, meet the teacher, and see if Crescendo is the right fit. Just fill out the form above to schedule one.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours' notice for lesson cancellations. Lessons cancelled with at least 24 hours' notice can be rescheduled within the same month at no charge. Same-day cancellations are unfortunately non-refundable, except in cases of illness or emergency.

Can I switch instructors or instruments?

Absolutely. We want every student to find the right match. If you'd like to try a different instructor or explore a new instrument, just let us know. We'll set up a trial with someone new at no extra cost. Many of our students study multiple instruments over time.

Do you offer family or sibling discounts?

Yes. When two or more members of the same household are enrolled, each additional student receives 10% off their monthly tuition. This applies to any combination of private lessons, group classes, or summer camps. The discount is applied automatically to your monthly billing.
