Getting Here

We're on SE Division between 28th and 29th, in the heart of one of Portland's most bike-friendly corridors. There's bike parking out front and a car spot in the alley.

Bus
TriMet Line 4 (Division) stops half a block away. Line 10 (Harold) is two blocks north.
Bike
Clinton Street Greenway and the SE Woodward protected bike lanes are both nearby. Secure bike parking out front.
Car
Street parking on Division and adjacent side streets. Free parking in the alley behind the shop (first come).
