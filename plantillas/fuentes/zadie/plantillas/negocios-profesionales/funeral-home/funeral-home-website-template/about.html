Our History

A Family Business Built on Trust

Harold Whitmore founded Evergreen Memorial Chapel in 1962 with a simple conviction: that every family deserves the same quality of care, regardless of their circumstances. He built the chapel himself on a quiet street in Dayton, and for twenty years served his neighbors with quiet dedication.

In 1987, Harold's son Thomas returned to Dayton after earning his mortuary science degree and his funeral director's license. He brought new ideas about grief counseling, pre-planning, and family support—while keeping the same unhurried, personal approach his father had established.

Today, Thomas's daughter Claire leads the chapel alongside her father. A licensed funeral director and certified grief counselor, Claire has expanded the chapel's services while honoring the values that have made Evergreen a trusted name in the community for more than sixty years.

We are not a franchise or a corporate chain. Every decision is made here, by people who live in this community, worship alongside you, and genuinely care about the families we serve.

Our Chapel & Facilities

A Peaceful Space for Every Gathering

Our building was designed with families in mind—quiet, comfortable, and private. Everything your family needs is under one roof.

The Main Chapel

Our primary chapel seats up to 200 guests and features warm wood accents, adjustable lighting, and a sound system to accommodate live music or recorded audio. It can be arranged for services of any faith tradition.

Visitation Rooms

Three private visitation rooms accommodate intimate gatherings of 10 to 75 guests. Each room has its own entrance and can be individually customized with photos, music, and personal mementos.

Reception Room

A comfortable gathering space adjacent to the chapel, available for post-service receptions. Seats up to 80 and is equipped with a full kitchen for catered meals or simple refreshments.

Memorial Garden

Our enclosed memorial garden provides a quiet, outdoor space for reflection. Families are welcome to visit during daylight hours. It's also available for intimate outdoor services in mild weather.

Family Lounge

A private room exclusively for the immediate family—a quiet retreat during visitations and services. Stocked with refreshments and comfortable seating, away from the main gathering areas.

Planning Suite

A private, comfortable office where families meet with our directors to make arrangements. Designed to feel welcoming rather than clinical, with ample time and no sense of hurry.

In the Community

More Than a Business—A Neighbor

Evergreen Memorial Chapel is woven into the fabric of the Dayton community. We believe a funeral home has a responsibility that extends beyond its front door.

Every year, we host free community events on grief, end-of-life planning, and senior wellness. We sponsor local youth programs, partner with area hospices, and support veterans' organizations throughout the region.

  • Annual free grief workshop series for the public
  • Partnerships with Hospice of Dayton and other local hospices
  • Sponsor of the Dayton Veterans Memorial Foundation
  • Free "Planning Ahead" seminars for seniors and their families
  • Scholarship fund for mortuary science students
  • Supporter of the Montgomery County NAMI chapter
