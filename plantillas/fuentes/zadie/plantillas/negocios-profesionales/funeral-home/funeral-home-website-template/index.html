Our Services

Honoring Every Life with Dignity

From traditional funerals to cremation, pre-planning, and grief support, we offer a full range of services thoughtfully designed around your family's wishes and values.

Traditional Funeral Services

A full-service funeral with visitation, ceremony, and burial. We guide your family through every detail, coordinating with clergy, florists, and cemeteries so you can focus on what matters.

Cremation Options

From simple direct cremation to a full memorial service with cremation, we offer dignified options at every level. Our cremation center maintains the highest standards of care and accountability.

Learn more →

Pre-Planning

Planning ahead is one of the most thoughtful gifts you can give your family. Pre-arranging your funeral relieves them of difficult decisions during a painful time and locks in today's prices.

Learn more →

Grief Support

Our care doesn't end after the service. We provide ongoing grief resources, referrals to licensed counselors, and support groups throughout the Dayton area to help families heal.

Learn more →

Graveside Services

A meaningful, intimate service held at the graveside for family and close friends. We work closely with cemetery staff to ensure the ceremony proceeds with quiet dignity.

Learn more →

Memorial Services

A celebration of life held without the body present—ideal for families who have chosen cremation or burial in another location. We help create a gathering that truly honors who they were.

Why Families Choose Us

Rooted in Community, Guided by Compassion

For more than six decades, Dayton families have trusted Evergreen Memorial Chapel. Here's what sets us apart.

60+

Years Serving Dayton

Since 1962, we've served thousands of families across Montgomery, Greene, and Warren counties. Our deep roots in the community mean we understand local customs, cemeteries, and clergy relationships.

24/7

Always Available

Loss doesn't follow a schedule. A licensed funeral director is available any hour of the day or night to answer your call, arrange immediate needs, and provide calm guidance when it's needed most.

3rd

Generation Family Business

The Whitmore family has guided this chapel through three generations. We're not a corporate chain. Decisions are made here, by people who live in this community and care about it deeply.

100%

Transparent Pricing

We provide a full General Price List to every family—no surprises, no pressure. You'll understand every cost before making any decisions, and we'll work within whatever budget you have.

All

Faiths and Traditions Welcome

We've conducted services honoring every faith background and cultural tradition. Our staff is trained to accommodate your specific religious, cultural, and personal wishes with full respect.
