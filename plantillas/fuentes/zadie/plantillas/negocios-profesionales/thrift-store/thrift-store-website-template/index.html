Our mission

Good things deserve a second story.

We started Second Story because we believe the things people make deserve more than one life. Every lamp, coat, and bookshelf that comes through our door has a history. We find it a future.

We keep our standards high and our prices fair. That means not everything makes the cut — but everything that does is something we'd buy ourselves. No sifting. No guessing. Just good finds.
