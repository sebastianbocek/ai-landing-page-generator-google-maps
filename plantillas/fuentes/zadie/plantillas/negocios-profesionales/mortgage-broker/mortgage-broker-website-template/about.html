About KeyRate Mortgage Group

Our Story

We Started KeyRate Because Borrowers Deserve Better

Before founding KeyRate Mortgage Group, our principal broker spent over a decade as a loan officer at a major national bank. The experience was eye-opening, but not always in a good way.

Working inside a bank means selling that bank's products, whether or not they're the best fit for the client. When a borrower doesn't quite fit the box, the answer is usually "sorry, we can't help" rather than "let me find someone who can."

That didn't sit right. So in 2015, KeyRate was born as an independent mortgage brokerage with one clear mission: give every borrower access to the full market of lenders and the honest guidance to choose wisely.

Today, we partner with over 50 banks, credit unions, and wholesale lenders. When you work with us, you get the same rate comparison that would take you weeks of phone calls, all in one conversation.

Our Approach

We Work for You, Not a Bank

The difference between a broker and a bank is simple: we have no loyalty to any single lender. Our only loyalty is to you.

Unbiased Comparison

We present options from 50+ lenders side by side, with full transparency on rates, fees, and terms. You choose what works best, not what makes us the most money.

Honest Conversations

If now isn't the right time to buy, we'll tell you. If your credit needs work before applying, we'll help you build a plan. Our advice is always in your best interest.

Full-Service Support

From your first question to your closing day handshake, we handle the details. Paperwork, underwriting coordination, lender communication, and status updates are all on us.

Community

Free monthly workshops covering the entire home buying process, from budgeting and credit building to understanding closing costs. Open to anyone in the community, not just our clients. We partner with local real estate agents and housing counselors to provide a complete picture.
