Browse & Events

Explore our shelves, join a book club, or find your next favorite author at one of our events.

Browse by Section

Every section is hand-curated. Ask any of our staff for a personal recommendation.

Fiction

Literary fiction, contemporary novels, short story collections, and genre fiction. Our largest section, spanning from classics to this week's debuts.

Featured: "James" by Percival Everett, "Intermezzo" by Sally Rooney

Non-Fiction

History, science, essays, memoir, current affairs, and narrative non-fiction. For the curious minds who want to understand the world a little better.

Featured: "Supercommunicators" by Charles Duhigg, "Same as Ever" by Morgan Housel

Poetry

Contemporary poets, classic anthologies, and small-press collections. Our poetry shelf is one of the best in the Pacific Northwest.

Featured: "Devotions" by Mary Oliver, "Postcolonial Love Poem" by Natalie Diaz

Children's & YA

Picture books, early readers, middle grade adventures, and young adult novels. Organized by age range with staff picks clearly marked.

Featured: "The Wild Robot" by Peter Brown, "Before the Coffee Gets Cold" (YA edition)

Rare & Collectible

First editions, signed copies, vintage paperbacks, and out-of-print treasures. Our locked cabinet holds some real gems for serious collectors.

Featured: Signed first edition of "Beloved" by Toni Morrison

Local Authors

Portland-area writers across every genre. We are proud to stock over 80 titles from our neighbors and host monthly spotlights on local talent.

Featured: "The Body" by Bill Bryson (honorary Portlander), local debut novels

Book Clubs

Three clubs, three vibes. All meetings are held in-store with complimentary coffee and pastries.

Fiction Circle

Monthly -- Third Saturday, 6:30 PM

Our flagship club. One novel per month, ranging from literary fiction to translated works to the occasional genre pick. Lively discussion, no pretension.

Currently reading: "Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow" by Gabrielle Zevin

Next up: "The Ministry of Time" by Kaliane Bradley

Non-Fiction Quarterly

Quarterly -- First Sunday, 4:00 PM

One big non-fiction read every three months. We take our time with these. Science, history, memoir, investigative journalism -- whatever's making us think.

Currently reading: "Braiding Sweetgrass" by Robin Wall Kimmerer

Next up: "The Warmth of Other Suns" by Isabel Wilkerson

Young Readers Club

Monthly -- Second Saturday, 11:00 AM

For ages 10-14. Middle grade and YA titles chosen by the members themselves. Snacks, crafts, and conversations about books in a judgment-free zone.

Currently reading: "The Wild Robot" by Peter Brown

Next up: voted on by members each month

Our Services

Beyond the shelves, here is how we help you find, give, and enjoy books.

Special Orders

Can't find what you're looking for? We can order any book in print and have it in-store within 3-5 business days. No extra charge, no minimum order.

Gift Wrapping

Free gift wrapping with any purchase. We use recycled kraft paper and hand-stamp each package with our bookmark seal. Personalized notes available.

Monthly Book Box

A curated surprise delivered to your door each month. Tell us your taste and we'll pick a book, wrap it beautifully, and include a handwritten note. $25/month.

Gift Cards

Available in any amount, in-store or by mail. The perfect gift for the reader in your life -- or the person you're trying to turn into one.
