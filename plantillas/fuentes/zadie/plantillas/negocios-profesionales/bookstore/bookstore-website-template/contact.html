Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do you buy used books?

We do accept used books in good condition on a case-by-case basis. Bring them in during business hours and one of our staff will take a look. We offer store credit rather than cash. We're especially interested in literary fiction, poetry, and any titles that complement our existing collection.

Can you special order a book for me?

Absolutely. We can order any book currently in print and most out-of-print titles through our network of distributors. Special orders typically arrive within 3-5 business days. There's no extra charge and no minimum order. Just ask at the counter, call us, or send us an email with the title and author.

Do you have an online store?

We're primarily a brick-and-mortar shop, and that's by design. We believe the best way to discover a book is to hold it, flip through it, and have a conversation about it. That said, we do offer our Monthly Book Box subscription by mail and can ship special orders anywhere in the US. Give us a call for details.

Is the coffee bar open to everyone?

Yes, completely. You don't need to buy a book to enjoy a coffee, and you don't need to buy a coffee to browse the shelves. The coffee bar is open during all regular store hours. Come in, grab a latte, and settle into the reading nook for as long as you like.

How do the book clubs work?

We run three book clubs with different schedules and focuses. There's no membership fee or sign-up required -- just show up. We announce each month's selection on our social media and in-store. Members receive a 15% discount on the current book club pick. Complimentary coffee and pastries are provided at every meeting.

Do you host private events?

We do. The shop is available for private events on select evenings, including book launch parties, literary-themed gatherings, corporate book clubs, and small celebrations. We can accommodate up to 40 guests and offer catering through our coffee bar. Contact us at hello@pageandbinding.com for availability and pricing.
