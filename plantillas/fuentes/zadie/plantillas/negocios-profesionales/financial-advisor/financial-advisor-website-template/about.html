Why We Started Crestline

After a decade at one of the country's largest wire houses, Margaret Holloway saw the same pattern repeat itself: clients who trusted their advisors, not realizing those advisors were steered toward products that benefited the firm more than the client.

She left in 2012 to build something different. Crestline Wealth Advisors was founded on a single principle: your advisor should work for you. Full stop. That means no commissions, no product incentives, no conflicts of interest from outside relationships.

Today, Crestline serves more than 300 client families across North Carolina and beyond. The firm has grown through referrals only — which we think says something about the work we do.

How We Work

Our Planning Philosophy

01

Long-Term Relationships

We don't want to be your advisor for a transaction. We want to be your advisor for the long haul — through career changes, market downturns, inheritances, and retirement. Our average client tenure is 11 years.

02

Holistic Planning

Your financial life doesn't live in a single account. We look at everything: investments, insurance, taxes, estate, and cash flow. Then we coordinate those pieces into a plan that actually holds together.

03

Radical Transparency

You receive a written financial plan, clear fee disclosures, and an annual review with documented recommendations. We believe informed clients make better decisions — and we want you informed.

What You Pay

Our Fee Structure

We are a fee-only firm. We earn no commissions, receive no referral payments, and accept no compensation from product providers. Our only revenue comes from fees you pay us directly.

Investment Management

0.75–1.25%

Annual percentage of assets under management, billed quarterly. The rate scales down as assets increase. Includes all financial planning services.

Financial Planning Only

$4,000–$8,000

One-time or annual retainer fee for comprehensive financial plan development, without investment management. Appropriate for those with existing investment arrangements.

Hourly Consulting

$350/hr

For targeted questions or second-opinion consultations. Minimum two-hour engagement. Includes a written summary of recommendations.

All fees are disclosed in writing before engagement. Please review our ADV Part 2 for complete fee schedules and disclosures.
