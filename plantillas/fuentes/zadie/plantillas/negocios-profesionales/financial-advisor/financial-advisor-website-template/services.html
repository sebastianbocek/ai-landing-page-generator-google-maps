What We Offer

Coordinated Planning Across Your Entire Financial Life

Our services are not sold separately. Every client receives integrated planning that spans investments, taxes, estate, and retirement.

Retirement Planning

A comprehensive retirement plan built around your income needs, lifestyle goals, and the realities of how long you'll need your money to last.

Included in all AUM plans

Social Security Optimization

We model every filing scenario — spousal benefits, delayed credits, early filing trade-offs — to determine the strategy that maximizes your lifetime income. This decision alone can be worth hundreds of thousands of dollars.

401(k) Rollover & Distribution Planning

We manage the transition from accumulation to distribution, including 401(k) and IRA rollovers, Required Minimum Distribution planning, and sequencing withdrawals across account types to minimize taxes.

Income Planning

Building a reliable income stream that covers your expenses and sustains your lifestyle requires careful coordination of pensions, Social Security, portfolio withdrawals, and any rental or business income.

Healthcare & Medicare Strategy

We help you navigate Medicare Part A, B, D, and supplemental coverage options, including IRMAA surcharge planning and long-term care insurance analysis so healthcare costs don't derail your plan.

Investment Management

Disciplined, evidence-based portfolio management that aligns with your risk tolerance, time horizon, and tax situation.

0.75 – 1.25% AUM annually

Portfolio Construction

We build diversified, low-cost portfolios using evidence-based investing principles. Asset allocation is determined by your risk tolerance, goals, and time horizon — not market predictions or hot sectors.

Risk Management

We stress-test your portfolio against historical downturns and model the impact on your financial plan. Our goal is a portfolio you can actually stay invested in during difficult markets.

Systematic Rebalancing

We monitor your portfolio and rebalance when allocations drift beyond target thresholds. Rebalancing is done with tax consequences in mind — we sell in retirement accounts first wherever possible.

Performance Reporting & Transparency

You receive quarterly performance reports with full holdings disclosure, fee breakdown, and benchmark comparison. We use custodial accounts at Schwab or Fidelity — you retain direct ownership of all assets.

Estate Planning

Protecting your estate and ensuring your wishes are carried out requires more than a will. We coordinate across legal, tax, and financial dimensions.

Included in all AUM plans

Trust Coordination

We work alongside your estate attorney to review trust structures, ensure accounts are titled correctly, and integrate your trust into your overall financial plan. We don't draft legal documents — but we make sure the financial picture aligns with your estate documents.

Beneficiary Review

Beneficiary designations on IRAs, 401(k)s, life insurance, and annuities supersede your will. We audit all designations and flag conflicts or outdated entries that could lead to unintended outcomes.

Legacy Planning

For clients who wish to leave a meaningful legacy, we build charitable gifting strategies, donor-advised fund structures, and multi-generational transfer plans that balance your retirement security with your values.

Power of Attorney & Incapacity Planning

We review your existing documents and work with your attorney to ensure your financial affairs can be managed if you become incapacitated. A well-coordinated plan prevents hardship for your family.

Tax Planning

We are not CPAs and we don't prepare tax returns. But tax efficiency runs through every recommendation we make, and we work closely with your tax professional.

Included in all AUM plans

Tax-Efficient Investing

Asset location — placing the right investments in the right accounts — can meaningfully improve after-tax returns over time. We optimize account structure to minimize annual tax drag across your portfolio.

Roth Conversion Strategy

Converting traditional IRA assets to Roth during low-income years can eliminate future RMDs and reduce lifetime taxes significantly. We model conversions annually and recommend amounts that stay within target tax brackets.

Charitable Giving Strategy

Qualified Charitable Distributions, donor-advised funds, and bunching strategies can make your giving more tax-efficient. We help you give more to causes you care about while reducing your tax bill.

Tax Loss Harvesting

During market downturns, we systematically harvest losses to offset gains elsewhere in your portfolio, creating tax savings that compound over time without disrupting your long-term strategy.

Business Owner Services

Business owners face a set of financial planning challenges distinct from those of employees. We have deep experience working with owners preparing for growth, transition, or exit.

Included in all AUM plans

Business Exit Planning

Whether you're planning a sale to a third party, a management buyout, or an ESOP, we help you structure the transaction to minimize taxes, protect the proceeds, and integrate the sale into your retirement plan.

Key Person Insurance Coordination

We assess your need for key person, buy-sell, and disability coverage and coordinate with insurance professionals to ensure your business and personal finances are protected if something happens to you or a partner.

SEP-IRA, SIMPLE IRA & Solo 401(k)

We evaluate and establish retirement plans appropriate to your business structure, employee count, and goals. The right plan can dramatically reduce your taxable income while accelerating your personal retirement savings.

Executive Compensation Planning

For business owners and executives with deferred compensation, stock options, or equity arrangements, we model vesting schedules, tax exposure, and diversification timelines to protect what you've earned.

Who We Serve

Who We Work With

We serve individuals and families who are serious about their financial futures and ready to engage in a real planning relationship.

Pre-Retirees

Typically ages 50 to 65, navigating the transition from accumulation to income. Often have a combination of 401(k), IRA, brokerage, and pension assets and need a coordinated strategy for the next chapter.

Business Owners

Owners of established businesses who have significant personal wealth tied to their company and need help separating personal and business finances, planning for exit, and building retirement assets.

Recent Retirees & Widows

Clients who have recently retired or experienced a major life transition and need to reorganize their finances, understand their income sources, and ensure their plan reflects their new reality.

Minimum: $500,000 in investable assets. Exceptions may be made for clients with strong savings trajectories.
