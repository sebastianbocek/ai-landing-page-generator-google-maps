Our Story

Started by a Builder Who Wanted to Do Right by Buyers

Marcus Webb spent eleven years in residential construction — framing, systems rough-in, finish work. He understood houses from the inside out. When he transitioned to inspection work in 2012, he brought something most inspectors don't have: firsthand knowledge of how construction shortcuts happen, what they look like fifteen years later, and what it actually costs to fix them.

TrueView was founded on a straightforward premise: buyers deserve an inspector who tells the full truth, explains it clearly, and has no financial stake in whether the deal closes or falls apart. No referral relationships with repair contractors. No incentive to minimize what's found.

Today we serve buyers, sellers, real estate agents, and property investors across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Our team of three licensed inspectors has completed over 1,400 inspections since opening. Every one of them includes a full walkthrough with the client and a same-day digital report.

How We Work

Our Inspection Philosophy

Three principles guide every inspection we do, regardless of the property type or size.

01

Thorough Reporting Over Rushed Volume

We schedule one to two inspections per inspector per day. Not three or four. That means the inspector you hired is on your property as long as it takes — not rushing to get to the next job. A 2,200 square foot home typically takes 3.5 to 4 hours. We don't cut that short.

02

Education During the Inspection

We strongly encourage clients to attend their inspection and walk the property with the inspector. You'll learn things about your home that no report alone can communicate — how to maintain systems, what to watch over time, and what actually requires immediate attention versus what can wait.

03

Zero Conflicts of Interest

We do not accept referrals from contractors, real estate agents, or any third party with a financial interest in the outcome. We are paid by you, the client, and our only obligation is to you. If a finding would kill a deal, that's information you deserve — not something to be softened.
