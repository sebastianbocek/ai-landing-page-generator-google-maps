Full Coverage

What Every Standard Inspection Covers

Our 800+ point inspection checklist covers every accessible area and system in the home. Here's a breakdown by category.

Structural Systems

  • Foundation type and visible condition
  • Framing members — joists, rafters, beams
  • Load-bearing wall condition
  • Crawl space or basement moisture
  • Settlement and structural movement
  • Visible concrete cracks (type and severity)

Roofing System

  • Shingle condition and estimated life
  • Flashing at penetrations and edges
  • Gutters and downspout extension
  • Attic ventilation and insulation
  • Chimney, caps, and fireplace damper
  • Skylights and roof windows

Electrical

  • Main service panel size and condition
  • Breaker labeling and function
  • GFCI and AFCI protection
  • Visible branch wiring type
  • Receptacle function throughout
  • Smoke and CO detector presence

HVAC Systems

  • Furnace / heat pump function and age
  • Air conditioning function and refrigerant
  • Ductwork condition and distribution
  • Filters, coils, and blower condition
  • Thermostat response
  • Carbon monoxide at all burners

Plumbing

  • Supply pressure and flow at fixtures
  • Water heater condition, age, and safety
  • Drain function at all fixtures
  • Visible supply line material
  • Shut-off valve locations and function
  • Exterior hose bibs and irrigation

Interior & Exterior

  • Window and door operation
  • Floor, wall, and ceiling condition
  • Stairways and handrail safety
  • Siding, trim, and caulking
  • Deck, porch, and balcony condition
  • Grading and site drainage

Questions about a specific system or feature? Call us before booking and we'll confirm whether it falls within our standard scope.
