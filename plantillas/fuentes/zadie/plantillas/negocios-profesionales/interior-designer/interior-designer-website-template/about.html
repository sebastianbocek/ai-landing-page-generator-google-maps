Our Story

About the Studio

We're a small team of designers who believe every home should reflect the people who live in it. No two projects are alike because no two clients are alike.

The Beginning

Founded on Intention

Atelier Home Design was founded in 2011 by Claire Duval, a graduate of Parsons School of Design with a deep conviction that interior design should be personal, not performative.

After a decade working at leading firms in New York and Los Angeles, Claire noticed a pattern: clients were being given beautiful rooms that didn't feel like their own. Spaces designed to impress, not to inhabit.

She started Atelier with a different philosophy. Every project begins with deep listening. We learn how you cook, how you entertain, where you read, how light moves through your home. The design follows from there.

Fifteen years later, that approach hasn't changed. We've grown from a solo practice to a team of three, but the work remains intimate, collaborative, and rooted in the belief that your home should tell your story.

Philosophy

What We Believe

Design isn't decoration. It's the art of shaping how a space makes you feel. A well-designed room doesn't just look good in photographs. It works for the people who use it every day.

We gravitate toward natural materials, considered proportions, and a palette that ages gracefully. But more than any style, we're committed to authenticity. Your home should feel like an extension of who you are, not a replica of someone else's taste.

That's why we never start with a mood board. We start with a conversation.

Our Values

What Guides Us

Intentional Design

Every decision has a reason. We don't add elements for the sake of filling space. Each piece, material, and color serves a purpose in the overall composition of your home.

Quality Over Trends

Trends fade. Quality endures. We invest in materials and craftsmanship that will look and feel as good in ten years as they do today. Timeless always outperforms trendy.

Collaborative Process

Your input isn't just welcome, it's essential. We work alongside you at every stage, ensuring the final result reflects your vision, your lifestyle, and your personality.
