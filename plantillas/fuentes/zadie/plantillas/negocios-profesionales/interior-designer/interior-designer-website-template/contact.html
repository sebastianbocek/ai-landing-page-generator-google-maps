Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does interior design cost?

Every project is different, so our fees depend on scope and complexity. We offer both flat-fee and hourly billing structures. During your complimentary consultation, we'll provide a detailed proposal tailored to your specific project.

How long does a typical project take?

A single room refresh typically takes 6-8 weeks. A full home renovation can take 4-8 months depending on the scope of construction involved. We'll provide a realistic timeline during the concept phase.

Do you work with existing furniture?

Absolutely. We're happy to incorporate pieces you already own and love. Part of our process is identifying what to keep, what to reupholster or refinish, and what to replace for the best overall result.

What's your design style?

We don't have a single signature style. Our designs are driven by our clients' preferences and lifestyles. We're known for warm, layered interiors with natural materials, but every project is uniquely tailored.

Do you work outside your local area?

Yes. While our studio is based in San Francisco, we take on select projects nationwide. For out-of-area projects, we combine on-site visits with virtual collaboration to keep the process seamless.

What should I have ready for our first meeting?

Come with inspiration images (Pinterest boards are great), a rough budget in mind, and a sense of your timeline. Photos of your current space are helpful too. Beyond that, just bring your ideas and enthusiasm.
