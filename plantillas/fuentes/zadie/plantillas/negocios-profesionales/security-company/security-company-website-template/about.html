Our Story

From patrol to protection

Sentinel Security Systems was founded in 2007 by Marcus Webb, a 12-year Phoenix PD veteran who spent years responding to burglaries and property crimes across the valley. He kept seeing the same thing: systems that didn't work right, monitoring companies that were slow to respond, and homeowners who had no idea their setup had gaps.

He left the force with one goal — to build a security company that operated with the same discipline and standards as law enforcement. No shortcuts. No overselling. Just systems that work when you need them to.

Today, Sentinel has grown to a team of 22 certified technicians and analysts serving over 4,200 clients across the Phoenix metro area. We're still independently owned, still based in Phoenix, and still held to the same standards Marcus set on day one.

Independently owned and operated — no franchise, no corporate playbook

Founded by former law enforcement with real security expertise

Serving Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, and Gilbert since 2007

How We Operate

The principles behind everything we do

Honest Assessment

We tell you what you actually need — not the most expensive system that'll earn us the biggest commission. If your existing equipment is salvageable, we'll tell you. If you need to start fresh, we'll explain exactly why.

Correct Installation

A security system is only as good as its installation. Our technicians take the time to route wiring properly, test every sensor, and walk you through the system before they leave the job site.

Reliable Response

When an alarm triggers, our monitoring center responds in under 30 seconds — not 3 minutes. We know the difference can matter. Our UL-listed facility has redundant power, redundant communications, and 24/7 staffing.

Long-Term Partnership

We don't sign you up and disappear. Our technicians are available for service calls, system upgrades, and questions as your property or business needs change over time.

No Pressure Sales

Free assessments are genuinely free. We show you what we found, explain your options, and let you decide. Our sales process is designed to inform, not to overwhelm.

Community First

Sentinel is a Phoenix company. Our team lives and works here. We sponsor local neighborhood watch programs and partner with the Phoenix PD on community safety initiatives.
