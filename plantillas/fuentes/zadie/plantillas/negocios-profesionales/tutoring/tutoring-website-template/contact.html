Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from parents.

How do I know if my child needs tutoring?

If your child is struggling with homework, losing confidence, or falling behind in a subject, tutoring can help. Our free assessment identifies specific gaps so you'll know exactly where they stand. There's no obligation.

How are tutors matched with students?

We match based on subject expertise, grade level experience, and personality fit. If the match isn't right after the first couple sessions, we'll switch tutors at no extra cost. The right fit matters.

How long until we see improvement?

Most families see noticeable improvement within 4-6 weeks. Some students show progress faster, especially in specific subjects. We set realistic goals upfront and track progress monthly so you can see the change.

Can I cancel or change my plan?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel with 7 days notice before your next billing cycle. We want you here because it's working, not because you're locked in.

Do you offer online tutoring?

We do. All of our programs are available in-person at our Austin location or online via video call. Online sessions use a shared digital whiteboard and screen sharing for an interactive experience.

What if we need to reschedule a session?

We understand schedules change. Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours notice and there's no charge. Same-day cancellations are counted as a used session to be fair to our tutors.
