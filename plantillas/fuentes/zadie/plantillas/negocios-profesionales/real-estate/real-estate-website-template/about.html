Our Story

25 Years of Excellence in Maplewood

Cornerstone Realty Group was founded in 1999 by Robert Chen with a simple vision: bring integrity, expertise, and personal service back to real estate. As a Maplewood native, Robert saw an opportunity to create an agency that truly understood the community it served.

What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted team of expert agents, but our core values remain unchanged. We believe every client deserves honest guidance, local expertise, and unwavering commitment to their goals.

Over the past 25 years, we've helped more than 500 families buy and sell homes in Maplewood. We've celebrated first-time buyers getting their keys, growing families finding more space, and retirees downsizing to perfect homes. Each transaction reminds us why we do this work.

Today, Cornerstone Realty Group stands as one of Maplewood's most respected real estate agencies. We're not the biggest, and we're okay with that. We measure success by client satisfaction, not sales volume. When our clients refer their friends and family, that's the only recognition we need.

Our Approach

How We Work

Our process is designed to deliver results while keeping you informed and confident every step of the way.

Listen First

We start every relationship by listening. What are your goals? What matters most to you? What concerns do you have? Understanding your unique situation allows us to create a customized strategy that works.

Market Analysis

We provide detailed market data and analysis so you can make informed decisions. No guesswork—just honest numbers, comparable sales, and realistic expectations based on current market conditions.

Strategic Execution

Whether buying or selling, we develop a strategic plan tailored to your goals. From pricing strategy to marketing plans to negotiation tactics, every decision is purposeful and designed to achieve the best outcome.

Clear Communication

You'll never wonder what's happening with your transaction. We provide regular updates, answer questions promptly, and keep you informed through every stage of the process. Transparency builds trust.

Attention to Detail

Real estate transactions involve countless moving pieces. We manage every detail—inspections, appraisals, paperwork, deadlines—so you don't have to worry. Our systems ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Follow-Through

Our relationship doesn't end at closing. We're here to answer questions, provide resources, and offer guidance long after the transaction is complete. Many of our clients become lifelong friends.
