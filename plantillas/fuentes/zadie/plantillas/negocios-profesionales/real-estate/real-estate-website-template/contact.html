Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to work with Cornerstone Realty Group?

For buyers, our services are typically free—we're paid by the seller when the transaction closes. For sellers, we charge a competitive commission that's discussed during your initial consultation. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

How long does it take to buy or sell a home?

Timelines vary based on market conditions, property type, and individual circumstances. On average, it takes 30-45 days from accepted offer to closing for buyers. For sellers, homes typically spend 30-60 days on the market in Maplewood, though well-priced properties can sell much faster. We'll provide a realistic timeline during your consultation.

What's the current real estate market like in Maplewood?

Market conditions change regularly. We provide current market analysis during every consultation, including inventory levels, average days on market, and pricing trends. Contact us for the most up-to-date information about your specific neighborhood or property type.

Do I need to get pre-approved for a mortgage before looking at homes?

While not absolutely required for initial home tours, pre-approval is strongly recommended. It helps you understand your budget, makes your offers more competitive, and speeds up the closing process. We can connect you with trusted local lenders who make the pre-approval process simple.

How do you determine what my home is worth?

We conduct a comprehensive market analysis that includes recent comparable sales, current market conditions, your home's unique features, and neighborhood trends. We'll walk you through the data and explain our pricing recommendation in detail. This analysis is free with no obligation.

What should I look for when buying a home?

Beyond the obvious factors like price and location, consider school districts, commute times, future resale value, property taxes, HOA fees, and the home's condition. We help you evaluate all these factors and identify potential red flags during the inspection process.

Should I make repairs before selling my home?

It depends on the repair and current market conditions. Some repairs offer strong ROI, while others don't impact selling price enough to justify the cost. We'll provide specific recommendations based on your home and target market during our listing consultation.

How do you market properties you list?

We use a multi-channel approach including professional photography, virtual tours, MLS listing optimization, social media advertising, email marketing to our buyer database, open houses, and targeted digital campaigns. Each property gets a customized marketing plan designed to reach qualified buyers.

What areas of Maplewood do you specialize in?

We serve all of Maplewood and surrounding communities, including Downtown Maplewood, Maplewood Heights, Riverside District, Oak Ridge, Sunset Hills, and Meadowbrook. Our team has deep knowledge of each neighborhood's character, schools, amenities, and market trends.

Can you help if I'm relocating from out of state?

Absolutely. We have extensive experience helping families relocate to Maplewood. We offer virtual home tours, detailed neighborhood guides, school information, and can coordinate the entire process remotely if needed. Many of our clients have successfully purchased homes without visiting in person until closing.
