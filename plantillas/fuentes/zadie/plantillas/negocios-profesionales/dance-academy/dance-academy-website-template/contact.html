First Visit

What to Expect

Here's what happens when you visit Rhythm & Motion for a trial class.

Warm Welcome

Our friendly front desk staff will greet you, help with any paperwork, and show you around the facility. Feel free to ask questions!

No Dancewear Needed

For trial classes, just wear comfortable athletic clothing. We'll provide information about required dancewear if you decide to enroll.

Join a Real Class

You'll participate in a regular class with other students at your level. Our instructors will make sure you feel comfortable and have fun!

Parents Welcome

Parents are invited to watch from our comfortable viewing areas. WiFi is available if you need to work while your dancer is in class.

Questions Answered

After class, we'll discuss enrollment, tuition, schedules, and answer any questions. No pressure—take your time deciding!

Easy Enrollment

Ready to join? Enrollment can be completed online or in person. We'll help you choose the right classes and get started!

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a trial class?

Simply call us at (555) 987-6543 or fill out the contact form above. We'll help you choose the right class based on age and experience level, then schedule your free trial!

What should my child wear to dance class?

Each dance style has specific dress code requirements. For trial classes, just wear comfortable athletic clothing. After enrollment, we'll provide detailed information about required dancewear and where to purchase it.

Do you offer classes for adults?

Yes! We offer adult classes in Ballet, Jazz, Hip-Hop, and Contemporary. No prior experience necessary—our adult classes welcome beginners and experienced dancers alike.

How much does it cost?

Monthly tuition ranges from $75-$150 depending on class length and frequency. Registration fee is $50 annually. Multi-class discounts available. Contact us for a complete tuition schedule.

What is the competition team?

Our Company Competition Team is for dedicated dancers ages 8+ who want intensive training and competition opportunities. Auditions are held in May and August. Team members commit to weekly rehearsals and 6-8 competitions per year.

Do I have to participate in recitals?

Recitals are optional but highly encouraged! Our annual spring and winter recitals give students a chance to perform and showcase what they've learned. Recital participation includes costume fees ($60-$80 per costume).

Can my child take multiple classes?

Absolutely! Many students take classes in multiple styles. We offer multi-class discounts: 10% off 2nd class, 15% off 3rd class, 20% off 4th+ classes. Taking multiple styles improves overall technique and versatility.

What if my child has never danced before?

Perfect! Most of our students start with no prior experience. We have beginner classes in every style specifically designed for new dancers. Our patient instructors make learning fun and build confidence from day one.
