All Ages Welcome

Find Your Perfect Class

From beginner to advanced, ages 4 to adult, we offer dance classes in five exciting styles. Every class focuses on proper technique, creative expression, and having fun!

Ages 4+

Ballet

Classical technique and grace

Ballet is the foundation of all dance styles. Our classical ballet classes emphasize proper technique, posture, and grace while building strength, flexibility, and discipline. Students learn traditional barre work, center combinations, and beautiful choreography set to classical music.

Ages: 4-6 (Pre-Ballet), 7-9 (Ballet I), 10-12 (Ballet II), 13+ (Ballet III/IV)
Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Pointe (by invitation)
Benefits: Improved posture, flexibility, discipline, and grace
What to Wear: Pink leotard, pink tights, ballet slippers, hair in bun

Ages 6+

Hip-Hop

Urban styles and high energy

Get your groove on with high-energy hip-hop! Learn the latest urban dance styles including breaking, popping, locking, and freestyle. Hip-hop classes focus on rhythm, coordination, and self-expression through street dance. Students learn age-appropriate choreography to current music while developing their own unique style.

Ages: 6-8 (Mini Hip-Hop), 9-12 (Hip-Hop I), 13+ (Hip-Hop II/III)
Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
Benefits: Improved rhythm, coordination, confidence, and creativity
What to Wear: Comfortable athletic wear, clean sneakers

Ages 8+

Contemporary

Modern and expressive movement

Contemporary dance blends ballet technique with modern movement, emphasizing emotional expression and fluidity. Students explore floor work, improvisation, and lyrical movement while developing their artistic voice. Contemporary classes encourage dancers to connect with the music on a deeper level and tell stories through movement.

Ages: 8-10 (Contemporary I), 11-14 (Contemporary II), 15+ (Contemporary III)
Prerequisites: At least 1 year of ballet training recommended
Benefits: Enhanced creativity, emotional expression, flexibility, and artistry
What to Wear: Form-fitting dancewear, bare feet or foot undies

Ages 5+

Jazz

Fun, upbeat, and theatrical

Jazz dance is energetic, fun, and expressive! Classes include turns, leaps, kicks, and stylized movement set to upbeat music. Students learn proper jazz technique including isolations, syncopation, and performance quality. Jazz classes incorporate elements of Broadway, commercial dance, and musical theater, making them perfect for students who love to perform.

Ages: 5-7 (Pre-Jazz), 8-10 (Jazz I), 11-14 (Jazz II), 15+ (Jazz III)
Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
Benefits: Improved coordination, showmanship, energy, and performance skills
What to Wear: Black leotard, tan tights, tan jazz shoes

Ages 6+

Acro

Tumbling meets dance

Acro dance combines classical dance technique with acrobatic elements. Students learn cartwheels, handstands, back walkovers, aerials, and other gymnastic skills while maintaining the grace and fluidity of dance. Classes focus on strength, flexibility, balance, and control. Acro is perfect for dancers who love to flip, tumble, and defy gravity!

Ages: 6-8 (Acro I), 9-12 (Acro II), 13+ (Acro III/IV)
Levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
Benefits: Increased strength, flexibility, body awareness, and confidence
What to Wear: Form-fitting dancewear, bare feet

Elite Training

Company Competition Team

Take your dancing to the next level with our award-winning competition team.

What is Company Team?

Our Company Competition Team is designed for dedicated dancers who want intensive training and performance opportunities. Team members receive advanced technique classes, work with professional choreographers, and compete at 6-8 regional and national competitions per year.

Requirements

  • Ages 8+ with intermediate to advanced skill level
  • Audition required (held in May and August)
  • Commitment to weekly rehearsals and choreography sessions
  • Participation in all assigned competitions and performances
  • Enrollment in at least 2 weekly technique classes
  • Strong work ethic, positive attitude, and team spirit

What You'll Gain

  • Intensive training from professional instructors
  • Guest choreographers and master classes
  • Travel to exciting competition destinations
  • Lifelong friendships with dedicated dancers
  • Scholarships and performance opportunities
  • College dance program preparation

Our Track Record

Over 15 national titles, 50+ regional first-place wins, and numerous special awards including Best Choreography, Judges' Choice, and Entertainers of the Year. Our alumni have gone on to dance professionally, perform on Broadway, and teach at prestigious dance institutions.

Try Before You Commit

Free Trial Class

Not sure which class is right for you? Come try a free class and see what Rhythm & Motion is all about!

How It Works

Simply contact us to schedule your free trial class. We'll help you choose the right class based on age and experience level. No dancewear required for your first class—just wear comfortable athletic clothing and be ready to have fun!

What to Expect

You'll join a regular class with other students at your level. Our friendly instructors will make you feel welcome and help you learn the basics. Parents are welcome to watch from our viewing area. After class, we'll answer any questions and provide enrollment information.

Tuition Information

Monthly tuition varies by class length and frequency. Most classes range from $75-$150 per month. Multi-class discounts available. Registration fee: $50 annually. Costume fees apply for recitals (typically $60-$80 per costume).

Payment Plans

We offer flexible payment options including monthly auto-pay, sibling discounts, and need-based scholarships. No dancer is turned away due to financial hardship—please inquire about our scholarship program.
