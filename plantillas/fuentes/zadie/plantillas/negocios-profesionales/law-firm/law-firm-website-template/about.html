About Our Firm

Three decades of dedicated legal service to the Charleston community and beyond.

Our Story

In 1994, Robert Caldwell Sr. left a prestigious regional firm to pursue a different vision of practicing law. He believed that clients deserved more than billable hours and form letters. They deserved a lawyer who knew their name, understood their situation, and would fight as hard for them as he would for his own family.

He started with a single office on Broad Street, a paralegal, and a commitment to doing things right. Word spread quickly. Within five years, the firm had outgrown its original space and welcomed its first associate attorney. By 2005, Caldwell & Associates had moved to Commerce Plaza and grown to a team of four lawyers.

Today, our firm employs eight attorneys and a support staff of twelve, serving clients across Charleston County and throughout the South Carolina Lowcountry. Despite our growth, we have never wavered from the founding principle that every client deserves personal, dedicated attention from an experienced attorney.

Our Attorneys

Each member of our team brings specialized expertise and a shared commitment to achieving the best possible outcomes for our clients.

Robert Caldwell Sr.

Founding Partner

With 35 years of trial experience, Robert has built a reputation as one of Charleston's most respected family law attorneys. A graduate of the University of South Carolina School of Law, he has been recognized by South Carolina Super Lawyers every year since 2008. Robert's approach combines thorough preparation with a deep understanding of the human elements in every case.

Margaret Chen

Managing Partner

Margaret joined the firm in 2004 and became managing partner in 2016. She leads our business and real estate practice, bringing 20 years of experience in commercial transactions, corporate governance, and business litigation. A graduate of Duke Law School, Margaret is known for her strategic thinking and her ability to find practical solutions to complex business problems.

David Okafor

Senior Associate

David brings 12 years of courtroom experience to our personal injury and criminal defense practices. Before joining Caldwell & Associates, he spent four years as an assistant public defender, where he tried over 50 jury cases. His aggressive advocacy and meticulous case preparation have resulted in millions of dollars in settlements and verdicts for injured clients.

Sarah Whitfield

Associate

Sarah heads our estate planning and probate practice. In her seven years with the firm, she has drafted hundreds of wills, trusts, and estate plans for individuals and families of all sizes. A graduate of Charleston School of Law, Sarah is also a certified mediator and frequently lectures on elder law topics at community organizations throughout the region.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make and every case we take.

Integrity

We are honest with our clients about the strengths and challenges of their cases. We never overpromise, and we always deliver on our commitments. Our reputation is built on trust.

Accessibility

Your attorney should be available when you need them. We return calls within 24 hours, provide regular case updates, and make sure you always have a direct line to your legal team.

Results

We measure our success by the outcomes we achieve for our clients. Every strategy, every motion, every negotiation is focused on securing the best possible result in your case.

Personal Attention

We limit our caseload deliberately so every client receives the individualized attention they deserve. You are never just a file number at Caldwell & Associates.

Rooted in Our Community

Caldwell & Associates is more than a law firm. We are part of the fabric of Charleston. Our attorneys serve on the boards of local nonprofits, coach youth sports, and volunteer with legal aid organizations that provide free representation to those who cannot afford it.

We are proud sponsors of the Charleston Legal Aid Society, the Lowcountry Food Bank, and the MUSC Children's Hospital Annual Gala. Robert Caldwell Sr. has served on the Charleston County Bar Association's Pro Bono Committee for over 15 years, and our firm provides more than 200 hours of pro bono legal services annually.

We believe that a law firm has a responsibility to give back to the community it serves. When Charleston thrives, we all thrive.
