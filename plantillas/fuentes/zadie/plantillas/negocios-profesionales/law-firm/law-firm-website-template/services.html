Practice Areas

Experienced legal representation across a full range of practice areas, from sensitive family matters to complex business litigation.

Family Law

Family legal matters are among the most emotionally charged situations you will ever face. At Caldwell & Associates, we approach every family law case with the sensitivity it demands and the legal skill it requires. Our attorneys have decades of combined experience helping families navigate divorce, custody disputes, and other deeply personal transitions.

We work to achieve fair resolutions through negotiation whenever possible, but we are fully prepared to litigate aggressively when your interests demand it. Our goal is always to protect your rights and your children's wellbeing while minimizing the emotional and financial toll of the legal process.

  • Divorce and legal separation
  • Child custody and visitation
  • Child support and spousal support
  • Prenuptial and postnuptial agreements
  • Adoption and guardianship
  • Domestic violence protective orders

Business Law

Whether you are launching a startup or managing a multi-million dollar enterprise, sound legal counsel is essential to protecting your investment and fueling your growth. Our business law practice serves companies at every stage, from formation through succession planning and everything in between.

Margaret Chen and our business law team bring a pragmatic, commercially minded approach to legal challenges. We understand that legal advice must be practical and actionable, not just technically correct. Our clients rely on us as trusted advisors who understand their business as well as the law.

  • Business formation and entity selection
  • Contract drafting, review, and negotiation
  • Commercial disputes and business litigation
  • Mergers, acquisitions, and asset purchases
  • Regulatory compliance and licensing
  • Partnership and shareholder agreements

Estate Planning

A well-crafted estate plan is one of the most important gifts you can give your family. It ensures your wishes are honored, your assets are protected, and your loved ones are spared the burden of uncertainty during an already difficult time. At Caldwell & Associates, we create comprehensive estate plans tailored to your unique family situation and financial circumstances.

Sarah Whitfield leads our estate planning practice with a focus on clarity, thoroughness, and personal attention. She takes the time to understand your family dynamics, financial goals, and concerns before recommending a plan that addresses every contingency.

  • Last wills and testaments
  • Revocable and irrevocable trusts
  • Powers of attorney and healthcare directives
  • Probate and estate administration
  • Trust administration and modification
  • Elder law and Medicaid planning

Personal Injury

When someone else's negligence causes you harm, the physical, emotional, and financial consequences can be overwhelming. Insurance companies have teams of adjusters and lawyers working to minimize your claim. You deserve someone equally committed to fighting for your rights.

David Okafor and our personal injury team handle cases on a contingency fee basis, meaning you pay nothing unless we win. We investigate every aspect of your case, work with medical experts to document your injuries, and negotiate aggressively with insurance companies. When a fair settlement cannot be reached, we are fully prepared to take your case to trial.

  • Auto, truck, and motorcycle accidents
  • Workplace injuries and workers' compensation
  • Medical malpractice
  • Slip and fall / premises liability
  • Wrongful death claims
  • Product liability

Real Estate Law

Real estate transactions are among the largest financial decisions most people ever make. Whether you are buying your first home, developing a commercial property, or dealing with a boundary dispute, you need an attorney who understands the complexities of South Carolina real estate law and can protect your interests at every stage.

Our real estate practice handles everything from straightforward residential closings to complex commercial transactions. We conduct thorough title searches, review contracts with a fine-tooth comb, and anticipate potential issues before they become expensive problems.

  • Residential and commercial closings
  • Commercial lease negotiation
  • Landlord-tenant disputes
  • Title examination and insurance
  • Zoning and land use matters
  • Construction contracts and disputes

Criminal Defense

Being charged with a crime is one of the most frightening experiences a person can face. Your freedom, your reputation, and your future are all at stake. At Caldwell & Associates, we believe that everyone charged with a crime deserves vigorous, competent defense, and that is exactly what we provide.

David Okafor brings his extensive trial experience and his background as a former public defender to every criminal case. He understands how prosecutors build their cases, and he knows how to dismantle them. From the initial investigation through trial and appeal, we are relentless in defending your rights.

  • DUI and traffic offenses
  • Misdemeanors and felonies
  • White collar crimes and fraud
  • Drug offenses
  • Expungement and record sealing
  • Probation violations
