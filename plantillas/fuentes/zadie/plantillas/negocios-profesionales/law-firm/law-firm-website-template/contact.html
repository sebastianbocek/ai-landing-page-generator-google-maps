Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with our firm.

How much does an initial consultation cost?

Your initial consultation is completely free and carries no obligation. During this meeting, we will discuss your situation, evaluate your legal options, and help you understand the potential costs and timeline for your case. For personal injury cases, we work on a contingency fee basis, meaning you pay nothing unless we recover compensation for you. For other matters, we will provide a clear fee structure before you commit to anything.

How long will my case take?

The timeline for any legal matter depends on its complexity, the cooperation of the parties involved, and whether the case goes to trial. A straightforward real estate closing might take a few weeks, while a contested divorce or personal injury lawsuit could take several months to over a year. During your initial consultation, we will provide a realistic estimate based on the specifics of your situation and keep you informed of any developments that affect the timeline.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring any documents related to your legal matter, such as contracts, correspondence, police reports, medical records, court filings, or insurance information. Also bring a list of questions you would like answered and a timeline of key events. The more information you can provide during our initial meeting, the better we can evaluate your case and advise you on next steps. If you are unsure whether a document is relevant, bring it anyway.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we understand that legal representation is a significant expense, and we work with our clients to find manageable payment arrangements. We accept major credit cards and can arrange monthly payment plans for many types of cases. For personal injury matters, our contingency fee arrangement means no upfront costs at all. We will discuss all fees and payment options transparently during your initial consultation so there are no surprises.

Can I reach my attorney directly?

Absolutely. One of the most common complaints people have about their lawyers is that they can never get them on the phone. At Caldwell & Associates, we take a different approach. Your attorney will provide you with their direct phone number and email address. We commit to returning all client calls and emails within 24 hours. For urgent matters, we have an after-hours answering service that can reach your attorney immediately.

Many legal issues are time-sensitive. Statutes of limitations, filing deadlines, and opposing counsel do not wait. Contact us today and let us start working for you.
