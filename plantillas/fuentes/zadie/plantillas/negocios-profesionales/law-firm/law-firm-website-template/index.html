A Tradition of Excellence Since 1994

When Robert Caldwell Sr. opened the doors of his solo practice in downtown Charleston over three decades ago, he made a simple promise: every client would receive the personal attention and zealous advocacy they deserve. That promise still guides everything we do.

Today, our team of eight experienced attorneys brings that same commitment to every case. We combine the resources and sophistication of a large firm with the accessibility and care of a small one. At Caldwell & Associates, you will always know the attorney handling your case, and they will always know you.
